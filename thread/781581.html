试过了，可以
  1. 3;minecraft:bedrock,59*minecraft:stone,3*minecraft:dirt,minecraft:grass,minecraft:snow_layer:5;12;village,biome_1
复制代码

这个snow_layer:5就是代表6格雪
你要想改成整块雪的话就minecraft:snow_layer:7或者直接写minecraft:snow
