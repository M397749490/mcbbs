This, dear viewers, is the by far largest build I have ever tried to complete by myself.
The entire ship in its current shape is 504 blocks long and it took me a little over 5 weeks to build it to the state you can now see on the screenshots above.
Still, it is nowhere close to be finished and I cannot say if it will even look similar when I am done with it as I tend to change my mind alot while building along.
The ship, when finished, will be featured in a seperate PMC post so please do not wait for any kind of updates on this post after the frist upload as those changes will all be included in the following upload.
