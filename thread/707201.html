标题: 迷之毁存档指令
昨天做地形生成器的时候 决定用刷怪笼来刷草 然后顺手把talllgrass 打成 tall_grass了然后启动命令方块的一瞬间 存档崩溃了...重进继续崩...........然后默默的打开了mcedit
源指令：
  1. execute @e[name=craft_map,type=ArmorStand] ~ ~21 ~ /setblock ~ ~ ~ minecraft:mob_spawner 0 replace {EntityId:FallingSand,SpawnCount:1000,SpawnRange:100,RequiredPlayerRange:200,Delay:10,MinSpawnDelay:20,MaxSpawnDelay:20,MaxNearbyEntities:1000,SpawnData:{CustomName:kw,Time:1,Block:tall_grass,Data:1,Date:0}}
复制代码
简化版:
  1. /setblock ~ ~ ~ minecraft:mob_spawner 0 replace {EntityId:FallingSand,SpawnData:{Block:tall_grass,Data:1}}
复制代码
