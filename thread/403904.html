不会燃烧。
100%不会掉头的:
  1. /summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {Equipment:[{},{},{},{},{id:397,Count:1,Damage:2}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F]}
