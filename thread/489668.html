  1. /execute @e[type=Zombie,name=qishi] ~ ~ ~ summon Zombie ~ ~ ~ {Riding:{id:EntityHorse,Type:3,Tame:1},Equipment:[{id:276,Count:1},{},{},{},{}]}

  1. /tp @e[type=Zombie,name=qishi] ~ ~-9999 ~

作者: End-Enderman    时间: 2015-8-19 13:28
LZ很抱歉的告诉您，entitydata并无法改变Riding。若用entitydata改成有钻石剑的僵尸的话，可以用
  1. entitydata @e[type=Zombie,name=qishi] {Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:diamond_sword",Count:1b},{},{},{},{}]}
复制代码

若想生成这样的僵尸，请使用
  1. summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {Riding:{id:EntityHorse,Type:3,Tame:1},Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:diamond_sword",Count:1b},{},{},{},{}]}
复制代码

复制代码
然后kill僵尸
  1. /kill <span style="background-color: rgb(247, 247, 247); line-height: 1.5;">@e[type=Zombie,name=qishi]</span>
复制代码
即可
