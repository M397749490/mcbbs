标题: 关于药水云检测
请问一下如何检测药水云的不同药水属性？
比如我在原版酿造出滞留型剧毒药水和滞留型再生药水，把他们丢出去生成药水云，命令方块如何在世界中出现剧毒药水云时说“1”，在出现再生药水云时说“2”？
版本1.10.2

作者: Chelover_C60    时间: 2020-1-20 10:39
通过给指定药水云添加标签来区分不同nbt，然后检测到指定药水云后执行命令即可

/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add poison {Potion:"minecraft:poison"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add poison {Potion:"minecraft:long_poison"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add poison {Potion:"minecraft:strong_poison"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add regeneration {Potion:"minecraft:regeneration"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add regeneration {Potion:"minecraft:long_regeneration"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=AreaEffectCloud] add regeneration {Potion:"minecraft:strong_regeneration"}
/execute @e[type=AreaEffectCloud,tag=poison] ~ ~ ~ say 1
/execute @e[type=AreaEffectCloud,tag=regeneration] ~ ~ ~ say 2
