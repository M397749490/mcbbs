1.13以下
  1. /setblock ~ ~ ~ chest 0 destroy {CustomName:"名字",Items:[{Slot:13b,id:"minecraft:diamond",Count:1b,Damage:0s}]}
复制代码



1.13以上
  1. /setblock ~ ~ ~ minecraft:chest{CustomName:"{"text":"名字"}",Items:[{Slot:13b,id:"minecraft:diamond",Count:1b,Damage:0s}]} destroy
复制代码
