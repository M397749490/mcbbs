@jeb_
Reminder! ONLY enter username/passwords in the OFFICIAL Minecraft launcher! A number of other launchers are stealing login tokens :(

@jeb_
I don't want to point out a specific one, as it gives the false impression that the other ones are safe.
我不想举一个（会这么做的启动器）的例子，因为这样会给人造成一种感觉：其他的（第三方启动器）很安全。

@jeb_
A proper mod loader should install the files, folders and prepare profiles (in the official launcher), but not ask for login credentials.
一个制作得当的Mod加载器应该安装文件、文件夹以及修改（官方启动器的）profiles文件，但不应要求输入任何登录凭据。

备注：

1: 指用来存储登录信息的文件，类似浏览器中的cookie
