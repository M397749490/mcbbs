  1. effect @p 药水效果 持续时间 药水等级 粒子效果
复制代码
或是直接把下面的命令打入命令方块
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~1 ~2 {Time:1,Block:"minecraft:redstone_block",Passengers:[{id:"FallingSand",Time:1,Block:"minecraft:activator_rail",Passengers:[{id:"MinecartCommandBlock",Command:"setblock ~0 ~2 ~0 repeating_command_block 1 replace {auto:1b,Command:"effect @p 药水效果 持续时间 药水等级 粒子效果"}"},{id:"MinecartCommandBlock",Command:"/setblock ~ ~-1 ~ minecraft:lava 15"},{id:"MinecartCommandBlock",Command:"setblock ~ ~ ~ air 0"}]}]}
