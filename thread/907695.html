LexShino Shaders - 夺梦迷彩

作者原文：
Hello!! Finally, after several months of development, here is the official release of the 2th version of my shader! This version is a huge code re-writting and re-organisation, which probably make this shaderpack very cool to use.
-DOF "by DeDelner" thanks to him
-Blurred distance
-Galaxies, infinite Ocean, fantasy stars and Rainbow XP "by Builderb0y" thanks to him
-FXAA 3.11 "by Cap Tatsu" thanks to him
-Motion blur with settings
-Cinema mod with distortion of optional tapes
-Bloom with settings
-Dynamic Light with a lot of settings
-2 grains film style with settings ( do not choose both at the same time)
-Berserk mod with setting (just red vignette or red vignette + red hand) with a weapon equipped
-Possibility to choose 64 color for bloc selection
-2 Dirty Lens effect options just for to have dirty screen
-Shadow with filter and settings for opacity, resolution...
-Rainbow with settings (I used the idea of Builderb0y)
-Sun and Moon rays with settings
-FOG with settings
-Raindrop on the screen
-Chromatic abberation
-New rain
-Wind field with settings for speed
-Animal Crossing style with settings for curve
-Water blur with blur adjustment
-Hand movement (2 options)
-Mod Flash Victim for see Mobs in dark
-Water movement when you have your head under water
-Ambient occlusion
-Cel-Shading with settings
-Ultra Vignette with settings
-ToneMap with brightness, saturation, contrast, exposure settings
-Possibility to configure the brightness of the blocks that emit light (block in hands)
-Possibility to configure the lighting of the blocks that emit light (block in hands)
-Possibility to configure the lighting of the blocks that emit light (block on the ground)
-Possibility to configure the brightness of the blocks that emit light (block on the ground)
... and many more

Install-材质安装

对1.7.10以上（包括1.7.10）用户:
1、确保你安装了Optifine，
2、在.minecraft文件夹内找到shaderpacks文件夹，
3、把下载的光影包直接放到shaderpacks文件夹里面。

对1.7.10以下用户：
1、确保你安装了Optifine及光影Mod（如GLSL），
2、在.minecraft文件夹内找到shaderpacks文件夹，
3、把下载的光影包直接放到shaderpacks文件夹里面。
