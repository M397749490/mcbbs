标题: 关于魔改的一个小提醒
用MineTweakerRecipeMaker mod改的时候没办法保存物品的数据，但是只要我们对他的代码进行一点手动调整原来的代码

recipes.addShaped(<Thaumcraft:ItemPrimalCrusher>), [[<minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>], [<minecraft:diamond>, <Thaumcraft:ItemPrimalCrusher>, <minecraft:diamond>], [<minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>]]);


手动把物品改成无限耐久后：recipes.addShaped(<Thaumcraft:ItemPrimalCrusher>.withTag({Unbreakable: 1 as byte}), [[<minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>], [<minecraft:diamond>, <Thaumcraft:ItemPrimalCrusher>, <minecraft:diamond>], [<minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>, <minecraft:diamond>]]);
就可以方便的解决这个问题啦(不做修改的话就是用普通的元始杵合成普通的元始杵，那也太蠢了)
