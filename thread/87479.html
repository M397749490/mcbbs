無須任何MOD
輸入/gamerule


doFireTick         = toggles fire spread on/off(火焰擴散開/關)
doMobLoot          = toggles drop items on/off (xp still drops)(怪物掉落物開/關)經驗仍會掉
doMobSpawning      = toggles on/off mob spawning(生怪開/關)
doTileDrops        = toggles farmed items (wheat, melon slices, etc.) drops on/off(是否掉落農作物開/關)種子仍會掉
                     (still drop seeds even if off)
keepInventory      = toggles keeping your inventory when you die on/off(死亡保留倉庫物品開/關)
mobGriefing        = toggles Creeper block damage, Wither explosions and Enderman block pickup on/off(控制苦力怕.凋零妖方塊破                       壞.enderman拾取開/關)
