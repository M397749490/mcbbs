Rules of The Survival Games:             饥饿游戏规则
  • Only one player can win The Survival Games.     只能有一个玩家赢饥饿游戏。
  • You can not break any blocks, expect for leaves, vines and mushrooms. 你不能毁方块，除了树叶，藤蔓和蘑菇。
  • You are only allowed to place blocks you find in chests. 你只能放方块在箱子里 找到的方块 。
  • Your inventory must be empty before you start the game. 你不能有任何东西在游戏开始前。
  • No player is allowed to enter the host house. 不能有任何人进入主机房。
