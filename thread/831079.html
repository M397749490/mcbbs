PILLAGERS AND MORE GREAT FEATURES NOW IN BETA!
掠夺者以及很多新特性进入 Beta 测试阶段！

Available in betas on Xbox One, Windows 10 and Android!
在 Xbox One、Windows 10 和安卓设备上可用！


Have you tried playing a Minecraft beta? It's where we test upcoming features of the game! To try these features, you have to play a version of Minecraft that supports betas (so that's the Windows 10, Xbox One and Android versions). You can see how to sign up for a Minecraft beta on each version here. You can also find more help regarding this specific beta on our feedback site.
你试过 Minecraft beta 吗？我们在 beta 中测试游戏的新特性！如果你想尝试这些新特性，你需要玩支持 beta 的 Minecraft 版本（即 Windows 10、Xbox One 以及安卓版）。你可以在这里看到适用于不同版本的注册 Minecraft beta 的方法（英文页面）。你也可以在我们的反馈网站（英文）中找到更多有关 beta 的帮助。

Remember, beta features can be unstable – the whole reason we want players trying them this early is to give us feedback and catch bugs for us to fix – so backup your worlds! We've added loads of new stuff to for the 1.9.0.0 beta, so we thought we'd update this article with all the new features you can try, like Pillagers, Bamboo, new flowers and tons of new stairs and slabs! For a full list of changes and fixes, as well as a great place to give us feedback about the beta, head to our feedback site.

PARTICLE EFFECTS AND ANIMATION ADD-ONS
粒子和动画 ADD-ON

Not familiar with add-ons? They're a fantastic way to bring even greater levels of customisation to all editions of Minecraft. You can learn more about them here!
不熟悉 add-on？它们是对任何版本（译注：指任何基岩版）的 Minecraft 进行极自由的自定义的方式。你可以在这里了解更多（英文页面）。

Previously, add-ons only allowed you to modify existing mobs in the game, but with New Entities you can add more mobs. You can use Data Driven Spawning to control which mobs spawn in your worlds. Animations will give you more customisation options, so you’ll be able to craft the horrifying eight-legged mob of your dreams/our nightmares. Particles will let you pretty up your mobs with cool effects.
以前，add-on 只允许你修改游戏中现存的生物，不过有了添加实体这一功能，你可以添加更多生物了。你可以使用数据驱动生成来控制那些生物可以在你的世界里面生成。动画给了你更多的自定义选项，所以你可以制作恐怖的八条腿的生物来实现你的梦想/我们的噩梦。粒子允许你能通过添加很酷的效果来美化你的生物。

These options are now in the beta, but we've also added resource and behaviour packs that you can download to access some special example mobs we showed off at MINECON Earth! Are they as cute and cuddly as the cats and pandas? Er... well...
尽管这些选项现在仍处于 beta 阶段，我们提供了一套资源包和行为包，你可以下载它们并查看我们在 MINECON Earth 上展示的一些特殊的生物例子！它们是否和猫和熊猫一样可爱呢？嗯…啊…


看，我们在以自己的方式美丽，不是吗？

First of all, make sure you're signed up for the beta. You'll be able to tell because the word 'Beta!' will be in large yellow text below the title on the main menu. 首先，确保你注册了 beta。你可以通过看主界面上有没有大黄字「Beta!」来得知你是否成功注册了。顶部的小白字显示了你所注册的 beta 版本，确保它是「beta 1.8.0.8」。如果你需要帮助，看看这个链接（英文）
