计分板确实有这个判据，不过这个判据名在不同版本不一样。1.12及之前是stat.craftItem.minecraft.物品，1.13是minecraft.crafted:minecraft.物品
示例
  1. /scoreboard objectives add craft minecraft.crafted:minecraft.diamond_block
  2. /scoreboard objectives add craft stat.craftItem.minecraft.diamond_block
