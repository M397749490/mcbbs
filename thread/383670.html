故事：
Long ago, throughout the realms of Minecraftia, a band of brothers was set out to locate the lost citadel of Atlas. After years and years of traveling, they came upon a large sphere floating throughout the clouds. Most of the band thought it as Gods head, but as they drew closer and closer, "Gods head" turned out to be a duplicate world of Minecraftia. Which was held by Atlas himself. Many readings have said Atlas is surrounded by ice mountains, dangerous waterfalls, and many more treacherous items. As the band grew closer and closer to the base of Atlas, they suddenly felt a bump, and could no longer steer the boat. As the band was stuck there for all eternity, they built and built, a magical kingdom for Atlas to watch over.
