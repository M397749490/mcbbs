复制代码

SOUND-VOLUME-PITCH-DELAY
声音类型(上面的类型)-音量(0~1之间是音量, 超过1会增加范围)-偏移(一般填写1)-延迟(20=1秒)

例子
  1. Shooting:
  2.         Cancel_Left_Click_Block_Damage: true
  3.         Cancel_Right_Click_Interactions: true
  4.         Projectile_Amount: 1
  5.         Projectile_Type: energy
  6.         Projectile_Subtype: 40-1-NONE-0
  7.         Projectile_Damage: 12
  8.         Sounds_Shoot: WITHER_SHOOT-1-2-0,EXPLODE-1-2-0
  9.     Reload:
  10.         Enable: true
  11.         Reload_Amount: 8
  12.         Reload_Bullets_Individually: true
  13.         Reload_Duration: 10
  14.         Sounds_Reloading: IRONGOLEM_THROW-1-2-2
复制代码

Sounds_Shoot: WITHER_SHOOT-1-2-0,EXPLODE-1-2-0
指的是但射击时同时播放凋灵凋灵声音和爆炸声音, 无延迟
Sounds_Reloading: IRONGOLEM_THROW-1-2-2
指的是当换弹时播放铁傀儡扔生物的声音在20分之2秒后
