Emeralds2Levels



【绿色天空的绿宝石系列插件搬运及汉化第七弹——Emeralds2Levels】

Emeralds2Levels is a plugin in development being used to give functionality to emeralds besides trading them to NPC's. It's part of a full Emerald reward/functionality plugin I'm working on for my RP PvP server. This is my first plugin and there are more planned features in future updates including turning levels back into emeralds and trading emeralds out for other items.
Emeralds2Levels是一个用于给绿宝石除了交易以外更多功能的插件。这是作者的第一个插件。作者还没完成就弃坑了= =

安装

扔进plugins文件夹

使用

把一定数量的绿宝石拿在手上，输入/buylevels

作者还提供了几个作死的绿宝石合成= =


权限
  1. 换级别的权限: Emeralds2Levels.buylevel
  2. 合成里的绿宝石炸弹的权限: Emeralds2Levels.grenade
