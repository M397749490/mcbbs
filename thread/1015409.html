附魔，这里有一个办法（举例蓝的，其他自己摸索）
前置：/scoreboard objectives add a dummy ["死亡"]
1.存档点，重复用/spawnpoint @a[team=队伍名称] 某个小黑屋的位置
然后在小黑屋里放个/tesfor @e[r=3]，下面中继器连接几个命令方块，脉冲命令方块1./scoreboard player tag add @p 死亡
脉冲命令方块2./scoreboard players set @a[tag=死亡] a 100
脉冲命令方块3./gamemode 3 @a[tag=死亡]
重复执行（分开的，全部是始终执行）
重复执行命令方块1./tp @a 地图中间坐标（说白了就是死亡平台的位置）
重复执行命令方块2./title @a[tag=死亡,score_a=100,score_a_min=100] title  [{"text":"5","color":"gold","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false},{"text":"秒后即将复活","color":"aqua","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false}]
重复执行命令方块3./title @a[tag=死亡,score_a=80,score_a_min=80] title [{"text":"4","color":"gold","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false},{"text":"秒后即将复活","color":"aqua","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false}]
重复执行命令方块4./title @a[tag=死亡,score_a=60,score_a_min=60] title  [{"text":"3","color":"gold","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false},{"text":"秒后即将复活","color":"aqua","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false}]
重复执行命令方块5./title @a[tag=死亡,score_a=40,score_a_min=40] title  [{"text":"2","color":"gold","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false},{"text":"秒后即将复活","color":"aqua","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false}]
重复执行命令方块6./title @a[tag=死亡,score_a=20,score_a_min=20] title  [{"text":"1","color":"gold","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false},{"text":"秒后即将复活","color":"aqua","bold":true,"italic":false,"underlined":false,"strikethrough":false,"obfuscated":false}]
重复执行命令方块7./execute @a[tag=死亡,score_a=0,score_a_min=0,team=蓝] tp @p 另外一个拿装备的自闭小屋
连锁（和上面的）命令方块7./execute @a[tag=死亡,score_a=0,score_a_min=0] /scoreboard player tag remove 死亡
拿装备的自闭小屋
小屋下面放给重复命令方块：/tesfor @p[r=3]
