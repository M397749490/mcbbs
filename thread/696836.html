作者原话

  1. Ever wanted to allow your players to show their items in chat, just to be sure they really have the item they want to sell to you? Well, this plugin does just that.
  2. It's a lightweight plugin that works with ALL chat formatting plugins, including Essentials, Factions, DeluxeChat and ANY OTHER PLUGIN you can think of.
  3. It also supports offering in depth info about the item, just like you'd hover your cursor over the real item in your inventory.
  4. All you have to do is type your message while having the item you want to show in your hand, and using the configured placeholder.
