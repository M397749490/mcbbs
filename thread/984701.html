排名第x玩家岛屿积分值
%Level_bskyblock_top_value_#RANK#%
排名第x玩家岛屿名字      
%Level_bskyblock_top_name_#RANK#%

如你想做一个显示前五名玩家的浮空字，就这样写：
  • %Level_bskyblock_top_name_1% 的空岛等级: %Level_bskyblock_top_value_1%
  • %Level_bskyblock_top_name_2% 的空岛等级: %Level_bskyblock_top_value_2%
  • %Level_bskyblock_top_name_3% 的空岛等级: %Level_bskyblock_top_value_3%
  • %Level_bskyblock_top_name_4% 的空岛等级: %Level_bskyblock_top_value_4%
  • %Level_bskyblock_top_name_5% 的空岛等级: %Level_bskyblock_top_value_5%
