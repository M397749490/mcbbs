· 简介
这是一个轻量级的插件
它可以在你睡觉的时候给你回血
当你睡醒时血会自动回满
且它可以让你白天睡觉/设置出生点

A simple plugin which adds the following improvements to beds:
  • Players are fully healed after using a bed.
  • Beds can be used to set your spawn point and heal during the day.
