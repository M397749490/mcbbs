必须用命令方块

不不不，我4#的方法是错误的
比如你想给玩家从效率附魔和水下速掘附魔中选一个
你先在第一个命令方块里输入
/give @p minecraft:enchanted_book 1 0 {ench:[{id:32,lvl:1}]}
第二个
/give @p minecraft:enchanted_book 1 0 {ench:[{id:6,lvl:1}]}
然后随机触发一个命令方块就行
随机触发指令的话就这样发射器随缘玩法（可以多层的哟）

我回来补指令了
五楼的方法太硬核了233我给个简单的方法
前置工作：
/summon minecraft:armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"0"}
/summon minecraft:armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"1"}
/summon minecraft:armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"2"}
/scoreboard players tag @e[name=0] 0
/scoreboard players tag @e[name=1] 1
/scoreboard players tag @e[name=2] 2
/scoreboard objectives add cs dummy
/scoreboard players add @e[tag=1] cs 1
/scoreboard players add @e[tag=2] cs 2
下面两个循环
execute @e[score_cs_min=1,tag=0] ~ ~ ~ give @p minecraft:enchanted_book 1 0 {ench:[{id:0,lvl:1}]}
execute @e[score_cs_min=2,tag=0] ~ ~ ~ give @p minecraft:enchanted_book 1 0 {ench:[{id:32,lvl:1}]}
后接连锁
scoreboard players reset @e[tag=0] cs
scoreboard players reset @e[tag=0] cs
启动
scoreboard players operation @e[tag=0] cs = @r[type=armor_stand] cs
效果：
按下脉冲命令方块，使tag0的盔甲架分数随机等于一个盔甲架，检测盔甲架的分数给予对应的附魔书（我这里设置的是保护和效率）
