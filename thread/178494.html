作者: shxq    时间: 2013-10-1 22:40
For Minecraft 1.6.4ShadersMod v2.0.1 beta 18 also works in Minecraft 1.6.4 (with FML or Forge for Minecraft 1.6.4).

But Optifine in mods folder is not compatible because of change in Forge/FML.
Forge 9.11.0.897/FML 6.4.16.765 can load Optifine in mods folder but it runs Optifine's class transformer after ShadersMod, so Optifine overwrite the change that ShadersMod does, so ShadersMod is not functional.  


我看了官方对Optifine不支持的解释。就是一个先后的问题。要Optifine先载入，再载入Shaders。mod目录是平级的不能分出现后，这时Json的载入优先级就很高了。
