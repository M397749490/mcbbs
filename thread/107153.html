Version 1.5.2 (1.5.2) Release Added Forge Biome Dictionary Support
  • Now works with mods which support the Biome Dictionary

Items:
  • General:
    • Added Element of Water, Fiery Soul and Soulfire
    • Renamed duration of Enchanted Items
      • removed "for"

  • Specific:
    • Ancient Scroll of Repair replaced with Ancient Repair Hammer
      • Ancient Repair Hammer can now repair damaged Anvils
    • Blaze Stave now converts Ender Pearls to Blaze Rods in crafting
      • Durability -45
    • Ender Stave now converts Blaze Rods to Ender Pearls in crafting
      • Durability -45
    • Mandrake now inflicts Nausea for (0:20)
    • Mandrake Stew can now only be consumed once
      • Recovers 4 hearts
      • Restores 4 Hunger
      • 0.8 Saturation
      • Always Edible
    • Mandrake Stew buff increased:
      • Night Vision for (3:00)
      • Water Breathing for (3:00)
    • Meat saturation reduced to 0.8
    • Meat Stew can now be made of (4) Meat Morsel and a Poisonous Potato
    • Meat Stew now only recovers 2 Hunger
      • Meat Stew now has 0.8 Saturation per consumption
    • Ornate Fire Fan now requires Fiery Soul to be repaired instead of Coal
    • Ornate Ice Fan now requires Soulfire to be repaired instead of Snowball

Mobs:
  • Specific:
    • Bone Knight spawn height increased to (20Y)
    • Chochin-Obake health reduced by 20
    • Cyan Flower death animation removed
    • Cyan Flower shadow removed
    • Cyan Flower can be given Element of Water
      • 30% to obtain a Mandrake in exchange
    • Creep increased default spawn rate (+2)
    • Dryad now obtains Slowness and Weakness for (0:05) when on fire
    • Ender Eye increased default spawn rate (+2)
    • Flesh Lich spawn height increased to (20Y)
    • Flesh Lich health reduced by 20
    • Garden Gnome trade table reconfigured:
      • Garden Gnome no longer trades Emeralds/Emerald Shards or Combat Book
        • Garden Gnome now converts (1) Wheat into (2) Seeds
      • Garden Gnome now trades depending on amount of output
        • Maximum of 64 items will the Garden Gnome provide
        • Minimum of remaining 32 items available is required to be able to trade for a Leather Repair Kit
      • Unsuccessful trades result in (2) Pumpkin Seeds
    • Gryphon now plays sound effect (Enderdragon.Wings) when it leaps
    • Harpy now plays sound effect (Enderdragon.Wings) when it leaps
    • Mermaid now obtains Restoration (0:05) when touching water
    • Minotaur model scale increased
    • Naga now obtains Restoration (0:05) when touching water
    • Sahuagin now obtains Restoration (0:05) when touching water
    • Siren now obtains Restoration (0:05) when touching water
    • Spriggan now obtains Slowness and Weakness for (0:05) when on fire
    • Succubus increased default spawn rate (+2)
    • Witch health increased by 20
      • Fixed bug with Witch spawning Spider when initially spawned
    • Wither Cow increased default spawn rate (+2)
    • Yeti model scale increased
    • Yeti now obtains Slowness and Weakness for (0:05)
    • Yuki-Onna now obtains Slowness and Weakness for (0:05)

Mob Drops:
  • General:
    • Rewrote rare drop code
      • Mobs will now only drop one rare drop instead of multiple drops
    • Level 1 Mobs which drop (1) item have a 1/10 chance to drop this item

  • Specific:
    • Baphomet now drops Soulfire instead of Blaze Powder
    • Banshee now drops Soulfire instead of Coal
    • Centaur now drops (1) Meat instead of Meat Morsel
    • Chochin-Obake drops Soulfire instead of Coal
    • Cockatrice now drops Meat Morsel instead of Seeds
    • Dullahan now drops (1) Soulfire
      • Dullahan now only drops Roses
    • Ghost now drops (1) Soulfire
      • Ghost now only drops paper
    • Gnome now drops Seeds instead of Cocoa Beans
    • Gryphon now drops Leather instead of Meat Morsel
    • Mermaid now drops Element of Water instead of Gold Shards
    • Naga now drops Element of Water instead of Meat
    • Sahuagin now drops (1) Element of Water
      • Sahuagin now only drops Raw/Cooked Fish
    • Siren now drops Element of Water instead of Raw/Cooked Fish
    • Succubus now drops (1) Fiery Soul
      • Succubus now only drops Red Mushrooms
    • Wither Cow now drops Meat Morsel instead of Bones




Version 1.5.1 (1.5.2) Release Fixed bug with Mandrake not being consumed when eaten

Items:
  • General:
    • Added Ancient Shovel, Ancient Hoe and Meat Stew

  • Modified:
    • Ancient Axe durability halved
    • Ancient Pickaxe durability halved
    • Mandrake is now always edible
      • Mandrake now inflicts Nausea for (0:10)
    • Mandrake Stew can now be used 2 times
      • Mandrake Stew heals 3 hearts per consumption (total of 6 hearts)
      • Mandrake Stew restores 3 hunger per consumption (total of 6 hunger)
      • Mandrake Stew restores 0.4 saturation per consumption (total of 0.8 saturation)
      • Mandrake Stew inflicts Night Vision for (0:60) per consumption

Mobs:
  • Specific:
    • Cyan Flower is no longer removed when in Peaceful
    • Garden Gnome now looks at other Garden Gnomes
    • Garden Gnome is no longer removed when in Peaceful

Mob Drops:
  • Specific:
    • Bone Knight held sword changed to Iron
    • Cyan Flower has a rare drop of Mandrake
    • Spriggan now has a rare drop of Ancient Shovel instead of Ancient Axe
    • Minotaurus has a rare drop of Ancient Axe
    • Naga has a rare drop of Ancient Hoe
