描述：
这款插件实现了从击杀玩家/怪物上获取预配置好的钱的功能。（意译） This plugin implements the ability to a configurable amount of money from another player after you kill them. You can also get paid for killing mobs.

注意：
1. 钱是从被杀死的玩家身上转移到杀死的人身上
2.为了平衡，获得钱的数量基于受害者的百分比
3.受害者一定比例的钱将全部消失（感觉翻译的不对）
4.玩家也可以通过杀怪挣钱
5.杀死不同怪物可以获得不同的钱
6.从被杀死的玩家身上获得的百分比的钱将永远消失，并且杀死不同怪物获得的钱的数量可以在配置文件中修改
7.在杀死一个怪物或玩家后，你将听到一个“customizable”的声音
E文对照：（有翻译的不对的自己对比下，我都是意译）

Features
  • Money is taking from the victim, and given to the killer
  • The amount of money taken is based on a percentage of the victim's balance
  • A percentage of the victim's money is lost altogether.
  • Players can also make money by killing mobs
  • Each mob can have a different value associated with them.
  • The percentage of money stolen from the victim, percentage of money lost forever, and value of each mob is configurable in the config file
  • Upon killing a mob/player, or dying from a player, you will hear a customizable fanfare play.
指令&权限：
  • /moneythief reloadconfig - 重载插件
  • /moneythief worth - 展示怪物清单与杀死他们获得的金钱
  • /moneythief v - 插件版本查询
权限：

  • moneythief.reloadconfig
  • moneythief.worth

这两个是以前的，其他的如下：（These two are for the previous commands. The rest are as follow）
moneythief.PVE - 杀怪得钱
moneythief.PVP - 杀人得钱
moneythief.bypassPVP - （不会翻译！！）If you have this, you will not activate the PVP event upon your death. You keep you money!
