Overworld Pigman Tower
主世界猪人塔
        As we all know, since the 1.16 update, the consumption of gold ingots and other gold items has increased, and in the past, gold ingots have become a burden for some players.
        众所周知，自从1.16更新以后，金锭金块等金质物品的消耗量变大了，金锭反而成了一些玩家的负担。


        So how can we get gold ingots quickly? So today I will bring you--The main world pig tower.
        那么怎么样才能快速的获得金锭呢？所以今天我就为大家带来----主世界猪人塔。
Principle
原理

       Zombie piglins can spawn on the obsidian at the bottom of the Nether Portal frame in the Overworld. Each nether portal block has a very small probability in each block tick (1⁄2000 in simple mode, 2⁄2000 in normal mode, 3⁄2000 in hard mode), in the Nether Portal A zombie piglin is spawned on the bottom frame of.
       僵尸猪灵能够在主世界的下界传送门框架底部的黑曜石上生成。每一个下界传送门方块在每个方块刻中，都有一个极小的概率（简单模式下为1⁄2000，普通模式下为2⁄2000，困难模式下为3⁄2000），在下界传送门的底部框架上生成一只僵尸猪灵。

      ④ Make a killing device, put a honey block in the middle, put a cube in the lower left and right corners of the honey block, and connect a high-frequency piston next to it. Note that it is an ordinary piston! Place tridents on the squares in the lower left and right corners. The more the better, there is a killing module that is placed down to kill the cubs. (After the trident is pushed and pulled several times, if it is not facing the picture, then turn left 90 degrees or right 90 degrees.)
        ④制作处死装置，中间放蜂蜜块，蜂蜜块的左下角和右下角放方块，旁边接上有高频的活塞，注意是普通活塞！在左下角和右下角的方块上放三叉戟，越多越好，有一个处死模块之所以往下放一个是为了干掉幼崽，不然到时候死机了不怪我！（ 三叉戟被推拉几次后，如果朝向不是朝向图片的那样子的话，那就要左拐90度或者右拐90度。）

          ⑥ Then we will make a rapid fire device, first according to the picture, two investigators face to face, produce high frequency, and then connect the investigator behind, forming a high frequency circuit. Then put the workbench next to the scout (because the workbench will be ignited, but will not be burned), put another row of workbenches on the workbench, pour magma in the middle, and the rapid fire device is ready.
        ⑥然后我们来做快速起火装置，先按图片那样子，两个侦查者面对面，产生高频，然后在后面也接上侦查者，形成一条高频线路。然后在侦查者边上放上工作台（因为工作台虽然会被点燃，但是不会被烧毁）在工作台上再放一排工作台，中间倒上岩浆，快速起火装置就做好了。
