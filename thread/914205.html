config里面也可以调整
也能在服务端的配置文件categories下查看该分类
Categories:
  weapons_armor:
    id: '283'
    name: '&eWeapons&f/&eArmor'
    lore:
    - '&7Here you can find the &cbest'
    - '&7equipment of the server.'
    slot: 12
  others:
    id: '351:10'
    name: '&dOther Crafts'
    lore:
    - '&7A lot of stuff you can craft,'
    - '&7like the &bVIP &6rank.'
    slot: 14
按照格式填写分类即可
