作者原文：
Introduction
After the great pestilent war, much of the respect men had for old magic was lost. What followed was a period of growth in population and the territory of men, as need for resources grew. As territory was claimed it was garrisoned by armies, whom were led by knights, whom were loyal to lords, whom knelt before Kings.
These Kings became increasingly powerful and before long came into conflict with each other. The era of conflict between the many elemental kings is known as the ‘two hundred years of blood’. This ended with the final crowning of three Kings and the establishment of the Air Templar.
These four realms adhered to the lay of their land and the principals that had led them to settle. People from these realms are defined and worship the power of their founding fathers, masters of the wind, sea, sun and earth.
Within this world there are rumored to be the old Kings weapons of war, granting the owner complete control of a single element… but they have been missing for centuries.
You arrive on this strange continent with wishes of wealth, glory and exploration! Are you ready to unlock the secrets of this tumultuous land or will you succumb to its many dangers?

Background
This map was largely influenced by the worlds of ‘A Game of Thrones’, ‘Avatar: the Last Airbender’, ‘Tamriel’, ‘The Shadow of Israphel’ and many others. It is a combination of many of their concepts, included feudal kingdoms, elemental themed nations and the practical magic. The spawn will allow you to specialise in a class to explore the world in as well as a steed. Lastly the map is large and thus below you will find a map to help navigate below;

Install
This map is compatible with the latest version of Minecraft (1.13.1) and can be installed by extracting the compressed folder to the saves subfolder of the . minecraft directory. From here it should be possible to play in single player. The Texture Pack used to take the screenshots and the map was created with was :https://www.johnsmithlegacy.co.uk/.
Have fun!
番番译文：
介绍
在瘟疫大爆发后，人们对魔法大大失望。随之而来的是人口增长和人口增长的时期，因为资源的需求在增长。据称领地是由军队驻扎的，他们是由骑士领导的，他们忠于贵族，跪在国王面前。
这些国王变得越来越强大，不久就发生了冲突。许多元素王之间的冲突时代被称为“二百年血”。这结束了三国王的最后加冕和空中圣堂的建立。
这四个王国坚持他们的土地和引导他们定居的主体。来自这些领域的人们被定义并崇拜他们的开国元勋们，风、海、太阳和地球的主人。
在这个世界上，有传言说有老国王的战争武器，让所有者完全控制一个单一的因素…但是他们已经失踪几个世纪了。
你来到这个陌生的大陆，拥有财富、荣耀和探索的愿望！你准备好解开这片混乱的土地的秘密了吗？或者你会屈服于它的许多危险吗？

背景
这张地图在很大程度上受到《王座游戏》、《阿凡达：最后的气宗》、《坦瑞尔》、《伊斯拉斐尔的影子》等诸多游戏影响。它是许多概念的组合，包括封建王国、元素主题的国家和实用的魔法。最后，地图是非常大，因此在下面你会看到一个地图来帮助你游玩该地图；

[Map Installation | 地图安装]
Save存档(正版用户):关闭Minecraft，在Windows开始菜单的搜索框中输入%appdata%/.minecraft，然后按下Enter（回车）按键。把从百度网盘下载回来的地图存档解压缩放到.minecraft\saves文件夹里面。Schematics存档：这类型需要MCEdit。下载完成后解压到桌面，关闭Minecraft，双击mcedit.exe，点击下方import按钮，打开你下载的文件到你需要放的地方，最后点import，按Ctrl+s保存，Ctrl+w关掉存档，点Quit退出。
