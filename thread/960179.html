A Curious Friendship
奇妙的友谊
Crafted by PearlescentMoon
由PearlescentMoon打造

Designed as part of PearlescentMoon’s #ABuildADay project, this personal challenge was designed to help her learn various styles outside her comfort zone during January 2020. Each day she researched a general topic and narrowed it down to find inspiration. Thanks for getting our imaginations buzzing!
作为PearlescentMoon的#ABuildADay（每日建筑）项目的作品之一，这项个人挑战旨在帮助她在2020年1月学习舒适区以外的各种建筑风格。她每天研究一个概括性的话题，并持续地缩小话题范围以寻找灵感。感谢你让我们如同脑子被蜜蜂叮了一样脑洞大开！
