可以添加启动命令来优化服务器
  1. -server?-d64 -Xincgc -Xss512K -XX:+AggressiveOpts -XX:+UseCompressedOops -XX:+UseFastAccessorMethods -XX:+UseBiasedLocking -XX:+DisableExplicitGC -XX:+UseConcMarkSweepGC -XX:+UseCMSCompactAtFullCollection -XX:CMSFullGCsBeforeCompaction=3 -XX:+CMSScavengeBeforeRemark -XX:CMSInitiatingOccupancyFraction=72 -XX:+UseCMSInitiatingOccupancyOnly -XX:+ExplicitGCInvokesConcurrent -XX:+ExplicitGCInvokesConcurrentAndUnloadsClasses -XX:+CMSClassUnloadingEnabled -XX:TargetSurvivorRatio=90 -XX:ParallelGCThreads=4
复制代码

添加这一段在java后面，最大和最小内存自行设置

启动命令：java -Xmx“最大内存” -Xms“最小内存” “启动命令” -jar 服务端核心名称.jar

参照帖子：https://www.mcbbs.net/thread-876594-1-1.html
