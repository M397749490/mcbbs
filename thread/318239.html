使用：
SHIFT +鼠标右键单击该炉会吐出成品内容。（懒人专用{:10_512:}）
合成与普通熔炉相同；
在工作台/背包可以和普通熔炉互换。（见下图）
和普通熔炉一样有光照作用。（见下图）

更新：
1.4：改变了配方，要求石头，而不是鹅卵石
原文（English）
Changed the recipe to require smooth stone instead of cobblestone, which was messing with vanilla achievement progress.

其它更新日志（English）
v1.4 Changed the recipe to require smooth stone instead of cobblestone, which was messing with vanilla achievement progress.  v1.3 Moved output rendering inside the furnace on the top shelf.  v1.2 Added ability to trade the 3D Furnace for a Vanilla one for recipes that require it.  v1.1 Rendered items in the furnace no longer rotate. If a block is on top of the furnace the output is rendered in front of the furnace. Fixed the rendered icon for the furnace in an inventory. If you are in an existing world you can change your Furnace into a 3D Furnace in the Crafting Grid.
