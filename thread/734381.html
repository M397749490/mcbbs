把你弄好材质的武器拿在手上输入/bs nbt add
然后去插件文件夹 找到nbt.yml

然后把物品的NBT 复制好 类似于 1508759906_IRON_SWORD

然后去商店配置文件：举个例子
shop:
  wbf:
    RewardType: Command
    PriceType: points
    Price: 99
    Reward:
    - rpgitem lqwbf give %player_timestamp% 1
    MenuItem:
    - type:EMERALD
    - lore:&f价格:&b99&e点券
    - nbt:1508759906_IRON_SWORD
    Message: '&e购买了XX符'
    InventoryLocation: 1
    ExtraPermission: ''
