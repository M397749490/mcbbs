指令介绍权限
/ArchonCrates打开帮助菜单archoncrates.help
/ArchonCrates key [player] [amount] [keyName]给你的玩家钥匙archoncrates.key
/ArchonCrates giveall [keyName] [amount]给所有在线的玩家钥匙archoncrates.key.all
/ArchonCrates keys查看箱子列表archoncrates.keys
/ArchonCrates crate [keyName]打开一个箱子archoncrates.crate.command
/ArchonCrates create创建一个箱子archoncrates.create
/ArchonCrates remove删除前面的箱子，前面的箱子会变成普通的箱子archoncrates.remove
/ArchonCrates reload重载插件archoncrates.reload
/ArchonCrates info查看有关这个插件的信息archoncrates.info
/ArchonCrates checkupdate检查更新archoncrates.checkupdate
使用钥匙archoncrates.crate.use
创建一把购物标志archoncrates.create.sign.buy
删除一把购物标志archoncrates.remove.sign.buy
使用购物标志archoncrates.sign.buy.use
