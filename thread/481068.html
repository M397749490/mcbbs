The Kraken's Lair







◆Minecraft的一个村庄中一直流传着海怪的传说◆



“海怪”他是海洋之王。
他掌管着全世界的海洋和河流。
如果你被他发现了，那么你将无法逃避他的追杀。
如果你曾经尝试征服海洋，那么他会随时随地的追杀你。
他会将船拖到深渊，然后吃光船员。
所以。。。
千万别忘记带指南针，
别忘记地图，
别在雨天航行，
别进入海怪的领地，
别再去海上了，不然你有可能到他的肚子内。
他有点饿了，所以你逃脱不掉了。

  'The Kraken'  He is the king of the bottom of the sea  
  He rules all the seas and oceans of the world

  If you are found by him, you can't escape anywhere

  He'll chase you anywhere if you ever manage to try and cross the sea

  He'll drag a ship, into the depths of the sea and eat every crew  

  So..

  Don't forget a compass

  Don't forget a map

  Don't sail a ship in a rainy day

  Don't go into The Kraken's Lair

  Don't go against the sea, or you'll be thrown into his stomach   

  He is starting to become hungry, so you might not escape
