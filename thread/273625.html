This is a simple open-source mod made by me that adds a couple of new drinks improving vanilla food and the use of flowers. This new drinks can heal or poison you but always feed you when you need. I have more ideas in mind but I will add them at a time in the futures updates. If you like this mod please comment. Hope you will enjoy it!
