EnderBow

你想传送的时候却没有末影珍珠？
你可以造一把末影弓（EnderBow）来进行传送！
并不用消耗一颗末影珍珠来进行传送!  （但消耗一个箭）
箭的落点在哪你就会被传送到哪！爬多高的山都不怕~

Plugin Metrics is used in lots of big, small, popular and not so popular plugins, to allow authors to see how much their plugin(s) are used as well as where they are being used (this can help with picking which translations to do for example).
