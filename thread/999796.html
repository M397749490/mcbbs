◆介绍◆
A full horse armor overhaul plugin, with enchantments, crafting recipes and durability!
一个拥有完整功能的修改马铠插件，可以让马铠附魔、合成、增加耐久。


Usage:
Enchanting the horse armor is not implemented yet, but you can add an enchantment to armor using an anvil and a book, put the armor on the horse and it will now have the effects of the enchantments!

使用方法：
目前仍不可以直接附魔马铠，你可以用铁砧和附魔书给马铠附魔。把马铠穿在马身上即可。


Durability:
Horse armor now has durability, it will break from consent use. You can use unbreaking so armor lasts longer.
You can also repair it.

耐久相关：
马铠现在有耐久度，它会被损坏。你可以附魔耐久让它使用更长时间，也可以修复它。


附魔:
保护 I - V
火焰保护 I - V
荆棘 I - II
冰霜行者 I - II
耐久 I - III
更多附魔将会加入!
命令/权限
Commands:
/horsearmor - 插件信息
/horsearmor reload - 重新加载(OP)

Permissions:
horsearmor.enchant (Default)
horsearmor.anvil (Default)
horsearmor.frostwalker (Default)
horsearmor.reload (OP)
