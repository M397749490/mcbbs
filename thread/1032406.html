Minecraft Earth R17 Patch Notes – April 28, 2020
我的世界：地球 R17补丁更新公告 — 2020年4月28日
New Stuff!
新东西！
  • Are you up for a Challenge? Build, craft, and collect your way through the all-new Season Challenge system to earn exclusive rewards:
    准备好接受挑战了吗？快来在全新的季度挑战(Season Challenge)系统中来建造、制作出你自己的世界吧！
    • Sign-in for daily Challenges while the Season is active.
      季度挑战开启期间登录即开启每日挑战
    • Some Tappables contain additional Challenges for you to conquer.
      一些可点击物中还会包括额外挑战供你探秘攻坚
    • If you're feeling fancy, complete the entire Season for exclusive Character Creator rewards.
      如果你想变得更加独一无二的话，那就完成整个季度的挑战任务来领取独一无二的“角色创建模块”吧！
    • Season Challenges are only around for a limited time, so don’t dally!
      季度挑战马上就要开始了，所以别废话了，赶紧开始做你的挑战任务吧！
  • Calling all completionists! Keep track of the blocks, items, and Mobs you've collected in the new Player Journal.
    在此呼吁所有的完美主义者，快来新的玩家日志(Player Journal)中看看你迄今为止收集到的方块、物品和生物吧。
  • Bigger is sometimes better. You’re now able to load Buildplates in full-size from shared links.
    有时候越大才越好。现在你能从分享链接那里加载完整大小的建筑板(Buildplates)了。
  • A fancy new night themed Buildplate can now be found in store. Own the night!
    一个全新的超棒的、可适用于建筑底板的夜晚主题已于商店上线。快来感受一下吧！
  • A number of new Adventures have arrived to challenge your might.
    一批富有挑战性的新冒险现已上线。




New Mobs!
新生物！
  • With the addition of the Player Journal, we wanted to add some diversity to the Mobs you know and love! Please welcome the whole herd of new Mob variations to collect:
    因为新功能：玩家日志的引入，所以我们想为你们所喜爱的生物们增加一些多样性，来看看最新更新的的生物变种合集吧：
    • 琥珀鸡(Amber Chicken)
      风暴鸡(Stormy Chicken)
      浅橘牛(Sunset Chicken)
      灰白牛(Ashen Chicken)
      花斑猪(Piebald Pig)
      灰白猪(Pale Pig)
      斑点羊(Flecked Sheep)
      山岩羊(Rocky Sheep)
      西装兔(Vested Rabbit)
      泥足兔(Muddy Foot Rabbit)




Et Cetera!
还有更多！


    • Your people occlusion, bright mode, and precision mode preferences will stick around from session to session instead of resetting each time you play. Nifty!
      你的玩家屏蔽设置、光照等级设置和精度模式首选项设置现在能够保存了，而不是像以前那样每次重新打开就要重新设置。
    • Check out the new Activity Log and Player Stats in Profile screen for even more info about how you play Minecraft Earth.
      我们在个人资料界面上线了新的“活动日志(Activity Log)”和“玩家状态(Player Stats)”栏目，你可以通过这两个栏目来查询更多你在游戏内的“足迹”。
    • Peep that shiny new Adventure Crystal icon! It’s a real gem.
      快来看看新的冒险水晶图标吧！这可是一颗真的宝石呢。


    原文地址：https://help.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360037822211-Minecraft-Earth-Patch-Notes
