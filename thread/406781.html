Special Attacks mod is inspired by an MMORPG "RuneScape".
The mod makes it possible for you to charge your weapons and perform their special attacks. Special attacks will require special energy which can be restored in multiple ways. The mod is fully multiplayer compatible and can be great for PVP.

The mod description is about the 2.0+ (MC 1.8) version of the mod. For MC 1.6.4 info click here.
What does the mod add?
  • Ability to charge any vanilla Minecraft weapons to give them special attacks.
  • Specials attacks/effects for all charged weapons.
  • Special energy bar above your hunger bar whenever there is a charged weapon in your hotbar.
  • 2 categories of special attacks: special attacks and special effects.
Important information:
  • To charge a weapon, simply surround it with 4 Crystals of Energy in a pattern shown in the image below. Please note that the weapon has to be completely unused.
  • Charged weapons will also have increased durability. 100 more uses than the normal ones.
  • To activate or disactivate a special attack press R and the next time you hit a target a special attack will execute. When activated, if you have enough energy the name of the special attack will go green, if not - yellow.
  • Special effects will execute as soon as you press R.
  • All special attacks/effects require special energy which can be restored by drinking Energy Potions.
  • Energy Potions can be made by smelting Crystals of Energy and then combining Liquid Energy, which you get from smelting, with Glass Bottles. Crystals of Energy spawn exactly like diamonds but in bigger stacks.
  • Your energy also regenerates at a rate of 5% every 15 seconds. This effect can be boosted with a Helmet Of Energy.
  • Scroll down for crafting recipes and a list of all special attacks!
Items:
  • Crystal of Energy - Used to charge your weapons. Obtained from mining Energy Ore.
  • Liquid Energy - Obtained from smelting crystals of energy. Used to make potions of energy.
  • Potion of Energy - Can be made by combining liquid energy with normal glass bottles. When used, restores 50%of your special energy. You might need a lot of these.
  • Helmet of Energy - As good as a diamond helm but makes you regenerate energy 2x faster. Requires 5 iron ingots, 2 crystals of energy and 2 diamonds.
Blocks:
  • Energy Ore - Looks similar to gold but glows slightly in the dark. Gives you crystals of energy when mined. Can be mined with any pickaxe.


Special attacks:
  • Leech Food (wooden axe) - Restores hunger by 2.0 when hitting mobs or players. Target player also loses 2.0 of their hunger. Uses 15% of your energy bar.
  • Soulsplit (golden axe) - Restores health by 2.0 hearts when hitting mobs or players. Uses 15% of your energy bar.
  • Leech energy (stone axe) - 50% chance of restoring energy by 30% (45% if you count the used energy) when hitting mobs or players. Target player also loses 15% of their energy bar. Requires 15% of your energy bar.
  • Thunder (iron axe) - Hits your target with a lightning bolt. Uses 45% of your energy bar.
  • Vengeance (diamond axe) - Hits additional damage, which is 2 times your missing health. The less health you have the higher you will hit. Uses 45% of your energy bar.
  • Shotgun (bow) - shoots up to 9 arrows at once when the bow is fully pulled and special attack is active.Uses 5% of your energy bar.
  • Creeper blast (diamond sword) - Invisibility for 2 seconds. Launches you 5 blocks in the air and creates a huge explosion, which absolutely destroys everything on it's path. Uses 55% of your energy bar.
Special effects (execute instantly):
  • Hunger restore (wooden sword) - Saturation III for 15 seconds. Uses 100% of your energy.
  • Rapid heal (golden sword) - Regeneration III, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, Water Breathing for 15 seconds. Uses 50% of your energy.
  • Ninja escape (stone sword) - Creates a "zombie clone" of your character, then gives you Resistance II for 3 seconds, Invisibility for 2 seconds and launches you 18 blocks in the air, which combined with invisibility should look pretty sweet. Uses 50% of your energy.
  • Animal Instincts (iron sword) - Night Vision, Speed, Strength III for 10 seconds. Uses 60% of your energy.
Install guide for 1.8 / 1.6.4:
  • You can get forge here: http://files.minecraftforge.net
  • Download and run the latest or the recommended(if such exists) installer for the needed version. This should create a new or replace the old "Forge" profile in your Minecraft client.
  • Download the mod by clicking the golden "Download Mod" button. Choose the appropriate mod version and download it.
  • Go to "C:/Users/YOUR USER/AppData/Roaming/.minecraft/mods" or to an equivalent on other OS' and put the jar file you've just downloaded there.
  • Start your minecraft client and log in using the "Forge" profile.
  • If you are still having problems getting this mod to work, contact me via PM.
  • Have fun

武器效果如下：
饥饿回复（能量木剑）：饱和三15秒-使用你100%的能量。
快速治疗（能量金剑）：生命恢复三、抗性提升二、防火、水下呼吸各15秒-使用你50%的能量。
忍者躲避（能量石剑）：召唤一只你的替身（名字和你一样、带着和你一样装备的僵尸）然后将你升到离地面18格的空中、获得抗性提升二3秒、隐身2秒-使用你50%的能量。
变身狼人（能量铁剑）：夜视、速度、力量三各十秒、并在使用时发出狼叫-使用你60%的能量。
苦力怕式躲避（能量钻石剑）：产生爆炸并将你弹到空中5格高的地方，获得隐身、抗性提升两秒-使用你60%的能量。
霰弹枪（能量弓）：最多一次性射出9把剑，主要取决于你背包里还有几把箭和你还有多少能量-每把箭使用5%的能量。
回复加速（能量头盔）：让你的能量回复速度x2。
迅雷雷电（能量铁斧）：在攻击你的目标时放电产生雷电-使用你15%的能量。
复仇（能量钻石斧）：你的血量越低攻击力越高，最后一下会产生假的爆炸-每下使用你15%的能量。
水蛭能量（能量石斧）：有50%的可能给你回复30%的能量，同时如果你攻击的目标是玩家，则目标损失15%的能量-需要15%的能量。
精神分裂（能量金斧）：当你攻击生物时你会回复2点生命值-消耗15%的能量。
水蛭之食（能量木斧）：当你攻击生物时你会回复2点饱食度，同时如果你攻击的目标是玩家，则目标损2点饱食度-消耗15%的能量。
