AgeCraft is a mod that adds Ages to Minecraft. The key word of this mod is evolution. Evolution is what gets you to new ages and helps you explore all the features of this mod.
This mod is basically a combination of all sorts of mods. You start in the prehistory, where there are dinosaurs and other such creatures. You can just keep crafting better things and while time passes by you will go to another age. The way this will be done is like this: You basically get a message that says: “Get your stuff over here and move to the next age.”, this message won’t be like this, but it is just an example. You can then get some stuff in chests (?) and continue to the next age, which is another dimension. So the terrain regenerated with all the new stuff of that age. There will be serveral ages with all their own specilaties, items, blocks, mobs and terrains.
The Ages
  • Prehistory and the Hunter-Gatherer Society
  • Farming and Agriculture Society
  • Ancient Egypt
  • Ancient China
  • Roman and Greek Empires
  • Middle Ages
  • Early Modern Age
  • Industrial Age
  • Modern Age
  • Future
Features
  • Ages
  • New biomes
  • New ores
  • Villages, towns, cities, kingdoms and empires
  • Advanced NPCs
  • Magic
  • Advanced Crafting
  • More health (100 health points)
  • Mana (used for things like magic and specials)
  • Quests
  • Furniture and decoration
  • Chat system
  • Advanced Trading
  • More……
