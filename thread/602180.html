作者的话：
NoXray is a mixin plugin that works on SpongeVanilla and SpongeForge (has some weird issue atm). Thanks to mixins I was able to make a system that is effective and doesn't lag that much. It works by modifying the chunks at send time and updating surrounding blocks when players break a block. Please note that ores in caves are visible, due to the fact that their is an air-block next to it, but this should slow down people that xray very effectively .

翻译：（非机翻）
NoXray是mixin的插件,可以运行在SpongeVanilla和SpongeForge（异步传输功能有些问题），感谢mixin让我能够做一个这样的系统，而且不会很落后。当玩家打破一个方块时他通过发送时间来修改数据方块。注意，矿石在山洞内随处可见，因为他是在空的方块旁边，所以应该慢下来（等待方块变化，意思就是可能会出现周围的矿物发生变化被自己所看到，走慢点等他变回原样即可），因此这款插件是非常有效的。
