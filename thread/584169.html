作者: lichi2050    时间: 2016-5-15 01:50
用FallingSand
我直接给你的列子
1.8
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~1 ~
  2. {Block:redstone_block,Time:1,Riding:
  3. {id:FallingSand,Block:command_block,Time:1,TileEntityData:
  4. {Command:/fill ~ ~-1 ~-1 ~ ~50 ~-1 redstone_block},Riding:
  5. {id:FallingSand,Block:command_block,Time:1,TileEntityData:
  6. {Command:/fill ~5 ~-2 ~5 ~15 ~5 ~15 planks 0 hollow}}}}
复制代码

1.9
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~1 ~
  2. {Block:command_block,Time:1,TileEntityData:
  3. {Command:/fill ~5 ~-2 ~5 ~15 ~5 ~15 planks 0 hollow},Passengers:
  4. [{id:FallingSand,Block:command_block,Time:1,TileEntityData:
  5. {Command:/fill ~ ~-1 ~-1 ~ ~50 ~-1 redstone_block},Passengers:
  6. [{id:FallingSand,Block:redstone_block,Time:1}]}]}
复制代码
