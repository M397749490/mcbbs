Bomb Arrows are arrows that explode on contact with blocks and mobs. The will destroy most terrain blocks, although they have a smaller explosive radius
炸弹箭是一种接触到方块和生物后就会爆炸的一种箭。这种箭会破坏大多数地形方块，不过它们比TNT有更小的爆炸半径，
and deal less damage than TNT.

3.Dye Arrows
染色箭

Dye Arrows can be made from any dye color, and allow the player to dye various things, including:
染色箭是由任何染料颜色做成的一种箭，允许玩家为各种各样的东西染色，包括：
  • Wool Blocks
  • 羊毛方块
  • Carpet Blocks
  • 地毯方块
  • Hardened Clay Blocks
  • 硬化黏土块
  • Glass Blocks
  • 玻璃块
  • Glass Pane Blocks
  • 玻璃板块
  • Sheep
  • 羊上的毛
  • Wolf Collars
  • 狼的项圈
  • Players wearing Leather Armor
  • 穿着皮革盔甲的玩家
Dye Arrows deal 1/8th of the amount of damage that regular arrows do, so they are good to use for minigames with other players.
染色箭会造成普通箭1/8的伤害，所以用于和其他玩家一起玩迷你游戏是非常好的事情。

6.Fire Arrow
火焰箭


7.png (1.73 KB, 下载次数: 4)

下载附件

2016-8-29 11:36 上传


Fire Arrows can catch mobs on fire, as well as blocks. They can also melt ice and snow blocks, and turn packed ice blocks into ice blocks.
火焰箭可以使生物和方块燃烧。它们也可以融化冰块和雪块，并把浮冰块变成冰块。
You can disable Fire Arrows from catching blocks on fire in the configuration settings. They will still burn mobs, and melt ice/snow blocks, however.
你可以在配置设置里禁用火焰箭使方块燃烧。不过，它们还是会燃烧生物，融化冰/雪块。

PS：在1.0.3的更新里允许火焰箭可以点燃TNT。

9.Training Arrow
训练箭


9.png (1.52 KB, 下载次数: 0)

下载附件

2016-8-29 12:09 上传


Training Arrows are good for practicing your archery skills. They do not damage entities, or stick into them, so you can pick them back up if you hit one.
训练箭有益于练习你的箭术。它们不会伤害实体（但是实测会有时候造成1颗心的伤害），或者插入实体，所以如果你不小心击中了一个实体，你可以捡起来。
They are crafted with rabbit hide, a stick, and a feather.
它们是由一个兔子皮、一个木棍和一个羽毛合成的。
