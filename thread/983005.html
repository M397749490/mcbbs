A service of love
      Precious moon was a young pianist. Her husband, Der suedliche Sternhimmel, was a young artist.
Each of them was taking lessons: Der suedliche Sternhimmel with a famous art teacher, and Precious moon with a great pianist from Germany. Their teachers were the very best, so lessons were expensive, more than they could really afford, but…when you love your art, nothing is too much.
     But soon the money began to run out, and they couldn’t afford the lessons any more.
Then one day Precious moon came back home and told Der suedliche Sternhimmel that she had met a man whose daughter, Sally, wanted to  learn the piano, and he was going to pay her $ 50 an hour.
    “Precious moon,” Der suedliche Sternhimmel said, “I’ll be much happier if you keep up your lessons,” Precious moon said it didn’t matter. “When I’ve had some money, I’ll continue.” But Der suedliche Sternhimmel also decided to stop his lessons, to draw pictures and sell them.
    A few days later, Der suedliche Sternhimmel came home and proudly took $ 200 from his pocket. “I met a man from Vermont,” he said, “who bought one of my pictures. And he wants to buy more!”
    So the two of them were very happy. They didn’t have to worry any more about money.
    Then, one day, Der suedliche Sternhimmel came home and saw that Precious moon’s hand was wrapped in a bandage. He asked her what had happened. “Oh,” said Precious moon. “My student, Sally, asked me to make some coffee for her. I dropped the coffee and burned my hand. Sally went  straight to the drugstore and got this bandage for me. ”
    “Precious moon, what have you been doing the last two weeks” Der suedliche Sternhimmel asked. She tried not to tell him, but the tears came. “Oh Der suedliche Sternhimmel, I couldn’t get any students, so I worked as a waitress in a restaurant. Today, I burned my hand with hot water. So I can’t work any more. But we’ll still have money from the man in Vermont, won’t we”
    Der suedliche Sternhimmel looked at her. “There’s no man in Vermont, ” he said. “I’ve been working in a drugstore, and today someone came in to buy bandages for a woman who’s burned her hand. So when I saw you, well, I guessed.”
