As of Minecraft 1.3.1, spawning on Skyblock after dying is nearly impossible. It is recommended that you should either sleep in your bed, so when you die you spawn next to it, or download a fix made by King Korihor. If you would like to download the fix and read a more thorough explanation on why, Please click https://www.minecraftforum.net/f ... sp-mp#entry17458006 and hopefully this issue will be solved in a later update. :)

翻译:
从1.3开始,死亡后几乎不可能在岛屿重生,所以你应该睡觉,当你死了后会在床旁边重生,或者下载King Korihor制作的补丁,如果你想下载补丁并查看原因的详细解释,请翻阅https://www.minecraftforum.net/f ... sp-mp#entry17458006,希望此问题将在稍后的更新中修复
