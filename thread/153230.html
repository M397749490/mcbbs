  • 5 am: area 52 is having a blackout

  • You disguise as navy seals to get the classified documents back before they fall in the wrong hands.

  • Your mission:

  • - find 8 classified documents
  • - find the classified document "X"
  • - find 3 survivors
  • - return to the extraction point with all documents

  • Rules:

  • - 1-3 players (one kit per person)
  • - don't pick blocks
  • - don't sleep
  • - don't play in peacefull
  • - play with Sphax pure bdcraft Texture pack
  • - play with optifine with connected textures (recommended)
