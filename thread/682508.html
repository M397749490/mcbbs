简介 Description
With this map, you can play Slither.io in Minecraft. The game can have up to 10 people. You control your snake using the arrows in your inventory slot.
Eat mass to grow larger. If you crash into another snake, you die and your mass is dropped.
This map includes an options section where you choose how much mass is spawned in the game and a button to spectate the game.
This map requires a resource pack
这张地图，你可以在MC里玩贪吃蛇大作战。这款游戏可以支持最多10个玩家。你需要用在你物品栏里的箭头来控制你的蛇。吃食物来长大。如果你撞到了其他蛇，你就会死，并且你吃掉的食物也会掉落。
这张地图包含了一些设置，这些设置可以控制多少食物会生成，游戏里也有观察的选项。
此地图需要资源包。
