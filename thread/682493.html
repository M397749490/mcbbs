简介 Info
Flight Warfare is a vanilla Minecraft CTF mini-game where you glide down to the center to capture the flag while getting chased by your enemies. But try to stay alive!
Use any means to survive.
A combination of: Elytras, PVP & CTF.
If you run it on a server make sure you have command blocks enabled. To join teams right click the sign at spawn and step onto the pressure plate when you get teleported to proceed into the game.
飞行战争是一个原版MC的CTF小游戏地图，你可以在靠近对手的同时滑翔到中间并获得旗帜，试着活下来！
使用任何东西，只要能活下来
这是一个鞘翅+PVP+CTF的结合
如果你运行服务器记得让命令方块有权限使用。如果要加入队伍请邮件木牌然后踩踏板来继续游戏。
