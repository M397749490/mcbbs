什么是0x10c?
0x10c (stylized as 0x10c) is a sandbox science fiction video game previously under development by Mojang AB. It was announced via Twitter by the game's lead designer, Markus Persson on April 3, 2012. The game was eventually indefinitely postponed, however, as Persson lost interest in the project and opted to concentrate on smaller projects. The game has now been completely canceled but a team of fans are working on their own version of the game.
