介绍

Realistic flight in Minecraft has never really been implemented.  Even the elytra doesn't take into account true aerodynamics.  Minecraft Flight Simulator aims to change all this, by introducing planes that handle like actual planes!  To do this physics and aerodynamics is used, taking into account thrust, drag, lift, prop pitch, and much more.  Controls are also tailored to provide a simpler means of controlling planes than the current standard of 'mouse-yoke', which allows you to look around as you fly.  You can even customize your planes by giving them different propellers and engines, which affect their flight characteristics.
For a full list of controls and features, see the form page.  Please direct all posts, inquiries, general comments, and PMs there as well, as I don't check the Curse sites often.

在Minecraft中，出现的飞行器都是没有科学可言的
包括原版的滑翔翼，没有考虑到空气什么的能改变飞行的条件。
此模组将改写这一切，让飞行器落实真正的真实
我将此模组中的飞行器处理成与现实中一般
控制飞行需要衡量推力，阻力，升力，支距，等等各方面，而不是再仅仅的鼠标控制
发动机，螺旋桨，机翼都会影响飞行器本身

(大家自己摸索吧……我还没摸索透)

操纵
W:俯冲
　　S:搭帐篷
　　A:左方向
　　D:右方向
　　I:增加螺旋桨速度
　　K:减少螺旋桨速度
　　J:左转
    L:右转
    Y:放
襟翼

      H：收襟翼


      M:启动发动机
