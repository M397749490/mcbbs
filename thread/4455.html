More Stackables Beta 1.5_01
This mod makes food stackable up to 16 in stack, saddles, doors, and signs up to 8 in a stack, minecarts and boats up to 4 in a stack. When stacked, food and buckets are unusable, however, everything else is. This is great for storage and for using workbenches, chests, or furnaces, as you no longer consume your food! Requires ModLoader Beta 1.5_01.

這個MOD可以將食物最多疊加到16個，門，馬鞍，告示牌可以疊加到8個，船可以疊加到4個。不過疊加狀態的食物無法使用，但可以節省背包和儲物箱的空間，也更方便在火爐和工作臺的使用。
