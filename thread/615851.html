Add chairs on your server What is the difference with the other chairs plugin? I don't use arrow like other chairs plugin With other you have an arrow in the stairs. In my plugin I use ArmorStand is better. The ArmorStand detect if she have a passenger. If the ArmorStand don't have passenger. She remove them-self. You can sit on slab now. Enjoy You can regen when you are sit.(Default: false) see the config
