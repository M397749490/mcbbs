简单的光影包（原SSPE光影包）
Simple Shader is a lightweight shader pack which is the perfect solution for anyone who have got a low-end device and haven’t been able to use any of the other shader packs. It’s a much more simple pack which still got some of the essential features (such as more realistic skies) to make Minecraft much more beautiful and faithfully realistic.
SSPE是一个轻量级的光影包，但依然有一些基本特征（例如更真实的天空），完美解决了低配设备不能使用光影包的问题，让MC更加真实美丽。
