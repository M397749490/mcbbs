Type-126 天照 [ Amaterasu ] | Imperial Supercarrier
【中文译名】
Type-126 天照 [ Amaterasu ] | 帝国超级星舰
【作品展示】
【作品描述】
A supercarrier capable of both launching fighter assaults and heavy bombardment.

The type-126 is a heavily armed supercarrier and one of the largest ships in the fleet with a fighter equivalent of an entire assault carrier strike group.
The vessel features a turbocharged reactor bolstering its shielding capabilities and enough firepower to hold its own against small fleets.
Its heavy defensive capabilities allow it to ram enemy ships without sustaining damage to itself.
Considered a mobile fortress, these leviathans are a vital part of invasion fleets and defend important strategic locations.
这是一个能发射战斗机进行大规模轰炸的超级星舰而Type-126天照恰恰又正好是一艘舰队中最大的战斗星舰.
其战斗机群相当于一个完整的突击航母的战斗群.
该星舰能通过涡轮增压反应堆来增强它的屏蔽能力,同时又拥有足够的火力来抵抗小型舰队.
其独特的防御能力可以让它在危机时刻对敌方母舰进行一次毁灭性的撞击而不会对自身造成持续性伤害.
这个庞然大物被众人认为是星际中的一个移动堡垒.
其小型战斗机则是入侵敌方舰队的重要组成部分,同时也是保卫整个机群的重要部分
