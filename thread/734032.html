2.苦力怕头的僵尸，手拿石剑

4.骑着愤怒的狼的绿皮革衣的僵尸。

注意！以上回答出4个即可！
作者: xmdhs    时间: 2017-10-27 15:04
1. /summon Wolf ~ ~ ~ {Angry:true,ArmorDropChances:[0:0.085f,1:0.085f,2:0.085f,3:0.085f]}
2./summon minecraft:zombie ~ ~1 ~ {ArmorItems:[{},{},{},{id:"minecraft:skull",Count:1b,Damage:0s}],HandItems:[{id:"minecraft:stone_sword",Count:1b,Damage:0s}]}
3. 要两条 /summon minecraft:skeleton ~ ~1 ~ {ArmorItems:[{},{},{},{id:"minecraft:lapis_block",Count:1b,Damage:0s}]}
这条高频 /particle dragonbreath ~ ~ ~ 0 0 0 0.5 100 normal @e[type=minecraft:skeleton] 0 0
