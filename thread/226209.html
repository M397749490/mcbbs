意译：

     这个MOD加入了许多勺子进入你的Minecraft。实用的多功能挖掘勺，有效的战斗。如果你想用更好的工具，勺子是你必走的路。
     勺
     勺是用于挖掘和开采的新工具。（省略一句）勺可以用任何材料来制造，就像普通工具,只是需要两个材料和一个碗，勺的制作是非常便宜的。他既是一个铲也是一个镐，它们挖掘速度比一般工具快。而超级勺，超级勺是Minecraft最快的挖掘/采矿工具。（ps：前提是你没有装其他MOD）然而制造它的材料是非常难得的。
    勺的特点
1.他即是一个铲，也是一个镐。
2.便宜且具有特色的合成方式。
3.效率比一般工具快。
     战斗勺
       战斗勺的攻击比一般的剑攻击+1。（意思是:木剑的攻击是3，则木质战斗勺的攻击是4。）但他们的制作比一般的剑困难。它的制作需要1个任意材质的勺和5个铄石。（见下）超战斗勺，超战斗勺是Minecraft最有效的攻击武器（ps：前提是你没有装其他MOD），一次能造成10点攻击。
    Spoonite
    Spoonite是制作超级（战斗）勺的重要材料。它是由9个spoonite碎片合成的（碎片是由spoonite矿石掉落的）。他的分布和钻石一样，在底下16层出现，每窟有4个spoonite矿石。此外，spoonite矿石只能由钻石镐敲下。
    Spoonite碎片特点：
1.spoonite矿石只在地下16层出现，其中12层最多，每窟有4个。
2.spoonite矿石只能由钻石镐开采，他可以制成spoonite。
3.spoonite是Minecraft最佳的制作材料。（我不说你们也应该知道我要说什么。）

原文：

Minecraft Spoon mod!
This mod adds many types of spoons into your Minecraft game. Wooden spoons, diamond spoons, ultra spoons, battle spoons and much more! Enjoy mining with useful multi-purpose spoons, fighting with effective battle spoons and using amazing ultra spoons. If want better tools, spoons are the way to go!

Spoons
Spoons a new tool used for digging and mining. They are effective on both rock and dirt/sand/gravel, so no more wasting your pickaxes on annoying underground gravel or dirt! Spoons can be crafted with any material, just like regular tools, and require only two of that material and one bowl (See crafting recipe above). However, as spoons are so cheap to craft and work as both a shovel and a pickaxe, they have a slightly slower mining speed than the proper tools. That is, with the exception of the ultra spoon. An ultra spoon is the fastest and most effective mining/digging tool in Minecraft. However, the material required to make it, spoonite, is very difficult to obtain.
Features:
· Work as both a shovel and pickaxe.
· Cheap crafting recipe.
· Slightly slower than proper tools, except for ultra spoon.

Battle spoons
Battle spoons are more effective weapons than swords, dealing +1 more attack damage than the sword of the same material. However, they are more difficult to craft than swords, requiring one spoon and five flint (See recipe above). The ultra battle spoon, crafted with a regular ultra spoon, is the most effective weapon in Minecraft, dealing 10 attack damage.
Features:
?· More effective than swords.
?· Difficult to craft.
Spoonite
Spoonite is the material that is required to make ultra spoons. It is crafted with nine spoonite shards which come from spoonite ore. Spoonite ore spawn below layer 16, similarly to diamonds, but slightly more common. However, spoonite ore can only be found in concentrations of up to four. The best way to find spoonite is to mine at layer 12. Also, spoonite can only be successfully mined with a diamond spoon. Each block of spoonite will drop one spoonite shard.
Features:
· Spoonite Ore spawns below layer 16, most common at layer 12, spawns in concentrations of up to 4.
· Spoonite Ore can only be mined with a diamond spoon and drops spoonite shards, which can be crafted into spoonite.
· Spoonite is the best material in Minecraft.
