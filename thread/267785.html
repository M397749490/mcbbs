宙斯的闪电箭：
同一损害的钻石宝剑。灯小怪火。给玩家一个跳跃提升为35秒。推出敌对小怪到空气中。
Zeus' Lightning Bolt:
Same damage as a diamond sword. Lights mobs on fire. Gives player a jump boost for 35 seconds. Launches hostile mobs into the air.

托尔之锤：
同一损害的钻石宝剑。减慢小怪15秒。给播放器的电阻为35秒。
Thor's Hammer:
Same damage as a diamond sword. Slows mobs for 15 seconds. Gives player resistance for 35 seconds.

波塞冬的三叉戟：
三比钻石刀更大的伤害。让玩家可以在水下呼吸75秒。
Poseidon's Trident:
Three more damage than a diamond sword. Allows player to breathe underwater for 75 seconds.

战神'矛：
同一损害的钻石宝剑。毒药小怪5秒。给玩家一个速度提升35秒。
Ares' Spear:
Same damage as a diamond sword. Poisons mobs for 5 seconds. Gives player a speed boost for 35 seconds.

Annabeth的匕首：
1点伤害不到钻石的剑。小怪伤害3秒。
Annabeth's Dagger:
1 less damage than a diamond sword. Harms mobs for 3 seconds.

阿波罗之弓：
同一损害作为一个正常的弓。有无限的箭头。
Apollo's Bow:
Same damage as a normal bow. Has infinite arrows.

阿尔特弥斯的弓：
只能用“银箭”。（箭目前没有影响。）灯小怪火（如冲裁弓）。给出了隐形35秒（如果打孔与弓）。
Artemis' Bow:
Can only use 'Silver Arrows'.(Arrows currently have no effects.) Lights mobs on fire(If punched with bow). Gives invisibility for 35 seconds(If punched with bow).
