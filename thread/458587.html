First Spawn Section:

  • Drink the convenient magic liquid.
  • Walk to pressure plate #1
  • Walk to pressure plate #2
  • Open the custom crafting table and remove the piece of paper.
  • Take the loot from all six chests and craft the items shown.
  • Use the hoe on the dirt.
  • Use the rose bush on the grass.
  • Stand inside the rose bush.

Nature Cave Section:

  • Take the far left branch, and walk up to the pumpkin. Then walk away.
  • Toss the magic shard on top of the blue flower.
  • Pick up the iron ingot. (1/3)
  • Take the middle right branch and rotate the flowers until the correct combination is set up. (7, 3, 5)
  • Pick up the iron ingot. (2/3)
  • Take the far right branch and take all items from the chest.
  • Spawn and kill the creeper. If he does not drop two gunpowder, toss the reset disk and try again.
  • Craft the instant iron smelter.
  • Take the iron ingot. (3/3)
  • Craft the iron bucket.
  • Toss the iron bucket into the pool of water.
  • Take the filled iron bucket.
  • Stand inside the rose bush.

Intermediate Section:

  • Open the chest that has appeared, and take all the items.
  • Place the prismarine in the pattern shown on the map, and place the water in the center.
  • Once the fountain starts, stand in the water and look down.

Ancient Underwater Monument:

  • Go up the stairs, to the right, and into the giant dark room. Notice the power crystal.
  • Leave the room and turn left. Flick the lever. (1/4)
  • Go straight all the way, then turn right. Complete the parkour puzzle and flick the lever. (2/4)
  • Leave the room and turn right towards the stairs. Stand at the top of the stairs facing the cracked wall.
  • Sprint into the wall, and it will break inwards. Flick the lever. (3/4)
  • Walk straight out and down the stairs into the dark corridor that has opened.
  • Complete the parkour and flick the lever. (4/4)
  • Go back to the power crystal room, and wait for the power to overload. Jump into the pit.
  • Stand in the water and look straight down.

Intermediate Section:

  • Toss the prismarine crystals onto the ender portal blocks until they each have one crystal.
  • Note the colors of the smoke where the crystals used to be.
  • Flick the levers with the correct colors. (orange, red, green)
  • Parkour on the structure until you reach the pressure plate. Stand on it.
  • Stand on the glowstone that appeared and look at the structure. It now spells the word FLAME.
  • Use the anvil to rename the clay ball. (FLAME)
  • Wait for the cutscene to finish.

Lava Caverns Section:

  • Run straight ahead, and right click the sign. You will get the Ancient Book. (1/5)
  • Turn around and go right, towards the chest. Press the button and place the correct armor onto the armor stand.
  • Press the button again after the combination is correct. (Gold Helmet, Iron Chestplate, Chain Leggings, Diamond Boots)
  • Right click the sign to learn the spell of speed. (2/5)
  • Turn around and go straight to the pressure plate. Click the speed spell twice to amplify it, then sprint across the pressure plate until you reach the second safe zone.
  • Amplify the spell of speed again and sprint across the blocks and lava without jumping.
  • Right click the sign to learn the spell of leaping. (3/5)
  • Kill yourself in the lava.
  • Amplify the spell of leaping and jump up into the previously inaccessible passage. Continue jumping upwards until you reach the top. (You may need to amplify the spell half way through if it wears out.)
  • Right click the sign to learn the spell of flame. (4/5)
  • Go back to the falling lava and pressure plate. Use amplified speed to reach the second safe zone, then activate the spell of flame and swim through the lava to the third safe zone.
  • Right click the sign to learn the spell of the nether eye. (5/5)
  • Jump in the lava on the sides of the path to disable the fire resistance, and kill yourself. (Or walk all the way back through the lava.)
  • Jump over the fence and leave the fortress. Using speed and leaping parkour across the pillars until you reach the hidden passage.
  • Use amplified speed to run though before the wall shuts.
  • Use flame to swim up through the lava.
  • Cross the nether bridge to the ancient portal. Stand on the portal blocks and activate the spell of the nether eye.
  • After filling in every portal block with an eye, jump into the nether portal.
  • Wait for the cutscene to finish.

Intermediate Section:

  • Return to the custom crafting table and craft the two bows.
  • Wait for the boss to spawn.

Final Boss Battle:

  • The Sky Serpent will appear nearby. (1/3) Prepare for battle!
  • The boss is protected by a shield. When he roars, stand underneath him so he is struck with his own lightning.
  • After three hits, the shield will be removed. Start shooting his head with either the arrows or fireballs.
  • After his health is entirely removed, he will be revived as the Enraged Sky Serpent. (2/3)
  • His attacks are stronger and he moves faster. Shoot his head as before until he loses all his health.
  • After his health is entirely removed, he will be revived as the Severed Sky Serpent. (3/3)
  • His attacks are much stronger and he moves very fast. Continue shooting both segments of the sky serpent in the head as before.
  • After his health is entirely removed, he will die for good and you will be rewarded with four items: Fireworks Box, Sky Serpent Pet Egg, Redstone Tour, and your Ancient Book from before. Congratulations for completing Simplicity!
