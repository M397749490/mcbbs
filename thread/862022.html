作者: qsefthuopq    时间: 2019-5-1 22:51
使用Groupmanger新建VIP组
将玩家置入该组内 给予以下权限
  1.   VIP:
  2.     default: false
  3.     permissions:
  4.     - essentials.teleport.cooldown.bypass
  5.     - essentials.teleport.timer.bypass
  6.     - residence.tpdelaybypass
  7.     - residence.randomtp.delaybypass
  8.     - residence.randomtp.cooldownbypass
  9.     inheritance:
  10.     - builder
  11.     - g:essentials_builder
  12.     - g:towny_builder
  13.     info:
  14.       build: true
  15.       prefix: '&2'
  16.       suffix: ''
