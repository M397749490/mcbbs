不说版本系列，简直了
1.7-1.8
/summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~
{Equipment:[{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},{},{},{},{}]}

/entitydata @e[type=Zombie] {Equipment:[{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},{},{},{},{}]}

1.9-1.10
/summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {HandItems:[0:{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},1:{}]}

/entitydata @e[type=Zombie] {HandItems:[0:{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},1:{}]}

1.11+
/summon zombie ~ ~1 ~ {HandItems:[0:{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},1:{}]}

/entitydata @e[type=zombie] {HandItems:[0:{id:minecraft:stick,Count:1},1:{}]}
