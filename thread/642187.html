作者: skeleton小白    时间: 2016-10-16 16:36
你的意思是扔出去的雪球实体名字是1还是手上拿的物品雪球名字是1

如果是前一种情况
rcb:
  1. /execute @e[type=Snowball,name=1] ~ ~ ~ summon Fireball ~ ~ ~ {direction:[0.0,0.0,0.0],ExplosionPower:0}
复制代码



如果是后一种稍微麻烦点

rcb:
  1. /scoreboard players tag @a add hold {SelectedItem:{id:"minecraft:snowball",tag:{display:{Name:"1"}}}}
复制代码

ccb:
  1. /execute @a[tag=hold] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[type=Snowball,c=1,r=2] ~ ~ ~ summon Fireball ~ ~ ~ {direction:[0.0,0.0,0.0],ExplosionPower:0}
复制代码

ccb:
  1. /scoreboard players tag @a remove hold
复制代码
