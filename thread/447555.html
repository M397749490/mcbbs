来听听作者的话吧
Greetings friends! Here's my fifth addition to my "Of Lands Forgotten" collection. This landscape showcases a valley of many crystal-like structures grown on a barren landscape. Each of the large crystal structures is unique. Texture pack used is Chroma Hills. Tools used in the creation of this project were:  * Worldpainter  * Worldedit  * Chunky  * Picasa 3 Hope you enjoyed it!Progress:         100% CompleteTags:        Environment, Landscaping
