TAKING INVENTORY: STICK

背包盘点：木棍

Stick around for some pointers!
当头棒喝！

The humble stick might not be the first thing that comes to mind when you think about the most interesting items in Minecraft. But it's secretly one of the most important items –serving as a foundation for almost every part of the game's mechanics.
当你想起Minecraft中最有趣的事物时，也许不会想到不起眼的木棍，可它其实是不可或缺的秘密要角之一——作为游戏内大部分工具的基础材料。

Consider for a moment what Minecraft would be like without sticks. There'd be no pickaxes, swords, shovels, axes, or hoes – so no mining, combat, or farming. There'd be no rails, armour stands, bows,campfires, crossbows, arrows, banners, fishing rods, levers, ladders, item frames, paintings, grindstones, signs fences, tripwire hooks, ortorches either. What's left? Er... collecting flowers, I guess?
试想看看，假如 Minecraft没有木棍将会是什么光景：没有镐﹑剑﹑铲子﹑斧头或锄头，因此无法挖矿、战斗和农耕；没有铁轨、盔甲架、弓、营火、弩、箭、旗帜、钓竿、拉杆、梯子、物品展示框、画、砂轮、告示牌、绊线钩和火把。还剩啥能干？呃……摘花吧我想？

Asappropriate for its fundamental position at the bottom of Minecraft's tech tree, the stick was added to the game very early on – Indev version 0.31, to be precise.
基于它在 Minecraft科技树的根基定位，木棍很早就被加入游戏中——准确地说，是Indev 0.31版。

You know how to make them already – two planks arranged above each other in a crafting grid. Recently, though, we added another recipe –two bamboo in the same configuration also gets you a stick. Useful if you spawn in the middle of a bamboo forest biome.
你已经知道怎么做出它们——将两块木板放入工作台，但我们最近又添加了另一个合成配方——用两截竹子也能获得你需要的木棍。如果你生成在竹林中，这将很管用。

If you're stuck in a tree- or bamboo-less wasteland, there are a few other ways to acquire sticks. You can catch them while fishing(though you'll need a stick to make a fishing rod), they're sometimes dropped by witches on death (though you’ll need a stick to make a sword), and they also drop from dead bushes and leaves when they're destroyed or they decay. Finally, in a pinch,you can find them in several village chests.
如果你被困在没有树或竹子的荒地上，还有其他几种获取木棍的方法。你可以钓到它们（尽管你需要一根木棍来做钓竿），女巫死亡时也有一定机率会掉落（尽管你需要一根木棍来制剑），也能经由破坏枯灌木或树叶掉落。最后的最后，你也可以从村庄的储物箱中发现一些。

Howdo you use them? Well, other than crafting, which is theirsuperpower, you can burn them as furnace fuel. Two sticks will smeltone item, which is useful if you have one or two things to smelt upbut don’t want to use a whole lump of coal in the process. Fletcher villagers, who make arrows, also find sticks pretty useful –they'll buy 32 of them for an emerald.
木棍还有啥用？好吧，除了用来制造，它的特殊功能还有被当作燃料使用。两根木棍能熔一件物品，若你只有一两件东西要熔又不想为此耗费一块煤炭，这将很有用。制箭师也深明木棒的妙用——他们会以一颗绿宝石收购32根木棒。

Hey,don't be sad, dead bush. You're not completely useless!
嘿，别气馁，枯灌木。你还不到完全一无是处！

In the real world sticks are almost as important as in Minecraft. Our earliest ancestors, and a few indigenous cultures today, used them for digging out roots, tubers or burrowing animals, and in agriculture – making them one of the earliest tools.
木棍在现实世界中的地位同样举足轻重。早在我们的先祖及现存的一些原住民文化中，木棍就被用于挖掘块茎类或是穴居动物，以及作为农耕器具——它们是最早出现的工具之一。

Sticks are also used in many of the world's martial arts – the best known is probably Japanese kendo, but others include Myanmar's banshay, Ireland's bataireacht, Ethiopian donga, Indian silambam, Egyptian tahtib, and Algerian matrague. Sticks are useful in defence (if attacked while lightly armed) and also when training to use more dangerous weapons.
木棍也被广泛应用于世界各地的武术——其中以日本剑道（kendo）最出名，但也包括缅甸的宾谢（banshay），爱尔兰的短棍术（bataireacht），埃塞俄比亚的东嘎（donga），印度的施莱邦（silambam），埃及的泰比棍舞（tahtib）和阿尔及利亚的matrague。木棒能被用于自卫（如果遭到轻武装类的袭击），同时也可作为攻击性的危险武器使用。

Another common use of sticks is to help elderly people walk. The walking stick offers stability and support for people who aren't so steady on their feet, or are crossing uneven ground. They can also be a fashion accessory – often called a cane – but their popularity in fashion peaked in the 18th century. You don't see many fancy canes out on the streets today.
木棍的另外一个常见用途是辅助年长者行走。拐杖为不良于行者或不利行走的地形提供稳定性及支撑力。木棍也可作为时尚配件（俗称手杖），但它们仅流行于18世纪的时尚圈，如今并不常在街上看到花俏的手杖。

Possibly the most interesting real-world use of sticks is in ceremony – for which they're normally called a "wand". Traditionally made of oak or hazel, the concept of the magic wand was first used by the ancient Greek writer Homer, in his books The Iliad and The Odyssey, but they didn’t become serious part of the occult until the the 1200s latin grimoire The Sworn Book of Honorius. Today, both stage and real magicians carry them as a symbol of magical power and for reaching, pointing, and directing theiraudience's attention.
木棍在现实世界中最有趣的用途应该是魔法仪式——被称作「魔杖」。传统的魔杖由橡木或榛木制成，最早出现于古希腊作家荷马的《荷马史诗》（TheIliad and The Odyssey）中，但是直到1200年的拉丁魔法书《宣誓荣誉书》（The Sworn Book ofHonorius）才真正成为神秘学的重要一环。如今，魔术表演者和真正的魔法师都随身携带魔杖并视之为魔力的象征，以引导和攫取观众的心神。

Finally, musical conductors also use sticks (known as "batons") in a similar manner to direct the attention of their orchestra. So next time you're conducting an orchestra of note blocks, consider bringing along a stick with you to serve as a baton. It's sure to stick in the memory of the audience.
最后，指挥家也会使用到木棍（也就是「指挥棒」）以指挥乐队。所以下次指挥音阶盒乐团时，可以考虑带根木棍当作指挥棒，肯定能让观众印象深刻。
