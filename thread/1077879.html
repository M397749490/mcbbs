书接上回：灌水五十九首（其二）翻译
原文：
泥潭末地竦处尊，水笔罗立似儿孙。
安得木板水贴术，水到版主一头昏。
水笔遭封少归路，申诉求饶有一门。
稍待版主入睡后，高举水桶灌茶馆！
翻译：
The end in MCBBS like an old man,the watery posts like his children and grandchildren.
If I learn how to use glowing planks to pour water,I'll make the moderators get a headache with watery posts.
If the man who pours water is banned,it's difficult for him to come back.The only way for him to come back is to complaint  or beg for mercy.
After the moderators go to bed,I'll lift the bucket full of water and pour all the water into the miners' teahouse!
