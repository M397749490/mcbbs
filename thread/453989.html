1.加上CustomNameVisible:1,比如
  1. summon ArmorStand ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:ArmorStand,CustomNameVisible:1}
复制代码

2.加上ShowArms:1配合Equipment,比如
  1. summon ArmorStand ~ ~1 ~ {ShowArms:1,Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:iron_sword",Count:1},{},{},{},{}]}
复制代码

3.加上Invisible:1。比如summon ArmorStand ~ ~1 ~ {Invisible:1}
4.似乎没有这项。因为盔甲架貌似没有Life标签= =（区块格式里似乎找不到）
