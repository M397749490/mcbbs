原文地址：
https://minecrafteducation.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025667011-What-s-new-Spring-release-version-1-9-0-

We're excited to announce our Spring Release of Minecraft: Education Edition (Version 1.9.0). In this release we've expanded the platforms we support, added new features from recent Bedrock releases, and fixed various issues to help improve performance and stability. Users who already have Minecraft: Education Edition installed on Windows 10 and iPad will be updated automatically. For other platforms or to update now, visit our download page here.
我们非常兴奋地发布我们的春季更新—Minecraft：教育版（版本1.9.0）。 在此版本中，我们会支持更多的平台，并添加了最近基岩版的新特性，还修复了各种问题以帮助提高游戏性能和稳定性。 同时，Windows 10和iPad上的 Minecraft：教育版 的用户将自动更新。 对于其他平台的更新，请访问我们的下载页面。






What's in 1.9.0?
1.9.0版本中更新了什么？

Our latest release of Minecraft: Education Edition 1.9.0 brings some fun new updates:
我们对最新发布的Minecraft：教育版 1.9.0带来了一些有趣的更新：

Available on more versions of Windows
适用于更多版本的Windows

Based on community requests to support more versions of Windows, we are excited to introduce the availability of Minecraft: Education Edition for Windows Desktop as part of Version 1.9.0. Minecraft: Education Edition is now easier to update and deploy via SCCM or Intune, and is compatible with earlier versions of Windows. ?
根据社区中的强烈要求，我们已经支持更多的Windows版本，作为1.9.0版更新的一部分，我们很高兴能够介绍Minecraft：教育版在更多Windows桌面版本上的可用性。Minecraft：教育版现在可以更容易地通过SCCM或Intune更新和部署，并且能与早期版本的Windows兼容。

Cats, pandas, and scaffolding!
猫。熊猫，以及脚手架！

Cats, pandas, bamboo, and scaffolding blocks are now available in Minecraft: Education Edition. If you and your students have been waiting for these to show up in your school’s edition of Minecraft, the wait is over. ?
现在可以在Minecraft：教育版中找到猫，熊猫，竹子和脚手架方块。如果您和您的学生一直在期待着这些出现在您学校的Minecraft版本中，那么现在不用等待了。

Features from Minecraft Bedrock versions 1.9 and 1.8
来自Minecraft 基岩版1.9和1.8的新内容

New stair, slab, and wall variants have been added, along with new sign types and flowers. To see the full list of features from the 1.9 and 1.8 Bedrock editions which are available as part of this release, check out the change logs here and here. (Note that experimental features listed in these change logs are not included in Minecraft: Education Edition.) ?
添加了新的楼梯，台阶和墙体变体，以及新的标志类型和花朵。要查看本版本中来自基岩版1.9和1.8 的完整内容列表，请查看“此处“和“此处”的更改日志。 （请注意，这些更改日志中列出的实验性功能未包含在Minecraft：教育版中。）

Fixes
Bug修复
This release also includes various fixes to address known issues and help improve usability and stability of the game. Some specific fixes include:
此版本还包括各种修复，以解决已知问题并帮助提高游戏的可用性和稳定性。一些具体的修复包括：

  • Agent placing water crash
  • 代理人在放置水的时候崩溃
  • "Make visible to LAN" toggle functionality
  • “在局域网中可见”切换开关功能
  • Does not install on Windows 10 version 1511 and earlier
  • 不能安装在Windows 10 1511及更早版本上
  • Failed multiplayer connection on Windows 10 1607
  • Windows 10 1607上的多人游戏连接失败
  • Certain languages not displaying properly in-game
  • 某些语言无法正常显示在游戏中

Have questions or need help?
有疑问或需要帮助？
Please visit our help center and contribute in our forums or visit our knowledge base.
请访问我们的帮助中心，或者在我们的论坛中提供帮助或访问我们的知识库。
