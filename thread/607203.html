牛蛋：1.7-1.8：/summon Item ~ ~1 ~ {Item:{id:"minecraft:spawn_egg",Count:1,Damage:92}}
要生成其他生物按F3+H查看数据值

1.9+：/summon Item ~ ~1 ~ {Item:{id:"minecraft:spawn_egg",Count:1b,tag:{EntityTag:{id:"Cow"}},Damage:0s}}
要生成其他生物蛋就把Cow改掉就行生物ID：http://minecraft-ids.grahamedgecombe.com/entities

药水：1.7-1.8：/summon Item ~ ~1 ~ {Item:{id:"minecraft:potion",Count:1,Damage:8257}}
要生成其他药水按F3+H查看数据值
1.9+：/summon Item ~ ~1 ~ {Item:{id:"minecraft:splash_potion",Count:1b,tag:{Potion:"minecraft:long_regeneration"},Damage:0s}}
