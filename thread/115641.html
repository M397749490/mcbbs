The Growable Items Mod is a mod that will make some items in the Minecraft world growable. All you need is a sample of any item you want to grow and a reed. With the two materials well-combined, you will come up with a reed of that item that will grow when planted. Later on, when they have grown enough, you can turn them back into the item you have intended to acquire.
