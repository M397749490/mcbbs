一、模组介绍
介绍原文：
Are you tired of looking at the same teal and blue outfit on your Steve? Or maybe you found a great skin, but want to dress it up without sacrificing it to find a new outfit? Then this mod is for you. Juju’s ClothingCraft Mod 1.7.10 consists of a full wardrobe and accessories for all of your gaming needs.

翻译[机翻+人工]：
你是否厌倦了看着同蓝色的衣服的史提夫？或者你发现了一个很棒的皮肤，但要换上它而不牺牲它找到一个新的服装？那么这个mod是为你而准备的。JUJU的衣服工艺 MOD用满满的衣柜和配件满足您的所有游戏需求。
特征原文：
Features:
2 new world gens including grow-able cotton plant and halite ore
10 new tools & weapons + (some accessories offer damage abilities)
21 patterns for crafting clothes
25 different bottles of dye
26 different fabrics
35 different accessories + 1 edible (a hot dog!)
146 different clothing pieces (shoes, hats, shirts, dresses, skirts, etc.)

翻译[人工]：
○2个世界生成物:可生长的棉花和岩盐矿
○10个新的工具和武器（一些配件提供伤害能力）
○21种款式的服装
○25种不同的染料瓶
○26种不同的织物
○35个不同的配件+ 1食物（热狗）
○146种不同的衣服（鞋子，帽子，衬衫，连衣裙，裙子等）
