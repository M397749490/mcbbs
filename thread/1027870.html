作者的话
The vanilla armor bar doesn't accurately show "better than diamond" level armor, as it simply maxes out when its full.
原版的护甲并不能显示比钻石甲更高的护甲值，当你的护甲超过钻石甲时，只会显示护甲的最大值。
This mod allows armor values over 20 to be displayed as different colored (configurable) icons depending on how many times you fill the bar.
这个模组可以为你提供超过钻石套的护甲显示（它也取决于你在配置文件的配置）
However, without AttributeFix, the armor display will cap out at 30 Armor, or 1.5x diamond armor due to the vanilla armor cap.
但是，如果你不安装AttributeFix模组，它最多只能显示30点护甲，也就是1.5倍的钻石甲值
