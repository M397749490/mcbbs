[目录]
这是所有的生存服务器都适用的，因为只需要几天，服务器里的玩家就会建造大型养殖场来满足他们的生活，但是这是非常卡服的，作为一个管理，我发现这让我很沮丧，有没有办法让玩家努力生存，却让他们不能通过养殖场生活呢，Animal Plague是你最好的选择。这会让一些聚集在一起的动物生病，而且这个插件是可以配置的，有许多不同的方法控制瘟疫。
作者原话：
For any survival server, it takes only a few days for the player to get comfy with their giant animal farms providing everything they need. As an admin myself I found this quite frustrating. Isn't there a way to keep player working hard even after they have made a few farms and animal pens? Animal Plague is the answer! The idea is to create a way to naturally limit the number of animals a player can have at once. This causes them to work harder because they don't have as many resources. As an added bonus, Animal Plague also helps control lag caused by oversized animal farms! Animal Plague can be configured in many different ways to create a plague with very different effects!

[特色]
限制大型养殖场
让游戏更有挑战性
瘟疫可以传染!!!
瘟疫分为三个阶段
可以设置治疗瘟疫的物品
可以让死与瘟疫的动物的肉腐烂[其实就是变普通肉变腐肉]
可以让某些动物免疫
