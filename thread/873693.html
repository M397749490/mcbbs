Verseus - 多样战争

作者原文：
  
Welcome to Verseus!
  Verseus aims to improve your gameplay through minimizing laggy particles and textures, creating a more open view, while still keeping a vanilla like feel. This pack is not a hack pack (only non essential particles were removed), it's meant to be a performance booster.
  Verseus Doesn't just play good, It looks good as well. Keeping the vanilla look but with a new style. Hopefully it brings out a fun experience while playing Minecraft, whether It's Skywars, Bedwars, a Minigame, or just a simple survival world.

Features
List of Changed Items:
All Swords - have been minimized both textures as well as 1st person view
All Axes - new style, plus minimized 1st person view
All Pickaxes
All Shovels
Bow & Arrow - easier to see fully charged bow
Bow minimized (1.8 only)
Leather, Iron, Diamond and Gold armor - icons have been re-shaded
List of Changed Blocks:
Tall Grass - not so tall no more
Double Tall Grass - 3/2 tall grass :P
Sugar Cane - less dense
Webs - also less dense
Glass - less messy
Fire - low fire, but not too low
And more...

Other Features:
Lower resolution clouds & end sky
Smoother enchantment overlay
Better vision pumpkin head
Slimmer GUI
Attack icon under crosshair - bigger / more visible? (1.9+)
Removed unnecessary particles (not **s that would enable cheating)
Removed unnecessary animated textures
Removed animated ocean plant life (1.13 Only)
Always fast trees (1.9+)
Removed Leather armor overlay


译文：
  欢迎使用多样战争！
  多样战争旨在通过最大限度削弱导致你卡顿的粒子和材质、创造更开放的视野同时保持Minecraft原版材质的样貌来改善你的游戏体验。这个材质包不是一个作弊材质包（只是去除了一些不必要的颗粒），去除不必要的颗粒会让你的电脑在Minecraft的游戏性能得到提高。
  多样战争不仅仅好用，也兼顾了美观。保持了原版Minecraft样貌，不过也伴随着新的风格。希望它能在你游玩Minecraft的时候带给你有趣的体验，无论是在空岛战争、起床战争、街机游戏，还是仅仅在一个简单的生存世界。


材质特性：
①.一些经过修改的物品：
所有剑 - 已经在第一人称视角和握持视角最小化材质
所有斧 - 拥有新风格，并且在第一人称视角最小化材质
所有镐
所有铲子
弓箭 - 更容易看到拉满的弓
弓材质最小化（仅限1.8）
皮革，铁，钻石和金护甲 - 图标已重新绘制

②一些经过修改的方块：
较高的草 -  没有以往那么高了
更高的草 -  约为“较高的草”的1.5倍
甘蔗 - 降低密度
蜘蛛网 - 降低密集程度
玻璃 - 降低像素排布繁杂程度
火 - 降低火的高度，但没有很低
还有更多...

其他特性：
云朵和天空的分辨率下降
更顺滑的附魔效果
更好的南瓜头
更简洁的GUI界面
十字准线下的攻击图标 - 更大/更明显（在1.9+版本）
删除不必要的粒子（不是会删除后会造成作弊的粒子）
删除了不必要的动画
删除部分海洋更新动画（仅限1.13）
对树的材质进行优化（1.9+）
去除了皮革护甲的材质
