那我就在这里贴上吧。。
I found out I had hypertension at 30th Sep 2014 after feeling shortness of breath at school around 8AM. At 22th Sep 2014, I replayed Dead Island, played osu! and got my Wacom Pen and Touch Tablet. At that night I suddenly thought about zombie apocalypse, a few minutes later I started to think about my death at the future. The sudden panic and the heavy stress caused me cannot sleep at the next day and have no appetite for at least a week long.  7 days later, my two brothers, my mom and I were eating at our favourite chicken rice shop after school (a.k.a between 1:30PM to 2PM), the stray cat, which begged me for food and I gave it and acted friendly, came back after 1-2 months. Its front paw touched at my leg at the same place again (Goverment restrict secondary school students to wear trousers) and this time I touched that place and touched my wound, later starting to afraid of getting rabies. After I found out Malaysia (also Singapore) are free of rabies since 2009 according to WHO and the next day I asked doctor if I will possibly get rabies and he said no, I stopped worrying and it is when my stress since last week has been significantly reduced.  (UPDATE: The cat was eating the food I gave it, and since an answer from a Q&a site said if they get rabies they can’t eat or drink, eventually the virus will get into their saliva, but at that time it was eating)  Oh and, those days when I felt I am over-stressed while multiple online tests indicates that I am still at low/moderate stress, I also found many articles which is about sitting too long can cause you significant harm (I knew this 2 years ago when my favourite Sin Chew Daily posted it, but later ignored), which will be 60+% more risk to those who sit all days w/o moving dying of heart disease. Then I found a way to counteract it; stand up every 1 hour. Eventually I forced myself to stand every 5 minutes. :P  Since the day I found out I had hypertension, I stopped playing my computer for 2 days until 3 Oct 2014, only chatting with Steam friends and browsing Imgur which helped me to relieve stress at the days when I felt over-stressed. Also, I uninstalled Dead Island and delete osu! shortcut at my desktop. Right now I’ll use my Wacom tablet for learning to draw purpose only.  About my weight and everything above it (I told you already), right now only Accurian and SheepBaiz (III) knew it, so I’ll tell you here: 96KG in a few months ago, right now 94KG since I started to go to gym 2 days ago when my doctor told me to go do some exercise so I’ll don’t end us needing medicine to control hypertension like him and my dad, in which 90+% of the patients NEED medicine to control due to unknown reason causing their hypertension. I hope I’ll end up in the 10%. :'(  ORIGINAL WORDS: https://ngbeslhang.wordpress.com/2014/10/06/original/  UPDATE 10/10/2014: A few days ago, I used to be using my smartphone at late night, causing me to see mostly black and white flashing repeatedly while closing eyes trying to sleep and since yesterday, I felt that I started to have a bit sensitive to light because most bright things (usually sky) I always see without problem (I think) started to feel a bit too bright to me, eventually thinking if I had rabies or not. :’  (I know Malaysia was declared rabies-free since 2009 by WHO, but I doubt that due to my anxiety and gotten some symptoms listed at few websites (well, not flu, just headaches and eyes feel hurt, perhaps it’s because it’s caused by sleeping late. I slept at my school for like hours though))  No more flashes after buying Lux Auto Brightness (https://play.google.com/store/ap ... .vito.lux&hl=en), make brightness to negative and turn on night mode. But last night I was still disturbed by itchness which is caused by a skin disease I’ve gotten for a few years (since it’s cured last year but came back later, I suspect it’s eczema. Both of two skin doctors I’ve visited didn’t tell me what disease is it), went sleeping around 2:30AM. Also felt a bit sensitive to light at morning in school but not happening after at noon when coming back home + going out.  Yesterday, I bought Plague Inc: Evolved since I played it on my smartphone before and it’s not bad. But while playing I started to think about my fear again. Mostly because the game is a disease simulator which involves bacteria, virus and other microorganism. Today Morning I thought about rabies again and got some moderate panic attack but later calmed down a bit, I kept worrying about rabies even right now. 对了，关于高血压我怀疑是压力造成的

大致浏览了一遍，读出了两个信息：
一，高血压，（其实此前我对高血压除在电视上了解到的外几乎一无所知）对于此疾病知之甚少，所以仅能对你祝福，希望你的生活早日恢复
二，对死亡，尤其是疾病带来的死亡的恐惧，事实上我也常有这种念头，有时候我想我没什么恐惧的，但更多的，我真的，仗着生物的本能，我从心底里感到很害怕，这些恐惧也常笼罩着我，对于死亡后的未知的恐惧，以及对人生命之脆弱而感到不寒而栗……
我常常好奇死后会如何，但我转念一想——要想找到答案太简单了！然后这些胡思乱想一直缭绕着我，就如当初我对黑洞的恐惧一样，这些进去就没法出来，而里面又不能被外面观察的东西，有着不可名状的美丽和恐怖。
有时我甚至怀疑我是否能活到成年……

总之，这些东西曾困扰我很长时间
而我的建议是，心理咨询室，会帮上忙的，如果你仍然上学的话，学校里的免费条件再适合不过了

提醒一句，请务必分清心理咨询员和心理医生的区别
