不确定你用的是哪个菜单插件，猜测应该是chestCommands
首先，这个提示是菜单插件的提示信息，不是经济插件和点券插件的提示信息
以chestcommands为例，找到plugins\chestcommands\lang.yml
  1. no-points: '&cYou need {points} player points for this.'
  2. no-open-permission: '&cYou don''t have permission &e{permission} &cto use this menu.'
  3. default-no-icon-permission: '&cYou don''t have permission for this icon.'
  4. open-menu: '&aOpening the menu "{menu}".'
  5. no-exp: '&cYou need {levels} XP levels for this.'
  6. menu-not-found: '&cMenu not found! Please inform the staff.'
  7. no-money: '&cYou need {money}$ for this.'
  8. no-required-item: '&cYou must have &e{amount}x {material} &c(ID: {id}, data value:
  9.   {datavalue}) for this.'
  10. open-menu-others: '&aOpening the menu "{menu}" to {player}.'
  11. any: any
复制代码


将上面的内容自行修改成你想要的提示信息即可
比如：
  1. no-points: '&c你需要{points}点券来购买这个物品！'
