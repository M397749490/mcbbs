成就英文版
成就
Challanges:
  • Craft a bucket
  • Build a cobblestone generator
  • Build a melon house by your second minecraft day
  • Find 3 hidden chests
  • Craft a bed
  • Make a wheat farm
  • Make a Pumpkin farm (you must to find it first!)
  • Make a Sugarcane farm
  • Make a cactus farm
  • Go to the Nether
  • Find the grass
  • Find all the animals
  • Make a farm of Pigs/Cows (choose!)
  • Tame all the wolves
  • Catch 10 fish
  • Craft 5 books
  • Craft a painting
  • Find the secret zone
  • Find at least 2 underground lava lakes
  • Find the mob spawners
  • Build a monster trap
  • Make a snow golem
  • Find the ancient library
  • Find the abandoned mineshaft
  • Find/Craft 12 eyes of ender
  • Optional: Don't eat melons (HARDCORE!)
  • Optional: Go to the End and defeat the Ender Dragon
