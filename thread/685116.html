简介 Description
Welcome to the all new sequel to the popular Uniquely Parkour! With 25 new handcrafted levels, Uniquely Parkour 2 is bound to have something you haven't tried!
Remember, blue clay will give you a speed boost, green clay will make you jump higher and, now, white shulker boxes will levitate you! Beware!

欢迎来到最新最热的独一无二的跑酷系列！这次加上了25个纯手造的新关卡，独一无二的跑酷2有一些你并未尝试过的新鲜事物！

注意，蓝色的染色黏土会给你速度buff，绿色会给你跳跃提升，现在，白色的潜影箱会给你漂浮！注意！


特征 Features
  • 25 Levels (in order) and a bonus stage!
Rules and Requirements:
  • Singleplayer only, this map may not be multiplayer compatible (currently untested)
  • Don't edit terrain! If it's there, it should be there and STAY there
  • Don't cheat with creative mode! It ruins the fun, also removes the point of a parkour map
  • You may use the Toggledownfall command, as this world likes to rain a lot! (28 times during construction)
  • Have fun! You must! At all times! Even when you're not, you must have fun!

  • 25关
规则和需要：
  • 只能单人，此地图可能不能多人（因为尚未测试）
  • 别编辑地形，东西在哪，它就应该在那。
  • 别再创造作弊！这会毁掉喜悦，还同时毁掉了跑酷本身的意义。
  • 你可以使用Toggledownfall这指令，此地图会很平常下雨！（在制作时下了28次）
  • 享受吧！你必须！每时每刻！如果你不开心，那你也得享受！
