The only realistic texture pack at default resolution!

Includes blocks, items, armors, most mobs, weather, particles, custom water, custom lighting, a stylish, minimalist graphical interface and a few alternative optional textures.

Realistic colors, subtle shading, seamless tiling, unbelievable detail, everything was designed to make Minecraft look more real without sacrificing its blocky style. This is not your average flat, amateurish 16x pack!

Feel free to visit my thread on the Minecraft Forums.

Please don't redistribute my pack or use any part of it in other websites or in packs intended to be released to the public.

If you think my work or parts of it look good with your pack don't include it in your own, just put a link to PR and suggest people to download and use it together with your pack so everyone's happy. If you want to post it elsewhere do it by a link to this page or the forum thread so I get credit and downloads i.e. don't upload the pack of parts of it on a different website.

NOTE: you don't need mods to correctly view this pack in game, but if you use Optifine or MCPatcher you'll enjoy a much better experience, especially regarding lighting, colors and sky effects.


ALTERNATIVE PACKS

Pixel Reality Luminance: this pack features lighting effects only, all textures stay vanilla. Excellent if you just want a more atmosferic and realistic experience but don't care for different graphics.

Pixel Reality Atmosphere: another addon pack to add a realistic sky to vanilla or any pack, of any resolution.

Pixel Reality - Addendum: a few small optional addon packs that change some textures if applied on top of Pixel Reality on the resource pack menu. Open the .zip and follow the instructions about installing addons.

  • Adobe: changes stained hardened clay into pastel tinted adobe-like blocks good for building and pixel art (but bad in the Mesa).
  • Framed Glass: adds a frame to stained glass and makes it slightly more transparent so it looks better for modern buildings.
  • Bright Stone: makes any stone-based block brighter, pretty much as vanilla stone; this includes cobblestone, stone bricks, furnaces, slabs etc.
