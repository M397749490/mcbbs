原文：
Then try this mod. It adds three new backpacks with small, medium and large inventory.I am not a developer of any older backpack mod. Textures are from Might Porky's backpack mod, which he handed over to Eydamos. Because of that i think i'll change the texture to my own soon. I have no contact with Eydamos in any way.This mod can be packed in every modpack you want :)Sourcecode is linked above and is available under Apache License 2.0.Issues goes to the Issuetracker linked above.


翻译：
需要更多的库存存储吗？ 试试这个mod。增加了三个新的背包，分别有中、小、大、号。 我不是mod的开发人员。来自Might Porky的mod，他把mod转交给Eydamos 所以我想我很快就会将纹理更改为自己的纹理。我与Eydamos没有任何联系。 这个mod可以搬运到每个整合包中 源代码在上面链接里，可以在Apache许可证2.0版下获得
