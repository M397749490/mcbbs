Help streamers support charity: water!
Watch some great Minecraft streams and help a great cause!
帮助主播支持  慈善项目：水！
观赏伟大的我的世界交流，帮助一个伟大的慈善事业！


The Village & Pillage update is getting closer and closer and we’re trying to think of the best way to celebrate it. Refuse to say anything but ‘hrrm’ Villager-style until it’s released? Hrrm, perhaps not. OK, how about supporting charity: water, an incredible organisation that’s on a mission to bring clean water to every person on the planet? Hrrrm! I mean, that’s a great idea!
村庄&掠夺者的更新越来越近了，我们正在努力的想通过最好的方式来庆祝版本更新。在（正式版）发布之前除了村民“哼哼声”之外，啥也不说，也许不是。好吧，去支持charity: water感觉怎么样？这是一个神秘的组织，它的使命是为地球上每一个人带来干净的水。哼哼！我的意思是，这是一个好主意！


Water wells are being redesigned and expanded in the Village & Pillage update, which is all the excuse we need to support charity: water. With 663 million people currently lacking access to clean water (that’s almost 1 in 10 people), the great work of charity: water is absolutely essential. They want to bring clean water to as many people as possible, and we want to help!
在村庄&掠夺者版本更新中，我们对水井进行了重新设计和扩建，这是我们所有的借口，我们需要支持“charity: water”，由于目前有6.63亿人无法获得干净的水（这几乎是全世界十分之一的人），所以慈善项目主要目的：水是绝对需要的。他们想把干净的水带给尽可能多的人，我们也想帮忙！



This weekend, charity: water are aiming to raise $100,000 to bring clean water to over 3000 people through their livestreaming event, the ‘Weekend for Water’. So we’re teaming up with some of our favourite streamers, providing them with great Minecraft prizes to give away, and helping charity: water do just that! Does this mean we’re going to make an excellent joke about water and streaming in the next sentence of this post? No, we’ve got more important things to write. Like telling you how you can help!
本周末，“charity: water”计划筹集10万美元，通过他们的直播活动“Weekend for Water”，为3000多人带来干净的水。因此，我们正在与主播合作，为他们提供《我的世界》的大奖！并帮助慈善项目“charity: water”，这意味着在下一篇文章中，我们要讲一个关于水和水流的一个精彩的玩笑？不，我们还有更重要的东西需要写。比如让我告诉你，你能帮上什么忙！

During their streams, you’ll be able to make donations to charity:water and the streamers will be giving away loads of Minecoins that you can redeem for anything you like on the Minecraft Marketplace. All you have to do is tune in to their streams for your chance to win! Click their links to find out more:
