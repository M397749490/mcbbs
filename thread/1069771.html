标题: 猫我所欲也
猫，我所欲也；Minecraft，亦我所欲也。2者不可得兼，舍猫而取Minecraft者也。
Cats are what I want; so is Minecraft. When I can't have both, I should give up cats and take Minecraft.
钱，亦我所欲也；装备，亦我所欲也。2者不可得兼，舍钱而取装备者也。
Money is also what I want; so is equipment. When I can't have both, I should give up money and take equipment.
