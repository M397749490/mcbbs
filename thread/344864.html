关于编写音乐:
You do not need to use the template to write your music. All you need is the starting bit and armor stand. From there, you can opt to use repeaters instead of command blocks to play your music at a better pace for your needs. If you do use repeaters, the music stopping feature won't be possible, unless you make your own mechanism to stop the music, so keep that in mind. Theoretically, you could use Note Block Studio to make a schematic of your music, and use that by replacing all notes of a certain type with the corresponding command block using /fill replace, and adding in pedals and sound changes manually.
