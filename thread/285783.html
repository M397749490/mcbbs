「Bunnies were initially an idea for a mob in Survival Test. They would spawn randomly and jump around. When attacked, they would pop into many pieces and start bleeding.」——Minecraft Wiki

「兔子最初的主意来自生存测试。他们随机生成并到处乱跳（反正不是新闻就不太正经地翻译了。。）。当被攻击时，他们会爆成碎片并且开始流血。」

一天后，「Bunnies were ultimately not added at the time due to negative feedback received by Notch.」——Minecraft Wiki
「兔子最终没有加入因为Notch收到了一些负面的反馈」
