Kind of every single industrial mod but for fabric and with bad textures
MACHINES


This mod presents standard machines such as furnaces, pulverizers, compressors (and more) and each of them have 4 different tiers.


You can also improve them by using upgrades such as the speed upgrade.


It also contains other helpful machines such as the chopper for your wood farms, the rancher for your animal farms and the miner for, well, mining.


NOTES

All machines consume energy, known as LF or Lazuli Flux which is generated by other machines.

This mod is still in development and more things are yet to come (suggestions are welcome) and hopefully we'll have better textures in the future.

This is the first release of this mod, crashes and other bugs may occur and balancing needs to be done, which is why your suggestion is valuable.









模组简介


这是一个刚起步的工业模组，用的也是最新版的MC，目前还需要完善更多的内容，还有优化平衡性，大家可以直接到CF的帖子下留言，或者在这里留言让我告诉作者，正常科技模组有的这个模组都有，没有的以后也会完善,大家可以等待进一步的更新
