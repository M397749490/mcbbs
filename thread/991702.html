SKYBLOCK BEYOND

什么是SKYBLOCK BEYOND?
SKYBLOCK BEYOND是由KAMPFINATOR300制作的一张生存地图版本为1.14.4
这是他地图的介绍
In this map you will start on one oak tree with nothing but the vast void around you and through custom crafting you'll be able to get iron, gold, diamonds, go to the nether and at the end, well, go to the end and kill the ender dragon.

If you like this map, then please let me know in the comments because I would then start working on a dungeon update of the map where you can actually put the armor and accessories to good use.

在这张地图当中，你将从砍树开始，你的周围的方块非常的少，你要通过你的努力，获得铁，金，钻石，到达地狱，最后到达末地杀死末影龙如果你喜欢这种地图，请在评论(MCBBS回复)中让我知道，然后我将开始研究地图的更新，在这张地图中你可以添加其他的装备和武器
