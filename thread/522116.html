标题: [1.7.10][ssp/smp][汉化]洞穴装饰MOD—WildCaves 3
这MOD增加了一些钟乳石，石笋，发光蘑菇，草等等，使洞穴看起来更好看，更真实
不同的生物群系会生成不同的洞穴植物
新版本还加入了在洞穴生成骨头化石（一种矿石）
多人有效
以上引自78搬运的贴子，78的贴子是1.7.2，这是1.7.10的，而且他网盘也挂了，我就去原帖下了1.7.10并汉化了来造福大家
个人英语水平有限，而且这个作者把所有的长得相似的命名成一样的，所以我的翻译有些可能与原意偏差有点大，但符合其在游戏里的表现

原帖介绍
Decorates caves to make them more interesting
Stalactites and Stalagmites generate in caves.
Stalagmites will only generate under stalactites.
Stalactites, Stalagmites and vines generate dynamically and can be as long as you want (config option)
Entities can get damaged when falling on stalagmites
Different features depending on biome Stalactites and stalagmites in all biomes Vines on humid and jungle like biomes Icicles on cold biomes
More water sources on wet biomes
sand and sandstone generates on arid caves sandstone stalactites/stalagmites on arid biomes ice generates on frozen biomes iceshrooms on frozen caves stone stalactites/stalagmites drop cobblestone and they can be collected intact with silk touch
sandstone stalactites/stalagmites drop sandstone and they can be collected intact with silk touch spiderwebs now generate on caves
Glowing mushrooms and ice shrooms drop glow stone dust when destroyed and can be sheared
Properties file with many options to customize the mod to your liking, change block ids, the frequency a certain feature occurs, length of vines and stalactites/stalagmites, etc.
Compatible with ExtrabiomesXL skulls/heads spawn on dungeon chests skeleton skulls spawn rarely on caves


个人渣翻译
对洞穴进行装饰，使其更有趣
在洞穴中生成钟乳石和石笋
石笋只会在钟乳石下生成
在配置里可以调节钟乳石，石笋和藤蔓的生成
根据生物群系的不同特点会生成不同的装饰
精准采集可以获取钟乳石这些方块
洞穴中可能会大量生成蜘蛛网
蘑菇什么的可以用剪刀剪？
属性文件中有许多自定义选项：物品的id，某些特征发生的频率，藤蔓和钟乳石、石笋，长度等。
兼容extrabiomesxl
骷髅头会在洞穴里生成


研究了骨头化石，挖掘产物和打死小白差不多，挖掘等级是石镐，产物有小白的头，原石，骨头，箭，时运似乎对它无效（不确定）
