懂了，就是不断在掉落物上放方块，从而使物品被挤开，然后不断重复，从而实现生成一只蚯蚓的对吧233，关于怎样做到无序合成，举个栗子——按顺序执行：
  1. /scoreboard objectives add test dummy
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard players set @e[type=Item] test 1 {OnGround:1b,Item:{id:"minecraft:apple"}}
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard players set @e[type=Item] test 2 {OnGround:1b,Item:{id:"minecraft:egg"}}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=Item,score_test_min=1,score_test=1] ~ ~ ~ /execute @e[type=Item,score_test_min=2,score_test=2,r=0] ~ ~ ~ /give @p minecraft:stone 1 0
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=Item,score_test_min=1,score_test=1] ~ ~ ~ /execute @e[type=Item,score_test_min=2,score_test=2,r=0] ~ ~ ~ /kill @e[type=Item,r=0,score_test_min=2]
