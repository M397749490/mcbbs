What is ThrowCraft?

什么是ThrowCraft？With ThowCraft, you can fire any mob in an egg, like normal eggs spawning chickens, but the chance of spawning the mob is 100%, and you can make almost every mob in the game, with the exclusion of wolves (they are included, but you can't craft them without an inventory editor becuase I can't think of a decent crafting recipie yet) and Giants, becuase they aren't meant to be in the game.
在ThrowCraft中，你可以以蛋的形式发射任何生物，像发射鸡蛋有概率会生出小鸡一样，但是通过这种方式出生的生物的概率是100%，并有你可以发射几乎所有在游戏中的生物，但是不包括狼（作者还没想好一种合适的方式去合成狼蛋），也不包括巨人。

You can also throw a variety of bombs that do things to the enviroment, like water and lava bombs, instant TNT bombs and even lightning bombs (have fun combining this and the creeper egg!)
你还可以合成多样的炸弹去改变环境，像水弹和岩浆弹，立刻爆炸的TNT甚至是闪电炸弹，你可以有趣的尝试这些炸弹和JJ怪的组合（闪电+JJ=闪电JJ）

Also, every single egg is firable from dispensers, which, when SMP support comes out, will make for some very nice traps....
当然，每一种蛋都可以从发射器中发射出来，当SMP mod的支持完成时，我们可以使用这些做一些非常棒的陷阱（坏笑...）
