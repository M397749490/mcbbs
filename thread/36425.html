原文：
Until Beta 1.9, Minecraft had 12 unused Music Files.After that point, only one remained unused. These are stored along with 13.mus and cat.mus (which have been decoded as 13.ogg and cat.ogg respectively). The song "where are we now" was not added in Beta 1.9 Pre-release 2 because of problems with the spaces in the name.
翻译后：
在Beta 1.9版本之前，Minecraft有12个未使用的音乐档案。Beta 1.9版本后，只有一个音乐档案未使用。这些都存在13.mus和cat.mus（这些都分别被解码为13.ogg和cat.ogg）。其中一个歌曲“where are we now”没有加入到Beta 1.9 Pre-release 2版本，因为歌曲的名字里有空格。
