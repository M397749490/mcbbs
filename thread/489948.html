作者: sdlizhao9999    时间: 2015-8-19 20:36
banitem插件封禁了该物品，请在plugins/banitem/config.yml文件中删除该物品的封禁
封禁物品列表如下
Blacklist: []
Blacklist Placement: []
Blacklist Pickup: []
Blacklist Interaction: []
Blacklist Click: []
Blacklist Break: []
Blacklist World: []
