原文：
Goblins! is a single-player and LAN mod for Minecraft ,which adds Goblins and their Villages to your world! Those little buggers don;t like you at all and are more than willing to kill you! So get your weapons ready, because someone has to exterminate them!  Goblin villages are filled with great treasures and totems! Collected Totems can crafted into magical powders, which can be used to craft many powerful items (magical weapons and awesome utilities!). Villages are often located in swamps and plains, look there for them!
