直接tp就可以，只要每次tp的距离足够短，同时生物加上NoAI:1b的标签
  1. summon minecraft:zombie_pigman ~ ~ ~ {IsBaby:1b,NoAI:1b,Tags:["bot"],PersistenceRequired:1b}
  2. execute @e[tag=bot] ~ ~ ~ tp @s ~0.1 ~0 ~0 270 0
