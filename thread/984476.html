原版可以改一件盔甲的护甲值，比如这里我让一件皮革甲有钻石套的防御力（20点）：1.12-
  1. /give @p minecraft:leather_chestplate 1 0 {AttributeModifiers:[{Operation:0,Amount:20,UUIDLeast:1L,UUIDMost:1L,Slot:chest,AttributeName:"generic.armor",Name:"CB"}]}
复制代码
1.14+
  1. /give @p minecraft:leather_chestplate{AttributeModifiers:[{Operation:0,Amount:20,UUIDLeast:1L,UUIDMost:1L,Slot:chest,AttributeName:"generic.armor",Name:"CB"}]}} 1
