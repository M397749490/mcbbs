先新建VIP组后/mansave来保存修改
之后配置文件中VIP的继承项加Builder，并且build改为true，我的权限组配置：
  1. VIP:
  2.     default: false
  3.     permissions:
  4.     - essentials.fly
  5.     - essentials.teleport.bypass
  6.     - teleport.bypass
  7.     inheritance:
  8.     - builder
  9.     - g:essentials_builder
  10.     - g:towny_builder
  11.     info:
  12.       prefix: '&3VIP:'
  13.       build: true
  14.       suffix: ''
