简介 INFO
This is a map about jumping with different gravity than in normal Minecraft!
It includes 5 Levels + 3 Bonus-Levels and also a gallery.
You ask what the gallery is used for? Actually, it is used to teach you how the different Effect-Blocks work!
Yeah, you heard right, there are different Effect-Blocks, too!
So, what are you waiting for? If you ever wanted to jump upside-down in Minecraft, go ahead and play the map!
PS: Also if you aren't a parkour-pro, there's no problem because the parkour is easy.

这是一张在正常minecraft关于不同重力的跑酷。
有5关+3个附加关，还有一个陈列室。
如果你想问陈列室是干什么的？那么，这是用来告诉你地的效果方块的原理！
对！你猜对了，地图里有各种效果方块！
所以，你还在等什么？如果你想要体验在minecraft里反着跳，那就去玩吧！
PS：如果你是个跑酷渣，没关系，这个地图很简单！
