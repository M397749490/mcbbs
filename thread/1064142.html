标题: Mojang Studios将捐款支持黑人社区！
本帖最后由 Sinbad_Official 于 2020-6-19 21:13 编辑

NEWS
Join us in support of the Black community!
加入我们来一同支持黑人社区！
We are donating today’s proceeds from Minecraft, Minecraft Dungeons and the Minecraft Marketplace
我们将捐赠出今日（6月19日）的所有来自于《我的世界》《我的世界：地下城》以及市场的所有收益！
Cristina Anderca


We stand in unwavering support of the Black community, denouncing the racism, oppression, and suffering endured by so many for too long. As a team, we’ve been listening, learning, and considering how we can contribute to the powerful movements pushing for equality and justice in the US and around the world – because Black lives matter.
我们坚定地屹立于帮助黑人社区的洪流中，强烈谴责令人们长久以来深受苦难的无耻的种族主义。作为一个团队，我们向来在聆听，学习以及思索如何为美国乃至世界内的正义活动作出巨大贡献，因为BLM（黑人的命也是命）。

In commemoration of Juneteenth, this Friday, June 19th, 2020, we will be donating profits from digital storefront sales of Minecraft and Minecraft Dungeons, as well as purchases of content in the Minecraft Marketplace made from 12:00 AM PT to 11:59 PM PT to the following organizations: Black Lives Matter Global Network Foundation, Equal Justice Initiative, and the NAACP Legal Defense and Education Fund.
为了纪念2020年6月19日，这个不寻常的星期五，我们将捐赠出所有太平洋时间上午12:00至晚上11:59内所有售出的《我的世界》《我的世界：地下城》以及市场获得的所有收益，这笔钱将会捐赠给以下机构：黑人基金会、平等司法倡议组织以及美国全国有色人种协进会的法律辩护与教育基金会。

Thanks to help from partners such as Amazon who have waived their royalty fees, these donations will go even further.
十分感谢像 Amazon 一样帮助我们的合作伙伴，这些伙伴已经免除了它们的特许权使用费，因此这笔捐款将会造成更大的影响。

Learn more about these organizations at: Black Lives Matter Global Network Foundation at https://blacklivesmatter.com, Equal Justice Initiative at http://eji.org, and the NAACP LDF at https://naacpldf.org .
了解更多关于这些组织的信息吧：黑人基金会的官网为 https://blacklivesmatter.com ，平等司法倡议组织的官网为 http://eji.org，美国全国有色人种协进会的法律辩护与教育基金会的官网为 https://naacpldf.org。
