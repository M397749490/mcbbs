作者: 黑茶RGO    时间: 2019-11-17 12:12
II.summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"名字",CustomNameVisible:1}
III.summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {HandItems:[{id:"右手物品id",Count:1},{id:"左手物品id",Count:1}]}
IV.summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {NoBasePlate:1}
显示玩家全部皮肤需要修改材质或者模型，否则无法做到
