  1. 1.Remove weapon cooldown
  2. 2.Master toggle & per-world toggle for each module (feature)
  3. 3.Disabling player collisions (as in pre-1.9)
  4. 4.Setting tool damage as in pre-1.9
  5. 5.Disable sword sweep attack
  6. 6.Enchanted golden apple crafting & enchanted golden/normal golden apple potion effects as in pre-1.9
  7. 7.Fishing rods knockback and damage like in pre-1.9
  8. 8.Give players a shield while they right click with a sword
  9. 9.Have armour be as strong as in pre-1.9
  10. 10.Option to disable shield crafting
  11. 11.Disable off-hand
  12. 12.Make brewing stands work like in pre-1.9
