│概述Overview
When a player kills another, they receive a set amount of experience-levels!

"当玩家杀死另一个玩家时，他们会获得一定量的经验！"
XP Kill is fairly self-explanatory. It allows you to set the amount of XP levels a player will receive when they kill another player. Not much else to say; extremely basic, portable and light-weight. Just adds a little extra to the game if that's what you're looking for.?

"Xp Kill是不言自明的,它允许您设置玩家在杀死另一个玩家时将获得的经验数量,没有什么可以介绍; 极其的基础、便携、重量轻。如果这是你正在寻找的，那就只要把该插件加入到服务器即可"
