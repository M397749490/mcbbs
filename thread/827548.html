1.先确认难度是否被调成和平 "difficulty:PEACEFUL" 或  "difficulty:0"
若被调成和平 则将难度更改为EASY


  2.   world:
  3.     ==: MVWorld
  4.     hidden: 'false'
  5.     alias: §bworld1
  6.     color: WHITE
  7.     style: NORMAL
  8.     pvp: 'true'
  9.     scale: '1.0'
  10.     respawnWorld: ''
  11.     allowWeather: 'true'
  12.     difficulty: EASY
复制代码


2.找到spawning子项,确认monsters中的
spawn: '' 若为false 更改为true

  2.     spawning:
  3.       ==: MVSpawnSettings
  4.       animals:
  5.         ==: MVSpawnSubSettings
  6.         spawn: 'true'
  7.         spawnrate: '-1'
  8.         exceptions: []
  9.       monsters:
  10.         ==: MVSpawnSubSettings
  11.         spawn: 'true'
  12.         spawnrate: '-1'
  13.         exceptions: []
复制代码
