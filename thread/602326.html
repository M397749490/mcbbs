这个是无法关闭生成的，你只能禁止他传播
.minecraft\config\Thaumcraft.cfg
  1.     # The chance per block update (1 in n) of the Taint biome spreading. Setting it to 0 prevents the spread of Taint biomes.
  2.     I:biome_taint_spread=200
复制代码

将200改成0就可以禁止传播了
