Rodrigo's Pack - 通类方块

作者原文：
  Cartoon texture pack with radiant colors, with a brighter and colorful palette tones.
Each texture and color has been tested in different situations to ensure the best visual experience in the game. Also, it removes a lot of redundant detail which gives to the game a more simplistic and cartoony look.
