《紅色的Redstone》

In Very deep Underground，spiders and skulls are all around
在很深很深的地底，被蜘蛛和弓箭手圍繞
Shinning in red through these gray rocks，Waves my diamond pickaxe and knock it down
灰色岩石后發出了淡淡的红色光芒，挥舞我的钻石镐把他们都敲下来

I‘m really happy because it comes out four
我很开心因为一下子出来四个，
I can make these redstone form dust to torch
我可以把这些红石都做成火炬
All I need to do is just backing home
我只要赶快回家就好了，
But there’s some creepers meets me and they explode
但有些Creeper看到我就把我爆了

I lost everything when I woke up on my bed
醒来时我发现我身上的东西都没了
I see that even my pet wolf feel sad  
就连我的宠物狗都为我觉得悲伤
Right I’m still down in my own mood
当我仍然在为自己感到难过时
I see someone looks really green on the road
我看都有个绿色的东西走了过来

The creeper coming and stand in front of me
有只Creeper来到我的面前
He says they feel so sorry about what they did
他说他们对他们做的事情感到很抱歉
He pass me a package which is just like my bag
他递给我一个看起来就像是我掉的背包。
He say they only wanted to be my friend
他说他们只是想跟我做个朋友。

From that day everyone is not alone
从那天开始，大家都不再孤单
We’ll be together and search for more redstone
我们将在一起探寻更多的红石！
