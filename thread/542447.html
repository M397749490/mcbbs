The 0.14.0 update will come with more than just new mobs and and items; It will
also include a World Importing Option. You will be able to import worlds for the
first time in game, in Minecraft Pocket Edition.
As updates continue,
features are being added over time to give players more control of the file
system in Minecraft PE. There are plans to bring much more control in the
future, and will be reported here once that time comes.
Here’s a screenshot
of what this importing worlds will look like:

伴随着0.14.0更新而来的将不仅仅是新怪物、新物品；它将还会包括一个“世界导入”功能。你将能够在MCPE里导入你第一次游戏时世界的样子。[译注：可能就是刚刚进入时自然地形的样子...话说用种子不就好了吗]
更新还在继续着，特性也正在为了在MCPE里给玩家更多对于系统文件的控制权而随着时间的推移而增加。我们计划在以后带来更多（新的）管控权，并且每回都会在这里播报一次。
