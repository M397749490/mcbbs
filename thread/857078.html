PureEdge — 纯净边界

作者原文：
My texturepack was designed to look a lot like default without actually being default. I've kept all the original colours, and simplified the design of the blocks to my taste. One of the most differentiating blocks is perhaps the Glowstone, whichs looks nothing like default, but as mentioned before, it keeps all the default colours.

译文：
我的材质包看起来很像是原版材质包，实然不是。我保留了所有的原版颜色和并且按照我的个人爱好改变了方块设计。最能体现这种差异性的方块就是荧石，无论从任何一个角度看它都不像是一个原版方块，不过却保留了原版颜色。
