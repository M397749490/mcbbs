particles.pngAll ParticlesThe redstone particles' color cannot be changed.
pack.pngThumbnail of pack in texture selection list.Size should be 128x128 pixels.
pack.txtOptional description of texture in pack selection list.The text shouldn't be too long or it will not appear.
/titlepanorama pics, mclogo, black.png, mojang, and splashes.
/environmentSnow, rain and clouds.
/fontIn-game font.
/terrainSun and moon.The image is horizontally flipped in the game. (any words drawn will be backwards until sunset, and then they will be forwards)
/itemIn-game models for items such as signs, carts,boats, arrows, and Chests.The doors file is not used.
/guiItem hotbar, inventory screens, item thumbnails,Minecraft logo and menu background.
/artAll paintings in game.
/mobAll mobs in game.
/armorAll armor in game.
/achievementThe achievements screen GUI.
/miscBiome grass/foliage color, overlay for pumpkinhelmets, the clock dial, footprint, explosion particles, and the map background. (Plus a few things you should leave be.)
/title/bgMinecraft main menu backgroundNamed panorama0 - panorama5
