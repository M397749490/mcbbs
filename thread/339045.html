之前翻了一下国外论坛，发现了一个方法。能不能用我不确定，所以希望自己判断.....如有损失别找我QwQ

FOR IOS:

1) Play your old world then exit out of that world.

2) Download ifunbox on pc or if jailbroken use ifile.

3) Create a new survival/creative world in infinite terrain.

4) go into ifile or ifunbox, go to applications/minecraftpe/documents/com.mojang/minecraftworlds/NameOfOldWorldHere

5) copy the 'db folder' inside your old world then go to the folder of your new survival world and paste/overwrite that db folder with the db folder from your old world.

6) go on your new survival/creative world you created at the start and there you go your old world is infinite.

YAY

NOW FOR ANDROID:

1. Download PocketInvEditor on your ANDROID DEVICE.

2. Then PocketInvEditor will have an option to make your finite world infinite.
