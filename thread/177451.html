Hello, I am GruntTheDivine former member of what was the Halo: Ultimate mod.  I, and a handful of other members have split from Halo: Ultimate (Their name is yet to be decided, they have decided to not make their name Minecraft: Combat Evolved so we are going to use it for now). This is an exciting new mod that intends to add several new features from the Halo franchise to Minecraft. This includes, armour, weapons, and vehicles. In addition this mod adds several new blocks such as hard light emitters, and various other metals. This mod also adds several new ores, new tools, and nuclear weapons. We intend on adding as much content as possible to enhance the Minecraft experience.
以上为本MOD制作组的前成员，从他的话中可以看出他们现在在因为名字在闹分家。
