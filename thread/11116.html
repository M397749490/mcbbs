  • Glistering Melon added. Crafted with Melon and Gold Nugget. Non-edible.非食用的闪光西瓜，金粒跟西瓜合成
  • From Jeb: The exit from "The End" is the end. Then you respawn. (离开"The End"这世界将会死亡)
  • Cutting up melon gives you 1 melon slice. Bug. 这是Bug,一个西瓜只有一片西瓜
  • Enderdragons skins exist.加入龙的皮肤，但是龙不会自然出来
  • "The End" is the new realm. (新的世界"The End")
    • Cannot leave. You will have to die 离开这世界将会死亡
    • Hard to leave "The End in creative mode. 于创造模式很难离开这世界
    • Dragons are not available yet. 龙暂时不会出现
    • Ground breaks into cobblestone.
    • Obsidian pillars. 黑曜石支柱
    • Experience orbs are black.于这世界里面的经验宝珠是黑色的
    • Cannot build Netherportals in "The End". 无法建筑地狱传送门于The End
    • Use Eye of Ender on the 'portals' in the strongholds.用Ender眼睛可以传送到据点
    • Maps in the End are blank. 于The End无法使用地图
    • Large floating island.会生成大的浮空岛
    • Beds explode. 床会爆炸
    • Water is placeable. 可放置水
  • Glistering melon can be used for initiating brewing. 闪光西瓜可以用在酝酿中
  • Enchantments now have names. 附魔现在有名字
  • Villages a lot more common? Need more info on this. 生成更多村庄
  • Toss "Ender Eye" into sky to find location of strongholds. 抛出Ender眼睛到天空可以找到据点
  • Enchanted levels are 1-7. Adding bookshelves gets you more and more 'higher' levels. So far the report is up to 1-?. (Number of drops increase as enchantment lvl increases. Lighting can change the enchantment amount. Unconfirmed.)魔法等级是最高7等，书架中的书本可以让你提升到更高的水平
  • Enderpearls can be found in stronghold chests.据点宝箱可以找到小黑宝珠
  • Endermen seem to be stronger.小黑变得更强{:biggrin:}
  • Strongholds contain more chests.据点中将会有更多宝箱
  • Water freezes faster.水会更快结冰
  • No baby animals. Bug. 没有小动物(不是动物没有了，是交配出来的小动物),这是Bug
  • Enchantment Table now has a tooltip name on creative mode. 魔法桌于创造模式中没有名字Bug修复
  • The potion of swiftness now leaves a visible effect on the player. 敏捷药水有更明显的效果
  • Signs can be rotated easier. 木牌放置的角度自由了
  • You can attack/kill mobs through glass. 可以穿过玻璃杀怪物
  • Multiple enchantments can be placed on a weapon. 多种附魔能放置于武器上

当中毒的时候会有新的效果
官方服务端支持远程控制和查询
蜘蛛更多脚

Bug:
西瓜只会掉1片，不会掉3-4片
