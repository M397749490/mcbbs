作者: gamemode3    时间: 2015-2-18 11:17
1.7只能手动刷出不会燃烧的僵尸骷髅
僵尸:
  1. /summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {Equipment:[{},{},{},{},{id:261,Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F]}
复制代码

骷髅::/summon Skeleton ~ ~1 ~ {Equipment:[{},{},{},{},{id:261,Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F]}
1.8增加了可以改变生物NBT的指令,所以可以一个指令让所有僵尸骷髅不会阳光点燃,也可以手动刷出~
僵尸:
  1. /entitydata @e[type=Zombie] {Equipment:[{},{},{},{},{id:261,Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F]}
复制代码

骷髅:
  1. /entitydata @e[type=Skeleton] {Equipment:[{},{},{},{},{id:261,Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F]}
