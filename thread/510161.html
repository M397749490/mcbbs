大家好这里是新人CmdResearcher，又来混金粒了。今天给大家带来一个多方块结构，PM土豆煲！
这鸟玩意主要是通过stats来实现的
版本15w40b，开始！
首先这就是PM土豆机的多方块结构


投入一个水桶就可以激活


实现的话很简单，使用stats+testforblocks即可
变量：
Block探测影响方块
cooktime探测土豆煮的时间
potatoc探测有多少个土豆
命令：
//标记水桶
  1. /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Item] add wb {Item:{id:"minecraft:water_bucket",Count:1b}}
复制代码
//建立连接
  1. /stats entity @e[type=Item,tag=wb] set AffectedBlocks @e[tag=wb,c=1] Block
复制代码
//探测方块
  1. /scoreboard players set @e[tag=wb] Block 0
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=wb] ~ ~ ~ /testforblocks 223 56 -1026 227 59 -1022 ~-2 ~-3 ~-2
复制代码
//生成盔甲架并且产生水桶
  1. /execute @e[score_Block_min=100,score_Block=100] ~ ~ ~ summon ItemFrame ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"Block"}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=ItemFrame,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ /kill @e[tag=wb,r=1]
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=ItemFrame,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ /summon Item ~ ~ ~ {Item:{id:"minecraft:bucket",Count:1b}}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=ItemFrame,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ summon ArmorStand ~ ~ ~0.46875 {CustomName:Block,NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b}
复制代码
  1. /kill @e[name=Block,type=ItemFrame]
复制代码
//产生水和火焰
  1. /execute @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ fill ~1 ~ ~1 ~-1 ~ ~-1 water
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ setblock ~ ~-2 ~ fire
复制代码

丢出一个铁块就可以清除结构，这个也很简单


//给铁块标签
  1. /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Item] add ib {Item:{id:"minecraft:iron_block",Count:1b}}
复制代码
//给一个在土豆机旁边的铁块标签
  1. /execute @e[name=Block] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[tag=ib,r=0] add rmwater
复制代码
//移除土豆机结构
  1. /execute @e[tag=rmwater] ~ ~ ~ kill @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block,r=0]
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=rmwater] ~ ~ ~ fill ~-1 ~ ~-1 ~1 ~ ~1 air 0 replace water
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=rmwater] ~ ~ ~ /setblock ~ ~-2 ~ air
复制代码

现在丢入一个土豆就可以开始煮
丢入两个土豆就会被弹出


煮到10秒钟爆炸生成红烧PM煲


这个命令稍显复杂
但是仔细剖析也是非常简单的
//给土豆分数
  1. /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Item] add potato {Item:{id:"minecraft:potato",Count:1b}}
复制代码
//探测盔甲架旁边的土豆数量，如果超过2就弹出土豆
  1. /execute @e[type=Item,tag=potato] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players add @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block,r=1] potatoc 1
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[score_potatoc_min=2] ~ ~ ~ /entitydata @e[type=Item,tag=potato] {Motion:[0.0d,0.5d,0.2d]}
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard players set @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block] potatoc 0
复制代码
//增加煮土豆的时间
  1. /execute @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players add @e[tag=potato] cooktime 1
复制代码
//粒子效果
  1. /execute @e[type=ArmorStand,name=Block] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[tag=potato,r=1] ~ ~ ~ particle cloud ~ ~ ~ 0 0 0 0.1 5
复制代码
//生成红烧土豆
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ summon Item ~ ~ ~ {Item:{id:beetroot_soup,Count:1b,tag:{display:{Name:"红烧土豆煲",Lore:["PM土豆机荣誉出品"]}}},Motion:[0.2d,0.5d,0.0d]}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ summon Item ~ ~ ~ {Item:{id:beetroot_soup,Count:1b,tag:{display:{Name:"红烧土豆煲",Lore:["PM土豆机荣誉出品"]}}},Motion:[-0.2d,0.5d,0.0d]}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ summon Item ~ ~ ~ {Item:{id:beetroot_soup,Count:1b,tag:{display:{Name:"红烧土豆煲",Lore:["PM土豆机荣誉出品"]}}},Motion:[0.0d,0.5d,0.2d]}
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ summon Item ~ ~ ~ {Item:{id:beetroot_soup,Count:1b,tag:{display:{Name:"红烧土豆煲",Lore:["PM土豆机荣誉出品"]}}},Motion:[0.0d,0.5d,-0.2d]}
复制代码
//爆炸+清除
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ particle hugeexplosion ~ ~ ~ 1 1 1 1 1
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ playsound random.explode @a ~ ~ ~ 2
复制代码
  1. /execute @e[tag=potato,score_cooktime_min=200] ~ ~ ~ kill @e[tag=potato,r=0]
