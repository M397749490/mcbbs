用指令放置一个不会刷怪的boss刷怪笼就行了
如：/setblock ~ ~1 ~ minecraft:mob_spawner 0 replace {EntityId:EnderDragon,SpawnCount:0,SpawnRange:0,RequiredPlayerRange:0,Delay:999999,MinSpawnDelay:0,MaxSpawnDelay:0,MaxNearbyEntities:0,SpawnData:{CustomName:"求最佳"}}
