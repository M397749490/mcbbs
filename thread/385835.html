例子：/tellraw @a {"text": "选择队伍[","color":"black", "extra": [{"text":"红队","color":"red","clickEvent":{"action":"run_command","value":"/tp @p X Y Z"},"hoverEvent":{"action":"show_text","value":"红队点这个加入！"}},{"text":"][","color":"black"},{"text":"蓝队","color":"blue","clickEvent":{"action":"run_command","value":"/tp @p X Y Z"},"hoverEvent":{"action":"show_text","value":"蓝队点这个加入！"}},{"text":"]","color":"black"}]}效果：选择队伍[红队][蓝队]（红色和蓝色部分是可以点的）
指令讲解tellraw @a：告诉所有人；
"text": "选择队伍[","color":"black"：“选择队伍[”的字体颜色为黑；
"extra": [{"text":"红队","color":"red","clickEvent":运行一个可点击的字“红队”颜色为红色；
"action":"run_command","value":"/tp @p X Y Z"：运行指令为：创送某人到某地；
"hoverEvent":{"action":"show_text","value":"红队点这个加入！"；
鼠标停留在上面的提示语为：红队点这个加入！
讲解纯手写，望采纳！
