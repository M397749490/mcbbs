清怪神器——Delete

2017-7-14 21:10 上传

当此工具放在副手时，可以造成单体伤害；而当此工具放在主手时，则可以造成范围伤害。

命令

  1. give @p minecraft:iron_sword 1 0 {display:{Name:"Tool-Delete"},ench:[{id:16,lvl:32767},{id:22,lvl:32767}],AttributeModifiers:[{Slot:"mainhand",AttributeName:"generic.attackDamage",Name:"generic.attackDamage",Amount:2147483647.999,Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1},{Slot:"mainhand",AttributeName:"generic.attackSpeed",Name:"generic.attackSpeed",Amount:1024.000,Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1},{Slot:"offhand",AttributeName:"generic.attackDamage",Name:"generic.attackDamage",Amount:2147483647.999,Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1},{Slot:"offhand",AttributeName:"generic.attackSpeed",Name:"generic.attackSpeed",Amount:1024.000,Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1}],Unbreakable:1b}
复制代码
经过本人的测试，伤害为2147483648（即2的31次方）的时候是最高值，同时也希望能够有小伙伴可以超过这个最高值，并且与其他各位小伙伴分享。
