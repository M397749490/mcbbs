◆介绍◆


A very lightweight simple plugin to allow players to open iron doors and iron trap doors.
This will also play the use animation and sound effect.
Players can only open doors in places they can place blocks.
This should work with most protection plugins.

这是一个轻量级的插件，可以让玩家直接打开铁门和铁活板门。
打开时候还会有动画效果和音效。
玩家只能在他们的领地里打开铁门。
它可以支持大部分保护插件。
