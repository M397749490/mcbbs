<介绍>
曾经梦想过在您的服务器上有saico的自定义丹药,因为实在看不下去,Elixirs已经被我重新编码为所有使用! 使用所saico的自定义丹药,的丹药,支持Vault插件以及拥有一个很好的GUI，让玩家从中购买它们，这些丹药具有自定义效果，使您的服务器的战斗不像以前那样枯燥无味。这个插件还有一个内置的商人“阴暗术士”，你可以右键点击打开菜单，就像'saico'他卖的丹药一样。
Ever dreamed to have saicopvp's custom elixirs on your server, well look no further, as Elixirs has been re-coded by me for all to use! Use all saicopvp's custom elixirs with a premade economy and a nice GUI for your players to buy them from. These elixirs have custom effects to make pvp on your server like never before. This plugin also has a built in merchant "shady warlock" who you can right-click to open up the menu, as on saico he sells the elixirs.

权限

命令

-  /elixirs - 基本帮助命令。
-  /elixirs menu - 打开GUI菜单 (需要前置 Vault)
- /elixirs merchant - 生成一个Npc商人 (需要前置 citizens)
- /elixirs removeall -删除所有生成的Npc商人 ([size=14.6667px]需要前置 citizens)
