查找 essentials_builder
以下就是builder的权限，你那个就是
  1.   - essentials.back
  2.   - essentials.msg.color
  3.   - essentials.chat.color
  4.   - essentials.tpa
  5.   - essentials.tpaccept
  6.   - essentials.tpahere
  7.   - essentials.tpdeny
