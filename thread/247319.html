好的那我们来看看Poxelman 对此mod的介绍
Pixelmon for Minecraft 1.6.4 is a modification for Minecraft inspired from a Anime series ‘Pokemon’. It’s a multiplayer supported mod that adds 200+ alive Pokemons inside your Minecraft world. Every Pixelmon has its own attributes, habits, damage, armor, aggression and training. The fun part is; they can be captured inside the 3D Pokeballs after a battle. Pixelmon mod has an enhanced and easy to use GUI. The latest version is; Pixelmon v3.0..1




Pixelmon为1.6.4的Minecraft修改从动画系列“口袋妖怪”的启发。这是一个多人支持MOD ，增加了Minecraft 200里面活着的小宠物。每Pixelmon都有自己的属性，习惯，破坏，装甲，侵略和培训。最有趣的部分是，他们可以在里面的3D Pokeballs战斗后捕获。 Pixelmon国防部有一个增强的和易于使用的图形用户界面。最新的版本是; Pixelmon V3.0 .. 1
