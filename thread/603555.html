01

“Puff”

An ordinary iron pickaxe broke through the layers of snow and into the ice-cold rocks of Mt. Crystal. A young man, probably in his late-twenties, made his final step up Mt. Crystal as picking up his iron pickaxe that he used as a snow axe which was way too expensive.

The man looked nothing more than an ordinary explorer or some random guy that you would find on the streets. Interesting, despite the white furry coat and the also white trousers, there’s a well-crafted sword inlayed with crystals shining in the darkness as if it was covered with chrona currents.

“Finall…yyyyy” The explorer tripped over a gleaming blue gemstone, “Ugh sh*t.”

        The gemstone blinked with a dimmer light.

“Hey… Wait, look at this sh*t,” the gemstone was giving off a “chrona blue” light, “it’s… warm?”

The gemstone blinked again.

And again.

Another.

Blinked.

The blinking stopped.

Everything froze.

The explorer, holding a scorching crystal in his hand, was slowly dying as the blood vessels started to expand and eventually…

The crystal exploded. Ashes. Ruins. Dust. Sand. Dirt. Everything was everywhere.

A gigantic chrona current followed.

Mt. Crystal was empty. There were no trees, nor animals, no dirt, nor an explorer.

Everything was gone.

Silence.

03

The throne room was decorated with tons and tons of gemstones; yet, they were carefully placed as having the effect as a chrona field. Interestingly, the room was lit up by Mechilian lanterns instead of the commonly used chrona torches. The marble walls were curved into a symbol of phoenix, while the throne curved as a lion. It was indeed a fancy room with well-made decorations around; yet, no one seemed to have a nice day today.

“During the explosion at Mt. Crystal, it unleashed a massive chrona current. The chrona current sets anything it touches on fire as leaving ashes and blood behind. For the ones that have a stronger link with chrona, the chrona current can cause their body to explode due to the outrage of chrona inside them,” an armed man walked slowly towards the center of the throne room, “your majesty.”

“And the damage?”

“Several villages near Mt. Crystal were completely destroyed…” the man continues, “yet, interestingly, there was this one village survived the chrona current. According to the royal warlock, they’re mostly chromesses.”

“Hmm… There must be something up there…”

“One our men found a sword on the explosion site, it was found inlayed with chrona crystals as well as probably being enchanted but requires further examination…”

“God… The prophecy…” His hands were trembling.

05

“Most people went down into the anti-chrona shelters immediately after hearing the explosion; yet, some were slower or simply did not followed their instructions and were killed.” The messenger stood at the entrance of the conference room, the smell of oil from the motorbikes still remains on him.

It was a standard room that you would find in a factory, white walls with stone floors. There were nothing fancy, the only things were a table and some seats.

“Any data from the detectors?” There was a woman sitting on the table, spinning her pistol as if nothing had happened.

“The only thing the detectors caught before they exploded were that the power that thing produced is even higher than all of our steam factories added together. It’s… Incredible.”

“Well… We are running out of power indeed, and I believe that the war machines will love some chrona cannons?”

“Uh… The engineers were complaining about not having enough power to run their machines.”

“Anything else?”

“Well… They did say that if this thing can release chrona like it did, then there’s some chances that we can use them on machines.”

“Hmm… But then there would be Necroman and Aggresia to deal with.” The pistols stopped spinning in mid-air, “we can probably get Lion on our side if we give them some war machines… Yep, ready the war machines, prepare the steam cannons, and find those grandpas. Oh yeah, don’t forget the airships!”



06

“You don’t need to bullsh*t about how chrona works, ok? Just tell me can we use this thing as a weapon or something?” A heavily armed man said as he sat on the throne while the chrona blazed.

“Well… There is a high possibility that we can use in in some chrona weapons; from the chrona current that it gave off, it can deal tons of damage.”

“There we go, go tell the commander that we will go for it and tell him to get the army ready. We will be attacking soon. Let's show them how Aggresia attacks...” The man smirked with deep piercing eyes.

Green, deep, dark light filtered through the top branches, the aroma of floras bloomed into the air. Enjoying the earthy smell of chrona plants, the soldiers slowly traveled though the creaking branches. The logs and twigs danced among the leaves as the harmless insects crawled from one branch to another.

“Hey… Did something mov…” A chrona blast crashed onto the soldier.

09

Months passed after the discovery of the chrona core…

“Today, we will fight and we will win. Warriors of the phoenix, hold your swords, and fight with faith!” The army of Lion Kingdom marched towards the summit of Mt. Crystal, “we shall win this fight, and go back with honor! Do not fear the enemy…”

…

“My fellow Mechilian soldiers… It’s time to evolve and move into the next era of steam-power!” The Mechilia army were moving as well, “let’s burst some steam canons, shall we?”

…

“Ugh sh*t, it’s Aggresia again! They’re moving towards us, prepare for battle!”

While the Lion army and the Mechilian army were progressing uphill, the Aggresian army and the Necroman army were already in combat…

…

If there’s something that the Aggresian army didn’t know, it’s that Mechilia and Lion were in an alliance. While Aggresia and Necroman were fighting, Mechilia and Lion already made it to the top.

“Hey… That’s the core isn’t it?”

There was a gleaming blue gemstone hovering above an after-explosion crater, as sucking in every single bit of chrona around.

“Bring back the chrona core,” the King commanded.

“As you wish, my majesty.”

“Hold on a second, Mechilia should be the one to have control of it,” the Mechilian commander intruded the soldier, “you agreed to it.”

“Well, we just need it to test something,” the King frowned, “we’ll need it for a few days and then you can do your things.”

Abruptly, sounds of footsteps were getting closer and closer, “Wow, wow, wow. You guys are already dividing the crystal? Without us?”

The Aggresian army and Necroman army all arrived on the summit, ready to take a share of the chrona core. The soldiers looked tired as having blood all over them and cuts over their skins. The Aggresian commander stepped ahead, “well… How about… My troops and I beat the sh*t out of you guys and then take back the crystal? Or… I mean… If you guys don’t mind, you can give me the crystal right now.”

“Beat the sh*t out of us? I’d suggest you go home and cry like a baby.” The Lion army raised their shields, swords were out, arrows were on their bows…

“Then we’ll do it the hard way…” The Aggresian army started to charge, and so are the other armies.

The sounds of blasts and screams were everywhere, the barks of orders broke through the explosions as commanders slowly leading their men into an uncertain death. Time seemed slow, every moment of fear and every arrow making its own existence that is agony. The freezing earth, the torn, blasted earth; deaths centered on the crystal, leaving blood and deaths…

The chrona core blinked, but no one seemed to notice.

Again.

Another.

Blinked.

And stopped.

The crystal shattered into pieces.

Everything froze.

A blazing outburst of light and sound sprayed into the air; a fist of flame engulfed the soldiers, annihilating everything within its radius…

Silence.

The smoke and dust boile the ground as corpses smoldering with flame.

There were not a single living creature on the summit of Mt. Crystal.
