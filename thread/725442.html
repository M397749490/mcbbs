作者: 不动要塞    时间: 2017-8-28 13:13
先直接放命令：
/give @p minecraft:mob_spawner 1 0 {BlockEntityTag:{id:"MobSpawner",SpawnData:{id:"minecraft:xp_orb",},display:{Name:"经验球"},SpawnCount:3,SpawnRange:4,RequiredPlayerRange:30,Delay:0,MinSpawnDelay:300,MaxSpawnDelay:1800,MaxNearbyEntities:6,SpawnPotentials:[]}}
必须在命令方块中运行，聊天有字数限制，1.11.2测试可用


最后送你一个20倍速的经验刷怪笼，注意不要放多，容易卡。
/give @p minecraft:mob_spawner 1 0 {BlockEntityTag:{id:"MobSpawner",SpawnData:{id:"minecraft:xp_orb",},display:{Name:"经验球"},SpawnCount:12,SpawnRange:4,RequiredPlayerRange:30,Delay:0,MinSpawnDelay:50,MaxSpawnDelay:400,MaxNearbyEntities:20,SpawnPotentials:[]}}
