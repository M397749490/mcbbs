创建sneak1记分板，判据为stat.sneakTime
创建sneak2记分板，判据为dummy

  1. ###按顺序高频以下命令
  2. /scoreboard players tag @a remove sneak.1
  3. /scoreboard players tag @a remove sneak.2
  4. /scoreboard players tag @a remove sneak.3

  6. #下蹲状态判断
  7. /scoreboard players tag @a[score_sneak1_min=1,score_sneak2_min=0,score_sneak2=0] add sneak.2
  8. /scoreboard players tag @a[score_sneak1_min=1,tag=!sneak.2] add sneak.1
  9. /scoreboard players tag @a[score_sneak1_min=0,score_sneak1=0,score_sneak2_min=1] add sneak.3

  11. #执行
  12. /execute @a[tag=sneak.1] ~ ~ ~ say 持续下蹲
  13. /execute @a[tag=sneak.2] ~ ~ ~ say 开始下蹲
  14. /execute @a[tag=sneak.3] ~ ~ ~ say 结束下蹲

  16. #归位/给下tick做准备
  17. /execute @a ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players operation @s sneak2 = @s sneak1
  18. /scoreboard players set @a sneak1 0
