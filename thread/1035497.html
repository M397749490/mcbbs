首先用这个网址
https://yupana-engineering.com/online-uuid-to-c-array-converter

41e91665-09dc-4500-b020-f9c81f179ac1转化后是
int uuid_byte_array[16u] = {0x41,0xe9,0x16,0x65,0x09,0xdc,0x45,0x00,0xb0,0x20,0xf9,0xc8,0x1f,0x17,0x9a,0xc1};

4部分16进制为
41e91665 09dc4500 b020f9c8 1f179ac1

然后去https://www.rapidtables.com/convert/number/hex-to-decimal.html
把hex转dec

比如41e91665就是1105794661，也就是你的新uuid第一部分
