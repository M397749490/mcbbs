4J Studios：一个小的bug修复更新现在在Xbox 360平台上发布了.
更新日志在这里 - http://www.minecraftforum.net/forums/minecraft-xbox-360-edition/mcx360-recent-upcoming-updates/2064202-mcxbla-title-update-change-logs

Change log for Title Update 50 - March 14th 2017
TU 50 更新日志 - 2017-3-14
  • 修复了MCCE-4226 - UI中的非方块材质出现渲染错误.
Fix for MCCE-4226 - Wrong LOD showing for non-block item icons in the UI.

  • 修复了MCCE-4227 - 下界传送门会将玩家传送到错误的传送门.
Fix for MCCE-4227 - Nether portals sending players to the wrong portals.

  • 修复了MCCE-4321 - 玩家可能会在狭窄的地方跳跃的时候穿过下面的方块,例如一个小隧道.
Fix for MCCE-4321 - Player may fall through the block below them when jumping in a confined space e.g small tunnel.
