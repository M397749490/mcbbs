     原文：
well your doing your regular patrols of your territory or whatever It might come in handy to know these tips when you
run into a creeper,
1: Never turn your back on them otherwise if you fail to run BOOM you become ash
2: If you get into combat with one don't get to close otherwise you'll blow up(or at least some of your hearts will)
3: when attacking one its easy to just sprint attack and back off he may hiss and blow up but it wont be near you
4: try to get to higher ground and to a jump attack and run like from a tree or hill
5: using a bow is the best way of killing a creeper
6: if there is holes near by sprint attack him in it

I hope you found these tips useful I know it helps me a lot when I have to kill them!
please comment and diamond if you liked it!

翻译：好的，当你在巡视你的土地时或者无论什么时候，当你遇到一只苦力怕的时候，它可能会派上用场。
1.绝不要转过头来对着他们否则你可能会被炸成灰。
2.如果你一个人陷入战斗最好不要靠近否则你可能会被炸到（或者至少会损失一些血量）
3.当你攻击时,很容易冲刺攻击和退后,他可能会发出嘶嘶声和爆破但不会靠近你。
4.尝试到达高的地面或者做一次跳起来的攻击并且跑到一棵树下或者山丘上。
5.使用一把弓是最好的方法杀死苦力怕。
6.如果有一个洞在旁边你就可以把他打下去。

我希望我发现的这些技巧是有用的，当你不得不杀死他们的时候能帮助你。
