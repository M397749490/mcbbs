标题: [机械][娱乐]Real-Ice —— 让冰块更真实！可以做个跑酷啥的~[全版本]



Description - 介绍
Real-Ice was developed as I felt Ice wasn't realistic enough,
generally when someone walks/jumps on Ice it breaks,
therefore Real-Ice gives Ice this Feature,
when a player walks/jumps over ice, it breaks and turns into water!

作者认为让Minecraft里的冰块不够真实,通常有人走的时候冰会破,而Minecraft不会！
因此这个插件诞生了,当有玩家在冰上走/跳的时候,他会边破变成水!




Commands + Permissions - 命令+权限
There are none needed for this project!
这个插件没有任何命令权限!
