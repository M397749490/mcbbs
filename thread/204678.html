Overview

PlayGrid for Minecraft gives your server a professional account backend that provides these great features with just minutes of setup time:

  • Game web page where your players can register, get the server IP, check server status and see who is online.
  • Online registration and account management. Get a verified email address, name, and minecraft account id for all of your players. They can even sign up for your game using Facebook, Twitter, or Google+.
  • Manage user status including timed suspensions and bans. And you have complete control over user access and permissions depending on player account status.
  • Web store create a web store to sell items that are automatically delivered to your players.
  • Integrated forums for your players to discuss your server, suggest ideas, and get support.
And coming soon:
  • Automated server monitoring and sms notifications to alert you if your server goes down.
  • Require players to agree to your Terms of Use statement.
  • Home page CMS providing customized layouts, widgets, themes, sub-pages and content.
  • Assign other users as delegates who can manage your games / users

  • customize key image and text
  • See a list of recent players
  • News feed of recent announcements
  • Check server status and get server IP address
  • View the latest player leaderboard
  • Signup and manage account
--------------------------------------------------Create a web store to sell in-game items and ranks:

  • Easily setup an integrated web-store to sell in-game items and ranks
  • No fees: receive funds directly by connecting your PayPal account
  • PlayGridMC automatically delivers purchased items to your players
  • Specify item delivery using any combination of server console commands
--------------------------------------------------Fully integrated forums to engage your community:

  • Make game announcements that are displayed on your home page
  • Only registered players can participate in the forums
  • Banned and suspended players are also banned on forums
  • Popular defualt forums with forum customization coming soon
--------------------------------------------------Instant game dashboard allows you to monitor and configure your server settings:

  • Check server status

--------------------------------------------------Manage your players with an easy to use web interface:

  • Send mail to your registered players

  • Suspend players for a limted time
  • Ban players indefinitely
  • The PlayGrid plugin automatically tracks player settings and allows or kicks players according to a number of detailed settings you can manage
--------------------------------------------------Complete control over player access and permissions:

  • Optionally require users to signup or verify their email address before joining your server

  • Grant specialized groups to VIP players
  • Easily view and manage your groups and players assigned to each group

怎么用啊...

1.Install and run CraftBukkit (http://dl.bukkit.org/downloads/craftbukkit/list/rb/)
2.Install Vault plugin (http://dev.bukkit.org/server-mods/vault/)
3.Add the PlayGridMC.jar plugin to your plugins directory.
4.Restart your server to unpack the PlayGrid config file.
5.Go to http://www.playgrid.com. Create an account, add your game, and copy your secret token.
6.Open /plugins/PlayGridMC/config.yml and paste your secret key into the secret_key config option.
7.Restart your server.
8.You’re done! Make sure you have a valid mincraft account associated with your PlayGrid account and a verified email address. When you login to your server, your player will be authenticated with your new account platform!
