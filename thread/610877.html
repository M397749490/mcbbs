1.跟你说吧，我试过修改slime的自定义属性，结果是基本上无法修改，所以你得看看你的生物。

2./tellraw @a {"text":"成就：","color":"yellow","extra":[{"text":"作死","color":white"}]}

3./tellraw @a {"text":"成就：","color":"yellow","extra":[{"text":"作死","color":white"},{"text":"\n点我作死","color":"blue","clickEvent":{"action":"run_command","value":"/kill @a"}}]}

4./testfor @a {Inventory:[{Slot:0b,id:"minecraft:diamond",tag:{ench:[{id:16,lvl1}]}}]}
关于slot的位置，看这里:http://minecraft-zh.gamepedia.com/Player.dat%E6%A0%BC%E5%BC%8F
