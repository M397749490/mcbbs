直接上原理：
  1. /scoreboard objectives add Kill dummy
复制代码
  1. /give @a golden_hoe 1 0 {Unbreakable:1,ench:[{id:16,lvl:10},{id:20,lvl:1}],display:{Name:"§e§l灵魂摄手",Lore:["","§9以灵魂换取灵魂 ？ 是的 ， 灵魂摄手掌握了这种恐怖的力量 ......","","§5-= 摄取灵魂 =-","","§9杀死生物时 ， 灵魂摄手将以灵魂换取灵魂 ， 制造一个鬼魅灵魂暂时攻击附近怪物 。","","§7Epic Item",""]},Tags:["SoulS"],AttributeModifiers:[{AttributeName:generic.attackDamage,Amount:80,Operation:0,UUIDMost:1,UUIDLeast:1,Name:max,Slot:mainhand},{AttributeName:generic.attackSpeed,Amount:10000,Operation:1,UUIDMost:1,UUIDLeast:1,Name:max,Slot:mainhand}]}
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard objectives add SoulS totalKillCount
复制代码
先给自己一个特殊锄头，以及创建杀怪变量
  1. 高频/scoreboard players tag @a add SoulS {SelectedItem:{tag:{Tags:["SoulS"]}}}
复制代码
给手持灵魂摄手的玩家加tag
  1. 连锁：/execute @a[tag=SoulS,score_SoulS_min=1] ~ ~ ~ summon villager_golem ~ ~1 ~ {Attributes:[{Name:generic.movementSpeed,Base:0.6}],Invulnerable:1,Silent:1,ActiveEffects:[{Id:14,Duration:10000000,Ambient:1,NoParticles:1}],Tags:["SoulSS"]}
复制代码
杀怪时触发摄手能力
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players reset @a SoulS
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @a remove SoulS
复制代码
清除记分板和玩家tag
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=SoulSS] ~ ~ ~ particle ~ ~0.1 ~ 0 0 0 0 1 force
复制代码
  1. 连锁:/scoreboard players add @e[tag=SoulSS] Kill 1
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/kill @e[tag=SoulSS,score_Kill_min=80]
复制代码
给灵魂摄手召唤的铁傀儡加粒子，以及Kill掉存在时间够长的铁傀儡
