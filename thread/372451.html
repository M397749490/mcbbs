1.8创意跑酷小游戏~Creeper Jump Parkour~苦力怕跑酷~利用苦力怕的爆炸来帮助你实现各种跑酷~




关键是要掌握好距离。


The game is simple.
  • Step 1: Spawn a creeper
  • Step 2: Aim yourself so that the creeper will blow you up and will give you a boost to the next block
  • Step 3: Blow the creeper up
  • Step 4: Do it again
