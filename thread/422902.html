Welcome to the Blanc Maison de Ville! A Versailles Inspired 3,420 sqft French Baroque Townhome, Built as the Second  Avalon Morrisetti Custom home on the World of Keralis Server!   

This Lavish 5 Story French Baroque Townhome Sports 2 Bedrooms each with their own Ensuite and Closet, A 2 Story Open Foyer with Staircase directly to Living Room, An Indoor Pool, A Dining room and an Oversized Kitchen. All the Rooms are Oversized to give the illusion of much more space than there actually is. a Private Parking Space is Reserved on the back for Residents. Custom Winding Staircases Flow along the back of the house Costing nearly 2 million dollars alone in Labor and Materials. Exquisite Furnishings, and Top of the line Appliances and fixtures all come together to create the one and only Blanc Maison De Ville.
