原帖的汉化

我的汉化


配置：
拍卖时间打开auctions修改
第三行default-time: 60就是默认时间60秒

命令权限：

/auctions数量 起始价 拍卖加价
/auctions info查看当前拍卖[Bug就是会是英文]
/auctions stop[没有推出]
/auctions cance[没有推出]
/auctions developer[查看插件开发者]
/bid出价
/auctioneauctions.auction[玩家权限]
/auction infoeauctions.auction.info[玩家权限]
/auction developereauctions.auction.developer[给不给无所谓]
/auc [item] [starting bid] [bid increasment]eauctions.auction.start[玩家权限]
/bideauctions.bid[玩家权限]
