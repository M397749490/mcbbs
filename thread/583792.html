1.9趣味益智游戏

《Negentrop》







Negentropy 是一张单人解密地图，它有48个关卡和4个不同的移动物品，每一个物品都
有独特的运动方式。你的目标是在每一个关卡里放置这些物品，但不要让他们互相接触



原帖英文介绍：

Negentropy Map is a Singleplayer Puzzle Map with 48 Challenging Levels. There are 4 different types of moving objects, every one behaving differently. Your goal in each Level is to place all of the pieces in your inventory down without them touching each other.
