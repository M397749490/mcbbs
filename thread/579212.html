根据名称及世界获取Region:
WorldGuardPlugin plugin=WorldGuardPlugin.inst();
RegionContainer container = plugin.getRegionContainer();
RegionManager regions = container.get(world);
ProtectedRegion region = regions.getRegion("spawn");
根据Location获取Region设置信息类成员?:
ApplicableRegionSet set=regions.getApplicableRegions(location);
此外
官网有API,以下链接为上面相关代码的教程:
