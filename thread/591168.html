/give @p 物品名 数量 数据值 {CanDestroy:["方块名","方块名"]}

例：只能打掉沙石和砖块的镐子：
/give @p minecraft:stone_pickaxe 1 0 {CanDestroy:["minecraft:sandstone","minecraft:stonebrick"]}
