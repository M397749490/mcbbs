精灵球
Cherish Ball——贵重球
A Cherish Ball is a type of Poké Ball that has a 1× catch rate, the same as a normal Poké Ball. It can be obtained as a tier 2 special drop.  Cherish Balls can also be used to make Poké Gifts.
贵重球是一种捕获率与普通精灵球相同，捕获率为1×的精灵球。它可以作为2级的特殊掉落物。贵重球也可以用来制作宝可梦礼物。

Dive Ball——潜水球
A Dive Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball that has a 3.5× catch rate on Pokémon that are in water. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
潜水球是一种与普通精灵球稍微不同的精灵球，它在水中具有3.5×的捕获率。它可以通过合成来获得，也可以作为1级的特殊掉落物。

Dusk Ball——黑暗球
A Dusk Ball is a Poké Ball that has a 3.5× catch rate in dark places. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  If it is currently dusk or night, the Dusk Ball will have its increased catch rate. Otherwise, the location of the Pokémon to be caught must meet the following requirements for the Dusk Ball to have a 3.5× catch rate:  The Pokémon must be located at a height level of 64 or lower. The Pokémon must be in a place with a light level of 14 or lower (i.e. not in direct sunlight). Rain in an area of direct sunlight will not trigger the Dusk Ball's effects. Snow in an area of direct sunlight will trigger the Dusk Ball's effects.
黑暗球是一种在昏暗的地方有3.5×捕获率的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。如果是在黄昏和夜晚，它的捕获率将会增加。

另外，如果满足这些条件，它的捕获率也会增加。

1.宝可梦必须在高度等于或低于64的地方。

2.宝可梦所在地的光照程度必须等于或低于14（即不在阳光直射下）

3.在阳光直射的情况下，下雨和下雪都不会触发黑暗球的效果。

Fast Ball——速度球
A Fast Ball is a Poké Ball that has a 4× catch rate on Pokémon that have a base Speed of 100 or more. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  A list of Pokémon that the Fast Ball gives an increased catch rate on can be found here.
速度球是一种对基础速度超过100的宝可梦有4×捕获率的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买和作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。
速度球可以作用的宝可梦列表见下方。

Friend Ball——友友球
A Friend Ball is a type of Poké Ball that sets a captured Pokémon's base happiness to 200. This effect does not occur if a Friend Ball is inherited by a Pokémon through breeding. Friend Balls have the same catch rates as normal Poké Balls. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
友友球是一种可以设置已被捕获的宝可梦的亲密度到200的精灵球。如果它接收到的宝可梦是通过繁殖产生的，那么这个效果就不会发生。友友球有和普通精灵球相等的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Great Ball——超级球
A Great Ball is a type of Poké Ball categorized by the red and blue coloring on its upper lid. It has a 1.5× the catch rate of a normal Poké Ball. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
超级球是一种上盖上游红色和蓝色分布的精灵球。它有1.5×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买和作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Heal Ball——治愈球
A Heal Ball is a remedial Poké Ball that restores the caught Pokémon's HP and eliminates any status problem upon capture. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
治愈球是一种可以恢复宝可梦的球。它将会在捕捉时治疗宝可梦的生命值，并消除状态对它们的影响。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物获得。

Heavy Ball——沉重球
A Heavy Ball is a Poké Ball for catching very heavy Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  Unlike most Poké Balls, the Heavy Ball's catch rate modifier is additive rather than multiplicative. The catch rate modifier for Heavy Balls based on Pokémon weight is as follows:
Weight                Modifier
<204.8 kg                 -20
204.8-307.2 kg        +20
307.2-409.6 kg        +30
>409.6 kg                +40
沉重球是一种用于捕捉很重的宝可梦的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。不同于大多数精灵球，沉重球的捕获率调整是增加而不是倍乘。捕获率随捕获目标重量的变化如下：
重量                        变化量
<204.8 千克                 -20
204.8-307.2 千克        +20
307.2-409.6 千克        +30
>409.6 千克                +40

Level Ball——等级球
A Level Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball which becomes more effective when used on Pokémon at a lower level than the lead Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  The catch rate for the Level Ball is:  1× if the lead Pokémon is the same level or a lower level than the wild Pokémon. 2× if the lead Pokémon is above the level and less than double the level of the wild Pokémon. 4× if the lead Pokémon is between double and quadruple the level of the wild Pokémon. 8× if the lead Pokémon is quadruple the level or more of the wild Pokémon.
等级球是一种与普通精灵球稍有不同的精灵球，它对比你的首选宝可梦等级低的宝可梦的捕捉率会更高。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。
等级球的捕获率如下：
1×当你的首选宝可梦比目标宝可梦的等级低或与其相同时
2×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高，但未达到2倍时
4×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高2~4倍时
8×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高超过4倍时

Love Ball——甜蜜球
A Love Ball is a Poké Ball that has an 8× catch rate on Pokémon of both the same species and opposite gender as the player's Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop, It is also a drop from wild Granbull.
甜蜜球是一种对与你的宝可梦相同种类且性别相反的宝可梦有8×捕获率的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。它也有5%的几率从野生的布卢皇身上掉落。

Lure Ball——诱饵球
A Lure Ball is a type of Poké Ball that has a 3× catch rate on Pokémon encountered by fishing. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
诱饵球是一种对钓鱼时遇到的宝可梦有3×捕获率的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Luxury Ball——豪华球
A Luxury Ball is a comfortable Poké Ball that makes a caught wild Pokémon quickly grow friendly. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  A Luxury Ball grants an extra +1 happiness to the multiple ways of obtaining happiness, although it does not affect events that lower happiness. The effects of the Luxury Ball stack with the effects of a Soothe Bell.
豪华球是一个舒适的精灵球，让被捕获的野生宝可梦迅速地变得更加友好。它可以通过合成、购买，或是作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。
豪华球会让提升亲密度的事件额外增加1亲密度，虽然他并不影响降低亲密度的事情。豪华球的效果会覆盖安抚之铃的效果。



Master Ball——大师球
The Master Ball is a type of Poké Ball that will always catch the Pokémon it is used on (unless the Pokémon cannot be caught, like a boss Pokémon or another trainer's Pokémon). Unlike all other Poké Balls, Master Balls have no chance of breaking if thrown against a block. A Master Ball can be obtained as a tier 3 special drop, or as a possible drop from legendary and ultimate boss Pokémon. Master Balls cannot be crafted.
大师球是一种必定能抓住宝可梦的精灵球（除非目标宝可梦不能被抓获，例如一个boss宝可梦或者其他人的宝可梦）。与其他的精灵球不同，大师球在贴墙投掷时没有几率被打破。大师球可以通过作为3级特殊掉落物获得，或者作为一个传说boss或最终boss的掉落物来掉落。大师球不能被合成。
（其实大师球以前是可以合成的）

Moon Ball——月亮球
A Moon Ball has a 4x catch rate on Pokémon that evolve with a Moon Stone or belong to evolutionary families of the former. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
月亮球对于使用月亮石进化的或者它进化前的形态的宝可梦有4×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Nest Ball——巢穴球
A Nest Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball that works especially well on weaker Pokémon in the wild. The Nest Ball's catch rate is based on the formula:  41-Pokémon's level 10 ×, with a minimum catch rate of 1×. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
巢穴球是一种与普通精灵球略有不同的精灵球，它对弱小的野生宝可梦尤其有效。捕获率计算公式：41-宝可梦等级 10×，最小为1×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物获得。

Net Ball——捕网球
A Net Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball that has a 3× catch rate on Water and Bug-type Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
捕网球是一种与普通精灵球略有不同的精灵球，它对昆虫系宝可梦有3×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。



Poké Ball——精灵球
This article is about the regular Poké Ball.  A regular Poké Ball is the most basic type of Poké Ball. It has a 1× catch rate on all Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
这篇文章是关于通常的精灵球。精灵球是精灵球中最普通的一种（迷之别扭）。它对所有宝可梦有1×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Relugar Poké balls are also used to craft an old rod and parts of trade machine.精灵球也被用于制作一个破旧钓竿和贸易机器的零件。
精灵球有5%的几率从顽皮雷弹和霹雳电球身上掉落。

Premier Ball——纪念球
A Premier Ball is a Poké Ball that has been specially made to commemorate an event of some sort. Premier Balls are unique in that they have particle effects when thrown. This is purely aesthetic–they have the same catch rate as normal Poké Balls. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
纪念球是一种专为纪念某些事件而制作的精灵球。它是独特的，因为它抛出时将会产生粒子特效。这纯粹是为了美感——因为它有着和普通精灵球一样的捕获率。它可以通过制作、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。



Quick Ball——先机球
A Quick Ball is a type of Poké Ball that has a 5× catch rate if used on the first turn of a wild Pokémon battle, having a 1× catch rate otherwise. The Pokémon must be engaged in battle to take advantage of the increased catch rate; throwing a Quick Ball at a Pokémon outside of battle will result in a 1× catch rate. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
先机球是一种在战斗的第一回合使用时具有5×捕获率的精灵球，但它在其他时候（例如战斗的其它回合或战斗外）只有1×的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。



Repeat Ball——重复球
A Repeat Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball that has a 3× catch rate on Pokémon species that were previously caught or otherwise owned by the Trainer. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
重复球是一种对已经被抓过或训练师已经拥有的种类的宝可梦有3×的捕获率的精灵球。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。



Safari Ball——狩猎球
A Safari Ball is a ball that has a 1.5× catch rate if used in a Plains biome.Otherwise, it has a catch rate of 1×.
狩猎球是一种在平原生态区有1.5×捕获率的精灵球。在其他地方，它只有1×捕获率。

Sport Ball——竞赛球
A Sport Ball is a type of Poké Ball that has a 1.5× catch rate on Bug-type Pokémon. It has a 1× catch rate otherwise. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
竞赛球是一种对昆虫系宝可梦有1.5×捕获率的精灵球。它在其他情况下只有1×捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Timer Ball——计时球
A Timer Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball that becomes progressively better the more turns there are in a battle. Every turn, starting at 1×, a Timer Ball will increase in effectiveness by 0.3× (additive), with a maximum catch rate of 4× (11th turn and onwards). It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
计时球是一种与普通精灵球有所不同的精灵球，它在随着回合数变多将会越来越有效。它的捕获率从1×开始，每一个回合都会增加0.3×，直到达到上线4×（从第11个回合起）。它可以通过合成、购买，或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Ultra Ball——高级球
An Ultra Ball has a catch rate of 2× the normal Poké Ball's catch rate. It is more effective than a Great Ball. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
高级球是一种有2×捕获率的精灵球。它比超级球更加有效。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

工具/护甲

Aluminum tools——铝制工具
Aluminum ingots can be crafted into the standard set of tools (axe, pickaxe, shovel, hoe, sword) along with the hammer introduced by Pixelmon. They are slightly inferior to iron tools, all having 201 durability (whereas iron tools have 251). Aluminum axes, pickaxes, and shovels break their respective blocks at the same speed as their iron counterparts, while all tools that deal more than half a heart of damage except for hammers (i.e., sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel) deal half a heart of damage less than their iron counterparts (e.g. an aluminum sword deals three hearts of damage per hit).
铝锭可以被制成标准工具（斧、镐、铲、锄、剑），也可以被制成Pixelmon特有的锤。它们稍逊于铁制工具，都有201耐久（铁制工具有251耐久）。铝制的斧、镐、铲都和相同类型铁制工具具有相同的方块破坏速度，但造成的伤害比相同类型的铁制工具低半颗心（比如铝剑每次攻击造成3心伤害我怎么没看出来哪里低了）
