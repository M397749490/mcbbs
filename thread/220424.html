生怪磚:限定生成地點
/setblock ~ ~1 ~ minecraft:mob_spawner 0 replace {EntityId:Zombie,SpawnData:{Pos:[-310.5,10.5,936.5],Equipment:[{id:1},{},{},{},{id:95,damage:6}]},SpawnCount:1,SpawnRange:10,MinSpawnDelay:1,MaxSpawnDelay:1,MaxNearbyEntities:1,RequiredPlayerRange:3}

只要修改下裡面的Pos:[-310.5,10.5,936.5]成你要的x,y,z座標就能限定生出的地點啦!!!
