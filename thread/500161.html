NOW WITH NEW ABBILITIES, CHECKPOINTS, MORE PARKOUR AND COOKIES!!!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

That's right, I'm back with a new Duck Hunt map
This time, again Drug Run. But a little bit different. I've added more parkour, a few checkpoints and some cookies.

Again, how to play is very simple. The Targets must reach the end of the parkour to kill the Drug King to stop his evil plans, and the Drug King must kill the Targets to stop them from killing him.
But like I said above, it's a bit more challenging now.

First, let me explain the perks of being a Target;
- You now have three lives instead of the useal one.
-When you reach a checkpoint, you will respawn there if you die.
-Evry now and then one of you will randomly recieve a splash potion of Leaping or 16 seconds of Invisibillity.
-And when you're doing a good job on the parkour, you get cookies!

Now for the perks of being the Drug King;
- You have permanent Speed III
-When you kill someone three times, all the other Targets that are still alive get 15 seconds of Nausea!

Also, since it's 1.8, we now have Spectator Mode
So if you don't feel like playing and want to skip a round? Totally Possible

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There is not really a must use resource pack, Faithfull will do jsut fine
But if you want that Drug Run feel to it, I suggest the Drug Run 2 Resource pack:D


玩法：

1.当你不是追杀者的时候，您拥有3条命

2.我们还是很人性化的，给你设置了记录点O(∩_∩)O

3.每隔一段时间，玩家们随机会得到随机的正面效果！

4.当你是追杀者时，你获得无限时间的速度3

5.当你击杀了3次跑酷者时，每个活着的跑酷者会得到15秒反胃3效果

6.增加了旁观者模式

7.跑酷者的目标：到达地图的终点！

8.击杀者的目标：尽力阻挡跑酷者到达终点！
