自行解决了，勿回,留给后来者参考...

  1.   Diamonds2:
  2.     MenuItem:
  3.     - type:DIAMOND
  4.     - amount:5
  5.     - name:&95个钻石
  6.     - 'lore1:&e'
  7.     - 'lore2:&e'
  8.     RewardType: PLAYERCOMMAND
  9.     Reward:
  10.     - p h
  11.     PriceType: NOTHING
  12.     Message: '&e你回到别墅区了'
  13.     #####这里是右键内容#####
  14.     RewardType_right: PLAYERCOMMAND
  15.     Reward_right:
  16.     - fly
  17.     PriceType_right: NOTHING
  18.     Message_right: '&e你飞了!'
  19.     ExtraPermission: ''
  20.     InventoryLocation: 9
