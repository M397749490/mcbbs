作者: ⊙u⊙    时间: 2019-7-18 17:45
既然你没有特别指定哪个物品。

一次
/scoreboard objectives add UseStick stat.useItem.minecraft.carrot_on_a_stick
/give @s minecraft:carrot_on_a_stick 1 0 {sp:1b}


很多次
/execute @a[score_UseStick_min=1] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @s add sp {SelectedItem:{id:"minecraft:carrot_on_a_stick",tag:{sp:1b}}}
/execute @a[tag=sp] ~ ~ ~ say awsl
/scoreboard players tag @a remove sp
/scoreboard players reset @a UseStick
