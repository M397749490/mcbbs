点击触发权限上一个op号输入以下：
/mangaddp builder scriptblock.interact.*
/mangaddp builder scriptblock.interact.use
如果是踩到触发：
/mangaddp builder scriptblock.walk.*
/mangaddp builder scriptblock.walk.use
/mangaddp builder scriptblock.walk.command.*
