提前告诉你们，这篇帖子会很短···


       最近，我研究了盘灵古域这张大地图中NPC命令方块的部分，再结合自己曾经针对SUMMON命令的研究，我做出了这一小串命令模板：

/summon Villager ~ ~1 ~

{Profession:种类,CustomName:名字”,CustomNameVisible:1,Attributes:[{Name:generic.maxHealth,Base:最高血量}],Offers:{Recipes:[{maxUses:可交易次数,buy(给村民的):{id物品id,Count数量,Damage:0},sell(村名卖给你的):{id:物品id,Count数量,Damage:0,Damage:0S}]}}

各位可以去尝试一下，如果出现错误，无法召唤的话，请在最后3个括号中任意一个地方加上加1个逗号。

  最后，给出一个强大的命令：

/summon Villager ~ ~5 ~{Profession:0,CustomName:MCBBS”,CustomNameVisible:1,Attributes:[{Name:generic.maxHealth,Base999999}],Offers:{Recipes:[{maxUses999999,buy:{id:3,Count:1,Damage:0},sell:{id:276,Count:1,Damage:0,Damage:0S,tag:{AttributeModifiers:[{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1,Amount3,AttributeName:generic.attackDamage,Name:G},{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:2,UUIDMost:2,Amount3,AttributeName:generic.followRange,Name:Z},{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:3,UUIDMost:3,Amount3,AttributeName:generic.knockbackResistance,Name:J},{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:4,UUIDMost:4,Amount3,AttributeName:generic.movementSpeed,Name:S},{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:5,UUIDMost:5,Amount3,AttributeName:generic.maxHealth,Name:H},],}}},]}}
