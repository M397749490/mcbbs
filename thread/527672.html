最畅销的开放世界的电子游戏
Best-selling indie open-world videogame
记录保持者: Minecraft
成绩: 卖出18314346份
打破时间: 2015年1月28日
　　截至2015年1月28日，Minecraft官网上列出了在PC上和Mac上销售了的游戏的数量——18,314,346份——这相当于自从2011年7月17日Alpha版发布以来，平均每天有13,546个人购买了该游戏。这个游戏，允许玩家们在三维空间里使用不同纹理的立方体来建造各种建筑，是由瑞典人马库斯·"Notch"·泊松(Markus “Notch” Persson)一个人使用Java软件平台编写的。Minecraft没有做任何广告但很快就成功获得了巨大的口碑，让泊松成立了自己的游戏公司并且雇佣更多的员工以进一步游戏的开发。2014年9月，微软以25亿美元收购了Minecraft位于瑞典的开发商Mojang。

原文
As of 28 January 2015, the official site for Minecraft lists PC and Mac sales at 18,314,346 – that's an average sign-up rate of 13,546 people every day since its alpha launch on 17 May 2011. The game, which allows players to create various constructions using textured cubes in a three-dimensional world, was written by one man, Markus “Notch” Persson (Sweden), using software platform Java. Minecraft had no advertising to speak of yet quickly became a huge word-of-mouth success, allowing Persson to form his own videogame company and employ more staff to further the game’s development. In September 2014, Microsoft bought out Minecraft's Swedish developer, Mojang, for $2.5 billion.


在Minecraft中最长的隧道
Longest Minecraft tunnel
记录保持者: LACHLAN ETHERTON
地点: 澳大利亚 Greenwith
打破时间: 2013年8月3日
　　在Minecraft中最长的隧道长度为10,502个方块（相当于10,502米）。该记录由澳大利亚人拉库伦·埃瑟顿（Lachlan Etherton）于2013年8月3日在澳大利亚的Greenwith*一家EB游戏商店创造。隧道的挖掘工作使用了大约50分钟。给隧道照明大约用了20分钟然后穿过整条隧道完成记录使用了10分钟。

原文
The longest Minecraft tunnel is 10,502 blocks (equating to 10,502 metres), and was achieved by Lachlan Etherton (Australia), at EB Games store, in Greenwith, Australia, on 3 August 2013. The tunnel digging/minding took around 50 minutes to do. The lighting of the tunnel took 20 minutes and the walk through of the tunnel to complete the record attempt took 10 minutes.

原文
The most sandcastles built in 3 minutes on Mincraft Pocket Edition is 14 and was achieved by Jake Edwards, (UK), at Minecon, in London, UK, on 05 July 2015.

为一个的游戏专门举行的最大的聚会
Largest convention for a single videogame
记录保持者: MINECON 2015
成绩: 10,000人
地点: 英国 伦敦
打破时间: 2015年6月5日
　　为一个单独的游戏举行的最大的聚会是于2015年7月4-5日在英国伦敦ExCel大楼举行的Minecon 2015，卖出了10,000张票。

　　于2015年7月4-5日在英国伦敦ExCel大楼举行的2015年度Minecraft聚会Minecon 2015，在2015年5月开始售票后不久，10,000张票就抢购一空。据报道，它第一批票在12分钟内就全部卖完。这是有史以来为了一个单独的游戏专门举行的最大的聚会，超越了了前两届Minecon以及像Summer of Sonic或EVE Online's EVE Fanfest之类的聚会，而后者有着4,000名与会者参加2013年的游戏10周年纪念日（Game's 10th Anniversary）的记录。

原文
The largest convention for a single videogame is Minecon 2015, which sold 10,000 tickets for the convention at the London ExCel, London, UK, on 4 – 5 July 2015.
The 2015 Minecraft convention Minecon 2015, at London ExCeL (UK) on 4–5 July, sold out its full allocation of 10,000 tickets soon after going on sale in March 2015. Its first batch of tickets was reported to have sold out within 12 minutes. It's the largest ever convention dedicated to a single videogame franchise, beating both previous Minecons and the likes of Summer of Sonic and EVE Online's EVE Fanfest, the latter of which recorded 4,000 attendees for the game’s 10th anniversary in 2013.
