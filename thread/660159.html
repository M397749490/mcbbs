WorldsEnd Surrealism
超现实末日主义


作者原文
This is a pack I started creating for a mod pack I am creating called "WorldsEnd Mod Pack" curently still in the works as I am creating all the mods for the mod pack.
What I tryed to bring with this pack is a surrealism feel where things are real and comical, with bright colors and lots of skins for mobs and creatures.
I was a tattoo artist for years and I have always loved the bright colors and hard lines in surreal art, I will be adding more of this feel over time to this pack.
When I first started creating this pack I was going to be doing it in 64x 128x 256x and 512, but as I am the only person doing this I am only doing this as a 256x pack. I will be making a 64bit one but it will be different as it is to hard to have realism in 64x 64x so it will be a toon pack, called, "ToonIt", but I wont be adding that tillI am done with the ModPack.
Install:
1)
Just add .zip to your Minecraft Resource Packs Folder
ALSO:
If you do not use MCPatcher or Optifine you will not see all the content from this pack.
Optifine, in addition to making most peoples' Minecraft run faster, also adds a whole slew of ingame visual settings!
