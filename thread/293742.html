Medieval and Fantasy themed mod, full of weapons and magic. This mod will create structures when you create a new world. Structures as watchtowers, castles, giantcamps and thiefshacks. It comes with some cool mobs: The Knight, Guard and Archer. These are the royal mobs of Minecraft. The giant Giant and the thief are creatures that want your stuff and want you dead. This mod comes with a lot of weapons, food, blocks and more.If you have a cool idea for this mod, let us know in the comments.
