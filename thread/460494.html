  1. /scoreboard players set @a stick 1 {SelectedItemSlot:0,Inventory:[{id:"minecraft:stick}]}
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard players set @a stick 1 {SelectedItemSlot:1,Inventory:[{id:"minecraft:stick}]}
复制代码

检测玩家是否拿着木棍，并将拿着木棍的玩家积分设为1，分别将 {SelectedItemSlot}设为0至8，全部连接高频，比较器输出

  1. /scoreboard teams add GreenTeam
复制代码

创建队伍GreenTeam【名字可改

  1. /scoreboard teams join GreenTeam [玩家]
复制代码

将玩家加入队伍，可以用@r随机

  1. /execute @a[score_stick_min=1] ~ ~ ~
