第一格RCB，后面两个CCB高频就可以了
  1. /scoreboard players tag @a remove fly
复制代码
  1. /scoreboard players tag @a add fly {SelectedItem:{id:minecraft:feather,,Count:1b,tag:{display:{Name:"喷气背包"}}}}
