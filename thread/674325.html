作者: ruhuasiyu    时间: 2017-2-15 19:17
直接给你一个指令好了
在圆心的西北角（1，1）的位置放置一个命令方块，输入

  2. summon falling_block ~ ~2 ~ {Time:1,Block:"minecraft:redstone_block",Passengers:[
  3. {id:"falling_block",Time:1,Block:"minecraft:activator_rail",Passengers:[

  5. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~-2 ~1 minecraft:repeating_command_block 1 replace {auto:1,Command:testfor @e[type=falling_block]}"},
  6. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~-1 ~1 minecraft:chain_command_block 9 replace {auto:1,Command:execute @e[type=falling_block,r=15] ~ 55 ~ setblock ~ ~ ~ grass}"},
  7. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~ ~1 minecraft:chain_command_block 9 replace {auto:1,Command:kill @e[type=falling_block]}"},
  8. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~1 ~1 minecraft:chain_command_block 9 replace {auto:1,Command:setblock ~-1 ~-4 ~-1 grass}"},
  9. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~2 ~1 minecraft:chain_command_block 9 replace {auto:1,Command:setblock ~ ~-5 ~ grass}"},
  10. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~1 ~3 ~1 minecraft:chain_command_block 9 replace {auto:1,Command:fill ~-15 ~ ~-15 ~15 ~-5 ~15 air}"},
  11. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"fill ~-14 ~ ~-14 ~16 ~ ~16 sand 0 keep"},

  13. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"setblock ~ ~-1 ~ minecraft:lava 7 replace"},
  14. {id:"commandblock_minecart",Command:"fill ~ ~ ~ ~ ~-1 ~ minecraft:air 0 replace"}]}]}
复制代码
即可创建一个由草方块构成的，半径为15的圆
