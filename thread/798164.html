作者介绍
Introduce

欢迎来到Skyland!（适用版本1.12）
Welcome to Skylands! (made for 1.12!)
在这张美丽的岛屿上探索、发展自己的文明吧！
Explorer, Craft and Build on the beautifull islands of Skylands!
寻找来自所有岛屿的隐藏地下城
Find dungeons and explorer the world

在这张地图中，你会生存在岛屿之间，探索世界，当然也会与世界各地的boss门对战！
In this map your survival is put to the test by traveling between islands, exploring the world and of course fighting the bosses around the world!

***最后一张图片（就是那张地图），可能会发生改变***
***The last pictures of the map (And the island group) are likely to change***

你从符文岛开始，从这里你可以前往其他的岛屿，在出生的岛屿上，你将能够找到一本关于方向（可以发现北方、南方等字样）信息的书。
You start at the Runic Isle, from here you can travel to the other islands, on the spawn island you'll be able to find a book that holds information about the direction (what can be found north, south etc.).
大部分新物品都是在符文岛周围的岛屿找到的，但是在其他岛屿可能会有旧的物品。
Most of the new items are found around the starting islands, other islands may still have the old ones.
自定义怪物也有一个等级系统，等级越高，怪物的攻击能力就越强。
Custom mobs also exist with a ''level'' system, the higher the level, the stronger the creature is.

截止目前，Skylands大部分地区正在发生较小或较大的变化，因此这段折叠的内容还不算最完整。
As of now most of Skylands is undergoing small or big changes so this information section is not complete.
在未来自定义怪物也会更新。
In the future custom mobs will be updated aswell.

【译者注】我也会对这张地图进行永久更新！

Some houses and other structures

The size of this map currently is at least 7000 x 7000 blocks
