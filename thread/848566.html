[ Harkenburg - 哈肯现代材质 ]

作者原文：
  With the classic clapboard siding, realistic windows, and detailed roads, if you're looking for a modern city themed texture pack then this is indeed for you. While the main feature of this pack is to add realistic road textures, it also has so much more to offer! Check out the detailed brick, or the vibrant grass, or even the rustic signs! Now your probably wondering, what is Harkenburg? Basically it's a city project that's a year in the making by a super great team of builders. I didn't just want to do a basic city project using the default textures, so I made this little thing. Also, this is still a developing pack and I'm open to ideas, so if you have any comments or suggestions, please don't hesitate to message me through PMC or posting your idea in the comments below. Its certainly highly appreciated! Hope all you guys and gals enjoy this pack! :D
译文：
  经典的墙壁、真实的窗户和拟真的道路，如果你在寻找一个用于建造现代城市的材质包，那么哈肯现代材质肯定是能给你心中所想的材质包！它最主要的特性就是添加了真实道路材质以及更多现代化贴图的加入！看看这些精心刻画的木头、鲜草、甚至是乡村的标志！现在你可能想要知道，什么是哈肯？哈肯基本上就是由某个超级伟大的团队（我 吹 我 自 己）在这一年内所进行设计的一个城市计划名称。我不想仅仅用默认材质做一个城市，所以我制作了这样一个材质包。同时，这个材质包也是公开给各位的，我也会吸取各位的意见，所以如果你有任何想法或者建议，请不要感到害羞！通过PMC将你的想法在材质留言栏告诉我，我会十分感谢各位！希望你们能享受这个材质带来的乐趣！:D
—987987(LittleHei)译
