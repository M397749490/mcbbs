作者原文：
      Hi guys.

First of all... Best of luck to all the entries, lots of really nice projects. Very ** to take part in this competition.

Here is my entry for the skyscraper competition. I call it Liberty Tower for now, I may change it to a city after the competition which will change the name also. The idea for this project is not based on any existing buildings and the goal for the project is to create something that could be built in real. I went for a streamlined modern block design with lots of color so it looks very vivid.

译文：

[地图安装 | The Installation]
Save存档(正版用户):关闭Minecraft，在Windows开始菜单的搜索框中输入%appdata%/.minecraft，然后按下Enter（回车）按键。把从百度网盘下载回来的地图存档解压缩放到.minecraft\saves文件夹里面。Schematics存档：这类型需要MCEdit。下载完成后解压到桌面，关闭Minecraft，双击mcedit.exe，点击下方import按钮，打开你下载的文件到你需要放的地方，最后点import，按Ctrl+s保存，Ctrl+w关掉存档，点Quit退出。
