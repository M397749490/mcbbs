作者原话
You must be wondering, “How much of this did you create yourself rather than adopt from DTG or whatever?” Well, since I was on the team that textured DTG’s original version (very shortly, however, and the version I helped with was never posted), I textured maybe 20 of the original block textures and all of the currently completed items.  Other than those textures, everything was either revamped or redone by me. No textures are the same as they were in the original version, especially not any mapping. Again, if you don’t believe me (which I’m sure someone won't considering this is, after all, PMC) then check out [url=http://www.planetminecraft.com/blog/candles-and-clockwork-revival---a-thanks-to-dat-tree-gaming/]THIS POST for proof and more info on DTG giving me ownership of their pack.
