reachDistance就是增加操作距离
所以可以做各种RPG/OOC武器
比如生成一个叫8888米大刀:
  1. /give @p minecraft:stick 1 0 {display:{Name:"8888米大刀"},Unbreakable:1b,AttributeModifiers:[{AttributeName:"generic.reachDistance",Name:"noName",Amount:100d,Operation:0,UUIDMost:5227005646895486L,UUIDLeast:5227005646895486L,Slot:mainhand},{AttributeName:"generic.attackDamage",Name:"noName",Amount:10d,Operation:0,UUIDMost:8987941689016318L,UUIDLeast:8987941689016318L}]}
