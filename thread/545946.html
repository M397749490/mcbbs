这个mod拥有许多的不同材料的熔炉！每个熔炉都有自己所要的材料！以及不同的速度！目前含有铁,黄金,钻石,和黑曜石炉,每个熔炉都有自己不同的速度。




合成表
Iron Furnace
Burn time of 150 ticks. The regular furnace has a burn time of 200 ticks.

Gold Furnace
Burn time of 100 ticks.

Diamond Furnace
Burn time of 50 ticks.

Obsidian Furnace
The same burn time as the diamond furnace, except with a blast resistance of 6,000.

Crystal Furnace:
A little fast than the Obsidian, or Diamond Furnaces.
