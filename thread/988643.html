介绍
Adorn是MC高版本的装饰模组
它增加了很多不同类型的装饰方块
包括桌子、椅子、沙发、厨房柜台、抽屉、烟囱、架子等等
Adorn is a decoration mod for Minecraft 1.14 and 1.15. It adds different types of decoration blocks, including tables, chairs, sofas, kitchen counters, drawers, chimneys and shelves.
