Shipyard Inc
此地图为realm地图（分类：示例）

简介
The primary creators of our map consist of: Celisio, zun, and b3nj1. Our map is inspiration of what a real world shipyard might look like in Minecraft and consists of many different trucks, containers and a variety of ships that can inspire players to build ships or industrial builds based off of real world concepts.

地图的创建者:Celisio, zun, 和 b3nj1.地图灵感来自真实世界造船厂在Minecraft中的样子,包括许多不同的卡车，集装箱和各种船只，可以激励玩家根据现实世界的概念建造船只或工业建筑。

为什么这个地图与众不同
We feel our map is unique because of our attention to detail in real world concepts with the aim to inspire others to do the same.

我们注重现实世界的概念，旨在激励其他人也这样做。
