原贴内容


Do you want to be a God of Gravity? Do youwant to control villagers' lives? Do you want to change gravity? Then welcometo map "Lord of Gravitation"!
In this map you'll need to change gravityand lead the poor villager to netherbrick - he will die, but you can proceed tonext room.
There are 10 levels, from 1 villager to 4!
To change the gravity just select thecorrect item in your inventory. There are 6 directions of gravity: Up, Down,East, West, South, and North.
When the villager steps on a block of magma- he dies, but don't forget that you aren't invulnerable too.

翻译



你想成为掌控重力的神吗？你想掌控村民的生命吗？你想改变重力吗？快来玩这张地图吧！
你需要改变重力，让可怜的村民跑到下界石砖上（不全是石砖，应该是作者没写好），他会死亡，你会进入下一个房间。
一共有10关，从控制1个村民到控制4个。
改变重力只需要变换手中拿着的道具，他们分别对应6个方向，上下东西南北。
村民碰到熔岩方块时，他会死亡，当然，你也会受伤。
