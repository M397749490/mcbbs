作者: xiang_xge    时间: 2020-4-13 07:58
给予物品（生存不可放置）：
  1. /give @p minecraft:spawner{BlockEntityTag:{SpawnData:{id:"minecraft:wither_skeleton"},SpawnPotentials:[{Weight:1,Entity:{id:"minecraft:wither_skeleton"}}]}} 1
复制代码

放置方块（默认放置于站立位置，~可调为坐标）：
  1. /setblock ~ ~ ~ minecraft:spawner{SpawnData:{id:"minecraft:wither_skeleton"},SpawnPotentials:[{Weight:1,Entity:{id:"minecraft:wither_skeleton"}}]} replace
