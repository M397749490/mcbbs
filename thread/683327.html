简介 Description
*Warning: this map is rage inducing
Using your tools and surroundings, you need to figure out a way to break into five different building without setting of any cameras.
Tiptoe away across the room as you try to steal many different items like a prisoners diary or a kings crown.
Currently five differently levels you can break into buildings ranging from a simple house to an art gallery.
In this frustrating map, you will have to combine your parkour and puzzle solving skills to make it home.
Map play time: around 20 minutes.

*注意：这张地图会令你发狂
使用你周围的工具，你需要摸出一条到五个建筑物里并且不被摄像头发现的路。
垫着脚到不同的房间来偷一些不同的物品，比如犯罪分子的日记或王冠。
目前有5个关卡，从一个简单的屋子到一个艺术展览。
在这个令人发狂的地图里，你需要结合你的跑酷和解密技巧来回家。
游玩时间：大概20分钟
