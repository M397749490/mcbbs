◆ 指令&权限 ◆
指令
权限
简介
/cp
无
显示指令列表
/cp [panel]
commandpanel.panel.[panel perm]
打开指定菜单
/cp [panel] [player]
commandpanel.other
给指定玩家打开指定菜单
/cp [panel] item [optional player name]
commandpanel.item.[panel perm]
Permission to open panels for other players and to give other players panel hot-bar items.
/cpl
commandpanel.list
显示GUI列表
/cpc
commandpanel.close
关闭GUI，写入执行指令中关闭GUI
/cpr
commandpanel.reload
重载插件
/cpv
commandpanel.version
检查插件版本
~
commandpanel.*
全部指令权限
/cpd
commandpanel.database
 View the Command Panels Database, where you can share premade panels.
/cpg
commandpanel.generate
Enable/Disable Panel generate mode.
