It’s time for a brand new community spotlight! During our first foray into the world of fan art, we were delighted to learn about the abundance of talent just waiting for us within the Minecraft community. We’ve started off this collection with ditto’s, beautiful render of Alex balancing on a lily pad, but we have much more to share. Let’s take a look at the latest creations created by our community members.
