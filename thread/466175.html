[white_wool]作者的原文|Original Text[white_wool]


This is a new map I made called One More Room. It's a puzzle map, and it's VERY challenging. From hidden buttons to mazes to redstone, your brain will be fried by the end. This is the first in a series of maps, so I hope you enjoy!

Story:
You can't remember where you are when you wake up. Your memories are gone, and you're left in a lab. You look around to find JerMM. An A.I. that wants you to go clinically insane. He thrusts you into an endless stream of puzzles to penetrate your sanity. You have to escape. And more importantly, figure out what happened before you woke up.

This is a singleplayer map. SINGLEplayer. No mods or resource packs are needed. And if the map gets good feedback, I'll start working on the sequel! Screenshots have been attached.


Rules：

Only break Chiselled Quartz Blocks.

If you're playing on a server, make sure Command Blocks are enabled.

Every room IS beatable, so don't tell me they're not.

I've tried my best to make sure there are no bugs, but I'm not 100% sure, so if you find any, please let me know!
