作者: jiuerd    时间: 2014-7-31 08:06
essentials.build.break.<id>[:datavalue]Allows breaking of a block.破坏
essentials.build.craft.<id>[:datavalue]Allows crafting of an item.合成
essentials.build.drop.<id>[:datavalue]Allows dropping of an item.丢弃
essentials.build.interact.<id>[:datavalue]Allows interacting with a blocks or item.互动
essentials.build.pickup.<id>[:datavalue]Allows collecting of an item.拾取
essentials.build.place.<id>[:datavalue]Allows placing a block.放置
