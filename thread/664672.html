这个PlaceholderAPI插件是sponge海绵版
目前还处于婴儿时期
前期变量不会太多
但是呢，未来，你要知道它未来成为怎样

请参考PlaceholderAPI的Spigot版
MCBBS已有搬运贴


变量

Player
%player_name%
%player_displayname%
%player_prefix%
%player_suffix%

Server
%server_online%
%server_max_players%
%server_motd%
%server_ram_used%
%server_ram_free%
%server_ram_total%
%server_ram_max%
%server_cores%

Rank
%rank_name%
%rank_prefix%
%rank_suffix%
%rank_option_{option}%
%rank_perm_{permission}%

Sound
%sound_{sound-id}_{volume}_{pitch}% - Plays the sound at the player's location
