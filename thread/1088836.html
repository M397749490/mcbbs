Amethyst Ore——紫水晶矿石
Do you like Amethysts? Then this addon is for you! Amethysts is a unique ore, in this addon you can craft Amethyst Sword. Amethyst Enchanted Apple and Amethyst Block!The Amethyst Ore appears on layer 1-16 coordinates Position Y (0,16), but it is most abundant on layer 12. but check your sorroundings because lava appears between layers 4-10
你喜欢紫水晶吗？你可以看看这个插件!紫水晶矿是一种独特的矿石，在这个插件里你可以制作紫水晶剑。附魔紫水晶苹果和紫水晶块!紫水晶矿生成在1~16层Y（0,16），在12层最丰富，但是，注意你周围，因为岩浆出现在4~10层。
The amethyst ore drops 2 Amethysts when you mined it.
每个紫水晶矿石会掉落2个紫水晶。
Before you start playing, put your world to Experimental Gameplay
注意：在开始游戏之前，请打开实验玩法!
