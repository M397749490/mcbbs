指令：
  1. 武器获取指令：/give @p minecraft:diamond_sword 1 0 {display:{Name:"冰霜剑",Lore:["Q键技能：冰封附近生物"]}}
  2. 计分版创建1：/scoreboard objectives add ice dummy
  3. 计分版创建2：/scoreboard objectives add bhtime dummy
  4. 命令方块1：/scoreboard players tag [url=home.php?mod=space&uid=1702895]@e[type=item][/url] add bs {Item:{id:"minecraft:diamond_sword",tag:{display:{Name:"冰霜剑",Lore:["Q键技能：冰封附近生物"]}}},OnGround:1b}
  5. 命令方块2：/execute @e[type=item,tag=bs] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @p[r=5] add bs2
  6. 命令方块3：/execute @e[type=item,tag=bs] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @p[r=5] add bs3
  7. 命令方块4：/execute @e[type=item,tag=bs] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[r=7,tag=!bs2] add bh
  8. 命令方块5：/kill @e[type=item,tag=bs]
  9. 命令方块6：/execute @e[tag=bh] ~ ~ ~ summon minecraft:item ~ ~2 ~ {CustomName:"bd",CustomNameVisible:0,Tags:["bd"],Item:{id:"minecraft:ice",Count:1b,Damage:0s},PickupDelay:32767s,Age:-32767s,NoGravity:1b}
  10. 命令方块7：/execute @e[tag=bh] ~ ~ ~ tp @e[type=item,tag=bd,c=1] @e[tag=bh,c=1]
  11. 命令方块8：/execute @a[tag=bs3] ~ ~ ~ effect @e[r=7,tag=!bs3] 2 4 29 true
  12. 命令方块9：/scoreboard players tag @a[tag=bs2] remove bs3
  13. 命令方块10：/scoreboard players add @a[tag=bs2] bhtime 1
  14. 命令方块11：/give @a[tag=bs2,score_bhtime_min=260] minecraft:diamond_sword 1 0 {display:{Name:"冰霜剑",Lore:["Q键技能：冰封附近生物"]}}
  15. 命令方块12：/scoreboard players tag @a[score_bhtime_min=260] remove bs2
  16. 命令方块13：/scoreboard players reset @a[score_bhtime_min=260] bhtime
  17. 命令方块14：/scoreboard players add @e[type=item,tag=bd] ice 1
  18. 命令方块15：/kill @e[type=item,tag=bd,score_ice_min=88]
  19. 命令方块16：/scoreboard players tag @e remove bh
