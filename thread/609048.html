地狱生物的技能表单>
  1. Powers:
  2. 技能:
  3. Poisonous: Gives player the poison effect.
  4. 药剂使: 给予玩家负面状态.
  5. Armoured: Give mobs full diamond armour or a resistance effect.
  6. 装甲: 生物将拥有全套钻石护甲.
  7. Blinding: Gives player the blindness effect.
  8. 失明: 给予玩家失明状态.
  9. Withering: Gives player the withering effect.
  10. 凋零: 给予玩家凋零状态.
  11. Tosser: Tosses the player around.
  12. 投掷: 向周围弹开玩家.
  13. Thief: Steals the item the player is holding.
  14. 盗窃: 偷走玩家手中的物品.
  15. Quicksand: Gives player the slowness effect.
  16. 迟缓: 将玩家减速.
  17. Bullwark: Takes less damage.
  18. 坚硬: 受到更少的伤害.
  19. Rust: Damages the tool the player is holding.
  20. 损毁: 减少玩家手中工具的耐久.
  21. Sapper: Gives player the hunger effect.
  22. 饥荒: 给予玩家饥饿状态.
  23. 1up: Has 2 lives.
  24. 涅槃: 死亡后拥有一次重生机会.
  25. Cloaked: Is invisible.
  26. 隐身: 不可见.
  27. Ender: Can teleport.
  28. 末影: 可以瞬间移动.
  29. Ghastly: Shoots fireballs.
  30. 烈焰: 投掷火球.
  31. Lifesteal: Gets health from damaging player.
  32. 吸血: 偷取玩家生命值.
  33. Sprint: Can go fast.
  34. 快银: 拥有更快的速度.
  35. Storm: Strikes the player with lightning.
  36. 天罚: 召唤闪电攻击玩家.
  37. Webber: Makes a web at the players feet.
  38. 束缚: 使用蜘蛛网困住玩家.
  39. Vengeance: Gives damage from being hit.
  40. 荆棘: 受到伤害时反射伤害.
  41. Weakness: Gives player the weakness effect.
  42. 肾宝: 使玩家虚弱.
  43. Berserk: Damages the player more, but takes damage from attacking.
  44. 两败俱伤: 造成更多伤害, 同时也会伤到自己.
  45. Explode: Explodes on death.
  46. 爆裂: 死亡时爆炸.
  47. Potions: Throws harmful potions at the player.
  48. 剧毒: 向玩家投掷剧毒药水.
  49. Mama: Has a slim chance to spawn three babies of its type.
  50. 母巢: 有几率生成一定数量的子代个体.
  51. Molten: Is immune to fire/lava, starts a fire on death and lights the player on fie.
  52. 火焰亲和: 对火焰免疫, 死亡后燃起火焰.
  53. Archer: Randomly shoots a volley of arrows at player.
  54. 弓手: 弓箭射击玩家.
  55. Necromancer: Shoots wither skulls.
  56. 凋零弓手: 发射凋零骷髅头.
  57. Firework: Fireworks explode from mob when hit.
  58. 愉悦: 点燃烟花.
  59. Gravity: Levitates the player for a while.
  60. 万有引力: 令玩家失重一段时间.
  61. Flying: Mob can fly.
  62. 飞行: 拥有飞行的能力.
  63. Mounted: Riding on another mob.
  64. 骑乘: 可以骑乘在另一个生物上.
  65. Morph: Can infrequently morph into another infernal mob.
  66. 相位映射: 转换为另一种地狱生物.
  67. Ghost: On death a ghost is spawned.
  68. 鬼: 死亡时生成幽灵.
  69. Confusing: Gives player the confusion effect.
  70. 混乱: 给予玩家混乱状态
