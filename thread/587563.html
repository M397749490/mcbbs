10 drop challenge Wool
10次坠落挑战



10次坠落挑战的目标是在坠落的过程中躲避不同的障碍物，最后落在水中即为获胜。

你仅有十次下坠机会。这个挑战可能比较难，但你可以选择难度——正常|简单|十分简单

正常模式就是正常的下坠，只要碰到一个东西就会失败，然而简单模式有1次机会，十分简单有3次机会



英文原帖介绍：
The objective is to dodge different obstacles while falling and land in the water at the bottom. The catch? You only get 10 drops to beat it. 10 Drops Challenge Wool map is made to be hard. but you can choose 3 different difficulties: Normal, Easy, Very Easy. The normal difficulty is like a normal dropper when you hit a obstacle you die, but the easy difficulty has 1 invulnerability and the very easy difficulty has 3. What is invulnerability? It will keep you alive even if you fall very high and hit an obstacle. After you hit an obstacle it will remove one of your lives, and if you’re out of lives you will now be vulnerable to die.
