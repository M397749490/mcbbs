例子1.12
①
/scoreboard objectives add qx stat.horseOneCm
之后选择器[score_qx_min=1]就可以指定骑马的玩家
如/testfor @a[score_qx_min=1]


②
不受制约>保持开启>：
/scoreboard players tag @e[type=item] add xq1 {Item:{id:"minecraft:snowball",tag:{display:{Name:"abc"}}}}
之后/kill @e[tag=xq1]就只能kill掉名字是abc的雪球了。

（均为不受制约>保持开启）左往右按顺序：
1.scoreboard players tag @a remove abc
2.scoreboard players tag @a add abc {SelectedItem:{id:"minecraft:snowball",Count:1b,Damage:0s,tag:{display:{Name:"abc"}}}}
3.execute @e[tag=abc] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[r=2,type=snowball] add xq
4.execute @e[tag=xq] ~ ~ ~ summon Fireball ~ ~ ~ {direction:[0.0,0.0,0.0],ExplosionPower:2}
玩家只有拿着名为abc的雪球并扔出的时候，才会创造火焰弹，其他情况不行。
