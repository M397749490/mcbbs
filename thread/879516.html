作者: a1294790523    时间: 2019-7-1 14:17
https://github.com/asiekierka/FoamFix/issues/166
请编辑在config文件夹中的foamfix.cfg
将patchChunkSerialization和optimizedBlockPos设置为false即可：
  1.     # Optimizes BlockPos mutable/immutable getters to run on the same variables, letting them be inlined and thus theoretically increasing performance. [default: true]
  2.     B:optimizedBlockPos=false
  3.     # Fix a bug in chunk serialization leading to crashes. Originally found and fixed by Aaron1011 of Sponge. [default: true]
  4.     B:patchChunkSerialization=false
