简介  （作者：Lykrast）（UpcraftLP 参与维护）（此排版来自PCD小组）
Glassential为大家提供了几种船新的玻璃类型！
而且不仅仅可以拿来做装饰哦，玻璃们本身可都自带技能呢~

就比如：
会发光的玻璃、能挡光的玻璃、提供红石信号的玻璃、没有碰撞箱的玻璃、对玩家没有但对其他生物有碰撞箱的玻璃
等等等等。

目前1.1.5版本的Glassential已经有了相当不错的汉化和内置物品介绍了。

作者介绍：

Several mods, such as Extra Utilities, add a bunch of cool glasses to your game. Like glass that you can see through but blocks light or glass that emits light.
Have you ever wanted to get that cool stuff, but without all the overhead of putting a major content mod in your modpack? Well look no further than Glassential.
Wiki上的介绍：

Glassential is a mod by Lykrast, maintained by UpcraftLP on Fabric. It adds several types of glasses each with special properties, as well as a few items related to glass.
Despite the current Forge and Fabric versions having their latest release on different minecraft versions, they both add exactly the same features. The only difference is that the Fabric version has a creative tab containing all blocks from the mod on top of them being in the Minecraft creative tabs.

  玻璃介绍

作者的介绍:
  • Luminous Glass: emits light, just like Glowstone.
  • Redstone Glass: emits a strong redstone signal, just like a Block of Redstone.
  • Dark Glass: doesn't let actual light go through, ideal for mob farms.
  • Ghostly Glass: non-solid to all entities of any kind.
  • Ethereal Glass: non-solid to players.
  • Reverse Ethereal Glass: only solid to players (and fluids).
  • Dark Ethereal Glass: the properties of both Dark Glass and Ethereal Glass.
  • Dark Reverse Ethereal Glass: the properties of both Dark Glass and Reverse Ethereal Glass.
