!           前言          !

这款插件是一款高质量，简易操作并且轻量的插件。这允许你的玩家们发起交易PY。该插件有很多的交易方式，例如金币交易，经验交易等。并且还有可自定义的GUI！
  1. Trade+ is a high quality, easy-to-use and -setup, lightweight trading plugin. It allows your players to send trade requests and trade items and other configurable extras, such as economy money via Vault and XP, through an aesthetically pleasing, highly configurable GUI.

!           功能          !

个性化
支持 1.7.10-1.13.x
隔世界交易
交易物品
交易金钱 (可选) - 需要Vault与一个经济插件(Essential)
交易经验 (可选)
交易点券 (可选) - 需要PlayerPoints
交易 GriefPrevention 物品 (可选)
交易 Enjin 点数 (可选)
下蹲 + 右键发起交易 (可选) - 配置中设置为 "disabled" 来关闭
点击聊天板内的按钮来确定/取消交易
管理员可以强制性发起交易或监视交易
物品黑名单
支付额外的手续费 (金钱, 经验)
声音特效
防止上当/scamming with configurable anti-scam features
可配置最大交易距离
将来会有更多..


!           指令          !

/trade <玩家> 发起一个交易请求
/tradeplus reload 重载插件
/tradeplus force <玩家1> <玩家2> 强制 2 位玩家进行交易
/tradeplus spectate <玩家1> <玩家2> 监视一场交易


!           权限          !

tradeplus.admin - 使用/tradeplus指令的权限 建议给管理员
tradeplus.admin.silent - 关闭交易提醒 建议给管理员
tradeplus.trade - 使用/trade 交易的权限 默认开启

I've officially made Trade+ open-source. What this basically means is that you or another member of the community will be able to add stuff to and fix bugs in Trade+ to then be submitted for my approval to be added to the plugin. It also means that Trade+ is technically free, though it will remain a premium plugin. Buying Trade+ here on Spigot will now basically be considered a donation, and it is still very much appreciated. Hopefully, this change will be positive for you guys as well as for the plugin.
