标题: Minecraft 基岩版 1.14.60 发布
本帖最后由 zyjking 于 2020-4-16 00:27 编辑

Minecraft基岩版是指包括Minecraft手机版（Android、IOS）、Win10版（VR）、主机版（XboxOne、NS、PS4）在内，使用“基岩引擎”（C++语言）开发的Minecraft统一版本。

正式版是Minecraft基岩版经过一段时间的测试版测试之后得到的稳定版本，也是众多材质、Addon和官方领域服会逐渐跟进的版本。与此同时Google Play、Win10 Store等官方软件商店也会推送此次更新。

https://feedback.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/360041875292

Our developers have been hard at work to bring you another hotfix. Some of these issues were reported by players, so keep those reports coming at bugs.mojang.com!
我们的开发者一直在努力工作以推送另一更新。请继续在bugs.mojang.com上反馈问题，因为有些bug就是由玩家汇报的！

Fixes:
bug修复：
  • Crashes / Performance
  • 崩溃/性能
    • Fixed an issue that could cause Minecraft not to launch past the loading screen after updating (MCPE-58897)
    • 修复了更新游戏并启动后卡在加载界面的问题
    • Fixed a crash that could occur when exiting a world
    • 修复了退出世界时可能发生的崩溃问题
    • Fixed a crash that could occur when a split-screen player left the game
    • 修复了分屏玩家退出游玩时可能发生的崩溃问题
    • Fixed a crash that could occur during gameplay on Nintendo Switch
    • 修复了在任天堂Switch上游玩时可能发生的崩溃问题

  • General
  • 常规
    • Fixed an issue that showed a false prompt that a device was out of storage space (MCPE-32501)
    • 修复了错误提示设备存储空间已满的问题
    • Fixed an issue with the Marketplace not loading when not signed into a Microsoft account
    • 修复了没有登录微软账户时市场内容无法加载的问题
    • Fixed issues with content not transferring to a Microsoft account if the content was purchased without being signed in
    • 修复了没有登录账号购买物品时无法将购买转移到微软账户的问题

  • Gameplay
  • 游玩
    • Fixed some special characters not appearing on Signs or Book & Quill
    • 修复了书与笔无法显示某些特殊字符的问题

  • Add-Ons
    • Animation Controllers in Behavior Packs can now run commands on Realms (MCPE-59881)
    • 行为包中的动画控制器可以在Realms上运行了
