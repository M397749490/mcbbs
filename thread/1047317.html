After a long absence and while waiting for publishing our last project, we are pleased to present you today a reproduction of the Casa Brutale. Symbol of Brutalism, this house was created by OPA (Open Platform for Architecture) in 2015 and will be built in Lebanon.

经过超长时间的咸鱼与等待我们发布最新的作品，今天，我们很高兴向你介绍Casa Brutale的复制品。这个建筑是野兽派艺术的代表，由OPA（建筑自由平台）设计的概念性房屋并且将会在黎巴嫩建造。
