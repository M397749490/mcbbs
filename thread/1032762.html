预发布版是Minecraft Java版的测试机制，主要用于下一个正式版的特性预览。

然而，然而，预发布版主要用于服主和Mod制作者的预先体验，如果发现重大漏洞，该预发布版会被新的预发布版代替。因此建议普通玩家持观望态度

Minecraft 1.16 仍未发布，1.16-pre-1 为其第 14 个预览版。

Minecraft 1.16 Pre-Release 1
Minecraft 1.16 Pre-Release 1
A Minecraft Java Pre-Release
Minecraft 1.16预发布版

We are pleased to receive your feedback on the lower bounds update. Based on the feedback from the players, we have added tons more features in this preview and fixed the headaches of the players' current masters.We decided to make minecraft more difficult to survive, more exciting battles, more interesting new items and features, and more technical conveniences for original players and supporters of the snapshot version.

收到大家对下界更新的反馈，我们很高兴，根据玩家的反馈，我们在这次预览版里面又添加了成吨的特性，并且修复了玩家当前师傅呢头疼的问题。我们决定，给minecraft带来更困难的生存，更精彩的战斗，好看有趣的新物品新特性，并且给予原版玩家，快照版本支持者更多的技术性便利。


Thanks for all your support for bugjang, here we release a pre-release version of the lower 1.16 update. The official version will come soon, so stay tuned.

感谢大家对bugjang的大力支持，在此我们发布1.16下界更新的预发布版，正式版很快到来，敬请期待。



New Features in 1.16 Pre-Release 1

1.16第一预览版的新特性
Added soul campfire
新增灵魂营火
Right - click to grill the evil spirit on it
可以右键将恶魂放在上面烧烤
Added soul pumpkin lantern
新增灵魂南瓜灯
Soul pumpkin lantern will scare off players within 24 squares
灵魂南瓜灯将会吓退周围24格内的玩家

Farming
养殖
Now you can breed a new player by killing two players.
现在通过杀死两个玩家可以繁殖一个新的玩家。

Thirst
口渴值
Now add the thirst value to 20 points, the property is minecraft:generic.max_thirst and minecraft:generic.thirst
现在加入口渴值，默认为20点，属性名为 minecraft:generic.max_thirst 和 minecraft:generic.thirst
Deals the same damage when thirst is 0 as when hunger is 0.
当口渴值为0时，给予与饥饿值为0时相同效果的伤害。
Thirst can be supplemented by dyeing crucible, magma swimming, coolie afraid of explosion, etc., temporarily not supported from the bucket, food, potion, etc
口渴值可以由染色坩埚，岩浆游泳，苦力怕爆炸等方式补充，暂不支持来自水桶，食物，药水等方式补充

Hell Invation
下界入侵
As long as your lower portal is large enough, there is a chance that every other game day you will come out of hell with a ghost on the halter.
只要你的下界传送门足够大，每隔一定游戏日都有几率生成凋零使用缰绳带着恶魂从地狱门中出来。

Battle Update
战斗机制更新
Monsters can now prowl and pass through 1.5 high channels.
怪物现在可以潜行，并通过1.5格高的通道。
Dual weapon adds new attack action, and a single tool/weapon in the secondary bar can be used for normal attacks
双持武器添加新的攻击动作，并且在副手栏的单个工具/武器可以正常用于攻击
Monster attacks now have a chance to penetrate the player's armor
现在怪物的攻击有几率透过玩家的护甲
Monster attacks now have a more noticeable particle effect
怪物的攻击现在有更明显的粒子效果

Ender City
末影城
The shulker can be reborn in modi and cannot ride harvesters or ships
潜影贝可以在末地城重生,并且无法骑乘矿车和船

Technical Changes in 1.16 Pre-Release 1
中的技术性改动
Server administrators can fine-tune the TPS of the server through /gamerule maxTPS [value], and it is recommended that servers with tianhe-1 use this feature.
服务器管理员可以通过/gamerule maxTPS [value] 精确调整服务器的TPS，建议拥有天河一号的服务器使用此功能。
You can now use /gamerule DropUp [value] to control how high an item is thrown when it drops
现在可以通过 /gamerule DropUp [value] 控制物品在掉落时向上抛的高度
Now the /data block command can modify the NBT of all kinds of blocks
现在/data block命令，可以修改所有种类方块的nbt了
The server will not crash until the physical machine accelerates to failure.
服务器直到物理机加速坏掉前都不会崩溃。


Fixed bugs in 1.16 Pre-Release 1
1.16第一预览版中修复的漏洞
Mc-114514 repairs a rail machine that consists of a detector and a slime block
MC-114514 修复由侦测器和粘液块组成的铁轨机
Mc-114515 fixed carpet failure on coral
MC-114515 修复了地毯在珊瑚上无法正常掉落的问题
Mc-114516 will now be able to drop squares correctly to the destination instead of remaining in the main world
MC-114516 现在可坠落方块可正确传送入末地而不是留在主世界
Mc-114517 solves the problem of legitimate players being able to log on to pirated servers
MC-114517 解决了正版玩家可以登陆盗版服务器的问题
Mc-114518 fixed a problem where wheat could not ride in harvesters like other plants
MC-114518 修复了小麦不能和其他植物一样乘坐矿车的问题
Mc-114519 fixes pathfinding problems in creatures, such as half brick, that allow monsters to pass without material obstacles.
MC-114519 修复了生物的寻路问题，如半砖等若无实质性阻碍怪物能正常通过。
Mc-114520 fixed a bug where monsters could not be created on half a brick and a layer of water.
MC-114520 修复了怪物不可以在半砖和一层水面上生成

对于正版用户
官方启动器是跟进最及时、运行最稳定的启动器，每次启动均会自动检查并下载启动器最新版本。Java版的启动器下载地址在上文已经提供。
对于非正版用户
非正版用户完全可以体验预览版本，请于推荐启动器列表寻找合适的启动器。目前绝大多数主流启动器都带有游戏下载功能。如有仍疑惑请到原版问答板块提问。
