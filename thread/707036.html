◆ Furnace Notify ◆
Receive actionbar with the progress of the furnace when you look at furnace.

  • furnace.reload = 允许你重载配置文件.
  • furnace.notify = 允许你接收熔炉通知.
  • furnace.disableworld = 允许你使用 /furnace disableworld 指令.
  • furnace.info = 允许你使用 /furnace info 指令.
  • furnace.toggle = 允许你开启或关闭熔炉通知, 感谢 @Zedwick 提出的建议!
  • furnace.toggle.exempt = 一般的用户无法使用 /furnace toggle on ，你可以给他们 furnace.toggle.bypass 权限.
  • furnace.toggle.bypass = 允许你绕过 furnace.toggle.exempt 权限.
  • furnace.toggle.others = 允许你对其他玩家使用 /furnace toggle 指令.
  • furnace.plotbypass = allow you to receive the actionbar even if you are not the owner or the member of the plot.
  • furnace.factionbypass = allow you to receive the actionbar even if you are not the leader or the member of the faction.
  • furnace.skyblockbypass = allow you to receive the actionbar even if you are not the leader or the member of the island.
  • furnace.cooldownbypass = 允许你绕过冷却时间.
注意！权限可在配置文件中更改，以上是缺省权限。

/furnace reload = 重载配置文件.
/furnace toggle = 切换通知显示，关闭后将不会收到通知.
/furnace info = 你可以收到关于玩家的一些信息，例如：

/furnace disableplayer = 你可以选择要关闭的玩家，关闭的玩家将无法使用熔炉通知，例如：


注意！！你可以自己在配置文件中改变GUI面板中玻璃板的颜色！

可用的颜色：
White, Orange, Magenta, Yellow, Lime, Pink, Gray, Cyan, Purple, Blue, Brown, Green, Red and Black.
例如：

CooldownEnabled = 启用或禁用 /furnace toggle 指令的冷却时间
CooldownTime = 冷却时间，单位为秒

注意！每个消息都可以在配置文件中更改

  • {Prefix} = 替换config中的前缀.
  • {PlayerName} = 替换玩家名.
  • {PlayerDisplayName} = 替换玩家昵称.
  • {PlayerIP} = 替换玩家IP.
  • {PlayerX} = 替换 X 方位.
  • {PlayerY} = 替换 Y 方位.
  • {PlayerZ} = 替换 Z 方位.
  • {PlayerUUID} = 替换玩家uuid.
  • {ServerName} = 替换服务器名.
  • {ServerIP} = 替换服务器IP.
  • {PlayerXP} = 替换玩家经验等级.
  • {PlayerGamemode} = 替换玩家游戏模式.
  • {PlayerHealth} = 替换玩家血量.
  • {World} = 替换世界名.
  • {CookTime} = 替换剩余时间.
  • {SmeltItemAmount} = 替换熔炉中物品的数量.
  • {SmeltItem} = 替换熔炉中物品的名字.
  • {Fuel} = 替换燃料名字.
  • {FuelAmount} = 替换剩余燃料的数量.
