Dinnerbone: Working on a new feature that will, theoretically, let custom maps change mob drops. Also, it'll solve some future proofing issues we have!
Dinnerbone: 正在添加一个新特性，它理论上能允许自定义地图改变生物的掉落物。而且，它可以解决一些未来的问题！
Dinnerbone: Basically I'm trying to make all loot (from mobs, blocks, chests, etc) data driven which lets us do really cool things, mapmakers too.
Dinnerbone: 简单来说，我正试图使所有掉落物（生物，实体，箱子以及更多都由数据控制，这使得我们能做很酷的事，也是地图制作者的福利哦。
Dinnerbone: (Cool things includes "potion of luck", for example. In theory. No promises.)
Dinnerbone: （很酷的事情包含例如“potion of luck”【直译为好运药水】之类的东西。理论上。没有承诺。）
