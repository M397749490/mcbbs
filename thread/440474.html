中世纪小房子
Medieval Mondays #3: Small House


Hey there! Guess what, it's monday again - welcome to the third Medieval Mondays build!
As for today, I chose to go for a small, rather poor house; there's some little holes, cobwebs, dirt or missing things to the house here and there. Again, the building is completely furnished, which was by the way quite a challenge considering the fact that the house really is small so the space to add furniture is limited. However I still prefer small interiors over the big ones which you have to fill huge empty space to actually achieve something good-looking.
The list below represents the builds that I'd like to cover in this series. The ones marked blue are thsoe that I've already built - click on it to check them out!

今天，我选择了做一个小而差的房子；有一些小洞，蜘蛛网，灰尘或丢失的东西在这里。
建筑需要完善的内饰，这是相当大的挑战，考虑到房子真的很小所以空间增添家具。
不过我还是更喜欢小房子，如果太大你就必须填补巨大的空的空间才能实现一些好看的效果。
