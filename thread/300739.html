巨人手持（装备）的物品及方块都会变大，为了让这个方块看的真实，于是将生物倒立（改名Dinnerbone）
  1. /summon Giant ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:"Dinnerbone",Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:command_block",Count:1}],DropChances:[1.0F,1.0F,1.0F,1.0F,1.0F]}
复制代码


我们给巨人加上隐身效果：
  1. /summon Giant ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:"Dinnerbone",Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:command_block",Count:1}<span style="line-height: 1.5;">],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F],ActiveEffects:[{Id:14,Duration:9999999,Amplifier:50,Ambient:1}]}</span>
复制代码


（但是由于巨人不支持头戴方块，所以无法生成正立的超大方块）

缩小方块：
同理，其他生物（这里指的是可持方块生物）装备显示的方块是缩小的，同样我们可以颠倒生物。
  1. /summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:"Dinnerbone",Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:command_block",Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F],ActiveEffects:[{Id:14,Duration:9999999,Amplifier:50,Ambient:1}]}
复制代码

再给它加上隐身效果：
  1. /summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:"Dinnerbone",Equipment:[{id:"minecraft:command_block",Count:1}],DropChances:[1.0F,1.0F,1.0F,1.0F,1.0F]}
复制代码

同时我们还可以完整平面方块，做到一种伪头颅的效果：（头戴方块）
  1. /summon Zombie ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:"Dinnerbone",Equipment:[{id:air,Count:1},{id:air,Count:1},{id:air,Count:1},{id:air,Count:1},{id:"minecraft:command_block",Count:1}],DropChances:[0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F,0.0F],ActiveEffects:[{Id:14,Duration:9999999,Amplifier:50,Ambient:1}]}
复制代码

同样可以增加Rotation标签来固定"方块"的朝向，就不会局限于头颅的16面。（不过当生物低头是个斜面方块）
