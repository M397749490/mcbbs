CB摆放方法

的顺序摆放(注意全部设为Always Active)
以下内容请从左往右(由repeat至chain方向)塞 入命令方块(无视最左边的那个Impluse)
0.
  1. /scoreboard players add @e ExistTime 1
复制代码
ExistTime自加1

1.
  1. /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Arrow] remove Nuke
复制代码
2.
  1. /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Arrow] add Nuke {CustomPotionEffects:[0:{Id:5b,Amplifier:-2b}]}
复制代码
1~2:判断是否为核 弹~

3.
  1. /entitydata @e[tag=Nuke] {pickup:0b}
复制代码
防止核 弹被捡起而露馅~

4.
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,tag=Nuke] ~ ~ ~ /particle lava ~ ~ ~ 0.1 0.05 0.1 0.2 2 force
复制代码
特技~

引 线特技

2016-2-12 21:38 上传

5.
  1. /scoreboard players add @e[type=Arrow,tag=Nuke] Fuse 1 {inGround:1b}
复制代码
引线计时,每gametick加1

6.
  1. /execute @a ~ ~ ~ /scoreboard players set @e[type=Arrow,r=1,score_ExistTime_min=7,tag=Nuke] Fuse 200 {inGround:0b}
复制代码
如果撞到人则瞬间爆炸~

7.
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse=1,score_Fuse_min=1] ~ ~ ~ /execute @a[r=20] ~ ~ ~ /playsound minecraft:entity.creeper.primed master @a[r=0] ~ ~ ~ 1 0.5 1
复制代码
8(去掉text中的空格).
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse=1,score_Fuse_min=1] ~ ~ ~ /tellraw @a[r=17] {"text":"探测到附近有核 弹","color":"red"}
复制代码
依旧是特技~

9.
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse=200,score_Fuse_min=200] ~ ~ ~ /summon Creeper ~ ~ ~ {ignited:1b,Fuse:0,CustomName:"螺旋剑II",CustomNameVisible:0b,Silent:1b,ExplosionRadius:20,ActiveEffects:[{Id:14,Duration:9999999,ShowParticles:0b}]}
复制代码
10.
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse=200,score_Fuse_min=200] ~ ~ ~ /particle largeexplode ~ ~ ~ 20 20 20 0 3000 force
复制代码
11.
  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse=200,score_Fuse_min=200] ~ ~ ~ /particle largesmoke ~ ~ ~ 20 20 20 0 6000 force
复制代码

(如果太卡的话吧3000和6000改小点)

  1. /execute @e[type=Arrow,score_Fuse_min=202] ~ ~ ~ /kill @e[r=12]
