插件功能十分简单，功能就是将骚扰你的钉子户或在你领地里关了PVP挂机的玩家踢出去
作者原文：
We all know that one guy who outstays their welcome... The guy who just won't leave your base... But don't worry, we've got you covered.? Introducing the power of eleven secret Functions and Algorithms...? Straight up, this plugin is an extension for GriefPrevention. It won't work with WorldGuard, and you will need to have GriefPrevention installed. And yes, I do have to mention this...

指令/限权
/kfc <player>
将玩家踢出领地     
kickfromclaim.kick
/petkick
打开宠物踢出模式
kickfromclaim.petkick
/kfcr | /rkfc
重载插件
kickfromclaim.reload
kickfromclaim.exempt 防止被踢出领地
