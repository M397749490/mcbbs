-All Ores can now be smelted to produce their respective ore


0.3.5

-Potion effects have gone back to needing the whole set of armor for it to be applied on the wearer

-Server side support

-Fixed Farmer's Magic Rake disappearing in one use(notify me if it isn't)

-Achievements have been removed

-Ore and armour texture touch ups

-Ores are a tad bit more rarer

-Super Drill recipe is changed and durability is increased to 3154

-Changed lapis lazuli gem (because of issues!)
