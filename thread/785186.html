删除Essentials就可以了
在后台使用minecraft:clear <player>或者编辑Essentials的plugin.yml
把clearinventory命令的aliases中的clear删掉
修改前
  1.   clearinventory:
  2.     description: Clear all items in your inventory.
  3.     usage: /<command> [player|*] [item[:<data>]|*|**] [amount]
  4.     aliases: [ci,eci,clean,eclean,clear,eclear,clearinvent,eclearinvent,eclearinventory]
复制代码

修改后
  1.   clearinventory:
  2.     description: Clear all items in your inventory.
  3.     usage: /<command> [player|*] [item[:<data>]|*|**] [amount]
  4.     aliases: [ci,eci,clean,eclean,eclear,clearinvent,eclearinvent,eclearinventory]
