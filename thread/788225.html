最新快照18w11a
这个版本屎一样。两个都无法直接实现，但我们可以取巧。
1. /summon minecraft:drowned ~ ~ ~ {IsBaby:1b,ArmorItems:[{},{},{},{id:"minecraft:player_head",Count:1b,tag:{SkullOwner:"MHF_Squid"}}],HandItems:[{id:"minecraft:diamond_sword",Count:1b},{}]}
/summon minecraft:armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {Invisible:1b,Small:1b,ArmorItems:[{id:"minecraft:diamond_boots",Count:1b},{id:"minecraft:diamond_leggings",Count:1b},{id:"minecraft:diamond_chestplate",Count:1b},{id:"minecraft:player_head",Count:1b,tag:{SkullOwner:"MHF_Squid"}}],Tags:["A"]}
#minecraft:tick: /teleport @e[tag=A,type=minecraft:armor_stand] @e[tag=A,type=minecraft:drowned,limit=1]

原理是让小盔甲架带上头颅，并和溺尸重叠。溺尸也带着头颅（虽然不显示），因此不会燃烧。
2. /weather thunder
/summon minecraft:trident ~ ~3 ~ {Trident:{id:"minecraft:trident",Count:1b,tag:{ench:[{lvl:1s,id:68s}]}},Tags:["B"]}
/summon bat ~ ~ ~ {Tags:["B"]}
/kill @e[tag=B]
/weather clear
原理是利用“引雷”附魔
