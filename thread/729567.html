关于(英语原文)
If you are the owner of a factions, survival or RPG kind of server, then you should know for sure how much of an issue horses are. Going on expeditions just to find horses than having to bring them back home on a usually long, frustrating trip that almost certainly has to go over rivers or oceans (which horses can't cross) just doesn't seem to be worth it, leaving many players without a horse. In a role-play server, horses should be essential, as well as in a factions server, where horse battles between factions are just glorious. Those are all just several reasons a server owner should promote horses to their players, but the limitations of vanilla minecraft make this almost impossible.  However, metalhedd solved this issue a long time ago with the release of portable horses, an awesome plugin that allows players to wield horses right into their saddles, giving them the opportunity to store them in chests (making them ideal raid targets in factions) or trade them with others (great for survival servers). I have myself used PortableHorses and my players simply loved it. When it was discontinued in 1.7.9, although I tried to replace it with other horse management plugins, like Stables, players simply didn't like it's system as much as the good old PortableHorses, so I ended up coming back to the vanilla behavior. Now however, after spending several weeks into creating this plugin as configurable and with as many features as possible, I think it's ready for the ultimate test: hitting the road!
特征
  • 非常轻巧, 优化
  • 支持很多版本 (1.8 - 1.12 )
  • 兼容其他插件 ( 区域保护和作战记录 )
  • 一切都可以配置
  • 没有数据库或储存文件! 用NBT储存在马鞍里!
  • 有防盗选项!
  • 有两种把马放到马鞍的方式!
  • 特殊马鞍的选择使用!
  • 支持不同种类的马!
  • 储存马的所有数据, 包括其他插件 ( 或命令 ) .
  • 权限支持!
  • 重载指令!
权限
portablehorses.command: 允许使用 /ph commandportablehorses.command.help: 允许使用 /ph help commandportablehorses.command.reload: 允许使用 /ph reload commandportablehorses.command.givesaddle: 允许使用 /ph givesaddle commandportablehorses.use: 允许玩家使用马鞍将马放入portablehorses.*: 可以访问 PortableHorses 所有相关的权限.portablehorses.playerpack: 默认情况下新手获得的基本权限包，这使得他们可以访问/ ph帮助，并将马带入马鞍.
指令
  • /ph reload: 重制配置文件
  • /ph help: 帮助命令显示有关插件的信息以及如何使用它. 它可以在配置中禁用，它显示的信息也可以编辑.
  • /ph givesaddle: 给发出命令的玩家一个特殊的PortableHorse马鞍, 可以用来将马带入. 只有在配置中启用了特殊的马鞍才可用。
