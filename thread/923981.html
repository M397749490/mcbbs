Lost Magic是以魔法为主题的
Minecraft Mod。


这个名字你们听着可能很陌生但我告诉你们



它1.7.10版本，名叫禁忌魔法(伪经) ，到了18.9版本，名叫魔法学院 ，几经迭代终于迎来了1.12.2的时代。

Lost Magic（失落魔法）他继承禁忌魔法原来的形式并移植到1.12.2，但是更新速度缓慢，目前只制作了2个没什么用的工具


Mod介绍


A small add-on I made for Thaumcraft in 1.12 because there were items from Forbidden Magic that I missed, but didn't want to stick to the Dark 'n Edgy theme.

Features
- Ring of Nutrition: Throw off the tyranny of food!
- Shovel of the Purifier: Break those nasty purple taint blocks when they show up on your property
- Bloody Scrivener's Tools: Use LP instead of having to refill them with ink. (Only if Blood Magic is installed)
