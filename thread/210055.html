The year is 1775. The 13 Colonies of America and The British Empire are on the brink of all-out war. Blood will be shed, Lives will be lost.
Your time has come, to determine whether Americans shall be free men, or slaves.
[size=+0]You are a simple american, who will take up the task of fighting for your country. Whether you choose to do it under cover of darkness or in daylight is up to you.
[size=+0]You will use redesigned armor & weapons, such as the Sword, the Musket and The Tomahawk to annihilate your Redcoat Enemies and protect your land.
You will rebuild your village and craft new tools to help you in your fight.
[size=+0]You will fight for your independence.
[size=+0]You will RISE.
[size=+0]

Designed from the ground up over more than a year, The American Revolution Resource Pack takes one of history's most significant moments and puts it in your hands.&Acirc; This texture pack is historically themed to 1770's America, where the 13 Colonies of America fight for their Independence from their British Overlords. (Yes, it is also Assassin's Creed based, but not very much) The pack is supposed to add a darker tone to the environment, and a much better environment to play in.

2.0 NOW INCLUDES:




  • As a small story, You play the role of an American, fighting for your country. Enemies are dressed up as British Redcoats, who will attack you on sight (Zombies, skeletons, Creepers, etc).&Acirc;
  • The New Animus 3.0 Format, bringing better GUI, Menu, Font, Colors, Vectors, a new Language, a new Main Menu background and more. Note, to use the language it must be set to "English (US)". A separate language file will be available soon.
  • The New Eagle-Sans Minecraft font, designed purely for the American Revolution.
  • All-new Sound Effects! As you traverse the vast frontier you will hear ambient music and whistles, as well as new sounds for eating, taking damage, muskets and for the main menu.
  • You asked for it, and I have delivered it. American Revolution 2.0 Now features Muskets and Bullets! Gone are the days when you need a silent bow, a real patriot fills their enemy full of lead.
  • Redesigned more than 20 Blocks, including Bricks and Cobblestone, taking the look from Assassin's Creed III but still giving it a minecraft feel.
  • Redesigned more than 15 Items.
  • Remastered mobs, Villagers now have two forms, a redcoat (zombie) form and a patriot form.
  • Added more than 20 Alternates, giving you the opportunity to mix and match items, including Connor Kenway's Armor, Redcoat Armor, and add older blocks.
  • Redesigned Balkon's Weapon Mod.
  • Remastered Particles.
  • Bug Fixes, including Clocks and Compass fixes.
  • Added all new 1.6+ Items, and more.


See you on the Frontier, Crafters!

【尺寸= + 0 ]你将采用重新设计的装甲和武器，如剑，步枪和战斧要消灭你的英国军人的敌人和保护你的土地。
你将你的村庄，构思新的工具来帮助你在战斗重建。
【尺寸= + 0 ]你会争取你的独立。
【尺寸= + 0 ]你将上升。

作为一个小故事，你扮演美国的角色，为国战斗。敌人被打扮成英国兵，谁将视线（僵尸攻击你，骷髅，爬山虎，所等）；
新的恶意3格式，带来更好的图形用户界面，菜单，字体，颜色，矢量，一种新的语言，新的主菜单背景和更多。注意，使用的语言必须被设置为“英语（美国）”。一种独立的语言文件也将很快面市。
新的鹰SANS Minecraft字体设计，纯粹的美国革命。
所有新的音响效果！当你穿越广阔边疆你会听到周围的音乐和口哨声，以及新的声音吃，以损害，步枪和主菜单。

重新设计的20多块，包括砖，鹅卵石，把目光从刺客信条3但仍然给它一个Minecraft的感觉。
重新设计的15多个项目。
重新灌录的暴徒，村民们现在有两种形式，一个英国军人（僵尸）的形式和爱国者的形式。
增加超过20时，让你可以混合和匹配的项目，包括康纳建华的盔甲，英军装甲，并增加老街区。
重新设计的阳台上的武器模型。
重新灌录的颗粒。
错误修正，包括时钟和罗盘修正。
加入了新的1.6 +项目，和更多。
