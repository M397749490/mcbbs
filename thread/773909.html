作者: 玄素    时间: 2018-1-2 10:47
这种东西直接用tag会舒服一点，没必要去建个记分板。
  1. scoreboard players tag @e[type=item] add onground {Item:{id:"minecraft:chest",tag:{display:{Name:"假箱子",Lore:["我真的是个假箱子"]}}},OnGorund:1b}
  2. execute @e[tag=onground] ~ ~ ~ setblock ~ ~ ~ minecraft:chest 0 {Items:[{Slot:13b,id:"minecraft:diamond",Count:1b}]}
  3. kill @e[tag =onground]
