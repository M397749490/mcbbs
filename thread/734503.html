首先这个插件还是bate版可能有BUG



默认指令/sc /sct /cps



钻石铲： /fly 可以让管理员飞行

火焰棒：/Launcher 改变管理员的速度

钻石[雾]：/RandomTP 随机tp一名玩家



权限：

staffx.reload:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /staffx reload

staffx.freeze:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /freeze

staffx.mode:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /staff and all in it

staffx.sv:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /sv (vanish)

staffx.svlist:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /svlist

staffx.svsee:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to see users in /sv

staffx.sc:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /sc and /sct

staffx.cps:
[size=14.6667px] Permission to /cps

staffx.mode.blockedit:
[size=14.6667px] Allows breaking/placing blocks in /staff

staffx.mode.invedit:
[size=14.6667px] Allows editing inventory in /staff



命令：

() indicates mandatory arguments, <> indicates optional arguments

[size=14.6667px](StaffVanish) - /sv:
[size=14.6667px] A nice and simple vanish command that will hide you from all players without the vanish-see permission node.

[size=14.6667px](StaffVanishList) - /svlist:
[size=14.6667px] Will list all users that are currently in staff-vanish(/sv).

[size=14.6667px](StaffMode) - /staff:
[size=14.6667px] Will put you in a convenient staff-mode with your inventory safely stored and the ability to restore it/exit staff-mode by simply running the command again.

[size=14.6667px](StaffChat) - /sc (message...):
[size=14.6667px] Will send all online users with the correct permission whatever the specified message is. The (message...) represents whatever you wish your message to be.

[size=14.6667px](StaffChatToggle) - /sct:
[size=14.6667px] Toggles staff-chat on. Indentical to /sc, but this one is a nice toggle on/off for it.

[size=14.6667px](Freeze) - /freeze (target):
[size=14.6667px] Will deny block-breaking and movement for the frozen target. To ensure no one is accidentally frozen forever, frozen players reset upon server reload/restart.

[size=14.6667px](ClicksPerSecond) - /cps (target) <length in seconds>:
[size=14.6667px] Will run a very accurate click test on the specified player. If you do not specifiy the length of the click test, it will be 10 seconds long by default.

[size=14.6667px](StaffXHelp) - /staffx help:
[size=14.6667px] Will display a handy-dandy menu of all the commands and their syntax/format to ensure you know the primary commands for the plugin.

[size=14.6667px](StaffXReload) - /staffx reload:
[size=14.6667px] This will reload the config(s), therefore updating any changes made in the configs without having to reload/restart the server.
