Backto Nature
返璞归真
A city on a cliff and a mysterious monolith
悬崖上的城市与神秘的巨石柱


I grew up in the British countryside. We had rolling fields, deer in ourback garden, and occasionally the whole place would smell like the back end ofa cow. It was great!

我在英国的乡村长大。我们拥有高低起伏的田野，在我们的后花园还有鹿。有时，在这儿还能闻到牛粪的气味。这些真的棒极了！



Although... when you live in a cottage in the middle of nowhere, and you actually wantto get to the places where your friends are, you take it for granted. Insteadof the relaxing parts, all you can see is that you’re the only person under 50in this whole village, and the bus into town only comes once a week : (

尽管如此… 当你住在人迹罕至的村舍里时，你不要认为拜访朋友是一件轻松的事，这实际上很困难。代替了那些令人放松的部分，你会发现一些很糟糕的部分：你是这个村中唯一一个50岁以下的年轻人。通往城里的公交车，一周也只来一次。:(



Now, I live in a city, and though the buscomes once every 10 minutes, I sometimes miss the green, vibrant wildernessthat you just can’t get in a bustling metropolis.

现在，我住在城市里，公交车每十分钟就会经过一次，但有时，我还是会想念乡下的那一抹绿色与生机勃勃的原野。因为这些，你永远无法在喧闹的都市找到。





Daniel W?hlk’s build takes thatbeautiful wilderness to the next step. In the centre of an overgrown, lush jungle,filled with trailing vines and dangling leaves, stands a building - a temple,perhaps, or a palace - long abandoned and surrendered to nature.

Daniel W?hlk的建筑把原野的美丽带进了另一阶段。 那里杂草丛生，树林郁郁葱葱，藤蔓在树枝上蔓延，树枝上覆盖着浓密的树叶。在那中心，矗立着一座建筑——寺庙？或者是宫殿？ ——感觉被遗弃了很久 ，被交还给了大自然。



It stands alone andapart from the rest of the town, built with pillars and orange-red roofs in anAncient Romanesque style, which has trees and shrubs growing up from it as ifno foot had trod there in hundreds of years.

它独自矗立在那里，与周围的小镇分离。它根据古罗马建筑的风格，用石柱和红瓦搭建而成。树与灌木丛在其中生长，就像几百年都没有人到访过这里。

It’s a little bit like a ghost city in a zombie movie - think I AmLegend, or even Wall-E - where trees grow from skyscrapers, and wild animalsroam the streets that were once filled with pedestrians. It’s the city lifeturned back into the country life.

这有一点像《I Am Legend》或《Wall-E》等丧尸电影中的鬼城。树木生长在摩天大楼里。这些街道上，也许曾经是熙熙攘攘的人群，现在只有小动物在游荡，都市又变回了乡村。




“I was not,knowingly, inspired by any movies,” Daniel admits. “I would mostly describe it as my own pure fantasy. One of the sources is strangely ancient Indiancivilisations’ 'tower architecture' from around the Indus river valley area, aswell as the Roman architecture style. The mix is quite a nice result.”

“我并不是受到哪个电影的启发，”Daniel承认。“我更愿意形容它是我脑子纯粹的幻想。其灵感来源之一是神秘的古印度文明在印度河流域的‘塔建筑’，同样的罗马的建筑风格也是灵感之一。把它们混合起来，便得到了如此棒的成果。”




Pulling frommultiple real-world sources, Daniel has managed to create something that makesyou want to wander around on an archeological adventure, Lara Croft-style,despite his design being entirely made-up.。

Daniel从真实世界中提取多种要素，搭建了一个 ‘古墓丽影’ 风格的世界，尽管他的设计是完全虚构的，你也许会愿意在考古冒险中漫步。



For one thing, it’s very hard to build something in the middle of agiant hole. “The terrain is inspired by a sinkhole, really. Sheer cliffs,outcroppings in a forested area.” The tower in the middle of Daniel’s buildsits atop a perilous column of stone in the middle of a gigantic void - it’s nowonder this place is abandoned! Everyone probably fell into the hole : (


首先，在一个大洞中建造是很困难的。“说真的，这个建筑的灵感来自于一个天坑。森林里有着陡峭的悬崖和露出地面的岩石。”在Daniel的建筑中心，有一座塔矗立在危险的石柱顶部，周围是虚空。——难怪这个地方被遗弃了！人们随时都可能掉到洞里:(




There is a storybehind the tower, according to Daniel - a reason why it stands apart from thecity. “I made the project deliberately contrasting the styles from the towerand the city,” he says. “A civilization in its dawn stumbles upon the ancientmonolith of a tower, drawn to it by its magnificence and settling, almostcramming themselves into the little place on the ledges of the basin. They rulethe area for hundreds of years while the monolith falls further into ruin.”

据Daniel说，在塔的背后有一个故事——关于它和城市相间隔的原因。他说：“我故意的让这个塔与周围城市的建筑风格产生对比。”  “在黎明时分，某个文明偶然发现了这个建在巨石柱上的塔。他们被它的辉煌所吸引，定居在盆地边缘有岩石突出的地方。他们统治了这里数百年，而巨石柱也慢慢地变为废墟。”



But although thetower is the clear focus of Daniel’s piece, the city itself has its own senseof simple grandeur. Built in a simple style, to contrast with the monolith, thecity is made of only a handful of block types - “stone bricks, cracked stonebricks, and mossy stone bricks, basic stone, and bricks for the roofs,” saysDaniel - and the style itself is mostly inspired by Roman buildings, “mixedwith a little Greek and a bit of creative freedom.”



尽管这座塔是Daniel建筑的“全场焦点”，这个城市也拥有独特的宏伟感。以简单的风格，与中央的巨石形成对比。搭建城市的方块类型极少——“石砖，裂石砖，青苔石砖，石头和用来做屋顶的红砖。”Daniel说道。而建筑风格则是受到罗马的建筑启发，“并与一点希腊风和创造的自由感融合。”








The build took “50to 60 hours” in total to complete, which took place over the course of severalmonths. Daniel also had two months of university exams, and a deadline that heset himself: finishing the project for MCBCon, aMinecraft build convention, which stopped taking submissions on June 1st - “thebusiest point of my exam period!”

Daniel完成这个建筑总共花费了50到60小时，但这却横跨了好几个月。因为Daniel需要花费两个月去准备大学测验。Daniel给他自己设置了一个最后期限：在‘我的世界建筑嘉年华’——MCBCon截至投稿前——即6月1日之前完成这项工作。他说：“这大概是我考试时期最忙的一段。”

Despite all thesechallenges, Daniel managed to finish and submit his build in time, saying thattime management was the most important thing he learned during the build. Heactually admits that he doesn’t plan his builds before making them, because“planning too specifically will feel limiting to me, which will make me workslower.”

尽管这有很多困难，但是Daniel还是设法完成并及时上交了他的建筑作品。他说道：“我在建造这些的过程中，学到最重要的东西便是时间管理。”他承认，他没有在建造前规划他的建筑，因为“特地地去做计划让我感到有约束感，而且它会让我的工作速度变慢。”
I guess flying by the seat of your pants is faster than walking!

随心所欲地飞翔可比按计划走快多了！






Of course, Danielmight sound like he had a plan all along - having deadlines, a style ofarchitecture, and a build centered around a tower - but he might not have meantfor it to be such a large project. “The goal of the project was to make a fitting location for the tower,” he says. “Needless to say... it wentoverboard.”

当然，Daniel又好像是有事先规划的——‘最后期限’、‘建筑风格’、‘以塔为中心建筑’——但是他可能没想过把它变成如此之大的工程。“我的目标只是想为这座塔找一个合适的位置，” 他说。“不用说，我做过头了.....”



He’s happy with the final result, though, and it means that we alsoget to see and explore his abandoned Romanesque town and mysterious monolithfor ourselves. It’s all been worth it in the end, he admits.

尽管如此，他对结果很满意，这能让我们亲眼看这些——遗弃的罗马式小镇、还有那块神秘的巨石柱，也可以让我们亲自在其中探险。他认为，所有的一切，都是值得的。







“The view from the city looking towards the tower… it’s such a great view to have. Imagine seeingthat every day.”

“从那个城市里眺望塔楼 … 多么棒的景色。想想看，如果你能天天看到该有多好。”
