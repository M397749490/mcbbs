丢一本附魔书到地上，然后手里拿一样附了魔的装备，然后运行命令：
  1. data modify entity @e[nbt={Item:{id:"minecraft:enchanted_book"}},limit=1,sort=nearest] Item.tag.StoredEnchantments set from entity @s SelectedItem.tag.Enchantments
