这个插件是跨服聊天
你安装了以后到 config.要么里面把 你的MySQL信息加进去
  1. useMotdForServername: false
  2. servername: theNameYouCalledThisServer

  4. mysql:
  5.    User: username
  6.    Pass: password
  7.    Data: database
  8.    Host: localhost
  9.    Port: 3306
  10.    TableXServers: xservers
  11.    TableXGroups: xgroups
  12.    TableXServersGroups: xservers_xgroups
