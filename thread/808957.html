/scoreboard objectives add Walk stat.walkOneCm
高频:/scoreboard players remove @a[score_Walk_min=100] 你体力值设定的名字 10
连锁:/scoreboard reset @a[score_Walk_min=100] Walk
这是走多远后扣除体力的方式

/scoreboard objectives add Walk stat.walkOneCm
/scoreboard objectives add Time dummy
高频:/scoreboard players add @a[score_Walk_min=1] Time 1
连锁:/scoreboard players remove @a[score_Time_min=20] 你体力值的名字 10
连锁:/scoreboard players reset @a[score_Time_min=20] Time
连锁:/scoreboard players reset @a[score_Walk_min=1] Walk
这是走1秒就扣除体力的方式

注意执行顺序
