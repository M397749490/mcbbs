ABOUT

Notch Zombies is an zombie adventure survival with elements similar to Call of Duty's Nazi Zombies mode. You kill zombies in exchange for exp which is used to upgrade your weapons and armor. Use teamwork to unlock areas of the map piecing your way out to escape the clutches of the undead. Its highly suggested to play with friends, going solo only leads to one event - death.
