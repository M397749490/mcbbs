Well this texture pack was inspired by Minecraft Alpha with my own tweaks and awesomeness.

So today I was wondering around minecraft when I noticed it was really dull and bland. The colors where horrible, I noticed I really miss minecraft alpha's blocks and colors. I made my own pack that does just that, adds old looking textures but the textures are ones I made from scratch out of the new one. I hope you enjoy. :)
