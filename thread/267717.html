这次是飞行支付的插件










那么什么是飞行支付呢？？

就是玩家飞时需要花money


特点：
12 Custom groups  The ability to charge through Economy money (Like iConomy, BOSEconomy, etc.) or through items  Edit what item and how much of that item to charge for Flying  Edit how much EXP is charged  Edit how long the flying will be enabled until the player must buy it again  Edit how much money will be charged, including decimal values (if your economy plugin supports it)  Edit type of fly mode  Ban worlds that Fly Payment can be used in  Timed Free Fly mode  Fly ban system (ban on Fly Payment usage, not flying with any other plugin  Combat Temporary Ban System - if a player engages in PvP, you can temp ban them from flying  Custom messages  When flying is disabled, edit whether the player takes damage from the fall








命令 说明（所有命令都可以用/ FP使用，或/ FlyPayment）
/FP 显示所以命令
/FP on
 如果玩家有项目/经验/金钱，玩家将开始在config飞行了指定的时间量。
/FP OFF 禁止飞行
/FP Ban <Player> [Time]禁止玩家一个可选的时间
/FP RemoveBan <Player> 删除玩家禁令
/FP CheckBan <Player> 检查如果玩家是被禁止的,给的时间禁止
/FP FreeFly <on:off> [time] 打开自由飞翔，或关闭。 如果，你可以有一个时间限制。

计划：
原意：
Add a fly limit criteria in config. (For example, players can only fly 5 minutes every 5 hours, or 30 minutes every 2 days, etc.)Add the option to pay a multiple of the default amount, to increase the time (ex. If you pay double the default amount, you'll fly for double the time)Add a "Hunger Drain" option in configAdd 'No Fly" Zone as WorldGuard flagAdd a countdown timer (as in, 10, 9, 8, 7...)Add custom 'Fly Speed' optionAdd 'Flight enabled on world change' option(If you want another feature added, just say so in the comments.)
添加一个飞的限制标准配置。（例如，玩家只能飞5分钟或30分钟，每天5小时，每2天，等）添加选择支付的违约数量多，时间增加（例如，如果你付出双倍的违约金额，你会飞的时间双）添加一个“饥饿漏”选项在“禁飞区configadd worldguard flagadd倒数计时器（如，10，9，8，7……）添加自定义的飞行速度optionadd飞行使世界改变”选项（如果你想的另一个特点，就是这么说的意见。）
