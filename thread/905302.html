原文

FURNACES :



In a nutshell, it adds furnaces with a higher cook speed than the vanilla furnace :



The iron furnace is 300% faster

The gold furnace is 400% faster and has an output of 2

The diamond furnace is 600% faster and has an output of 4



ADVANCED FURNACES :



With these furnaces you can cook 3 items at the same time

They're a bit faster too.



The advanced gold furnace is 500% faster and has an output of 2

The advanced diamond furnace is 700% faster and has an output of 4
