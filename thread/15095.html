We have an exclusive sneak peek video of the next big Minecraft update! The most noticeable change is a complete rewrite of the lighting system. Minecraft now makes use of many graphical effects found in other “next gen” games, such as High Dynamic Range lighting, soft shadows and water reflections, as well as some rendering techniques that are simply not possible with more geometrically complex games, such as Real Time Global Illumination. We asked Notch about this impressive visual improvement.
(我们有独家溜聚醚醚酮的录像的下一个大Minecraft更新!最引人注目的变化是一个完整的重写的照明系统。现在Minecraft利用许多特效出现在其它“世代”游戏,如高动态范围照明、软阴影和水的思考,以及一些渲染技术,根本就不可能出现与更多的几何结构上复杂的游戏,例如实时全局照明。我们问切口对这个令人印象深刻的视觉的改进。)

P&#9829;G: Is this graphical update something that you’ve wanted to do for a while?

        (这是图形更新一些你一直想做一会儿吗?)

Notch: I’ve always believed that graphics are more important than gameplay, as it’s the graphics that really allow you to be immersed in a world. The gameplay comes second. I’ve worked really hard on this new rendering system, it’s still got a way to go before it’s stable enough for release, but it’s something I’m putting all my energy on for the foreseeable future. In fact, I think the next year or more of updates will just be making the game look prettier…I may work on the game content more after that if I feel up to it.

           (我一直相信图形更重要的游戏,因为它的图形,真的让你陷入了一个世界。游戏位居第二。我已经非常努力在这个新的渲染系统,它仍然还有很长的路要走之前,它的足够稳定为释放,但它是我把我的所有精力在可预见的未来。事实上,我认为在未来一年或更多的更新,才会使游戏看起来更漂亮…我可能会工作在游戏内容更在那之后我是否能做这事。)


P&#9829;G: A lot of people are fond of the old school graphics that Minecraft has at the moment. What do you say to that? Will there be an option to keep the graphics the way they were?     

          (很多人都喜欢那所旧学校图片,Minecraft目前的时刻。你觉得这个节目怎么样?会有一个选项,可以保持图形的方式,他们吗?)

Notch: Those people can suck my Mojang! People will always complain… The game looks better this way. It’s a compulsory update; it won’t be possible to switch the new graphics on and off.

           (那些人会舔我的Mojang !人们总是抱怨…游戏看起来更好的选择。这是一种强制性的更新;这不可能上切换全新的图形和关闭。)


P&#9829;G: What about system requirements? Will the same computers that could run Minecraft before still be able to run it smoothly?

        (对系统的要求是什么?将相同的计算机,能跑Minecraft之前仍然能够运行它顺利吗?)

Notch: Well that’s the bad news. Unfortunately the minimum system requirements will be quite steep. Even after I optimise it more, I expect it will still take at least an Intel i7, 4GB of RAM and a DX11 graphics card to run well. Which means it will only run on Vista or Windows 7. It’s a lot more computationally expensive as you can imagine, so you’re out of luck if you have an older computer. Time to upgrade I guess!

          (那是坏消息。不幸的是最小系统要求将会十分陡峭。甚至当我,我期望它更优化,还需要至少一个英特尔17,4 g b的公绵羊、一只DX11图形卡,运行状况良好。这意味着它将只运行在景象或视窗7。这真的多求取你可以想象到,所以你运气不好,如果你有一个更旧的计算机。我想时间升级!)


P&#9829;G: Is there anything you had to compromise or take out completely, to make this new lighting system work?

        (有什么是你不得不妥协或拿出完全,为了使这新的照明系统的工作吗?)

Notch: Yes. Mining and Crafting had to go. The world is very static now, but very beautiful.

           (是的。采矿和工艺去了。现在世界上很静,但是很漂亮。)

P&#9829;G: Thanks for your time Notch.

         (谢谢你的宝贵时间散失。)

Notch: Peace out BIATCHES!

          (BIATCHES和平出来!)

Minecraft - Now with more bloom!





UPDATE: We’ve also got information on a new multiplayer mode called “Monster Mines” inspired by Left 4 Dead. Players choose to be either a Monster or a human Miner.
The Miners work the way they do in single player – they can build and destroy blocks, as well as use tools and weapons.
The Monsters come in three varieties -Spiders (The can crawl on walls and shoot sticky web). Skeletons (They have a sword and a bow) and Creepers (They can self destruct). The monsters can build and destroy blocks, but unlike the humans they cannot use tools, so anything harder than dirt will slow them down considerably.
　　更新:我们有一个新的多人游戏模式的信息被称为“怪物”的灵感来自于矿山,导致4死了。玩家选择要么是个怪兽或人类的矿工。
　　
　　矿工的工作的方式就可以在单人-他们可以建立并摧毁街区、使用工具和武器。
　　
　　在三个品种的怪物来-Spiders(可以爬上爬下的围墙和射击粘粘的网页)。骷髅(他们有一把剑和一支弓)和被攀援植物(他们能自己破坏)。这些怪物可以建立并摧毁块,但不同于人类的工具,因此他们无法使用任何此类钢砂的硬度比污物会减慢了他们的速度相当。
