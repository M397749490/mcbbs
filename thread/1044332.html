Build with It: Brick!
与之搭建：砖块
It’s true we have focused on a number offlashy and colorful blocks for our Build With It series. Every oncein a while we like to revisit the basics like stone, or wood – or in thisweek’s case, brick. After all, even the brightest block can benefit from astrong foundation.
近期的“与之搭建“系列中介绍了不少闪闪发光，五彩斑斓的方块。正如之前一样，我们每隔一段时间都会回顾一下那些基本的方块，比如石头和木头，这周便是砖块！毕竟再华丽的建筑（方块）也需要牢靠的基础。

A classic building material, the brick hasbeen used throughouthistory and was one of Minecraft’s earliest materials.  But onequestion remains – why are they red? Since most common bricks are made of clayand then fired in a kiln, the various red hues are a result of  iron oxidewhich is drawn out as the temperature rises. Holding true to tradition,Minecraft bricks are also made of clay which is why most basic bricks are red.
砖块是一种源远流长的经典建材，也是Minecraft中最早出现的几种建造材料之一。即便如此，许多人对于砖块为什么是红色的仍是一头雾水。这其实是因为普通的砖块都是用粘土在窑中烧制而成的，正是窑中的高温使粘土中的铁元素结合氧气形成了大量红色的氧化铁。我的世界中沿用了粘土烧制的传统，这也是为什么砖块是红色的。

Available in slab, stair, brick, and wallform, the brick lends itself to all forms of builds, both classic and modern.You could use it as the flooring in your home, as a clever pathway, or evenbuild an entire brick house (like thecommonpeople has done above).Brick is a striking start for any foundation and ready to be paired with manyof our previous Build With It materials you may have lying around inabundance, like cobblestone and quartz.While real-world bricks typically take mortar or another adhesive to connect,laying brick in Minecraft is much easier – no extra materials required!
砖块能够做成台阶、楼梯和砖块墙。无论是古典还是现代风格的建筑，砖块总是能够胜任！你可以拿它铺地板或者铺路，甚至造一整栋砖块房子都没有问题。（就像大多数人那样）砖块非常适合用来打地基，在这之上你可以搭配许多之前“用它造“系列介绍过的方块，比如说多到泛滥的圆石和石英。在现实世界中砖块需要用水泥将他们砌在一起，但在我的世界中砌砖块可要轻松得多，不需要多余的材料！

One of the most common uses in both thereal world and our virtual one is using brick to create fireplaces. It’s agreat choice for any home. Bricks are durable, with the same blast resistanceas other stone material, and cannot be set on fire.
无论在现实还是在游戏中，砖头最广泛的用途之一便是用来造火炉。砖块很结实，（在游戏中）和别的石制方块一样耐炸，也不能够被点着。
