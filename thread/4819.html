MineColony MOD
http://sites.google.com/site/minecolony/home

Tools used by a delivery man
He is following signs you place along a road, they need to have at least one line of text with the number only as he follows the signs in a similar way as checkpoints works. So you have to put first sign with 0, second with 1 and so on.

[board]
简单说, 那十个牌子就是用来指示搬运工的移动路线.
你先在箱子旁放一个牌子, 在上头写上数字0
再来, 在要他去的地方再放一个牌子, 上头写上数字1
接着, 下个位置再放牌子, 上头写上数字2
后面以此类推...
搬运工会按照牌子上的数字顺序移动.
