Tumble Craft : Donuts Update——圆滑工艺：甜甜圈

MTunble Craft：Donuts
This addons add in a new series of foods, specifically donuts! Each of them has unique effects and crafting recipes. Donuts are stackable in 6 items per stack. Making them quite big, but, this item will definitely help you along your survival journey这个addon添加了一个新的食物系列，特别的甜甜圈！每个甜甜圈都有特殊的效果和配方。一组6个，这些甜甜圈一定会在生存之旅中帮助到你。
This addon require minimum minecraft 1.16 as the version
注意：此插件仅支持1.16版本
插件介绍
This addon is survival friendly, and multiplayer firendly. You can use it on your realms, servers, or multiplayer world. Besides that, this addon also add in porkchop sword and pumpkin sword (only for decoration purposes for now, also you can eat and cook the porkchop sword xD, this idea submited by GaloreInfernoLion and PlagueVamp).
You can add suggestions in the comment below.
这个插件可以用于生存和多人联机，你可以在服务器或多人生存中使用它，除了甜甜圈之外，这个插件还添加了猪排剑和南瓜剑（目前仅用于装饰，也可以食用或烹饪猪排剑，这个想法是由GalorelnfernoLion和PlagueVamp提出的）
