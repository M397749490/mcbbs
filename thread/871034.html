China→Chinese
“When I what to play bedwar”→what改为want
“I only wants to play bedwar...”→wants改为want
“...I hope you can help me to remove the banned.”→banned改为ban

“When I what to play bedwar,Then I was banned..."
语句不通顺，"Then"不用大写，可以改为：
"I was banned as I was about to play bedwar in your service."

“Hypixel is too laggy when I play....”
when用错，无“when do sth”这种用法，改为：
“Hypixel is too laggy when I playing in your service...”
