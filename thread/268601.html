大家好，我是小白，今天给大家搬运一个地图，hypixel的Death Sentence Arena(死刑场）。本作游玩性很强，为RPG地图。声明：本帖除了前面这段话，其它都来自百度贴吧某位大大的搬运，因为小白的机子坏了，所以开不了光影水反，只好搬运其他人的了。图挂请在回复中告诉我。原文：Death Sentence is a competitive arena map that challenges your Minecraft combat skills and agility.
Both Multiplayer and Singleplayer modes are available and have different gameplay.
The goal is to survive the customized monsters that are constantly coming at you and reach level 20 as fast as you can. Kill the chest-headed monsters to gear up, avoid the invincible heavy armored zombies and don't fall in the void!
The multiplayer mode is competitive, the first player to reach level 20 wins the game and get to choose one of the 3 death sentences for the losers. PvP has to be turned off but you can use your flint and steel or throw monsters at your opponent to disturb them!
If you want to play singleplayer mode, your goal is to get the best timer on the clock and post it in this thread and be #1
大概就是本地图有单人多人游戏模式，坚持越久越好。其他的不是重点。接下来，上图（转自百度贴吧）：

[size=13.63636302947998px]小帮助：为了节省快捷栏的空间和使用速度，可以把打火石先用光（三次）然后杀只僵尸，把腐肉放在背包里面，这样以后就不会占快捷栏地方了。除了牛排意外食物可以都丢掉，6块牛排应该够吃，不够的话回头捡就可以了，不要浪费快捷栏位置，因为后期怪太多根本没空开包。五把木剑不要分开，用完了会自动换下一把，很节省空间。药水有就喝掉，瓶子会自动去除。
