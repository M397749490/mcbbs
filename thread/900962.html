家具工艺——装饰你的世界吧！
This add-on adds many decorations, more villagers. There is everything from kitchen utensils and bathroom equipment to things like a stove, shower products and entertainment-related things, like a pool table and a PC for games. It is a great addon if you want more options to decorate your home.
这个MOD添加了许多装饰和更多的村民，从厨房餐具和浴FHC翻译室设备到火炉、淋浴产品和娱乐相关的家具，像桌球台、带有游戏的电脑，应有尽有。如果你想要更多的装饰选择，这个MOD可以满足你。
作者: ROBERTGAMER69, Twitter Account, YouTube
图片展示
它是如何使用的？
Since most furniture is an entity, this means that you can easily rotate the furniture in any direction you want. Some furniture, such as chairs and sofas, also have some functionality in that sense so you can sit on them. Currently all the furniture is in the creative inventory when looking for FURNICRAFT and you will see all the furniture there. You can use a hoe to rotate furniture.
因为家具是一个实体，所以你可以轻易地旋转到任何你想要的角度。一些家具，比如椅子和沙发，都可以坐上去。一般来说所有的家具都能在创造模式物品栏FURNICRAFT里找到。用锄头可以旋转家具。
