给个版本？
1.12
/summon falling_block ~ ~ ~ {Block:"minecraft:stone",NoGravity:1b,Time:-2147483647}
1.13+
/summon falling_block ~ ~ ~ {BlockState:{Name:"minecraft:stone"},NoGravity:1b,Time:-2147483647}
补充：对于1.13+版本，如需将假方块对准至方块，可以使用/execute align xyz run summon falling_block ~0.5 ~ ~0.5 {BlockState:{Name:"minecraft:stone"},NoGravity:1b,Time:-2147483647}
