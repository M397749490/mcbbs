Meet theNitwit
傻子村民
Minecraft’s least useful mob?
我的世界中最没用的生物？

The Exploration Update may have bought maps and mansions and other marvels, but arguably it made an even greater contribution to the texture of village living in the shape of the nitwit.
这次的探险更新也许带来了地图，豪宅以及其他奇观。但傻瓜的加入改变了村庄生活的结构，可以说是本次更新中最大的贡献了。

They may burble benevolently like their fellow villagers, but these green-robed layaboutsdon’t have any trade at all, and just spend each day pottering about aimlessly. You can wave all the emeralds you want - they have nothing for you. So why are they there?
他们也许会像其他村民一样，摇头晃脑的哼哼，但是这些穿绿袍的懒汉们可不会和你交换任何物品，只会成天在那里漫无目的地徘徊。即使你挥动手中所有的绿宝石，他们也不会理睬你。那么他们为什么会在那里呢?

It started because players could summon villagers without a career by using commands: it was the only way to get villagers with green robes. Whenever we discover we have a bug which is used by the community we just see it as 'undefined behaviour' - and ‘fix’ it by making it a feature. In this case we just needed a profession for the green-robed villager. I don’t remember what name we came up with first - I think it was ‘unemployed’ or something, but it doesn’t really fit in the world, because I don’t really think the other villagers are employed by anyone either. So I think the next suggestion was ‘village idiot’ but I thought ‘nitwit’was a more fun name.

------JENS BERGENSTEN


这一切都始于我们发现玩家们能使用指令召唤出没有职业的村民：这也是唯一能够弄出这些绿袍村民的方法。要知道每当我们发现一个被社区利用的漏洞，我们都只会把它当作一个“尚未定义的行为”。而将它变成一个游戏特色则是修补漏洞最佳方式，因此在这里我们只需给绿袍村民安排一个职业就行了。我不太记得当时给他起的什么名字了，似乎是叫“无业游民”来着。但我总觉得这个名字欠妥，因为其他村民也并不是在为谁工作不是吗？我下一个想到了“村民呆子villager idiot”这名字, 但后来我觉得似乎叫他“傻瓜nitwit”更加有趣。
------JensBergensten


So: are they Minecraft’s most useless mob? Only if you assume that everything in Minecraft is there to serve you! Personally, I think the nitwits are just having a jolly good time and/or protesting the imperatives of the commercial state by denying emerald acquisition through limited trading partners as the ideal system on which to predicate social stability. More power to ’em. (Also, bats are prettyuseless, too.)
所以，他们是我的世界中最没用的生物吗？只有当你觉得游戏中所有的东西都只是来为你所用的时候才是！个人认为，傻瓜们只是在享受自己的欢乐时光/或者以正在将通过拒绝有限的贸易伙伴的绿宝石当成来维系社会的安宁稳定的理想系统而抗议为当务之急。力挺他们！（而且蝙蝠也挺没用的。。）
