The "Sound Update"
November 13, 2011
This update brought additional and changes to sounds within the /.minecraft/resources folder; the client was not updated.
Sounds implemented in all versions:
  • New Explosion sounds  爆炸声音
  • New Door, Fence Gate and Trapdoor sounds  门声音
  • New Bow shooting sounds 弓声音
  • Proper Fishing Pole sounds 鱼竿声音
  • Proper Experience Orb sounds 经验宝珠声音
  • Proper Chest sounds 箱子声音
Sounds implemented in Minecraft 1.0RC2 and beyond:下面是1.0增加的声音
  • New arrow hit sounds  箭声音
  • New Player damage sounds 伤害声音
  • Proper Enderman sounds 小黑声音
  • Sounds for Blaze 火焰怪的声音
  • Sounds for Magma Cubes 地狱史莱姆声音
  • Sounds for Silverfish 银鱼声音
