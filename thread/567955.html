Welcome to the city of Blocks Angeles, a Minecraft Pocket Edition City created by Alexhead8835. This city first began taking shape in the October of 2014 as just a simple idea, but has now grown to one of the fastest growing maps on Pocket Edition. Blocks Angeles is meant to look like a realistic, modern city, inspired by how high class cities look like today. It includes a large transit system ([M] network), many different building designs, landscaping of all sorts, and lots of hard work. Please download it, give it a try, and heck, even share it with your friends.

欢迎来到Blocks Angeles城市，由Alexhead8835基于Minecraft Pocket Edition打造的城市. 这个城市在2014年10月已初具规模，当时只是有点简单的思路和想法，但是没想到现今会成为Pocket Edition发展最快的地图之一. Blocks Angeles主要是想发展成为一个真实的的，现代化的城市，例如如今的高楼大厦. 它拥有一个大型的公交系统([M] network), 许多不同的建筑风格，各种园林绿化，以及我们大量的努力. 请下载它，测试它，如果觉得不错的话，可以与基友分享哦.
