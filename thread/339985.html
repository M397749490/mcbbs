Notch(Markus Persson) 的离别信




I’m leaving Mojang
September 15th, 2014
I don’t see myself as a real game developer. Imake games because it’s fun, and because I love games and I love to program,but I don’t make games with the intention of them becoming huge hits, and Idon’t try to change the world. Minecraft certainly became a huge hit, andpeople are telling me it’s changed games. I never meant for it to do either.It’s certainly flattering, and to gradually get thrust into some kind of publicspotlight is interesting.
A relatively long time ago, I decided to stepdown from Minecraft development. Jens was the perfect person to take overleading it, and I wanted to try to do new things. At first, I failed by tryingto make something big again, but since I decided to just stick to smallprototypes and interesting challenges, I’ve had so much fun with work. I wasn’texactly sure how I fit into Mojang where people did actual work, but sincepeople said I was important for the culture, I stayed.
I was at home with abad cold a couple of weeks ago when the internet exploded with hate against meover some kind of EULA situation that I had nothing to do with. I was confused.I didn’t understand. I tweeted this infrustration. Later on, I watched the This is Phil Fish videoon YouTube and started to realize I didn’t have the connection to my fans Ithought I had. I’ve become a symbol. I don’t want to be a symbol, responsiblefor something huge that I don’t understand, that I don’t want to work on, thatkeeps coming back to me. I’m not an entrepreneur. I’m not a CEO. I’m a nerdycomputer programmer who likes to have opinions on Twitter.
As soon as this deal is finalized, I willleave Mojang and go back to doing Ludum Dares and small web experiments. If Iever accidentally make something that seems to gain traction, I’ll probablyabandon it immediately.
Considering the public image of me already isa bit skewed, I don’t expect to get away from negative comments by doing this,but at least now I won’t feel a responsibility to read them.
I’m aware this goes against a lot of what I’vesaid in public. I have no good response to that. I’m also aware a lot of youwere using me as a symbol of some perceived struggle. I’m not. I’m aperson, and I’m right there struggling with you.
I love you. All of you. Thank you for turningMinecraft into what it has become, but there are too many of you, and I can’tbe responsible for something this big. In one sense, it belongs to Microsoftnow. In a much bigger sense, it’s belonged to all of you for a long time, andthat will never change.
It’s not about the money.It’s aboutmy sanity.


全文大致意思：
我将要离开 Mojang
2014年 九月 15号
我不认为自己是一个真正的游戏开发者。 我之所以做游戏是因为它很有趣。 我爱游戏，我爱编程，但我不喜把一个游戏越做越大，我也不曾试着改变世界。 Minecraft 肯定会大受欢迎， 大家也一直告诉我改变了游戏。我从没意思去做这些事情。这当然是恭维，不过逐渐被大众所关注也是挺有趣的。
很久之前，我早已决定辞去Minecraft的开发工作。 Jens（Jens Bergensten 我们称之为jeb） 是接手的最好人选， 而我想尝试做一些新的东西。 刚开始，我想搞个大新闻，但是我失败了。之后我决定只坚持做小型原型和有趣的挑战的游戏。工作给我带了许多乐趣。我不是很确定我是为什么还留在Mojang，公司员工都是真的做实事。 但因为其他人说我对于文化环境很重要，所以我留下来了。
不久前互联网上因为EULA话题爆炸的时候我正在家里，身患重感冒。 我跟他们一点关系都没有。 我很困惑，我不明白怎么了，于是我无奈发了条推特。 随后我才来自Phil Fish的u2b视频，才意识到我和我的粉丝们之间的联系不是我想的那样。 我成为了一个代言象征， 但我不想。对于一些我不太清楚的大事情负责，我不想让他回来而这样进行下去。 我不是企业家，也不是一个CEO， 我只是一个死宅， 一个电脑程序员， 一个喜欢在推特发表个人意见的人。
一旦这次交易完成， 我会马上离开 Mojang，再回去加入Ludum Dares的游戏制作和一些小网页实验。 如果我一不小心又被推上了大众的舞台，我也许会马上弃坑。
考虑到我的公众形象已经有些扭曲，我不指望通过发表一篇文章能摆脱负面评论，但至少现在当我阅读他们的时候不会觉得有责任感在身。
我非常明白我上面写的这些和我之前在公众面前说的很不一样。对此我也不想再说什么了。我知道你们有些人把我当作一个某种抗争的代言符号。我不是，我只是一个和你一样的普通人，每天都在抗争。
我爱你们，在座的各位。感谢你们让 Minecraft 变成了今天这个样子，但是因为人太多，多到我已经无力去对一个巨大的事物所负责。从某种意义上来说，Minecraft 已经属于微软了。而在更大的意义上来说，Minecraft 在今后的漫漫长途中，都是属于你们玩家的，这一点永远都不会改变。
这不是钱的问题，这关乎我的理智。
