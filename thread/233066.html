原帖介绍：

Better Villages Mod

Information:

Hey there guys  :D Jstorey97 here with my first proper mod in months. I have another one in progress and I just started this one today. This mod does a couple of things to villages to improve them such as the following:
1. Makes villages more common.
2. Villages spawn in a new cluster idea. If one village can spawn a couple more will spawn near-bye. (Can get completely ridiculous on flat worlds, See images for examples)
3. Villages can spawn in almost every biome.
4. Made village torch look cooler.
5. Made both type of wheat fields look better with a surrounding fence and torches.
6. Replace the dirt in the wood hut with cobblestone and made the surrounding highlight smooth stone.


Villages spawning in a cluster format (Bit too common). Also notice how they are at sea.
