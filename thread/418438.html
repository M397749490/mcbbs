创世神爱好者的福音

WorldEditSelectionVisualizer（WESV）其实上是一个bukkit插件的形式，这就说我们不需要他们的客户端上安装任何东西WorldEditCUI Mod。当你选一个创世神区域，会生成周围形成一个可以选择的可视化网格粒子。WESV只显给自己。它目前支持选择长方体，多边形，圆柱体，椭圆体和球体。


ps：WorldEditSelectionVisualizer需要安装WorldEdit，否则会崩溃。

这个可视化网格不能离开距离它16格远的地方，不然的话会看不见粒子网格.


权限：
  1. wesv.toggle 丨模式切换的命令
  2.        wesv.use     丨可以选择工具来开启 （因为WESV是默认启用的这个权限，如果你需要强制其他人，你要撤销这个权限｝
复制代码
      命令：
  1. /wesv 切换可视化网格形状
复制代码
       配置：

  1. Configuration
  2. This plugin can be very resource expensive, try adjusting the configuration if you experience lag or clients being kicked.

  4. checkForAxe toggles whether the selection should only get displayed while the player is holding the WorldEdit selection item (usually a wooden axe). (v1.3+)
  5. selectionItem sets the WorldEdit selection item to look for. Possible options are listed here. (v1.3+)
  6. particleEffect lets you change the type of particle that is used to display the selection. Valid options are listed here. If the particle name can't be recognized, the plugin will fall back to RED_DUST. (v1.2+)
  7. horizontalLinesFor(Cuboid|Polygon|Cylinder|Ellipsoid (includes spheres)) enables horizontal filling lines that make the selection more clear. With this option disabled the WESV only spawns particles at the edges of the selection.
  8. gapBetweenPoints is the gap in blocks between particles when rendering lines. Higher numbers mean less particles in total.
  9. verticalGap is the gap in blocks between the horizontal filling lines that can be enabled with the first option. Higher numbers mean less particles in total.
  10. updateSelectionInterval is the interval in ticks in which the plugin looks of the selection of anyone has changed. This has to be done because WorldEdit fires no event when a player changes his selection. Higher numbers mean that players have to wait longer before their new selection is displayed to them.
  11. updateParticlesInterval is the interval in ticks in which the particles are refreshed (because they decay). Higher numbers mean less lag but it will probably look awful.
