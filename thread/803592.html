用盔甲架
1.8：/summon ArmorStand ~ ~ ~ {Invisible:1,NoGravity:1,Invulnerable:1,ShowArms:1,Equipment:[{id:map},{},{},{},{}]}
1.9~1.10:/summon ArmorStand ~ ~ ~ {Invisible:1,NoGravity:1,Invulnerable:1,ShowArms:1,HandItems:[{id:map,Count:1b},{}]}
1.11+：/summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {Invisible:1,NoGravity:1,Invulnerable:1,ShowArms:1,HandItems:[{id:map,Count:1b},{}]}
