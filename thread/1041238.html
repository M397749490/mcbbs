前言   introduction
该地图是在考虑了插件UhcCore的情况下制作的，但通常可以轻松用于PvP比赛。 外环包括12个重生室，每个产出室在中间都有一个青金石块，该在该处产出队伍/玩家。 尺寸：高67，宽192和长192（包括外部基岩层）。
作者原话：
作者：Nonatal99
This map was made with the plugin UhcCore in mind, but can easily be used for PvP matches in general. The outer ring consists of 12 spawn rooms, each with a lapis lazuli block in the middle where teams/players are supposed to spawn.

Dimensions: 67 high, 192 wide and 192 long (when including outer bedrock layer).
