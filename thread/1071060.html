ref: https://github.com/PaperMC/Paper/issues/2785 Mismatch in destroy block #2785
paper 端决定把这条消息的优先级下调到 DEBUG 频道，帮你屏蔽
ref: https://www.spigotmc.org/threads/error-mismatch-in-destroy-block-spam.410950/ Solved [ERROR] Mismatch in Destroy Block Spam
当客户端破坏方块的速度与服务端上不一样的时候会刷这条提示，在net.minecraft.server.v1_15_R1.PlayerInteractManager内，比较量this.g有时会与实际操作的方块坐标发生偏差，甚至其引用直接变成默认值{0, 0, 0}
