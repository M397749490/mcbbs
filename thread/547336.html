作者的原话[我就不翻译了]:
Inspired by the Sponge community. This is a new idea of using the chat. The chat is splitted into channels. You can switch the channels easily by just clicking on the channel name at the bottom of the chat window. You can still receive chat messages. If a new message was sent and you are in a different channel you get notification and could see the number of unread messages next to the channel name.  Moreover you don't need to invoke a special command in order to write in a channel. If you switch to channel, you can start writing your message. The message will be sent to your current viewing channel.

指令和权限：

/switch <名字> - 切换到某个聊天频道
其实切换完全用点的就可以了
/private <玩家> - 开始一个私人聊天


tabchannels.command.private——开始一个私人聊天
tabchannels.command.switch——切换频道
PS：两个权限都要给，不然玩家用不了
