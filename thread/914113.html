Decoration Furniture Addon
Add highly detailed models to your modern house or any kind of house, an iMac, coffee maker, a telephone, blender, entertainment system, a couch and a speaker, created focused on high detail and almost everything made block by block.
Add a modern and realistic touch to your house with our new modern looking furniture, which include an iMac, a telephone, a speaker, blender, entertainment system, a couch and a coffee maker. But this addon its not finished yet, you have to finish it. Tell us in the comment section what would you like to see in the next version (New version each 3-5 days) and we will maybe add it.
为你现代的房子或任何种类的房子添加了高细节的模型，现代且接近现实，一台iMac、咖啡机、电话、搅拌机、娱乐系统、沙发和音响等。专注于高细节的模型，几乎所有的模型都是一个方块一个方块做出来的。但是这个MOD还未完成，你可以帮助我们，告诉我们你想在下个版本中加入什么（每个新版本一般是3-5天发布），我们可能会加入你的想法。

作者：LANCELOTF
可以工作的家具
Some of the furniture actually works. For example, the chopping board can chop at the moment bread, the blender make a milkshake and the toaster toast bread. In this last update i added a grill and a gaming chair, but also added animations to make it look more realistic. I took a 2 week programation course, and that is why i took so long to update it. I will be making animations for each model gradually. To interact with the grill, just long tap on it with a raw steak in your hand. And to interact with the toaster, long tap with a sliced bread in your hand. It can toast up to 2 breads at the same time.
一些家具实际上可以使用，例如案板切面包，搅拌机可以做奶昔，烤面包机可以烤面包。在最近的更新中添加了烤炉和游戏椅，还添加了动画让他们看起来更加真实。后面会逐步为其他家具添加动画。用生牛排长按烤炉使用烤炉，用面包长按烤面包机使用烤面包机，最多可以同时烤2个面包










音响设备
In order to play music, first you have to place speakers (Put as many as you want, but be careful, you dont want to destroy your ears). Now, just place an audio player, and you will be able to hear the music. Then, to play the next song tap and hold the music player button, and if you want to play the previous one, do the same thing but sneak while pressing the button. You have to download music packs to be able to hear music. You can download them here. In the future i will be adding some new things like a 3-D audio option, more speakers like wall speakers, ceiling speakers, etc.
Note: To stop the music you have to run the next command: “/stopsound @a”. In the future i will add a button to the audio player that stops the music.
要播放音乐，首先要放音响（想放多少放多少，但是别伤到自己的耳朵）。现在，放一个音乐播放器，然后你就可以听到音乐了。要播放下一首歌曲，点击并按住音乐播放器按钮，如果你想听上一首，在潜行状态下执行上述操作即可。你需要去下面的网址下载你喜欢的音乐包。未来会添加一下环绕音设置和更多的音响，如挂墙的，还有在天花板上的。
注意：停下音乐可以输入/stopsound @a 指令，未来会添加一个暂停按钮的。









合成表
The crafting system in 1.12 and 1.13 still in development, and it has bugs. The crafting recipes could partially work or they could not, it depends on your device.I am sorry if the crafting recipes dont work on your device. Im looking for a fix or another way to craft this items.
在某些设备上合成表可能会不能用，作者正在尝试修复此问题
如何旋转家具
Get close to the object you want rotate and type /function rotate.
The item will rotate facing you.
靠近你要旋转的家具，输入/function rotate 后，家具就会面向你
注意
To use the coffee maker first take cocoa beans and load it by just holding or right clicking on it. Then take a mug and interact with it. It will fill up the mug. To make the mug, just use glass block.
拿着可可豆按住或者右键咖啡机，然后拿杯子右键它，杯子会被装满咖啡，顺带一提，可以用玻璃做一个杯子。
