Friend Ball——友友球
A Friend Ball is a type of Poké Ball that sets a captured Pokémon's base happiness to 200. This effect does not occur if a Friend Ball is inherited by a Pokémon through breeding. Friend Balls have the same catch rates as normal Poké Balls. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop.
友友球是一种可以设置已被捕获的宝可梦的亲密度到200的精灵球。如果它接收到的宝可梦是通过繁殖产生的，那么这个效果就不会发生。友友球有和普通精灵球相等的捕获率。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。

Heavy Ball——沉重球
A Heavy Ball is a Poké Ball for catching very heavy Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  Unlike most Poké Balls, the Heavy Ball's catch rate modifier is additive rather than multiplicative. The catch rate modifier for Heavy Balls based on Pokémon weight is as follows:

<204.8 kg                 -20

>409.6 kg                +40

Level Ball——等级球
A Level Ball is a somewhat different Poké Ball which becomes more effective when used on Pokémon at a lower level than the lead Pokémon. It can be obtained by crafting it, buying from a shopkeeper, or as a tier 1 special drop,  The catch rate for the Level Ball is:  1× if the lead Pokémon is the same level or a lower level than the wild Pokémon. 2× if the lead Pokémon is above the level and less than double the level of the wild Pokémon. 4× if the lead Pokémon is between double and quadruple the level of the wild Pokémon. 8× if the lead Pokémon is quadruple the level or more of the wild Pokémon.
等级球是一种与普通精灵球稍有不同的精灵球，它对比你的首选宝可梦等级低的宝可梦的捕捉率会更高。它可以通过合成、购买或作为1级特殊掉落物来获得。
等级球的捕获率如下：
1×当你的首选宝可梦比目标宝可梦的等级低或与其相同时
2×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高，但未达到2倍时
4×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高2~4倍时
8×当你的首选宝可梦的等级比目标宝可梦高超过4倍时

Master Ball——大师球
The Master Ball is a type of Poké Ball that will always catch the Pokémon it is used on (unless the Pokémon cannot be caught, like a boss Pokémon or another trainer's Pokémon). Unlike all other Poké Balls, Master Balls have no chance of breaking if thrown against a block. A Master Ball can be obtained as a tier 3 special drop, or as a possible drop from legendary and ultimate boss Pokémon. Master Balls cannot be crafted.
大师球是一种必定能抓住宝可梦的精灵球（除非目标宝可梦不能被抓获，例如一个boss宝可梦或者其他人的宝可梦）。与其他的精灵球不同，大师球在贴墙投掷时没有几率被打破。大师球可以通过作为3级特殊掉落物获得，或者作为一个传说boss或最终boss的掉落物来掉落。大师球不能被合成。

An aluminum base is created by placing an aluminum disc on an anvil (by using the anvil when holding a disc), then by hitting the anvil with a hammer on the anvil. Bauxite Ore——铝矿
Bauxite is a new ore introduced in Pixelmon. Its main usage is the manufacturing of several Pixelmon machines.  Bauxite can be found within stone at height levels between 30 and 60, in veins of up to eight blocks. It is also a drop from certain wild Pokémon.  Bauxite ore requires at least an iron pickaxe to break. Due to this, it is possible to tell bauxite from iron by trying to break it with a stone pickaxe, as bauxite can't be broken with it while iron can. The color is slightly different as well; bauxite ore is a little more dull than iron ore.
铝矿是一种Pixelmon mod新引进的矿石。它主要的作用是制造一些Pixelmon机器。铝矿可以在高度30~60的地方找到，每个矿脉生成8个。它也会从一些野生宝可梦处掉落。铝矿至少需要铁镐才能打破。由此，用石镐试图打破它是一种区分铝矿和铁矿的方式。当铁矿可以被打破的时候，铝矿并不能被打破。颜色也有一些区别，铝矿比铁矿稍微暗一些。
