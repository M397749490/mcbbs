直接上原理：
  1. /give @a bow 1 0 {Unbreakable:1,ench:[{id:48,lvl:54},{id:49,lvl:1}],display:{Name:"§e§l元素领主",Lore:["","§9元素领主 ， 弓中的领主 ！","","§5-= 元素多重箭 =-","","§9你射的箭将变成元素箭 ， 射中怪物时将令它们减速并遭受高伤害雷击 。","","§7Epic Item",""]},Tags:["Lightb"]}
复制代码
先给自己一把特殊弓
  1. 高频：/scoreboard players tag @a add Lightb {SelectedItem:{tag:{Tags:["Lightb"]}}}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @a add Lightb {Inventory:[{Slot:-106b,tag:{Tags:["Lightb"]}}]}
复制代码
检测是否手持特殊弓
  1. 连锁：/execute @a[tag=Lightb] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[type=arrow,r=2] add Lighta {pickup:1b}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/execute @a[tag=Lightb] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[type=arrow,r=2] add Lighta {pickup:2b}
复制代码
给附近玩家射的箭加tag
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Lighta] ~ ~ ~ particle fireworksSpark ~ ~ ~ 0 0 0 0 2 force
复制代码
粒子效果
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Lighta] ~ ~ ~ effect @e[r=3,type=!Player,tag=!Lighta] 2 2 0 true
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Lighta] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[r=3,type=!Player,tag=!Lighta] ~ ~ ~ summon lightning_bolt ~ ~ ~
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Lighta] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[r=3,type=!Player,tag=!Lighta] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[tag=Lighta,r=4] remove Lighta
复制代码
雷击以及给缓慢效果
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @a remove Lightb
复制代码
移除玩家tag
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @e[tag=Lighta] add Kill {inGround:1b}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/kill @e[tag=Kill]
复制代码
kill掉地面的箭
