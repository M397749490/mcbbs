Wool Pressure Plates

羊毛压力板


Wool Pressure Plates adds pressure plates made out of every available colored wool. These new pressure plates act the same way as wooden pressure plates. They will give off a redstone signal when walked on, and when an item falls onto them.
Additionally, they will negate a very tiny amount of fall damage.
You can craft the plates with a wooden pressure plate and any color wool you like (shapeless).

主要功能:
加入了16色羊毛压力板
(作用方式与木质压力板相同)
