  1. [07:47:54] [Client thread/ERROR]: Using missing texture, unable to load minecraft:textures/.png, java.io.FileNotFoundException
复制代码

接着1853行，程序试图载入一个高清材质包(然而分辨率不相等真的好吗……)
  1. [07:47:59] [Client thread/INFO]: Created: 4096x2048 textures-atlas

复制代码
