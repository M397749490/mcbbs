概述：
这个饮水mod致力于增加MC难度，通过增加一个口渴栏以及一些其他的东西
首次安装，一个饱食度的栏会出现在HUD

这个栏将会随着体力活动而消耗，比如行走速度，跳跃，是否破坏方块，打怪，死掉甚至是接近特定的方块(应该是指岩浆之类的)

随着身体缺水，会出现无法奔跑，走路蹒跚，慢慢失去生命，以及视力模糊等等问题(蹒跚debuff+扣血debuff+恶心debuff)


Overview:
The Thirst Mod aims to increase the difficulty of Minecraft by adding a Thirst Bar along with other goodies. Once installed, a bar similar to the Food Meter will appear in the HUD. The bar will begin to deplete during physical activity which is based upon a multitude of factors including movement speed, jumping, breaking blocks or not, attacking, losing health and even proximity to specific blocks. Once the bar begins to drop you can replenish the bar by consuming drinks to rehydrate yourself. With low hydration, several problems will arise beginning with the disablement of sprinting, and then slow health loss coupled with nausea effects.


特点：
这个mod增加了很多功能，这些对你MC过程有用

Features:
This mod adds many features, all which are created to help you in your journey in minecraft:



雨水收集器

这个方块可以帮你收集雨水。
这个机器可以将许多不同的容器放进去，比如水瓶，水桶和杯子
从这个方块里面收集到的水是干净的(全自动雨水过滤机？)，你可以直接喝而不会中毒。
而且这个方块还可以储存水，这样你在不下雨的时候也可以使用。
去使用这个机器，只要将上面讲到的容器放到下面这个格子里，水就会慢慢填装进去。
水瓶也可以用这种方法装水(感觉这句和上面那句重复了)


Rain Collector:
This blocks enables you to collect rain water. It functions with many different containers available in the game such as Bottles, Buckets and Cups. The water received from this block is clean and thus can be drunk without any chance of poisoning and can also be stored for later use when it is not raining. To use it, simply place a container(mentioned above) in the bottom slot and watch the water slowing enter the container. Make sure the block is placed within a clear view of the sky or the internal bucket will not fill. Canteens can also be refilled with this method.



饮料酿造机


这个饮料商店是你所有饮料的核心。(这个酿造机是你所有饮料的核心)这个允许你去自定义创造任何你可以喝的 装在一玻璃杯，一个物品来酿造
而且可以在下面加上任何游戏里的燃料(甚至是mod里面的)
在使用这个方块，首先你必须要填装燃料，不然不会工作。
酿造物可以是各种各样的，一般你可以尝试各种各样的酿造
当然，苹果汁肯定需要用到苹果

Drinks Brewer:
The Drinks Store is the core for all of your drinks. It allows you to create consumables which you can drink by placing a glass bottle, an item to 'brew' and any fuel source in the game (even mods) in their appropriate slots as indicated. To use this block, you must first install content packs and if the game detects no packs, this block will be useless. The brewing item can be anything and is usually indicated the drink you are trying to create. For eg: Apple Juice will definitely be made using Apples.


饮料店：


饮料店允许你去购买那些很难得到的饮料
在一些用到硬币的地方，选择你要的饮料，选择数量然后点击购买
这个银币会随着你购买而被带走(就是扣掉了·)
Drinks Store:
The Drinks Store allows you to purchase drinks that would be otherwise be difficult to obtain. To use place in some coins, select your drink, select the amount and click buy. The coins will be taken from you and used in an alternate dimension called (doesn't exist).

Drinks:
Without these you’d be dehydrating every second and dying. These drinks can be drunk when held and holding right click. The drinks replenish your bar differently in the level and saturation. More drinks can be created through the use of “txt” files and placing them in “/ThirstMod/Content Packs/.” Drinks contained in the core version include: Milk, Chocolate Milk, Fresh Water and also Boiled Water. Even though the mod edits no base classes, drinking from Soup, Potions, Water Bottles(poisonous) and Milk Buckets will also replenish your thirst.


饮料的创建

饮料可以 Drinks Brewer中创建来自其他mod的饮料
通过简单地创建“txt”文件,并将它们放到“/mods/ ThirstMod /Content/。
在饮料创建教程里有更多（这句好像不大对）

在饮水Mod文件夹。
Expansion Packs:
Drinks can be created in the Drinks Brewer from other mods by simply creating “txt” files and placing them in “/mods/ThirstMod/Content/.” Have a look at the Drinks Creating Tutorial later on to find out more! You can also add drinks from other mods in a similar fashion. A system is also available to enable drinks from other mods to heal the bar. Check out 'usage.info' in the Thirst Mod folder.
