Serene Seasons

静谧四季



作者的介绍
Serene Seasons is a Minecraft modthat adds seasons.  Grass and foliage colors will change throughout theyear, and in the winter, temperatures will shift and allow for snow to fall insome biomes that don't normally receive any.  Seasons also affect variousother features, like weather (More rain in Spring, more storms in Summer,etc.)!

静谧四季是增加季节的Minecraft mod。 草地和树叶的颜色会在四季发生不同的变化，而在冬季，温度会变化，并影响一些没有积雪的生物群系有积雪，当然水也是会受到影响。 季节还会影响到方面，比如天气（春季降雨多，夏季多风暴等）！
