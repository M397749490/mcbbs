指令是
/summon armor_stand ~-1 ~ ~ {Tags:["SAT",NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b]}
/summon armor_stand ~1 ~ ~ {Tags:["SAT",NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b]}
/summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~-1 {Tags:["SAT",NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b]}
/summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~1 {Tags:["SAT",NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b]}
就这样一直穷举下去,然后到达了指定的数量用计分板+传送指令,利用
/kill @e[tag=SAT,score_Score_min=7]清除掉盔甲架,检测到方块就放置一个带有SATS标签的盔甲架
