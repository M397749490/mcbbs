chestcommands.open.要打开的菜单配置文件名
比如chestcommands.open.example.yml
/mangaddp Builder chestcommands.open.菜单文件名.yml



/mangaddp Builder chestcommands.open.菜单文件名.yml
/mangaddp Builder chestcommands.item.菜单文件名.yml

建议2个都给，一个是指令打开菜单一个是右击物品打开菜单
假如您的插件文件夹menu内的菜单名为zhucaidan
给与权限：
/mangaddp Builder chestcommands.open.zhucaidan.yml
/mangaddp Builder chestcommands.item.zhucaidan.yml
