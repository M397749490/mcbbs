                                                                              Fame —— 声望|名气|判断玩家身
作者的一大堆废话：
  1. [size=4]Fame is a plugin that will let your players judge how good or bad a single player is by voting on them. Players can vote 1, -1, and 0 for someone, higher numbers meaning liking the player and vice versa. If a player's fame is negative, they are unpopular. Zero? Neutral or unvoted. Positive? Thought of nicely by the majority. Three values are displayed when someone checks a player's fame. Fame to average voter (ranges from -1 to 1), sum of fame votes (total votes added together), and number of voters. What's good about this plugin is if a player does evil deeds, they will be noted down and it will affect their fame. If you're a player on a server with this plugin, you can, say, have a requirement of fame of 0.5 or higher and 5 voters to join your faction, town, clan, or other group. Or you could decide to not try and trade with someone if they have a fame of -1 (they might be a scammer!). The ways fame can affect your server has endless possibilities and is all up to your community.[/size]

特征:投票表决一个玩家有多好-显示一个玩家的声誉-记录玩家的声望在文件，以及他们的票
