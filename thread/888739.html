教程:
放置一个旗子,颜色随意。
靠近旗子,打这条指令:
  1. /summon minecraft:armor_stand ~ ~-1 ~ {CustomName:"{"text":"[输入要打的字]"}",Tags:["CB Creator"],DisabledSlots:65793,CustomNameVisible:1b,Invisible:1b,NoGravity:1b}
