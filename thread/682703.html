What is SimplePets?
SimplePets是什么？
SimplePets was first started around 2-3 years ago on a server I develop for (CatsCraft). At the time, SimplePets only consisted of 5-8 pets. But now enough of the Back story. SimplePets is a companion/pets plugin, The plugin was originally created to be an Easy-to-use more user Friendly Alternative to the now discontinued EchoPets plugin.
SimplePets插件是在2-3年前我为一个服务器（CatsCraft）制作的。那时的SimplePets只由5-8个宠物组成，但是往事已经过去，SimplePets是一个伙伴/宠物插件，这个插件与一个不再更新的EchoPets插件相比更加简单，更加方便玩家使用
