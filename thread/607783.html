加最大生命值的钻石帽子/give @a minecraft:diamond_helmet 1 0 {AttributeModifiers:[{UUIDMost:1974184928523994532L,UUIDLeast:-9111405915925636465L,Amount:1.0d,AttributeName:"generic.maxHealth",Operation:0,Name:"My Modifier",}]}

加速度的钻石帽子/give @a minecraft:diamond_helmet 1 0 {AttributeModifiers:[{UUIDMost:4792943891141772862L,UUIDLeast:-7050069898918220157L,Amount:1.0d,AttributeName:"generic.movementSpeed",Operation:0,Name:"My Modifier",}]}

钻石衣服：minecraft:diamond_chestplate
钻石裤子：minecraft:diamond_leggings
钻石鞋子：minecraft:diamond_boots（代替minecraft:diamond_healmet即可）
