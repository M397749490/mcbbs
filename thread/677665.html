剧情 Story
You are a test subject in a testing chamber which is mostly made out of quartz. A computer named S.A.N. will guide you through the test.
While you are figuring out the tasks S.A.N. unexpectedly shut down. Now you must try to find the problem of S.A.N. and some events will happen while you are doing you are trying to find the problem.
你是一个测试石英做的密室的教学老师。有一个电脑名叫：“S.A.N”。它会帮你完成你的考试。
当你在试图找到S.A.N的任务时。这个电脑意外的关机了。你现在必须找到为什么会这样，在你找到问题的之前，发生了很多。
