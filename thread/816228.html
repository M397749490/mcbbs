Roguelike Adventures and Dungeons

Roguelike冒险与地牢

原作者页面：Curseforge

整合包介绍

原文：
Do you like roguelike dungeon crawling games? Or exploring Minecraft world for dungeons and structures for loot? Tired of all that tech stuff and just want more fun? Then this pack is for you. It is completely focused on adventuring and exploration, looting and fighting. Fight you way through the dungeons, improve your skills and gear, complete quests and conquer new undiscovered dangerous dimensions. There is no time to relax because as you grow stronger, gathering better equipment mobs will also gain health and becomes stronger!     Not enough for you? Want more? Explore new dimensions - Twilight Forest, The Betweenlands, Aether, The Beneath, Cavern II! Find new mobs, experience new landscapes and unravel more mysteries. Step through dimensional doors to find mazes, dungeons and traps. Lose yourself in enchanted forest where time stopped at twilight and encounter boss monster like Lich, Hydra, Naga and few more.  Make your way to the wonderful world of Aether... Explore an amazing realm in the sky with help of your newly crafted airship! Delve into labyrinths, aquire new equipment and fight even more bosses. Still reading and not dowloading the modpack? Got more for you - Betweenlands. It's a harsh unforgiving world of swamps and dangers with many new mobs and bosses with lots of new mechanics, blocks and items for you to explore. Experience all the beauty of new worlds.


译文：
你喜欢roguelike类型的地牢游戏吗？或者探索Minecraft世界的地牢和结构以获取战利品？厌倦了科技的各种装备，只是想要更多的乐♂趣？然后这个整合包适合你。它完全专注于冒险和探索，搜刮地牢和与怪物战♂斗。通过地牢的战斗，提高你的技能和装备，完成任务并征服新的未被发现的危险维度。没有时间让你停下来休息，因为随着你变得更强壮，打造更好的装备，怪物也在变得更加耐打并变得更强大！还不够吗？想要更多？探索新的维度 - 暮色森林，交错次元，天境，Cavern II！寻找新的怪物，体验新的景观并揭开更多的神秘面纱。通过次元门找到迷宫，地牢和陷阱。迷失在迷人的森林中，在时间停止的暮色森林，遭遇BOSS级怪物，巫妖王，九头蛇，娜迦王等。前往精彩的天境......在你刚做的的飞艇的帮助下探索天空中的神奇天境！钻研迷宫，获取新设备，与更多的BOSS进行战斗。还在阅读而不是下载这个整合包？再告诉你一些 - 在交错次元。这是一个严酷无情的沼泽和有许多危险的新怪物和BOSS的世界，以及有许多新的机械，方块和物品供你探索。体验新世界的所有的美好！


本整合亮点

  • tons of dungeons and new structures to find and explore. Delve underground or explore ruins on surface.
  • 成千上万的地牢以及新的建筑供你去寻找及探索。深入地下或者探索表上的遗迹。
  • new dimensions with own bosses and adventures. (Twilight Forest, Aether, The Betweenlands, Cavern II, Beneath, Hunting
  • Dimension);
  • 自身带有BOSS和成就的新维度（暮色森林，天境，交错次元，洞穴II，Beneath，狩猎纬度）
  • be a wizard! RPG-style system of magic spells to Minecraft with the aim of being as playable as possible. No crazy constructs, no perk trees, no complex recipes - simply find spell books, cast spells, and master the arcane!
  • 成为巫师吧！为Minecraft而设计的RPG风格的法术系统，旨在尽可能得有可玩性。没有疯狂的建筑，也没有科技树，更没有复杂的合成，能够简单地获得咒语书，法术卷轴以及掌握奥术！
  • door and portals that can teleport you to mazes, puzzles and traps.(Dimensional Doors);
  • 门以及传送门能把你传送到迷宫，谜题以及陷阱中（次元门）；
  • harder and smarter mobs. Diablo-like modifiers for mobs will make you tremble from fear...
  • 更难以及更聪明的怪物。对怪物修改得像暗黑破坏神那样，让你因恐惧而颤抖...
  • increased mobs variety: from peaceful chameleons to creepy creature.
  • 增加的怪物种类：从和平的变色龙到令人毛骨悚然的生物。
  • quests that will guide you through the game and give you some challenges.
  • 任务将指导你游玩并给你增加一些挑战。
  • earn coins for completing quests and exchange them for valuable rewards you like.
  • 完成任务赚取金币，并把它们交换成你喜欢的奖励。
  • scaling difficulty. As you become stronger mobs will too!
  • 难度度量，随着你变强，怪物也会变强！
  • airships! Perfect for your Aether exploration.
  • 飞艇！完美契合你的天境探索。
  • random modifiers for you weapon and tools.
  • 给予你的武器和工具随机的效果。
  • level up your skills and your equipment.
  • 升级你的技能以及装备。
  • simple and more advanced enderchest-like backpacks with own inventory for long journeys.
  • 简单且更高级的类似末影箱的背包，就算在在长途旅行也有自己独有的空间。
  • some improvements to wolves. Make a new friend so you don't travel alone.
  • 稍微改进了狼，找个新的朋友让你的旅程不再孤单。
  • ...and more for you to discover!
  • 还有更多等待你去发现！
