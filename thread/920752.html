作者原文
What can I do with Curses? This plugins allows you to curse your players in different ways using just a GUI Inventory. You can enable multiple curses per player, currently there are 20+ curses and more will be added soon!
When you milk a cow you receive a lava bucket
Breaking diamond ore in any way will always give you a diamond.
Placed blocks will disappear after a period of time.
The chat will be spammed constantly.
When you place a chest, it will explode. WARNING: It will destroy your surrounding.
You will take damage when touching water.
Ores will be replaced with Stone when they are broken.
Shearing sheeps will give you random colored wool.
Your damage will be reduced by 90%.
Fall damage will kill you instantaneously.
Placed blocks will turn into another one.
Eating rotten flesh will move your player randomly in every direction.
Tools will break after 1 use.
Throwing eggs will spawn villagers instead of chickens.
 You will be cursed with slowness for a long, long time.
 The player will be kicked from the server showing a mysterious message.
 A creeper will be spawned near the player.
 Leaving a bed will teleport you 20 (configurable) blocks up.
 Breaking blocks will drop random items.
 Killing monsters or animals will spawn 2 more in the same location.
Every 30 seconds (configurable) you will receive a random item.

亲 测 感 言
这个插件更适合和谐有爱的养老服
为平静的生活增加乐趣的那种
请确认自己的服务器环境再考虑加入


食 用 方 法
/curse 即可打开玩家GUI列表，选择玩家点击给予效果即可（嗯，这就是全部操作了，真的很简单）
/curse 玩家ID 可以直接给予玩家效果
/curse reload 重载插件
curses.admin 使用上面三个指令的权限
你可以在config里面关闭你想关闭的效果，然后安全的把这些权限给予某些玩家

所有的效果都会在玩家下线后关闭!
