BareBones - 创元卡通

作者原文：
  This texture pack has gotten a name change! Bare Bones is a texture pack with the purpose of bringing your world and the default Minecraft textures to it's 'bare bones'. It is a simplistic and vibrant pack that is fun to use and makes your own Minecraft worlds look colourful!
This resoucepack complete with new vanilla-ready 3D models and variable textures for a number of blocks and items!Enjoy!

Install-材质安装

对正版用户:
1、关闭Minecraft，
2、在Windows资源管理器的路径中输入 %appdata%/.minecraft 。
3、把下载的材质包直接放到. minecraft\resourcepacks 文件夹里面。

对非正版用户：
1、打开游戏文件夹中的.minecraft，
2、把下载的材质包直接放到. minecraft\resourcepacks 文件夹里面。
