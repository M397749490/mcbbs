What is King Arthur's Gold?

KAG is a game about mining, building castles and destroying your enemies. It is a side-scrolling 2D action multiplayer war game with focus on building, constructing siege machines and intense PvP combat. Some inspirations for the game are Soldat, Minecraft, Dungeon Keeper & Ace of Spades.

KAG是一个可以采集，建造城堡，摧毁你的敌人的游戏。这是一个注重建造，构造攻城器械，激烈的PvP战斗的2D横版卷轴式多人联机游戏。一些灵感来源于Soldat, Minecraft, Dungeon Keeper 与 Ace of Spades.. 游戏目前可运行于Win/Linux/OSX三平台





KAG的作者为Soldat（一款横版多人射击游戏）的开发者。

What can I do in this game?

There are 2 teams in KAG (red vs blue). Both struggle to dominate the other on giant randomly generated 2D maps. Nobody remembers why the war started, but the battle is ferocious and never ending!

KAG中有红蓝两队，在随机生成的地图上相互斗争。


KAG is a Player vs Player game in which you can pick one of 3 classes.

      KAG中有三种职业。






KAG is the first 2D game to introduce classic arcade game mechanics in a real-time 64+ player battlefield.

      KAG支持64+人的实时联机对战。



The Knight is a hero equipped with a sword and shield, and a little too much courage.

The sword allows for quick jabs or massive slashes + combo hits

The shield is used to block enemy strikes and allows knights to give other players

a helping hand up ledges.


骑士使用剑与盾。剑可以快速戳击或是蓄力斩击+连击

盾牌可以用来防御攻击或是当作人肉攻城梯




          The Archer is a swift medieval ninja armed with a bow, quiver full of arrows and a quick wit...

He can hide in grass and behind bodies allowing for deadly ambushes, and can climb walls by stepping on his arrows!

  弓手使用弓箭，可以潜伏在草、尸体背后，并且可以利用箭攀爬各种建筑。还可以自由的爬树并藏在树叶中。


           Builder constructs castle defences and traps.

This includes retracting from enemy bridges, spike traps and dropping huge castle blocks.

    建筑工建造城堡防御设施与陷阱。包括陷阱桥，钉子，与大型塌陷。


Build outpostswhich allow team mates to spawn farther into the battlefield (you need to defend them as well, because they can be captured!)

建造outposts可以设置重生点。







Storm the castle with a catapult! Load it with rocks and destroy the enemy walls!
建造投石机摧毁城墙（甚至可以投掷同伴）




EVERYTHING in KAG is destructible.
KAG中的一切都是可以破坏的


Not only that, but blocks support each other!


Destroying the foundation of a castle will make it collapse in full 2D collapsible physics glory!

破坏根基会引发坍塌。





The current default game mode is Capture the Flag.

This means the objective is to breach the enemy castle and take their flag.

默认游戏模式为CTF（夺旗模式）
