取自自己的两个废案，执行时几首同时播放

  1. playsound minecraft:entity.evoker.prepare_summon master @a ~ ~ ~ 1 2 0
  2. playsound minecraft:entity.firework_rocket.twinkle_far master @a ~ ~ ~ 1 1 0
  3. playsound minecraft:block.enchantment_table.use master @a ~ ~ ~ 1 1 0
复制代码

  1. playsound minecraft:entity.evoker.prepare_summon master @a ~ ~ ~ 0.3 2 0
  2. playsound minecraft:entity.firework_rocket.twinkle_far master @a ~ ~ ~ 0.4 1 0
  3. playsound minecraft:block.enchantment_table.use master @a ~ ~ ~ 1 1 0

  5. playsound minecraft:block.beacon.activate master @a ~ ~ ~ 1 2 0
