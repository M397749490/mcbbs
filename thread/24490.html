正文：



          FishCraftory adds  60 new fish into Minecraft. These fish can be foundas living mob entities  that spawn in specific biomes. The fish are dividedinto four categories based  on which biome(s) they spawn in.
  
          FishCraftory(垂钓物语)  给游戏添加了新的60种鱼类。这些鱼类可以在各种地区群落中找到。不同区域会产生四种不同种类的鱼类。
  
  

          Freshwater Fish  淡水鱼类(Spawn  in all normal biomes except for Swamp,River,  or Ocean)(将会产生在各种地区群落中找到,除了沼泽,河流或者海洋)
          River Fish  河鱼  (Spawn only in River biomes)(只会产生在河流地区)
          Ocean Fish  海鱼  (Spawn only in Ocean biomes)(只会产生在海洋地区)
          Swamp Fish  沼泽鱼类(Spawn  only in Swamp biomes)(只会产生在沼泽地 区)
  
          All fish have a  tendency to avoid and move away from the player ifthe fish is not tamed.  Un-tamed fish will also despawn if the playermoves far enough away but tamed  fish will not. To tame a fish, it must becaught with a special fishing rod  that has either bait or a lure attached.To craft these special rods, you will  first need bait or lures.
  
           所有没有驯服的鱼类都有&#12032;一种远离玩家的趋势。没有驯服的鱼类会在玩家远离&#12032;一段距离时消失,然而驯服了的鱼类则不会。去驯服&#12032;一种鱼类,你需要&#12032;一个特制的鱼竿和鱼饵。你需要&#12032;一些虫饵或假饵去制作这些鱼竿。

  
  
  
          Bait worms can be  found randomly by tilling farmland with a hoe. Thetype of hoe determines the  type of bait worm you will get. Better qualityhoe = better quality bait  worms. Having a higher quality of bait increasesthe rate fish are attracted  to your bobber. Baitworms can only be used tocatch Freshwater and River type  fish. Baitworms stack up to 64.
  
          你可以在用锄头耕地时随机发现虫饵。不同种类的锄头决定了种类的虫饵。更                                                    佳优秀的锄头=更好的虫饵。更好的虫饵更能增加鱼儿被你吸引的几率。虫饵只能用于调取淡水鱼和河流鱼。虫饵可以叠加至64个。
  
&#8226; Poor Baitworm (Black)  - Found with Wooden Hoe.   &#8226;  差的虫饵(黑)-用木制锄头得取
  
&#8226; Common Baitworm (Green) - Found  with Stone Hoe     .&#8226;  &#12032;一般的虫饵(绿)-用石制锄头得取
  
&#8226; Good Baitworm (Blue) - Found  with Iron Hoe     &#8226;  好的虫饵(蓝)-用铁制锄头得取
  
&#8226; Great Baitworm (Purple) - Found  with Diamond Hoe.&#8226;  优秀的虫饵(紫)-用钻石锄头得取
         
&#8226; Super Baitworm (Orange) - Found with  Golden Hoe.&#8226;  超级虫饵(橙)-用黄金制锄头得取

  
          Lures can be  crafted as well. Lures can only be used to catch Oceanand Swamp type fish.  Lures do not stack.
  
          假饵同样可以制作出来。假饵只能被用来钓取海鱼和沼泽鱼类。假饵不能被重叠。      


         &#8226; Minnow Lure  小鱼假饵-  Put  one stick, one red dye, and one bonemeal incraftingmenu.  
                制作:&#12032;一根木棍,&#12032;一个红色染料,&#12032;一个骨粉
  
         &#8226; Crank Lure  中型假饵  - Put one stick, one yellow dye, and one orangedye in crafting menu.
                 制作:&#12032;一根木棍,&#12032;一个红色染料和&#12032;一个橙色染料

          &#8226; Spoon Lure  大型假饵  - Put one iron ingot, one green dye, and one limedye in crafting menu.
                 制作:&#12032;一个铁砖,&#12032;一个绿色染料,&#12032;一个黄绿色染料
  
           &#8226; Jig Lure  重型假饵-  Put  one iron ingot, one orange dye, and one cocoabeans in crafting menu.
                 制作:&#12032;一个铁砖,&#12032;一个橙色染料,&#12032;一个可可豆



           A special set of  lures also exist and cannot be crafted. Instead, theyare dropped from mobs  and will retain some characteristics from theoriginal mob. There is a 10%  chance one of these lures will drop from themob. When you attach one of these  lures to a rod, and successfully catcha fish, you will also get an additional  item.
            还有&#12032;一组不能被制造出的假饵。然而,这些假饵在你杀死各种生物时有10%的几率掉落。这些由生物掉落的特殊假饵可以在你使用时掉落&#12032;一些特别物品。

           To attach bait or  lure to a rod, you will need to make the Tackleboxblock.
  
           你需要&#12032;一个渔具盒 把虫饵或假饵放到渔具上。

      

  
  
  
              After crafting the  Tacklebox, place a regular fishing rod into the firstslot, and your bait or  lure into the second. Press equip to get a newBait/Lure rod. Remember, bait  stacks up to 64, and lures don't stack atall.
              当你制作完成&#12032;一个渔具盒时,将渔具放入第&#12032;一个槽,虫饵或假饵放入第二个槽。点击Equip按钮得到&#12032;一个装好诱饵的渔具。记住,虫饵可以重叠放置,但是假饵不能重叠放置。
  




            You  can also add bait to an existing Bait rod as long as they are thesame type.
  
            只要是同&#12032;一种渔具,你就可以继续添加鱼饵去制作。                                                   
  
  
  
  
            You are now ready  to start fishing! Equip one of the new rods and findsome fish mobs.  (Remember: Bait Rods can only catch Freshwater &River fish and Lure Rods  can only catch Ocean & Swamp fish.)
  
             你现在已经准备好垂钓了!装备好你的渔具然后找到鱼类。(记住:虫饵只能用于调取淡水鱼和河流鱼,假饵只能被用来钓取海鱼和沼泽鱼类)                                          
  
  
  
  


               To cast out the  bobber, press the left mouse button (Attack), andpress the right mouse button  (Use Item) to reel the bobber back in.Nearby fish will eventually become  attracted to the bobber. After a setamount of time, the bobber will hook onto  the fish. (Remember: Betterbait/lure = The faster fish will hook on)
  
               点击鼠标左键去甩杆,点击右键去拉线。附近的鱼类会被鱼饵所吸引,再过&#12032;一段时间就会咬钩(记住:更好的鱼饵,更快的钓鱼速度)                                          
  
  
  
  
  
                 Fish will struggle  if they are hooked and will pull back away from you.On the side, you will see  a indicator for the current tension of your line.Tension only rises when you  reel in (right click). If you reach max tension(red), the line will break and  you'll have to start over again. Bait Rods willbe subtracted by one and Lure  Rods will take damage.
  
                 当鱼咬钩之后,它们会挣扎并且向你反方向拉。你可以在&#12032;一旁看到鱼竿的张力指数。当你拉线时会增加线的张力。当你的鱼线到达最大张力时,鱼线就会断,你只能重新开始。鱼竿会承受&#12032;一点耐久值,鱼饵则会损坏。                                          
  
  
  
  
  
                 Once you pull the  fish close enough and the fish is fatigued, you willsuccessfully catch the  fish! You will also get an experience orb towardsyour fishing level. Every 10  levels gives you less line tension, and the fishwill become fatigued faster which  means faster catching.
  
                 一旦你将鱼拉的很近鱼将会疲劳,这时你就可以成功捕到鱼了。同时你还可以得到&#12032;一些垂钓经验值。每升10级你就可以减少拉线时的张力,鱼类会变得跟容易疲劳而且更容易捕捉。
  
  
  
  
  
  
                    You now have a  tamed fish which you can place back into your ownpool, pond, aquarium, ect.  Right click on a block that is underneathwater to place down your new fish.
  
                      这样你就驯服了&#12032;一种鱼类。你可以把其放入你自己的游泳池,池塘,或是水族馆!右击将鱼类放入水中。

PS：这个mod的一大特色，利用这个可以做成一个像样的水族馆，真的有鱼在其中！                                          
  
  
  
  
  
                    To pick your fish back up, you will need to  craft a fish net. Right clickwith the fish net to pick it up. (Try as you  like, you can't pick up untamedfish.)
  
                   如果你想把鱼捞起来,你需要&#12032;一个渔网。右击使用渔网将鱼捞起。(你可以试试,但是你无法捞起没有驯服的鱼类)                                       




               Tamed fish are breedable. Successfully catch  two fish of the samekind and use the fish flakes item on the both of them.
  
              驯服的鱼类是可以延续后代的。但你抓到两种&#12032;一样的鱼类时,你可以在它们身上使用鱼剂。                                          



You now have an adorable baby fish

现在你就拥有了&#12032;一个可爱宝宝鱼
  


             If you prefer to show off your fish in a  different way, you can craft afish painting.

  

   

  
           如果你想用另&#12032;一种方式展示你的鱼,你可以把他们做成画展出。

安装说明：
1. Download ModLoader and Forge API.
2. Open "minecraft.jar" in your .minecraft folder.
3. Open ModLoader and drag files into the jar.
4. Open Forge and drag files into the jar.
5. Download the Craftory mods you want to install and drag the files into the jar.
6. If using FishCraftory or the RuneCraftory Collection pack, Download Custom Mob Spawner.
7. Drag "CustomSpawner.class" into the jar.
8. To install the fishing sound effects, drag the "resources" folder into your .minecraft folder

5. 最后把 resources 文件放入 .minecraft
6. 开始了！
