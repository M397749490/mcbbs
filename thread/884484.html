MINECRAFT EARTH CLOSED BETA IS ALMOSTHERE
MINECRAFT EARTH的封闭测试即将到来
Sign up now for a chance to play in thenext two weeks!
为了未来两周的游玩机会，现在马上来注册吧！

Ever since we celebrated Minecraft’s 10th birthday by releasing this trailer?, you have been asking three questions with alarming frequency:
自从我们用这段短片来庆祝Minecraft的十周年生日后，你就在以马不停蹄的频率询问着我们下列的三个问题：

  • When can I play Minecraft Earth?
  • 我什么时候能玩到Minecraft Earth？
  • Wait, how do I actually play Minecraft Earth?
  • 等等，怎么才能真正地玩到Minecraft Earth？
  • Do I have to stand next to an Enderman like the girl in that trailer? PLEASE TELL ME I DON’T IT SCARES ME WITH ITS EERIE PURPLE GAZE?!
  • 我是不是必须得像那个短片的女孩那样站在一个末影人身边？请告诉我不会，它会用那双可怕的紫色眼睛盯着我！！！

As is customary when someone (or in this case, a few million people in perfect harmony) asks you a question over and over, you answer. In fact, I’ll answer all three, starting with the second one.
正如通常那样，当某人（或者几百万人异口同声地）反复地问你问题时，你将会回答。事实上，我们都回答了，但是从第二个问题开始的。

If you watched our on-stage live demo from Apple’s WWDC event?, youalready have some idea of how Minecraft Earth works. In a nutshell, you use the near-magic of augmented reality to overlay a beautifully blocky Minecraft world on top of the real one,where you can then build incredible structures together with others, collect these nifty things called tappables and level up to gain new and improved build plates. Easy!
如果你已经观看了我们在苹果开发者大会舞台上演示的demo视频，你应该已经有了一些对Minecraft Earth工作原理的猜测。在一个狭小的空间中，你使用着近乎魔法的AR技术来将美丽的Minecraft世界置于真实世界之上，接着，你可以在这里与其他人建造奇妙的建筑，收集这些被叫做可收集物的精致玩意，并通过升级来获取更新更好的建筑平台。就是这样！

Still not completely clear? Luckily, we’ve created a handy video to illustrate exactly what to expect from the Minecraft Earth beta! We’ve even included actual moving images from said beta (with an impossibly upbeat, yet beautifully pedagogical voice-over by Stephen Scott ofthe Minecraft Earth Design Team, as a fun bonus!)
还是不太明白游戏内容吗？没关系，我们已经做了一个解说视频来向你展示我们想在Minecraft Earth beta版本中包含的内容！我们甚至也展示了一些来自于beta版本的动图（里面还有一个极度乐观，但也非常优美的教学嗓音，它来自Minecraft Earth设计团队的Stephen Scott！与这些动图一起给你带来双倍快乐！）

So, on to the next (previous?) question:when can I play? If you’re really lucky, it could happen in the next two weeks! That’s right, a limited number of playersin a few select cities will be able to Minecraft their world before summer isover!
那么，来看看下一个（上一个？）问题：我什么时候能玩到它？如果你很幸运，它就能在接下来的两周内实现！是的，在夏天结束之前，在几个被选中的城市中有一部分玩家将能Minecraft化他们的世界！

Here are a few important details about theMinecraft Earth closed beta:
这里有一些关于Minecraft Earth封闭测试的重要细节：

  • The closed beta will launch for iOS in the next two weeks, with the Android version following soon thereafter.
  • 在接下来的两周内我们会先发布iOS的封闭测试版本，不久后也会发布安卓版本
  • As with most closed betas, the number of participants will be limited in numbers and locations. This is to make sure our servers are able to keep up with all the exploration, creation and, hopefully, surviving that is going on around the world. To learn about the current availability of the beta, follow Minecraft Earth on Twitter!
  • 与大多数封闭测试版本一样，参与者的位置和数量会被限制。这是为了确保我们的服务器集群能跟上全世界的探索、创造以及所期望的生存。要想知道目前beta版本的可用情况，就在Twitter上关注Minecraft Earth吧！
  • As is also common with beta versions, your progress will occasionally be reset as we test and develop various features of the game.
  • 这也和大多数封闭测试版本一样，在我们测试和开发游戏的各种功能时，你的游戏进度偶尔会被重置。
  • If you are selected to participate in the closed beta (congratulations!), you will receive an invitation email to the email address you have associated with the Microsoft Account or Xbox Live account you submitted in your registration.
  • 如果你被选中参与封闭测试版本（祝贺你！），你在注册中与微软账户或Xbox Live账户关联的邮箱将会收到一封邀请邮件。
  • If you are selected (congrats again!), you will need to play at least once every 7 days. If you don’t, we’ll give your spot to someone else, as space in the beta is very limited.
  • 如果你被选中（再次恭喜你！），你至少需要每隔7天登录游戏一次，否则，你的资格将会被让给其他人，因为测试名额有限。
  • You have to be 18 years or older to participate.
  • 你必须在18岁及以上才能参与测试。

Still not signed up for the beta? It’s not too late! Go here now and enter your information here for a chance to play!
还没有注册beta测试？时间还不算太晚！为了测试机会，现在来这里输入你的信息吧！
