特色：
  • Four new flintlock weapons
  • Two ammo types
  • Unique reloading system
  • Fitting and balanced with the game, not ridiculously OP like most gun mods
  • Taking suggestions from the community and actively looking to improve and update [color=rgb(22, 108, 173) !important][backcolor=transparent !important]the mod
  • Easy, quick [color=rgb(22, 108, 173) !important][backcolor=transparent !important]installation (only ModLoader required)

更新内容
  • v1.6_4
  • Updated to Minecraft 1.6.2
  • Updated to Npatterson11′s [color=rgb(22, 108, 173) !important][backcolor=transparent !important]textures, made specifically for this mod (Feb 15. 2013)
  • Balanced gun damage for revolver and buckshot, and prevented guns from killing boss mobs with one hit
