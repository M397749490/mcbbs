指令结构大概是这样的
entitydata @e[type=Item] {Item:{id:"minecraft:物品ID",Damage:物品损伤值s,Count:物品数量值b,tag:{物品数据标签}}}
例如
/execute @e[type=Item] ~ ~ ~ detect ~ ~-1 ~ iron_block -1 entitydata @e[type=Item,c=1,r=1] {Item:{id:"minecraft:stone",tag:{display:{Name:"Stone"}}}}
检测物品在铁方块上,并执行将物品更改为石头
