介绍 · Introduction
Kill Effects 可以在击杀玩家时显示粒子特效. Kill Effects 不会更改原版的死亡方式且兼容其他插件.
目前有54种粒子特效可供玩家选择.
该插件为DeathEffects的镜像插件

指令 & 权限
~ 管理员 ~
/ke open <player> - killeffects.gui.others - 为玩家打开菜单
/ke clear <player> - killeffects.clear.others - 清空玩家的击杀特效
/ke reload - killeffects.reload - 重载插件


~ 玩家 ~
/ke - killeffects.gui - 打开击杀特效菜单
/ke 2 - killeffects.gui2 - 打开击杀特效菜单 (第二页)
/ke clear - killeffects.clear - 清空击杀特效
/ke <particle> - killeffects.<particle> - 选择击杀特效
节日特效 - killeffects.holiday - 可使用所有节日击杀特效
击杀特效列表 =
flame, heart, rainbow, firework, cloud, lightning, coin, happy, potion, blood, note, flower, chess, tnt, treasure, water, lava, ender, warm, cold, pastel, fish, cookie, snow, smoke, meat, angry, enchanted, sun, thunder, coal, iron, diamond, disco, dragon, dna, emerald, bloodhelix, vortex, nuke, circle, pentagon

节日死亡特效列表 =
