直接上原理：
  1. //give @a minecraft:fishing_rod 1 0 {Unbreakable:1,ench:[{id:19,lvl:1}],display:{Name:"§e§l地雷创造者",Lore:["","§9听说地雷产生的威力高的无法想象 ？ ？","","§5-= 放置地雷 =-","","§9鱼钩上安装了地雷放置器 ， 甩在地上时会自动生成地雷 ， 怪物靠近会爆炸 。 地雷存在30秒 。","","§7Rare Item",""]},Tags:["MineM"],AttributeModifiers:[{AttributeName:generic.attackDamage,Amount:68,Operation:0,UUIDMost:1,UUIDLeast:1,Name:max,Slot:mainhand},{AttributeName:generic.attackSpeed,Amount:10000,Operation:1,UUIDMost:1,UUIDLeast:1,Name:max,Slot:mainhand}]}
复制代码
先给自己一个特殊鱼竿
  1. 高频：/scoreboard players tag @a add MineM {SelectedItem:{tag:{Tags:["MineM"]}}}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @a add MineM {Inventory:[{Slot:-106b,tag:{Tags:["MineM"]}}]}
复制代码
给拿鱼竿的玩家Tag
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=MineM] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[name=未知] ~ ~ ~ particle flame ~ ~ ~ 0 0 0 0 2 force
复制代码
给鱼线加粒子[“未知”是鱼线的名字，MC尿性]
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @e[name=未知] add MineL {OnGround:1b}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[name=未知,tag=MineL] ~ ~ ~ summon area_effect_cloud ~ ~0.1 ~ {Tags:["Mine"],Duration:600}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/kill @e[name=未知]
复制代码
给落地的鱼线召唤地雷，同时Kill掉落地的鱼线
  1. 连锁：/scoreboard players tag @a remove MineM
复制代码
移除玩家tag
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Mine] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[r=3,type=!Player,tag=!Mine,c=1] ~ ~ ~ summon fireball ~ ~1 ~ {direction:[0.0,-1.0,0.0],ExplosionPower:3}
复制代码
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Mine] ~ ~ ~ execute @e[r=3,type=!Player,tag=!Mine,c=1] ~ ~ ~ kill @e[tag=Mine,r=3]
复制代码
怪物靠近时地雷爆炸
  1. 连锁：/execute @e[tag=Mine] ~ ~ ~ particle flame ~ ~0.2 ~ 1 0 1 0 8 force
复制代码
给地雷加粒子
