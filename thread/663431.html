作者的话（介绍）:
What does this mod do?
Adds more organic fuel for the furnace.

You probably know the problem of too many seeds as soon as you got a good wheat farm going. So what to do with the seeds? Too much for feeding chickens... This mod adds a new use: Burn them in a furnace!
Apart from normal wheat seeds you can burn pumpkin or melon seeds, leaves, wheat (all of them give a quarter of the energy you need to cook one Item) and sugar cane (which gives half the energy, just like sticks do).
Best way to burn large quantities of seeds/anything else: connect a hopper to the side of a furnace and place a chest above the hopper: The hopper will automatically feed the fuel you put in the chest into the furnace.

楼主的话（介绍）:
这个插件有什么用的:
增加更多的有机燃料
你在生存有了一个小麦地，你就会有很多种子，那么，如何处理呢，喂鸡？这个MOD增加了一个新的用途，就是：在炉中燃烧
除了普通的小麦种子外,你还可以烧其他有机的植物。
最好就是烧小麦种子咯，就放在那烧，小麦种子没什么用。

南瓜种子≠南瓜

是只有种子，树叶，树苗，甘蔗才能当成有机燃料。
