建立计分板
  1. scoreboard objectives add attack stat.damageDealt
  2. scoreboard objectives add hurt stat.damageTaken
复制代码
指定常量
  1. scoreboard players set #10 hurt 10
复制代码
循环
  1. scoreboard players operation @a[score_attack_min=0] attack /= #10 hurt
  2. title @a[score_attack_min=0] title {"score":{"name":"*","objective":"attack"},"color":"red"}
  3. scoreboard players reset @a[score_attack_min=0] attack
  4. scoreboard players operation @a[score_hurt_min=0] hurt /= #10 hurt
  5. title @a[score_hurt_min=0] actionbar {"score":{"name":"*","objective":"hurt"},"color":"red"}
  6. scoreboard players reset @a[score_hurt_min=0] hurt
