在做1.11.2地图，生怪指令不会打，站内找的minecraft command helper,其他条件都行，只有血量改不了，MCH只调血量的例子：
  1. /summon minecraft:silverfish ~ ~1 ~ {Health:60f}
复制代码
无效  

又找了个增加血量可用的，可这个不知怎么添加其他属性
  1. /summon husk ~ ~1 ~ {Attributes:[{Name:generic.maxHealth,Base:200f}],Health:200.0f}
复制代码

其他帖子推荐找到这个http://minecraft.tools/en/spawn.php，随便设置下属性：
  1. /summon minecraft:husk ~ ~1 ~ {CustomName:HAH,CustomNameVisible:1,Health:100,Fire:0,Attributes:[{Name:"generic.followRange",Base:20},{Name:"generic.movementSpeed",Base:0.4f}],HandItems:[{id:"minecraft:iron_sword",Count:1},{id:"minecraft:shield",Count:1}],ArmorItems:[{Count:1,id:"minecraft:iron_boots"},{Count:1,id:"minecraft:iron_leggings"},{Count:1,id:"minecraft:iron_chestplate"},{id:"minecraft:skull",Damage:3}],ArmorDropChances:[0.35F,0.4F,0.4F,0F]}
