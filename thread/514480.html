原文：



Life Of Cow - A Minecraft Story.
An emotionless cow tells us the story of its life.




Day One - I was spawned.  I live in a forest with my mother and sister.  It is a very nice forest.

Day Two - My sister cow fell into an open pit of lava.  We are not sad, we just continue living.  But deep inside, I am sad my sister is dead.


Day Three - I have seen a human I call 'Steve' wonder around the forest.  He is cutting down trees and breaking the grass we like to eat.  He leaves ugly cobblestone everywhere.

Day Four - Steve has a small garden set up.  He is learning how to grow food, very slowly.

Day Five - Steve has very food in his hands.  Mother and I are hungry, so we follow him.

Day Six - Steve has taken us into his house, and we now live in a very small pen in the garden.  We cannot reach the food that is 2 blocks away from us.  Steve has no idea how to take care of cows.

Day Seven - My mother was pregnant, and she gave birth in the tiny pen.  Her child is very beautiful, and reminds me of my sister when she was tiny, right before she died.

Day Eight - A creeper blew up our fence keeping us from going to where we belong, home.  Mother has decided to go on without me.  I now travel alone.

Day Nine - I sometimes see other cows, but Steve comes and kills them all for food.  Foolish boy.

Day Ten - Steve has deleted his world.  I now live in the back-data of his computer.  I will wait for him to make another world, so I can find him.  So I can end him.

翻译文：


牛的一生 - A Minecraft Story.
一只对万物已经冷漠的牛跟我们简述了他的一生。


第一天 - 我诞生于这个世界了，我与母亲以及妹妹住在树林里，树林很美很温馨。

第二天 - 我妹妹不慎坠入露天的岩浆坑里，虽然表面上母亲和我却表现的没那么伤感，日子依旧继续过着，但是，在心理，我对妹妹的离去很痛心。

第三天 - 我瞧见了一个人在树林里瞎逛，我称他为“Steve”，他大肆破坏树木以及我们所补充饥饿的草，而且还到处留下了难看的原石。

第四天 - Steve建了座很小的菜园，并且正在学习如何种植食物，进度很慢。

第五天 - Steve手中拿着很美味的食物，因为我们太饿了，所以跟在他的后面。

第六天 - Steve把我们带进他的屋子里，然后住在菜园里的一个小围栏中，虽说住在菜园中，但是被围栏所“关押”着所以无法够不到俩格远的食物，况且 Steve还不知道如何照顾牛。

第七天 - 我的母亲怀孕了，并且在围栏里生下了小宝宝，她的宝宝非常漂亮，这使我回想起我妹妹小时候的样子，对，在她死之前。

第八天 - 一只Creeper（苦力怕）把我们的围栏炸毁了，这使我们可以回到我们的归宿，但母亲决定不跟随于我，我现在偶影独游。

第九天 - 我有时会望见其他的牛群，但Steve为了食物，都将他们杀死，鲁莽的男孩。

第十天 - Steve删除了他的世界，现在我在他电脑的后台内过活，我会等待他再次生成个世界，然后找到他，终结他。
