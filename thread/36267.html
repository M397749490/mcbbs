  1. Troubleshooting Communication between Hamachi Clients
  2. Symptoms
  3. You see a yellow triangle with an exclamation mark (a warning icon) next to the name of the peer you want to
  4. communicate with and no traffic is going through between the peers.
  5. Cause
  6. You generally see a warning next to a peer if the security settings block traffic between peers.
  7. Action/Workaround
  8. Click on the warning icon and then click on the description of the problem. This opens the Peer Properties window.
  9. The errors are marked in red.
  10. &#8226; If there is a connection error, on the Settings tab, click the change link next to Traffic. Then change the setting
  11. to Block unsecured or Allow all.
  12. Important: We do not recommend that you select Allow all.
  13. &#8226; If there is an authentication error, the peer is untrusted by your client. Click on the details link next to the error
  14. and then click on the description of the error. Then click Trust to authenticate the peer.

所以基本的起因是安全防护方面的，比如防火墙等过滤机制。
官方这里提供的两个解决办法：
其一，在对等机属性的设置页面，点击通信量右侧的更改，将阻止不可靠的改为全部允许（这种操作其实不安全）
其二，在对等机属性的摘要页面，点击详细信息，如果对方是未被信任的对等机，可以手动点击信任
