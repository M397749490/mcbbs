Stone Torch——石头火把

前期挖矿必备

mod介绍
This mod was made to put cobble stone to use for making torches so it makes the mining trip easier when u run out of torches just throw some cobble together to make a stone stick n some coal/charcoal

可以用鹅卵石（圆石）制作石棒，这样在前期挖矿的时候避免没木头做火把了
