插件指令：
/ultimatetnt
显示插件的版本和作者

/ultimatetnt reload
权限：ultimatetnt.reload
重新读取配置


配置：
  1. #Ultimate TNT config
  2. #by MrMicky
  3. #
  4. #Spigot link: https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/ultimatetnt.49388/

  6. UpdateChecker: true

  8. #TNT will not be modify in worlds below
  9. DisableWorlds:
  10. - 'DisableWorld1'
  11. - 'DisableWorld2'
  12. #TNT爆炸不会发生的世界
  13. #Automatically ignite TNT on place
  14. AutoIgnite: false

  16. #Should TNT have a custom name
  17. CustomName: true

  19. #List of names within which one will be randomly selected
  20. #'%timer' will be replaced by the time left before the TNT explodes
  21. Names:
  22. - '&4Run: %timer'
  23. - '&cBoom in %timer'
  24. #TNT介绍的名称
