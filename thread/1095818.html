动态光源——Dynamic Lighting
Do you want to experience dynamic lighting like using optifine in java? This add-on adds just that experience into minecraft bedrock! This addon’s lighting is not as smooth as optifine but it does the job.
你想体验像在java版optifine中的动态光源吗？这个addon在基岩版中增加了动态光源！这个addon的照明没有没有像optifine那样平滑但是也起到了作用
You can use different items that gives off light to light your surroundings by just holding it in your hands. The dynamic light has a fix light level of 8.
你可以拿着不同的物品来照亮周围的环境，动态光源的固定灯光级别是8
You can now explore dark caves easier if you don’t have enough torches to place.
如果你没有足够的火把，现在也可以更容易的探索黑暗的矿洞了

To wear it hold the item then use it like eating a food.
Make sure there is no armor on your head or it will overwrite it.
拿着它然后像吃食物一样使用它来戴在你的头上
确保你的头上没有头盔，否则会被覆盖掉
说明：
For some reason it does not work with sealantern.

由于一些原因，它不能在sealantern下工作

There are a few bugs where when you sprint jump it does not update quickly enough to remove the light. I recommend to sprint jump less while holding any items that gives off light.
This add-on might interfere with other add-on causing that add-on to not work or this add-on to not work.
存在bug：在你疾跑跳跃时，它更新光源不够快，我建议在拿着发光的物品时少疾跑跳跃
这个addon可能会干扰其他的addon导致其他addon不工作或这个addon不工作
Does not need experimental mode to work that means it can work in realms or servers that has this add-on.
不需要打开实验玩法，这意味着它可以在具有此插件的领域或服务器内工作
