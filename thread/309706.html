大家好~我是十字梦~

这次带了的是【避难所（Sanctuary）】



这张地图属于是末日废墟一类。我个人特别喜欢这种荒凉的美感。

本地图所描绘的场景是裂谷灾难后，世界分裂为一个个空岛，幸

存的人类在这些空岛上重建家园，以抵抗裂谷灾难所造成的影响。

本作为PMC “Head into the clouds”建筑大赛的参赛作品，遗憾的是

本作并没有获奖。同期的作品多为科幻和现代风的，而一些魔幻风格

的作品却在其中脱颖而出，所以大家看到前三作品都是作者瑰丽的

想象（脑洞）的表现。尽管如此本作在我心目中还是非常优秀的，

同时也希望大家能够喜欢这个作品，想要建造类似作品的建筑师也

可以拿去参考学习。对废墟风格建筑情有独钟的盆宇也千万不要错过呐~

原帖作者的话

This is my entry for the PMC Contest: Head into the Clouds


Project Sanctuary was humanity's many efforts to survive the aftermath of the rift. When rifts first started to appear in the world, like in the Nether, it caused similar electrical disturbances. Initially it began as low level electromagnetic interference, primarily affecting electronics. But as the months went on, the effects became stronger. Powerful electric fields around the rifts would pulse, causing iron deposits in the earth to be thrown into the atmosphere. Project Sanctuary was proposed as a fortified habitat that could be insulated from the effects of the electromagnetic pulses, and protect its inhabitants. The only problem was with a world beginning to shatter, there was a limited amount of space that these Sanctuaries could hold. Every major city built as many as they could. Lotteries were held to choose who could stay. When the rift let out a final burst of energy, it destroyed 95% of the existing landmass, causing mass flooding on the remaining 5%. Some of the landmass thrown into the sky held together enough, and some of the Sanctuary towers survived.


A Sanctuary tower had every necessity that a small settlement could need, with a maximum capacity of about 3000 people. Once things seemed to settle, they began to disassemble the roof, and begin building the agriculture tower. This ensured the Sanctuary would always have food and building supplies. All rooms are furnished with basic necessities.


Currently, what is completed is the Sanctuary tower, three partially destroyed bridges, twelve buildings of what remains of the city, and two highways. This is going to be the final major update for the contest. Until the contest ends (on the 30th), i will be searching my entry for mistakes. If you find something that isn't working, let me know, and i will tend to it immediately.


避难所是许多人类在裂谷产生后生存的地方。当裂谷开始出现在世界上，它引起了电磁干扰。最初，它只是低水平的电磁干扰，主要影响电子。但随着时间的推移，影响变得更强。裂谷周围强大的电磁脉冲导致地球的铁矿床被抛到大气层中。避难所项目作为一个坚固的栖息地可以绝缘电磁脉冲的影响，并保护其居民。唯一的问题是世界开始沦陷而这个避难所只能容纳有限的居民。许多大城市进他们所能的建造避难所，用抽奖的方式来决定谁能留下。当裂谷发出最后的能量爆发，它摧毁了95%的土地，并淹没了剩下的5%。一些陆地被抛上天空，一些避难所幸存了下来。
