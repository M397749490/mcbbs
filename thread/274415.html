介绍：
  你可以用本插件给MC中任何一个物品添加药水效果

命令和权限
命令 介绍 权限
/aieadd 药水效果 等级 给手中物品添加效果 AIE.add
/aieadd 粒子效果 给手中物品添加粒子效果 如火焰 同上
/aieremove 效果移除一个效果 AIE.remove
/aieclear 清理效果和LORE AIE.clear


可用效果
药水效果
"SPEED", "SLOW", "FAST_DIGGING", "SLOW_DIGGING", "INCREASE_DAMAGE", "HEAL", "HARM", "JUMP", "CONFUSION", "REGENERATION", "DAMAGE_RESISTANCE", "FIRE_RESISTANCE", "WATER_BREATHING", "INVISIBILITY", "BLINDNESS", "NIGHT_VISION", "HUNGER", "WEAKNESS", "POISON", "WITHER", "HEALTH_BOOST", "ABSORPTION", "SATURATION"
粒子效果
"ENDER_SIGNAL", "MOBSPAWNER_FLAMES", "POTION_BREAK", "SMOKE"

使用的时候注意去掉双引号
