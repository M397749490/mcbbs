16-03-03 作者修改版本Graves-1.0.0
Hello everyone.
Finally I got some free time to fix last issues in 1.8.9 version. So here it is: Gravestone mod - Graves 1.0.0 for Minecraft 1.8.9
As you may see its version is 1.0.0 - since Minecraft 1.8.* this mod splitted on 2 parts:
"Graves" part - only graves(at death) and nothing more
"Extended"(its a dev name) - all another features(mob spawn, fog, memorials, structures, mobs, corpses and etc)
As for other mod developers - I add an API which will allow you to do next things:
prevent placing of some items in graves at player death
add any additional items(for example from your custom inventory slots) in graves at death
prevent graves generation in some cases
generate graves at your own mobs death
allow to use your swords as gravestones
