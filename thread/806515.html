提问写一下版本号又不会怀孕

假方块：
首先，生成这个东西
1.8
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~ ~ {TileID:<方块数字ID>,Time:1}
复制代码

1.9
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~ ~ {Block:"<方块字母ID>",Time:1}
复制代码

1.12
  1. /summon falling_block ~ ~ ~ {Block:"<方块字母ID>",Time:1}
复制代码

1.13
  1. /summon falling_block ~ ~ ~ {BlockState:{Name:"<方块字母ID>"},Time:1}
复制代码
然后，高频：
1.8
  1. /entitydata @e[type=FallingSand] {Time:1}
复制代码

1.12
  1. /entitydata @e[type=falling_block] {Time:1}
复制代码

1.13
  1. /execute as @e[type=falling_block] run data merge entity @s {Time:1}
复制代码


流沙：
首先，生成这个东西
1.8
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~ ~ {TileID:12,Time:1}
  2. /setblock ~ ~ ~ web
复制代码

1.9
  1. /summon FallingSand ~ ~ ~ {Block:"minecraft:sand",Time:1}
  2. /setblock ~ ~ ~ web
