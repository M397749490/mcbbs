!           前言          !


这是一款可以为我的世界加上温度的独特插件。你需要看天气穿衣服，和热/冷饮，建房子，制造篝火来保持温暖，还有更多！
  1. A unique plugin for the minecraft that adds temperature.
  2. You will need to dress depending on the weather, use refreshing or warming drinks. Build house, kindle a fire to keep warm and etc!
