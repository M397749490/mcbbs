Welcome everybody to PARKOUR-ALOT! with da same name, gosh... This map promises 5 unique and catchy levels, very accurate buildings, and it's NOT so HARD.
It's a medium-easy parkour, and if you have problems with finishing a level there's always a skip button even tho those are very well hidden.
