权限
  • backpacks.* ||所有
  • backpacks.help ||/bp查看帮助
  • backpacks.littleBackPack || 下面都是XX背包的权限
  • backpacks.normalBackPack || for the NormalBackPack
  • backpacks.enderBackPack || for the EnderBackPack
  • backpacks.workbenchBackPack || for the WorkbenchBackPack
  • backpacks.enchantingBackPack || for the EnchantingBackPack
  • backpacks.furnaceBackPack || for the FurnaceBackPack
  • backpacks.give || 给予背包
  • backpacks.reload ||重读插件

PS.所有的合成方法都可以在配置内更改！
