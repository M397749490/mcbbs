Readme:
Read this, its going to tell you how to install this mod.
Should be compatible with most mods. Please contact me if issues arise.
This is my first mod, and was quite a pain in the ass to create. Many thanks to henry the coder, and Barry Burrd, the author of "Java for dummies"
~How to install~
Requirements: Basic knowledge of computers, (moving files, following directions)
Step 1: Open ToolsPlus_by_Warhawker. You should see 10 png files, a net folder, and 2 class files. (Ignore the text files.)
Step 2: Open your minecraft bin folder. (It contains your minecraft.jar)
This may be a bit confusing for PC users, please refer to an outside tutorial.
Step 3: make a backup of your minecraft.jar file.
Step 4: Convert the original minecraft.jar to "minecraft.zip"
Step 5: Open, "minecraft.zip" you should see 800 or so class files and assorted random things.
Step 6: in the folder "Toolsplus_by_Warhawker" Copy everything EXCEPT the "net" folder.
Step 7: PASTE the copied items into the minecraft.zip. it will ask you to replace the files, say yes to all.
Step 8: now OPEN the "net" folder in "Toolsplus_by_Warhawker" then OPEN
"minecraft" (Toolsplus_by_Warhawker --> net -->minecraft
Step 9: COPY the "src" folder
Step 10: OPEN the "net" folder in your minecraft.zip. (the one with the 800 classes)
then open "minecraft" you should see "client" and "isom" folders.
Step 11: PASTE "src" in. (the src folder you downloaded in my mod)
Step 12: IMPORTANT!! DELETE "META-INF" Folder located in your minecraft.zip
Step 13: Almost there, back out into your "bin" folder for minecraft.
Step 14: RENAME minecraft.zip to minecraft.jar.
Run, and enjoy
If you have a problem, please post on my planet minecraft page. Also, If you got the mod working correctly, and you enjoy it, next time you visit my page, hit that up arrow :D (or leave some feedback. I like feedback.)
