这个是要求:
We'll be writing a poem today, a lyric poem. Here are your requirements:
  • Topic: something you are learning about in one of your classes
  • Must be a lyric poem; cannot tell a story.
  • Must have a creative title. Don't call it "Math".
  • Must include at least one figure of speech (simile, metaphor, personification, or hyperbole) and HIGHLIGHT it in YELLOW.
  • Must include at least one example of a sound device (assonance, alliteration, onomatopoeia, repetition) and HIGHLIGHT it in another color.
  • Does NOT need to have end rhyme or meter, but if you use it, you must use it consistently.
  • Must be at least 10 lines long and not longer than 30 lines.

Your first draft will be due on Friday.



这个是我写的:

Math Book for Sale!

Algebra is difficult to understand.
Sometimes a letter is a number,
But not all numbers are letters

Graphing a function is confusing.
Sometimes it's hard to find a point,
And what is a y-interceptanyways?

Word problems I cannot understand.
Words mean so many different things;
They belong in a song or sad story.

Homework... every day and weekends?
Not just one or two or three problems,
But one, two, or three pages at least!

Mybrain is not like a computer.
I need time to relax and play my games.



大家看看写的怎么样?
