[stone]The Build 建造[stone]


原文：
This is one of my first attempts on making a survival fantasy island, trying to combine topographic and natural elements that I really like. Fields, steep slopes and cliffy coasts, these features make Feronia an island of contrasts, with a hot and dry south and a wet north.

The size of the island is 1500x1500 blocks aproximately, something small, just to get used once again to making maps (it was a long time ago I last made a map).

As always, I have only used WorldPainter to do the map, keeping it "old school" terraforming style. The results are pretty decent, but I am currently working on something with much better quality and, of course, better size.

Keep in mind that you can use this map for whatever purposes you want, but don't claim it as yours. If you edit the map, always give me the proper credit with a link back to this post.

I want to give a big hug to everyone who has supported me (and distracted me) during this period of time with many duties. As I said, good-quality content is about to come, so thanks subscribers for staying by me, and see you soon !

Now have fun !


這是我第一次做生存夢幻島，我嘗試結合我喜歡的地形和自然元素。田地，陡坡和峭壁嶙峋的海岸線。這些特點讓Feronia成為一個南部炎熱乾燥、北部潮濕的小島。

島的大小是1500x1500方塊，並不大。我只是在習慣製作地圖（上次做地圖已是很久以前）。

與往常一樣，我只用WorldPainter做地圖，保持它“老派”的地形風格。結果是相當不錯的，但我目前正在思索如何提高質量的，增加大小。

記住哦，你可以隨心所欲地使用這個地圖出，但是不要說是你做的（剽竊）。如果編輯或更改它，請把那個鏈接放到下方回復處。

我想給大家一個大大的擁抱，感謝大家在這段時間一直以來的支持。正如我想說的，高質量的地圖即將到來，另外感謝訂閱我的朋友，再見！

玩得開心！

[powered_minecart]The Installations 存档安装[powered_minecart]

Save存档(正版用户):关闭Minecraft，在Windows开始菜单中的搜索框中输入%appdata%/.minecraft，然后按下Enter（回车）键。把从百度网盘下载回来的地图存档解压缩放到.minecraft\saves文件夹里面。
Schematics存档：这类型需要MCEdit。下载完成后解压到桌面，关闭Minecraft，双击mcedit.exe，点下方import按钮，打开你下载的文件到你需要放的地方，最后点import，按Ctrl+s保存，Ctrl+w关掉存档，点Quit退出。
