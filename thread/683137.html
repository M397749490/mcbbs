简介 Description
This is a quick map with a mix of easy and difficult levels. The goal is to find the button in 10 different stages.
Each stage has a different theme ranging from rustic houses and castles to minions and youtube! You you get to enjoy the builds.
The button is not easy to find because the map might troll you and there is a little bit of parkour. Just try not to rage!
Are you ready to take up a challenge? ;)

这是一个快速制作的地图，混杂着一些简单和有一点难度的关卡。你的目标是要找到10个在不同关卡的按钮。
每一关都会有不一样的主题，从乡村小屋和城堡到小黄人还有Youtube！你会很喜欢这些建筑的。
按钮不是很容易找，因为地图可能会坑你然后还有一点点跑酷。保持冷静，不要发怒！
你准备好挑战了吗？

规则 Rules
