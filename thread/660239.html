Dark Souls Legacy
黑暗之魂
——————————————————————
这是一款由《黑暗之魂3》作画风的1.8材质包
这款材质包更改了原版minecraft 90%的纹理材质，且更新工作目前仍然进行中，许多方块具有不同的随机纹理，画风偏向中世纪黑暗，适用于1.8版本



■作者原文■


The extremely detailed Texture Pack and use Connected Textures. All textures takes from the game by a dumping and adapted to Minecraft. I change about 90% of all block textures (work still in process), a lot of objects. Many blocks has a different textures for different sides (sides, top, bottom) and consist of 4-16 pieces.
With this texture pack you can build your own Lordran. At least: Undead Asylum, Firelink Shrine, Undead Burg, Undead Parish, Sen's Fortress, Anor Londo, Demon Ruins.
