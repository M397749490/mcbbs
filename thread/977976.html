A FOREST GETAWAY
森林度假区
Designed by Antrelial
由Antrelial设计
     Is that a dragonfly we see? Tucked within a lush canopy of trees, this sprawling hideaway features curved staircases, tall towers, and an abundance of windows to gaze upon the moody blue landscape outside. Antrelial has spent the last 3 years incorporating various 3D programs to add lighting, volumetrics, and effects to give his Minecraft scenery life.



     我们是不是看到了蜻蜓？蜿蜒盘曲的楼梯，高高的塔楼以及大量的窗户是这个藏匿在森林树冠之下建筑的特点；透过其中，我们可以鸟瞰外界葱茏的蓝色风景。Antrelial花了三年的时间利用了各种3D程序添加光源，光束，特效；而这，给予了他的建筑蓬勃的生命力。
