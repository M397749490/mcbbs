看见别人在刷怪笼里召唤出各种各样的穿装备甚至带效果的僵尸和骷髅，有了个想法：末影人是否能够拥有带装备的效果呢？经过反复实验，我已经证实了一点：
用这种方法召唤出的末影人，看似是没有拿装备的，实际上会具备拿装备的效果。


代码：
  1. /setblock ~ ~1 ~ minecraft:mob_spawner 0 replace
  2. {EntityId:Enderman,SpawnData:{ActiveEffects:[{Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1},
  3. {Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1},
  4. {Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1},
  5. {Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1},
  6. {Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1},
  7. {Id:0,Amplifier:0,Duration:1}],
  8. Equipment:[{id:276,Count:1,tag:{ench:[{id:20,lvl:1},{id:20,lvl:1},{id:20,lvl:1},{id:20,lvl:1},{id:20,lvl:1},{id:20,lvl:1}]}},
  9. {id:0,Count:1,tag:{ench:[{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1}]}},
  10. {id:0,Count:1,tag:{ench:[{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1}]}},
  11. {id:0,Count:1,tag:{ench:[{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1},{id:0,lvl:1}]}},
  12. {id:0,Count:1,AttributeModifiers:[{Operation:0,UUIDLeast:1,UUIDMost:1,Amount:950.0,AttributeName:generic.attackDamage,Name:Attack_Glass},
  13. {Operation:0,UUIDLeast:2,UUIDMost:2,Amount:950.0,AttributeName:generic.knockbackResistance,Name:Knockback_Glass},
  14. {Operation:0,UUIDLeast:4,UUIDMost:4,Amount:990.0,AttributeName:generic.Name:Health_Glass}]}],
  15. Attributes:[{Name:generic.attackDamage,Base:1},
  16. {Name:generic.followRange,Base:45},
  17. {Name:generic.knockbackResistance,Base:1},
  18. {Name:generic.movementSpeed,Base:0.225},
  19. {Name:generic.maxHealth,Base:1000}],
  20. CustomName:"FireEnderman",CustomNameVisible:1},
  21. SpawnCount:1,SpawnRange:6,RequiredPlayerRange:256,Delay:7,MinSpawnDelay:7,MaxSpawnDelay:120,MaxNearbyEntities:20}
复制代码
followRange为侦测范围。就像僵尸探测村民的范围。movementSpend是速度，实际上0.3就有些偏快了。attackdamage应该是攻击伤害，填太高了会死的很惨...276id(钻石剑）中，20id为火焰伤害，就像附魔书附魔后的效果。这就等于是拿隐形装备的末影人。
也可以给它们加上头盔之类的，可以使末影人受到的伤害变少，但是看不到。（也算是安德比较烂的一个帖子吧，就是刷怪笼里的指令，当时就是复制下，以后我改一下）
