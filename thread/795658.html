TigerReports by MrTigreroux
[MySQL & BungeeCord support]
[Discord support by installing TigerReportsSupports plugin]


指令:
/report <player> (reason)举报一个特定的玩家.
/reports reload重新启动插件.
/reports打开所有的举报菜单.
/reports #<report id>打开特定玩家的举报菜单.
/reports user <player>打开数据菜单中的一个特定的玩家.
/reports stopcooldown <player>取消冷却时间的一个玩家.
/reports notify改变一个举报的状态通知给 (管理人员).
/reports archiveall举报标记为完成.
/reports archives显示所有存档的举报.
/reports deleteall删除所有举报的存档.

接下来是头疼的权限:  #权限汉化我会在之后发布
tigerreports.reportAllows to report a player with /report (not needed if PermissionRequired is disabled in config.yml).
tigerreports.report.exemptPrevent other players from reporting us.
tigerreports.staffAllows to see and process reports.
tigerreports.staff.teleportAllows to teleport to participants of report.
tigerreports.staff.archiveAllows to archive reports.
tigerreports.staff.archive.autoAllows to automatically archive when processing a report.
tigerreports.staff.deleteAllows to delete reports.
tigerreports.staff.advancedAllows to manage important and done reports and see the advanced data of the reports.
tigerreports.staff.*Gives all staff permissions.
tigerreports.manageAllows to reload the plugin and change statistics of players.
tigerreports.*Gives all permissions.
