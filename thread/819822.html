简单的想法是用雪球撞击火球
execute @e[type=minecraft:snowball,tag=!kill] ~ ~ ~ summon minecraft:fireball ~ ~ ~ {direction:[0d,0d,0d],power:[0d,-0.1d,0d]}
kill @e[tag=kill]
scoreboard players tag @e[type=minecraft:snowball] add kill
