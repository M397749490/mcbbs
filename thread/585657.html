如果你是指用工作台的话，没办法，只能用mod
但是用指令方块可以用发射器来代替工作台，比如这样：
  1. execute @p ~ ~ ~ testforblock ~ ~-1 ~ minecraft:dropper 1 {Items:[{Slot:0b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:1b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:2b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:3b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:4b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:5b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:6b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:7b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b},{Slot:8b,id:minecraft:string,Count:1b}]}
