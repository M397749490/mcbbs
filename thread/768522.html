; Base kinetic generation factors - increase for higher kinetic energy yield.
配置文件这里，修改的不是发电量，是倍率，自己调试试吧。
; Base kinetic generation factors - increase for higher kinetic energy yield.
[balance / energy / kineticgenerator]
water = 1.0
wind = 1.0
manual = 1.0
steam = 1.0
electric = 1.0
