我只发精品材质！
================================

Hello everyone,

this is "my" first texture pack. Actually it's the Vanilla texture pack resized to 256x with bumpmaps, and a few filters to make it look ... good.
Currently it retextures everything.
It might look a little bit darker than the default textures, which is due to the bumpmaps I used.

There also are 64x and 128x versions of the pack, those are based upon the 256x version and are downscaled, using different filters to keep the textures sharp.

高清材质都需要用到
MCPatcher HD fix
点击下载
使用方法
打开mcpatcher在第一栏中选择我的世界bin文件夹里的minecraft.jar
然后直接点击下面Patch
安装完成后
直接把材质包放入我的世界里的texturepacks文件夹
打开游戏Mods and Textrue packs选项
点击你的的材质包
会卡一会
过后直接点Done
完成啦
