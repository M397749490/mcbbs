作者: Chelover_C60    时间: 2019-9-28 11:44
建立计分板
  1. /scoreboard objectives add timer dummy
复制代码
生成辅助用盔甲架
  1. /summon armor_stand ~ ~ ~ {Marker:1b,NoGravity:1b,Invisible:1b,Tags:["zombiemarker"]}
复制代码
循环激活
  1. tp @e[tag=zombiemarker] @e[tag=zombie]
  2. scoreboard players tag @e[tag=zombiemarker] add dead
  3. execute @e[tag=zombie] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players tag @e[tag=zombiemarker] remove dead
  4. execute @e[tag=dead] ~ ~ ~ scoreboard players remove @s timer 1
  5. execute @e[tag=dead,score_timer=0] ~ ~ ~ summon zombie ~ ~ ~ {Tags:["zombie"]}
  6. scoreboard players set @e[tag=dead,score_timer=0] timer 100
