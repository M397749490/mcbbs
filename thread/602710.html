为什么要叫狼狗？

This is Thistle. She sits at my feet while I play Minecraft. She has a lot in common with the Minecraft wolves, specifically: intelligence. The cats beat her up and steal her bed.


Since I named my first pack after a dog, I decided to name my second after a dog as well - a much, much larger breed.
