本帖最后由 bobby825 于 2016-1-18 21:58 编辑

Minecraft PE（@MCPE_News)推特截图：
MCPE 新闻：在0.14.0版本中一些旧的移动设备将不被支持。IOS 6,IPOD touch 4g，等等
（来自：https://twitter.com/MCPE_News/status/688161994038317057）
MCPE新闻网：

Mojang has gone out of their way to keep the support of older devices – in order to keep as many players being able to play Minecraft Pocket Edition as possible.
Mojang已经用自己的方式为一些旧的移动设备进行支持，以至于玩家可以正常玩PE。但是现在不支持咯！
What devices will not be effected?
哪些设备不会被影响？
-iOS 7 +
-iPod Touch 5g, 6g
-iPad Mini 1,2,3,4
-iPhone 4s,5,5s
-iPhone 6, 6+ | 6s, 6+
IOS7+
iPod Touch 5g, 6g
iPad Mini 1,2,3,4
iPhone 4s，5,5s
iPhone 6,6+|6s，6+
Which iOS devices will lose support?
哪些IOS会被影响？
-iPad 1
-iPod touch 4g
iPad 1和iPod Touch 4g
Neither the iPad 1 or iPod touch 4g can upgrade past iOS 6, which is losing support for Minecraft PE. If you’re on iOS 6 and play MCPE, you’re going to be required to upgrade your device in order to play.
If you use Android, it varies depending on device. Google handles versions much differently, and most are currently supported by Minecraft Pocket Edition.
Hope you’re all looking forward to the upcoming 0.14.0 update!
不管是IPAD1或者IPOD TOUCH 4G升级IOS6都会失去MCPE的支持。如果你是在IOS6上玩MCPE，你就需要立刻升级你可耐的IOS版本！
如果你用安卓版本，根据不同的而定。Google*版本不同的是，大多数是支持MCPE的~
恩，希望大家都期盼着即将到来的0.14.0的更新咯！
（http://mcpenews.com/ios-6-support-drop-minecraft-pe/）
