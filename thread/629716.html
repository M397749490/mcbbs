介绍

Adds a crafting recipe that allows you to craft 5x Rotten Flesh into 1x Patchwork Flesh, and a smelting recipe that allows you to smelt Patchwork Flesh into Leather. Experience gained from smelting is 0.35, equivalent to cooking raw beef into steak.
Also optionally (disabled by default) adds a single smelting recipe that allows you to smelt Rotten Flesh directly into Leather. Experience gained from smelting is the minimum allowed (0.1, same as Cobblestone --> Stone). Turn this on in YALSM.cfg if you want it.
Versions for 1.8 and above need airbreathercore.
If you're on Forge 1.9, check the Forge build number to figure out what version I'm officially supporting (this matters more for airbreathercore, and mainly just if you use YAFM too, but nonetheless this is how I've tested it):
Builds 1804 and below require no higher than version 2.1.5 of YALSM, and no higher than 1.0.1 of airbreathercore.
Builds 1805 and above (tested up to 1865) require no lower than version 2.1.6 of YALSM, and no lower than 1.0.2 of airbreathercore.
