Credit goes to Scuttles ( ) first and foremost for the idea of a 256 texture pack and to the artists who contributed to his pack, as some of this pack remains unchanged from his, and I do not know the individual contributors. Most of the terrain.png is my own work except for a few things I haven't gotten to yet. For the time being, the textures that aren't mine are taken from the LB Photorealism Pack.
Also, I give thanks to Grugsy ( ), who helped save me from insanity by showing me a way to save time when creating moving textures. Not that it was a fast process after that, but it was comparably zippier.

Some screenshots of the pack in action on my server! [screenshots from version 1.01]
