如何安装
单人
如果你没有modloder的话，先下载 ModLoader


  • 备份你的 minecraft.jar
  • 需要 Risugami's ModLoader, 因此先装了它吧。【话说pd的有这个吧。。。前面的人名有什么特别的意义么】
  • 把在 "Files for mods folder" 的所有文件复制到 .minecraft/mods/ 文件夹
  • 删 META-INF。
  • (Optional) Install the HD textures to put them in your texture pack in the right folders or replace them with the standard ones in the WeaponMod.zip.这个他是说的材质，放到texture文件夹或者weaponmod.zip里【不确定】

A javelin
A javelin to throw at chickens! You can throw it by holding right mouse button while holding it. It does the more damage than arrows, but goes less far and has a large deviation. You don't need feathers to craft them, or a bow to shoot them.
If you fall downwards and throw the javelin downwards, it will have a critical hit. Same as when aiming upwards.

Don't mistake them for iron or stone spears.

A musket
A musket to shoot back at skeletons! The use is the same as a bow and arrow. The musket uses musket rounds to shoot and these do very much damage. You can also attach a bayonet to it, so you can stab opponents too. You can only shoot a limited amount of musket rounds, then the musket will break.
After you shot the round you need about 2 seconds of reload time.
Holding right mouse button reloads it, and when reloaded, it increases aim.
The musket is crafted with a wooden part and an iron part.

Throwable dynamite

A flail to swing at pigmen!
Right click to throw it. The ball will follow you where you go, right clicking it again will swing it.
If the ball is on fire (by lava) and you hit a mob, it will be set on fire too!
You can make one with wood, stone, iron, diamond and gold.
