REMEMBERING PENDAR2
追忆 PENDAR2

The Minecraft community come together forsomeone truly special
Minecraft玩家相聚，只为那个特别的人


This is very different from the kind ofstory we usually publish on Minecraft.net. I should warn you that it’s a verysad story, but also an inspiring one. It truly shows what the Minecraftcommunity are capable of at their best.
这个故事的风格和我们平常在Minecraft.net上发布的十分不同。或许我应该先提醒你这是一个悲伤的故事，但是你要知道，“沉舟侧畔千帆过，病树前头万木春”。这反而恰恰证明了我们的Minecraft社区有能力去展现他们最为强大的生命力。


Bryan, better known in the Minecraft worldas Pendar2, was the creator of the Emenbee Minecraft Server. Founded way backin 2011, this hugely popular community server would see over *1500 concurrentplayers* at its peak, and over a million unique visitors over the subsequentseven years. Those are amazing numbers, the kind you only see if you’re aserver moderator of great skill and care.
Bryan，（更广为人知地）在Minecraft的世界里被称为Pendar2，是Emenbee 服务器的创建者。建立于2011年，这个庞大且火爆的社区服务器同时在线玩家一度达到1500人；随后的七年期间，超过*一百万名*来自不同地方的访客来到了这个世界。这是个叹为观止的数字；常人自然无福看到，只有成为那名拥有高超技术和对服务器关切之至的管理员才能有暇看到。


But late last year, Bryan shut down theserver. He’d been suffering from cancer and the server was becoming impossibleto maintain. Sadly, Bryan recently passed away. He was “a genius, acompassionate person, and our hero,” his friend Mod_Masta wrote on the Emenbee website (the fullstatement is well worth reading – it’s a beautiful tribute to his friend).
但是在去年的晚些时候，Bryan不得不关掉了服务器——那时的他已经身患癌症，服务器自然也无力继续维护下去了。他是 “一个天才，一个富有同情心的人，我们心目中的英雄”，他的朋友Mod_Masta在Emenbee网站写道（整篇文章值得一读——这是他对一名挚友的悼词）。


But this was far from the only tribute madeto Bryan. Emenbee players have returned to the server to transform its hub intoa memorial for him. Just look at the staggering amount of signs and messagesplayers have left:
但是这还远远不是所有对Bryan的悼词。Emenbee的玩家们纷纷回到服务器，将服务器的大厅变成了一片纪念堂。瞥一眼下面那多的得难以置信的告示牌和悼词吧，那是玩家们留下的记与念：

This article took forever to write, becauseI couldn’t stop wandering around the hub, trying to read them all. Some of themare sweet, some are funny, and some of them tell amazing stories of friendshipsformed in a server that felt like home, thanks to Bryan’s tireless efforts tomake it that way. He clearly inspired a great many people in the Minecraftcommunity, and through their tributes to him, they’ve inspired me right back.
这篇文章将会永远地写下去，因为我没法在大厅中停下我漫游的脚步，尝试去阅览全部的悼言。有些悼言是甜美的，有些则是令人捧腹的，还有那令人神往的友谊：在这个服务器里，像家一样温馨；感谢Bryan不知疲倦的的辛劳，成功地做到了这一点。 显然，他的经历启发了Minecraft社区里很多人，而后通过他们悼念，他们又给了我一些启示。


Rest in peace, Bryan. On behalf of everyoneat Mojang, thank you for the incredible thing you built here.
安息吧，Bryan。谨代表Mojang的全体人员，感谢你在这里做出如此惊为天人的事物。
