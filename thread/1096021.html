我的世界地下城——方块
his addon adds blocks from Dungeons into Minecraft. There are a lot of blocks to choose from to complete your build. It’s now easier than ever to recreate the Camp, the Nether Portal, and other builds that caught your eye while going through a level!
这个addon增加了地下城方块到我的世界中。有许多方块可供选择以完成建造，现在比任何时候都更容易建造营地，地狱门和其他建筑来吸引你的眼睛


增加物品
-Soil with Grass
There are currently 4 blocks for the Soil with Grass series:
  • Soil (0% Grass)
  • Soil with Some Grass
  • Soil with More Grass
  • Soil Grass Block (100% Grass)
Each block has a different amount of grass on them. Dirt in Dungeons has a brighter and smoother texture than dirt in vanilla Minecraft. This is why I added Soil!
-带草的土壤
带草土壤系列目前有4个方块：

  • 土壤（0%草）
  • 带草的土壤
  • 多草的土壤
  • 草方块（100%草）
每个方块都有不同数量的草。地下城里的泥土比我的世界里的有更亮更光滑的纹理。这就是为什么我添加泥土

注意事项 Things to note
In 1.16, there is a bug where custom blocks from addons do not show up in the creative inventory. Therefore, I have added items that you can use to place the new blocks. They can be found in the inventory. There is 1 item for every block added. Because they are items, they appear 2D in your hand. There is nothing I can do about that! These items are temporary additions. They will be removed when Mojang fixes the bug that causes custom blocks to not appear in the creative inventory.
在1.16，有一个bug，来自addon的自定义方块不会出现在创造背包中。对此，我添加了可以用来放置新方块的项，它们可以在背包中找到，每一个新增的方块占据一项，因为它们是物品，所以他们在你手上的时候是平面的，对此我无能为力！这个是暂时添加的，当MOJANG修复了这个bug后它们将被移除。
