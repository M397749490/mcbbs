Valentine's Day Theme Park







介绍:
—这张地图是为PopularMMOs和GamingWithJen打造的，但是任何人都可以自由愉快地游玩这个地图

—这个地图是一个结合了不同的迷你游戏和建筑在一个巨大的情人节主题公园！到处都是游乐设施、商店、雕像和小游戏！

—很明显这是为情人节所打造的地图，所以在公园里的一切都是情人节主题的：-）

—这是我们新团队的第一张地图



游玩信息:
这个地图是运行在1.12.2版本上的,使用其它版本运行该地图可能会产生错误

这个地图有专用的材质包,它需要使用高清修复来使用,这个地图在没有高清修复的情况下可以正常运行但是建议你使用该材质包来获取完整的游戏体验

这个地图建议两个以上的玩家游玩,但是这个地图也有单人游玩的小游戏

请使用生存模式来游玩

每个小游戏结束后都会有小奖励哦，这些奖励可以在村民那换取物品和其它炫酷的小饰品

重要提示:当你第一次进入该地图时请确保地图的游戏规则doFireTick是关闭的,直到你进入有燃烧结构的地图部分，我建议你在地图可能被火灾摧毁之前，消灭火焰和找到一个按钮(可能是连接带有灭火的命令的命令方块)

此外，在该地图的公园部分外可能会有一些惊喜(至于是啥你们自己探索吧~)

—我们是新组建的团队：D

原文:

INFO:
- This map was made for PopularMMOs and GamingWithJen, but anyone else is free to play it as well!
- This is a combination of different mini-games and buildings all put together in one huge theme park! There are rides, shops, statues, and games all around!
- This was (obviously) made for Valentine's Day, so everything inside the park is Valentine-themed :)
- This is our first map as a new team

PLAYER INFO
- This map is in 1.12.2
- There is a custom texture-pack made for this map, but it does require you download OptiFine 1.12.2 to see certain models. The map is playable without OptiFine, but we recommend you use for the full experience.
- There are many games that involve two or more players, but there are some single player games too.
- Played in Survival Mode
- Set "Block" volume to 100 and Master Volume to at least 1
- The mini games have rewards at the end, and you can purchase items and other cool trinkets with those rewards.
- Important: When you first join the game, make sure to have the gamerule doFireTick false until you enter the burning structures, and I recommend you do Wipe out and the FTB (Find the Button) before the burning structures or else parts of the map might get destroyed due to fire.

In addition, there are some hidden treasures around the map. Look around!

ABOUT THE MAPMAKERS

- We're a newly formed team :D
- If you would like to learn more about us, there's lots of information about us in the map!
