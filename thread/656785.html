简介

[size=14.6667px]你想把液体叠加放在背包吗？这个插件可以让你在背包叠放液体！你可以放10桶特仑苏在背包内（不是打广告！）只占用一格位置，这个插件不大，却非常有用~
（官方：[size=14.6667px]Ever wanted to transport large amounts of liquids in survival mode but could bare the fact that buckets with something in them cant stack? ever wanted to just flood a whole place but dont have the inventory space for it? a plugin that allows you to drink 10 gallons of milk without wasting inventory space? or a plugin that is lightweight and resource friendly that allows you to trasport large amounts of liquids?, Well, this plugin is just for you! with big buckets you can create buckets with any size you want!）
[size=14.6667px]指令
/ bigbucket <大小> <液体> -给玩家与指定大小和盛有液体桶。
/ bbsetlimit <newlimit> -设置可以创建的最大尺寸桶
/ bbreload -重新加载config.yml文件。
（液体：water/empty/lava/milk）
权限
bigbuckets.create -使用/ bigbuckets命令的权限。
bigbuckets.unlimited -让你与你想创建任意大小创建桶。
bigbuckets.setlimit -允许/ bbsetlimit命令的用法。
bigbuckets.reload -允许/ bbreload命令的用法。
