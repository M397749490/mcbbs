Monas Roth – Industry’s Rise Map


——
介绍（英语原话）：
Far to the east, in the blasted, desolate Daggerlands, stands the machine-city of Monas Roth. Golgorai-Asthas, the Tyrant-God of Rust, once reigned over this land and its people as a cruel dictator; he viewed mortal-kind as little more than tools to be used in his burning factories and forges, or even as a gruesome fuel for his diabolical machines. The rule of Golgorai-Asthas was one of horror, torture, and neglect; his city was a prison, his people were slaves. So it had been for long ages, but it was not to last. In a series of events that would change the very nature of A’therys, the impossible came to pass: the immortal Golgorai-Asthas was slain by Mortal Heroes, and the Daggerlands were freed. This project was built on the RPG server

介绍（中文机翻）：
遥远的东方，在诅咒，荒凉Daggerlands，矗立着机器城市莫纳斯罗斯的。Golgorai-Asthas，防锈的霸天神，曾经统治了这片土地和人民的残忍的独裁者; 他认为凡人实物作为多一点在他的燃烧工厂和伪造所使用的工具，甚至是一个可怕的燃料，他那狰狞的机器。Golgorai-Asthas的规则是恐怖，酷刑，和忽视之一; 他的城市是一座监狱，他的人是奴隶。因此，它一直是漫长的时间，但它不是持久。在一个系列，将改变A'therys的性质的事件，不可能来传递：不朽Golgorai-Asthas是由凡人英雄被杀，Daggerlands中解脱出来。这个项目是建立在角色扮演服务器Atherys。
