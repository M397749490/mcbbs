1.8可行，只不过会比较麻烦


添加计分板：
  1. /scoreboard objectives add dirt stat.mineBlock.minecraft.dirt
复制代码

高频：
  1. /execute @a[score_dirt_min=1] ~-6 ~-6 ~-6 /scoreboard players tag @e[type=Item,dx=12,dy=12,dz=12,tag=!ns2] add ns {OnGround:1b,Item:{id:"minecraft:dirt"}}
复制代码
