简介 INFO
In Forest's Heart, players delve deep into the corrupted forest in a mission to destroy its corrupted heart.
Fight through the forest undergrowth, dive deep under the roots of the forest, and destroy the heart itself in this deadly adventure!
This map features a unique health mechanic where players will have to "capture" lost hearts to regain their health, but the choice may not always be straightforward as there are benefits to playing on the edge of death.
This map was created for the Strawberry Jam, a competition in which mapmakers make a mini-CTM in 72 hours. This was my entry into the jam.
Thanks for playing, and have fun!

在“森林之心”里，玩家需要进到腐化的森林里去毁坏已经腐化的心脏。
在森林里的灌木里战斗，潜入黑暗的森林树根，然后在这冒险里破坏那个心脏！
这个地图里有与众不同的血量系统，就是玩家需要“捕获”失落的心脏来回复玩家自己的血量，但是不一定会很容易得到他们。
这张地图是为了Strawberry Jam而制作的，Strawberry Jam是一个关于玩家在72小时内做一张CTM地图的比赛。
感谢游玩，祝你玩的开心！
