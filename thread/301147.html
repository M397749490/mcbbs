介绍
Crafting is an essential part to survival within Unturned 2. This list below provides all known crafting recipes currently in the game. This page can currently only be edited by trusted users due to deletions and griefing. If there is an incorrect recipe or something that we missed, please leave a constructive and accurate comment. The team is very busy working on the wikia, and we will update it as soon as possible.

If you have trouble finding a recipe, press Ctrl+F (find) and then type in the item you are looking for. Do not ask for it in the comments.

想要在未变异者中生存下来，合成是最重要的部分。以下的列表向您提供了目前游戏中所有已知的合成配方。为防止页面缺失或者被破坏，目前只有可以信任的用户才可以编辑这个页面。如果有配方错误或者缺失的，请你留下些有意义且准确的评论。团队正在忙碌的在Wikia上工作  ，我们会尽快更新它  。

如果你很难找到你想要的配方，可以按下Ctrl+F 再输入你需要查找的配方。不要在评论中询问这个

在游戏中访问这些合成配方
On the main menu of the game, there is also a button in the lower right hand corner that says "Community Wiki". When in game, simply press the Shift + Tab keys at the same time to open it! You could also grab some paper and a pencil to write down some important recipes.

Some recipes require leveling up the craftsman skill.

在游戏主菜单的右下角有一个“维基社区”的按钮。在游戏中你可以通过同时按下Shift + Tab打开它。当然，你也可以拿纸和笔记下其中的一些重要的配方。
