你这是 1.14，Lore 也改成 JSON 文本了。顺带，1.14 的 NBT 里支持用单引号包裹字符串，写 JSON 的时候不用那么麻烦地转义了：
/give @p minecraft:diamond_helmet{AttributeModifiers:[{Operation:0,Amount:10,UUIDLeast:1L,UUIDMost:1L,Slot:head,AttributeName:"generic.maxHealth",Name:"CBC"},{Operation:1,Amount:0.05,UUIDLeast:1L,UUIDMost:1L,Slot:head,AttributeName:"generic.movementSpeed",Name:"CBC"},{Operation:0,Amount:20,UUIDLeast:1L,UUIDMost:1L,Slot:head,AttributeName:"generic.armor",Name:"CBC"}],display:{Name:'{"text":"硬邦邦之头"}',Lore:['"我觉得你们才是带明星"']},Unbreakable:1,Enchantments:[{id:"minecraft:blast_protection",lvl:5},{id:"minecraft:feather_falling",lvl:5},{id:"minecraft:fire_protection",lvl:5},{id:"minecraft:projectile_protection",lvl:5}]} 1
