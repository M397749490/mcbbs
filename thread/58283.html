Let's run down the options that you are able to play with when creating a gun with examples and explanations in (parentheses).
  • Gun Name: The name of the gun. ("AK47", "M16", "Uzi", "UberSniper")
  • Bullet damage: The number of half hearts of damage that the gun will do to an entity. (4 = 2 hearts of damage, 10 = 5 hearts of damage, etc.)
  • Bullet range: The range of the bullet; how far can the bullet travel before it receives diminishing returns on its damage [you can't snipe people for full damage with a pistol if the pistol's range is short]. (2000 would be a sniper rifle, 100 might be a pistol)
  • Bullet item ID: The item ID of the bullet for the gun. This must be a unique ID that will not conflict with other items [whether they be items from other mods or items from the original game] (2000, 4051, etc.)
  • Rounds per minute: The number of rounds the gun can shoot in one minute. Note that the maximum number of rounds the game can handle per minute is around 4200 rpm. (60 for a sniper, 420 for a machine gun, etc.)
  • Reload time (ms): The length of time it will take to reload the gun in milliseconds. (1000 = 1 second, 2500 = 2.5 seconds)
  • Gun Item ID: The item ID of the gun. This must be a unique ID [and different from the bullet ID[ that will not conflict with other items [whether they be items from other mods or item from the original game (see Bullet item ID examples)
  • Clip size: The number of rounds the gun can hold in it's clip; how many bullets a full gun can shoot before it needs to reload (30 for an assault rifle, 6 for a sniper rifle, etc.)
  • Recoil: The vertical recoil of the gun (I would suggest to not put any value here for now until I fully implement recoil)
  • Horizontal recoil: The horizontal recoil of the gun (see Recoil explanation)
  • Fire mode: The mode of firing that the gun uses. (Single = you must click once for each round fired, Automatic = you can hold down the mouse to continuously fire, Burst = you must click once for every fire of a burst of three rounds)
  • Scope type: The scope that the gun has when pressing the zoom key. (Sniper = a crosshair with reduced vision)
  • Scope zoom: The factor that the scope zooms in by when pressing the zoom key. (1.0 = normal vision, 2.5 = 2.5x the vision of normal, etc.)
