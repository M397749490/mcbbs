  1. GlobalTexts:
  2. Miss: "&7*** You MISS <NPC>."
  3. Hit: "&f*** You hit <NPC> for <AMOUNT> damage."
  4. Crit1: "&e*** You injure <NPC> for <AMOUNT> damage."
  5. Crit2: "&6*** You MAIM <NPC> with your <ITEM> for <AMOUNT> damage."
  6. Crit3: "&c*** You DISEMBOWEL <NPC> with your <ITEM> for <AMOUNT> damage."
  7. Block: "&7*** You fail to penetrate <NPC>'s armor."
  8. Glance: "&7*** Your blow glances off <NPC> for <AMOUNT> damage."

改成
  1. GlobalTexts:
  2. Miss: ""
  3. Hit: ""
  4. Crit1: ""
  5. Crit2: ""
  6. Crit3: ""
  7. Block: ""
  8. Glance: ""
