原文：About Macaw's Roofs 1.0.1
Do you ever wanted to build a roof with actual roofs instead of stair blocks? Well if yes then you came to the right place!
Macaw's Roofs mod includes various types of roofs : Inner and Outer 1:1 Corners, Roof, Steep Roof, Lower Roof and a lot more!
They come in all log and plank variations aswell as 5 other color variations, also Rain Gutters!
You can craft them in vanilla, with really simple recipes! In the future updates I plan to add new types of roofs and a TON of variations (colors..).
This is the first release, a lot of things can be changed and a lot will be added!
翻译：
关于Macaw的屋顶v1.0.1
你想通过用真正的屋顶而不是台阶来制作屋顶吗？那你可真来对地方了！
Macaw的屋顶这个mod包含了各种类型的屋顶：45°转角（大概是这个意思吧），屋顶，陡坡屋顶，缓坡屋顶，水平屋顶和其他！
它包含了大量木板，原木和其他材质的变化，还涵盖五种颜色变化，同时也带有雨水槽的添加！
你可以用原版材料非常简单地合成这些物品！在未来版本中我计划添加更多种类的屋顶和大量颜色上的变化。
这是最初的版本，所以很多东西可能会改变或者添加！
