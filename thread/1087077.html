2.
  1. [09:41:33] [Server thread/WARN]: [Multiverse-Core] WorldManager: Can't load this world because the folder was deleted/moved: music
  2. [09:41:33] [Server thread/WARN]: [Multiverse-Core] Use '/mv remove' to remove it from the config!
复制代码
这是你已经删除了名为“music”的世界文件夹，而Multiverse-Core的config里仍有“music”世界的相关配置，只需在服务端运行的时候输入/mv remove music ；
