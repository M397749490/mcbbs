原帖变量
%EXP% To get your exp
%DISPLAYNAME% To get your displayname [CommingSoon]
%ONPL% To get the online players
%MAXPL% To get the max players
%FOODLEVEL% To get your foodlevel
%PLGAMEMODE% To get your gamemode
%PLNAME% To get your name
%SERVER% To get your server name Change into [Server.Properties]
%WORLDNAME% To see in wich world you are
%PLX% To get your X Location
%PLY% To get your Y Location
%PLZ% To get your Z Location
%DATE% To get your real-time in the world
