3d Item Physic Addon
        Do you want more realism in your game? You might realize that it wont look real because of the blocky nature, but what if blocks had weight and gravity? If you think that will be a cool addition, then this is the mod for you.
       你想在游戏中获得更真实的体验吗？你可能会意识到MC并不真实，因为它们都是块状的，但是假如物品都有质量和重力呢？如果你认为这个操作很棒，那么这个MOD适合你。
作者：DAKONBLACKROSE
介绍
        This addon changes items and blocks to be 3D drops. if you ever noticed the vanilla items and block float and spin on the ground till a player picks it up. well now you have to punch or click to pick the items up just like real life or crouch to pick up a lot near you that’s real to don’t ask questions. so if your tired of normal Minecraft drops because that’s to easy well now you can download this addon to make it real life drops try it out.
       这个MOD修改了方块和物品为3D样子的掉落。如果你曾经注意过物品漂浮在地面上旋转直到你捡起它，那么现在你需要点击才能捡起物品或蹲下去捡起很近的物品，这都不是问题，就像在现实生活中一样。所以，如果你厌倦了原版MC的简单掉落，下载这个MOD试试让掉落更加接近现实生活。
