总共四个命令方块

左前
  1. execute @e[type=Zombie,r=50] ~ ~ ~ /summon Bat ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"Vampire",CustomNameVisible:1}
复制代码
意识是在半径50米的范围内的僵尸处生成一只蝙蝠名字是vampire

左后
  1. tp @e[type=Zombie,r=50] ~ ~-200 ~
复制代码
把僵尸tp到基岩下了

右边也是如此
  1. /execute @e[type=Bat,r=50] ~ ~ ~ summon Zombie ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"Vampire",CustomNameVisible:1}
复制代码
  1. tp @e[type=Bat,r=50] ~ ~-200 ~
