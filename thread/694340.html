——工具说明——
到目前为止，它为Tinkers的Construct 2.0增加了4个新修饰符：
自动修复（与旧版本一样可升级；自动修复V上限）

原文：
It's a very straight forward mod with little needed documentation.
It adds (as of now) 4 new modifiers to Tinkers' Construct 2.0:
Auto-Repair (levelable like the old version; caps at Auto-Repair V)
Bonus Modifiers (level I, II and III), they work the same as the old bonus modifiers
Because of the nature of the new Tool Forge / Tool Station, I had to add new items to make working recipes for the bonus modifiers.
This is why there are now "Bonus Modifier" items added, as well as a moss ball. The bonus modifiers use (mostly) the same resources that the original Tinkers' tool station recipe did - but it's a normal crafting recipe that you then apply to the tool after crafting it.
注明：值得注意的是 每个工具箱只能用一次，改良修复速度慢，但是不需要消耗经验感谢反馈！
