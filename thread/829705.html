TO USERS上面的信息其实是给开发者看的
另外其实这并不是二楼说的问题 而是它找不到这个矿物词典名称对应的物品（It adds an item (type: class net.minecraft.item.Item) which is currently unknown to the game registry）
然后提示在像这样用矿物注册矿物词典物品的时候只支持一个值而已，然后说不打算关掉这个提示，要解决这个问题只要在GameRegistry阶段后注册矿物词典名即可（This dictionary item can only support a single value when registered with ores like this, and NO I am not going to turn this spam off. Just register your ore dictionary entries after the GameRegistry）
