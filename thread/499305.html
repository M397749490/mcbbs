[compass]About 关于[compass]

This is just a smaller build of ours that we created over a period of 48 hours on the BlockWorks server. The Theme for the build was "Down the rabbit Hole". We took this to mean that it was an Alice in Wonderland-themed build, and so we created this nice little house full of references to Lewis Carroll's works. We are very thankful for BlockWorks' hosting, judging, and topic choosing. Many thanks to Octovon for suggesting this build off and getting it organized, and to Splekh for the render! Go check these guys out if you haven't already haven't already seen their work.
这在我们已经运行48小时的BlockWorks服务器里只是一个较小的建筑，这个建筑的主题是“下到兔子洞”。我们认为这是一个关于爱丽丝梦游仙境的主题来构建。所以我们创造的这个作品里充满了对卡罗尔作品的引用。我们非常感谢Blockworks的主持评审与主题的选择，感谢octovon提出的建议。也感谢splekh的渲染！如果你还没有看到过他们的工作，那么就去看看他们吧！
