BetterEnchantment
1.BetterEnchantment
BetterEnchantment可以干啥？
  • 重命名附魔名称  √
  • 移除过长的附魔名字  √
  • 选择两种数字（罗马数字/阿拉伯数字）  √
  • 添加稀有值  √
1.BetterEnchantment
What is BetterEnchantment doing?
  • The ability to rename enchantments
  • Remove long enchantment names (such as enchantment.level.15)
  • Choose between roman numerals or numbers
  • Add a rarity value
