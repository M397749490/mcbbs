插件介绍：

About

xAuth was initially designed to protect a server and its players while running in offline-mode. As the plugin enhanced over the time the main purpose changed. Lets say you run a community page. With xAuth players can authenticate against their forum accounts or against a web driven account management. It is also possible to get the creditionals from a foreign database.
