== Verse 1 (First Load Up) ==
My best friend told me a year ago,
he'd found an awesome game.
I bought a code and when I logged on,
it was a giant world made up of blocks.
I turned my render distance up to far,
He said "Let's go build an epic base"
Roof over head with a wooden door,
That's when I stood up and said!

I'll stack a 64 set,

All the blocks just form this way!
Oh a Zombie runaway!
Found a sword, I'm gonna slay!
All the blocks just form this way!

Mine. Craft.

We craft a furnace, to smelt some gear.
And put our iron picks to use.
We start descending into the unknown,
But I'm running out of block to use!
(Can't use Gravel, it just falls down!)
I don't believe it, I think I see'im.
It's the mythical, he-ro-brine.
(He! Ro! Brine!)
