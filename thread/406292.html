Hi!   The Master Chef mod add's a lot of new foods, crops & blocks!  This mod is a very awesome, try it!   We have added tons of foods and 7 crops (strawberry, blueberry, tomato, bell pepper, onion, asparagus, and salad!)  U can find strawberry bushes randomly in all forest biomes. (Taiga, and other forests)  U can find blueberry bushes randomly in the cold forest biomes. (Taiga)  Tomato bushes can be found at the extreme hills biome.  Bell pepper bushes can be found also in the extreme hills biome, but is very rare.  The Onion bush can be found at birch forests.  Asparagus randomly generates in roofed(dark) forests.  Salad Seeds can be get from 1% chance in tall grass.   The other furnaces all have dual inputs.  In the sauce maker, you need to place a bowl in the second input.  In the Ice Cream Maker, you need to "store" the fuel in it.  One of the inputs needs to be filled with sugar, the other one needs something like strawberry, chocolate,...  In the Cook Furnace needs one input butter, in the other one just the chicken or things like that.  It cooks twice as fast, but you can only cook food in there.

大意：在大厨的mod的附加的很多新的食物，农作物和方块！
这个mod是一个非常真棒的MOD，试试吧！
我们增加了食物，7种作物（草莓，蓝莓，番茄，甜椒，洋葱，芦笋，沙拉！）
可以在所有森林生物群落发现草莓灌木丛随机。
可以在寒冷的森林生物群落发现蓝莓灌木丛随机。
番茄灌木丛可以在极端的山丘生物群落被发现。
柿子椒灌木丛也可以在极端的山丘生物群系发现，但是是非常罕见的。
洋葱灌木可以在白桦林找到。
芦笋随机生成的有顶（暗）的森林。
沙拉种子可以在高草丛中有1%的几率获得
其他炉都有双输入。
在配料炉，你需要把一个碗放在第一个口，在第二个输入原料。
在冰淇淋机，冰晶当燃料。
冰激凌机之一的输入需糖，另一个需要输入像草莓，巧克力，...类似的东西
厨子炉需要一个输入端输入黄油，在另一个端是鸡肉或类似的东西。
它烹制要比熔炉快一倍，但你只能在里面煮食物。
